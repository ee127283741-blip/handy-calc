- **Search intent:** Informational / how-to. Beginner wants a short method and examples that improve results immediately.

## Points competitors commonly cover
- Be specific, give context, state format and length, give examples, iterate.
- Break complex tasks into steps; assign a role.

## Gaps / weaknesses
- None of the pages read cite the vendors' own prompting documentation.
- Before/after examples are one-liners on consumer topics (travel, dinner), not office work.
- No discussion of token count or API cost of longer prompts.
- No checklist you can run before pressing Enter; no advice on checking the answer.

## Our outline
1. Two paragraphs: what makes a prompt good; what vendors themselves recommend.
2. 8-point checklist, each tied to an official guide.
3. Three office before/after pairs with described outputs.
4. Does a longer prompt cost more? Real tiktoken counts (11 vs 147 tokens) and cost math using ai-pricing.ts; reusable system prompt and caching example.
5. Checking the answer; FAQs.

Gap summary: Competitors give generic "be specific" advice with consumer examples and no sources; we tie each checklist item to OpenAI, Anthropic and Google's own prompting docs, show office before/after pairs, and measure the real token and dollar cost of a longer prompt.
