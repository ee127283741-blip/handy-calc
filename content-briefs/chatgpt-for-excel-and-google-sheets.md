## Points competitors commonly cover
- Describe what you want in plain language; AI returns a formula; paste and test.
- OpenAI's add-in for Excel/Sheets; Gemini in Sheets and Copilot in Excel.

## Gaps / weaknesses
- Pages read show prompts but almost no actual formulas, and none verified.
- No Excel vs Sheets argument differences (XLOOKUP if_not_found vs missing_value; version availability).
- No method for testing a formula on known answers.
- Little on what spreadsheet data is safe to paste.

## Our outline
1. Two paragraphs: AI is good at formula syntax, bad at knowing your data layout.
2. The prompt pattern: layout, sheet app/version, desired result, edge cases.
3. Sample data table (8 invoices) + 8 formulas, each with prompt, formula, explanation and verified result (recalculated in LibreOffice 25.2): SUMIFS, XLOOKUP, COUNTIFS by date, overdue IF/AND, PROPER(TRIM), NETWORKDAYS, AVERAGEIFS, SUMIFS date range.
4. How to check any AI formula (known-answer test, edge cases, hand count).
5. Excel vs Sheets differences from Microsoft/Google docs; data safety; FAQs.

Gap summary: Competitors show prompts but not formulas, and never verify them; we give eight real formulas on a sample invoice table with each result recalculated and explained, the Excel vs Google Sheets argument differences from official docs, and a known-answer test for any AI formula.
