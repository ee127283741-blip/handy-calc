Buying new wheels is a leap of faith: a photo on a product page rarely shows how they will look on your own car. Viztunr removes that guess for tuning shops. The one-person Polish company, founded in May 2026, had 8 paying shops and $742 in MRR by November 2026 ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/viztunr)). It is tiny, but it is a clear example of a narrow vertical AI product.

## How does the Viztunr try-on button work?

Viztunr adds a try-on button to a tuning shop's product page, letting customers see wheels, wraps, spoilers and tips on their own car before they book the install ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/viztunr)). The pitch is to remove hesitation between browsing and booking, increasing installs and reducing returns.

> "Try-on features for tuning shops" — Viztunr company description ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/viztunr))

The buyer is the shop, not the car owner, which makes this a B2B product.

The listing reports a team of 1 person ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/viztunr)), and the company is bootstrapped.

## Why does Viztunr price by renders?

Viztunr prices by renders, which likely tracks the compute cost of generating each try-on image. All plans include every feature, and unused renders roll over ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/viztunr)). Rollover removes the fear of paying for unused capacity, which matters for small shops with uneven traffic. Customers are found through cold email and SEO, and the product runs on Next.js, Supabase, Vercel and Stripe.

## What are the lessons from Viztunr for vertical AI?

- **Borrow a proven idea for a new niche.** Try-on works in fashion; Viztunr brings it to car tuning.
- **Sell to the merchant, not the shopper.** Shops pay because try-on can lead to booked installs.
- **Price on the cost driver.** Render-based plans track the expense of generating images.
- **Start small and verified.** $2,351 all time is modest, but every dollar is checked through Stripe.
