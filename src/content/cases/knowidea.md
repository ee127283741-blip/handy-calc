KNOWIDEA ARR reached about $500K four months after launch, and its co-founder did not build the product. Yatharth Sejpal studied organizational psychology and business at the University of Waterloo and worked as a project lead at TD Bank. He saw a problem, pitched a solution and recruited someone to build it, then sold it himself. He wrote on Indie Hackers that KNOWIDEA, a predictive intelligence platform, now has enterprise clients in mining, manufacturing, financial services and environmental services ([founder-reported, Indie Hackers](https://www.indiehackers.com/post/hitting-500k-arr-in-four-months-ZMs1IKVqpctHenipKau5)).

## Who is behind KNOWIDEA, and what is their background?

KNOWIDEA was co-founded in September 2025 by Yatharth Sejpal, whose training is in people and business, not software ([Indie Hackers](https://www.indiehackers.com/post/hitting-500k-arr-in-four-months-ZMs1IKVqpctHenipKau5)). He completed Honors Organizational Psychology and Business at Waterloo, then worked as a project lead at TD Bank, and left to start the company. The technical side came from Brian, a candidate with a Georgia Tech PhD in neurosymbolic AI whom Sejpal reached out to. Team size and Brian's full name are not disclosed in the post.

## How did KNOWIDEA go from idea to product in five weeks?

Sejpal described the problem and the solution he wanted, and Brian built it. The founder's own account of the handoff is short:

> "Five weeks later, Brian handed me a fully functioning first product." — Yatharth Sejpal ([founder-reported, Indie Hackers](https://www.indiehackers.com/post/hitting-500k-arr-in-four-months-ZMs1IKVqpctHenipKau5))

He mentions a Next.js and TypeScript front end. He does not credit an AI app builder; the speed came from hiring one deeply qualified person instead of a team. That is a different path from the vibe-coded apps elsewhere on this site.

## How much ARR has KNOWIDEA made since launch?

KNOWIDEA reached about $500K in ARR four months after launch, Sejpal wrote ([founder-reported, Indie Hackers](https://www.indiehackers.com/post/hitting-500k-arr-in-four-months-ZMs1IKVqpctHenipKau5)). That is about $42K a month (calc: ARR / 12). Pricing, the number of clients and total revenue are not disclosed, and no independent source confirms the figure.

## Why does KNOWIDEA sell decisions instead of AI?

Sejpal argues that the product is the decision a customer makes, and AI is only the mechanism, so KNOWIDEA does not compete on models ([Indie Hackers](https://www.indiehackers.com/post/hitting-500k-arr-in-four-months-ZMs1IKVqpctHenipKau5)). He sells personally, building relationships with buyers. He notes that a CFO who recommends a tool internally puts their own name on that decision. Our reading: in enterprise sales the buyer's personal risk is part of the product, and a founder who sells in person can address it directly.

## How does KNOWIDEA compare with Fyxer AI and EchoBase AI?

[Fyxer AI](/cases/fyxer-ai/) was started by people who ran an executive assistant agency, and [EchoBase AI](/cases/echobase-ai/) served consultants. All three sell AI to business buyers and were led by founders whose strength was the customer, not code. The difference is the build. EchoBase's founders said a cheap first developer cost them dearly in wasted payments; KNOWIDEA went the other way and recruited a PhD in neurosymbolic AI.

Our reading: a non-technical founder's most important technical decision is who builds. KNOWIDEA spent its effort on one strong hire and kept the founder on sales, which matches how [GenAIPI](/cases/genaipi/) relied on its founder's enterprise relationships.

## What are the takeaways from KNOWIDEA for non-technical founders?

- **Sell the outcome.** Sejpal frames the product as a better decision, not a smarter model.
- **Hire one strong builder.** A single PhD-level hire shipped a working product in five weeks.
- **Keep the founder on sales.** Sejpal leads every deal and builds trust with buyers like CFOs.
- **Treat self-reported ARR with care.** The $500K figure comes only from the founder's post.
