Nine dollars a month is cheap enough that nobody thinks twice. ThreadMaster has built a six-figure business on that price. Austin Armstrong's AI thread generator, founded in August 2025, had 928 active subscriptions, $4,646 in MRR and $122,089 in lifetime revenue by November 2026 ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/threadmaster)). It is a clear case of a low-price tool sold to a creator audience.

## What does ThreadMaster generate for creators?

ThreadMaster writes social media threads. It combines an AI generator, templates scored by performance and a library of viral images, with output ready to copy and paste into Facebook, LinkedIn, Twitter/X and Threads ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/threadmaster)). The audience is content creators and social media marketers.

> "Generate viral threads in seconds" — ThreadMaster product description on TrustMRR ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/threadmaster))

The product does one thing. It does not schedule posts or manage accounts; it produces the text and images a creator then posts.

## Why does Austin Armstrong's audience matter to ThreadMaster?

A $9 product needs many buyers, and a founder with an audience can reach them without paid ads. Austin Armstrong, ThreadMaster's founder, had 6,353 followers on X in November 2026 ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/threadmaster)). The website, threadmaster.ai, shows an Ahrefs Domain Rating of 42 out of 100 on the listing, a sign of an established backlink profile.

All-time revenue of $122,089 is roughly 26 months of current MRR (calc: $122,089 / $4,646, [from TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/threadmaster)), even though the company is about 15 months old. Earlier months likely brought in more than recent MRR suggests; the monthly history is not disclosed.

## How does ThreadMaster differ from Post Bridge and Draft AI?

All three help people post more on social media, but each does a different job.

| Company | Job | MRR | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| ThreadMaster | Writes threads and suggests images | $4,646 (Nov 2026) | [verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/threadmaster) |
| [Post Bridge](/cases/post-bridge/) | Publishes one post to many platforms | $49,650 (Nov 2026) | [verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/post-bridge) |
| [Draft AI](/cases/draft-ai/) | Turns voice notes into posts | $19,894 (Nov 2026) | [verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/draft-ai) |

Our observation: Post Bridge owns the distribution step and Draft AI the capture step, while ThreadMaster sits in the middle as a writing aid. Its low price makes it an easy add-on to either workflow, but also caps how much each customer is worth.

## What should Threads tool makers learn from ThreadMaster?

- **Cheap and focused can scale.** 928 subscriptions at $9 a month produced $122,089 lifetime revenue.
- **An audience replaces an ad budget.** A founder with 6,353 X followers and a Domain Rating of 42 starts with reach.
- **Watch revenue per user.** About $5 of MRR per subscription is thin, so churn matters a lot.
- **Lifetime revenue tells the backstory.** Totals far above current MRR suggest an earlier peak.
