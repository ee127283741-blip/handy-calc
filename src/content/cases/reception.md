Most mobile apps learn why users left only after they are gone. Reception wants app makers to hear about problems while the user is still inside the app. It is one of the youngest cases on this site: founded in October 2026, it showed $1,017 in MRR, 44 active subscriptions and $2,920 in all-time revenue by November 2026 ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/reception)).

## What does Reception add inside iOS apps?

Reception adds a way for app developers to talk to users inside their app. The company-written listing says it helps solve problems before churn, gather reviews and upsell, for iOS app developers ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/reception)). The stack lists Next.js, Swift, React Native and Expo, which covers both native and cross-platform apps.

> "Helping apps make more money." — Reception listing ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/reception))

The tagline frames support as revenue, not cost, which is the angle most likely to persuade an indie app maker.

## How much revenue has Reception made in its first weeks?

Reception had $1,017 in MRR and 44 active subscriptions in November 2026, and all $2,920 of its all-time revenue came in the last 30 days ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/reception)). TrustMRR checks these numbers through a Stripe API key.

## Who is behind Reception?

The listing names two founders, Frederick James and Andi, a team of 2-5 people in the United Kingdom, and bootstrapped funding ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/reception)). Marketing channels are not disclosed. The listing's audience estimate is small, about 8 users, which sits oddly beside 44 active subscriptions; the listing does not explain the difference.

## How does Reception compare with Chatbase and SiteGPT?

All three sit in customer support, but Reception is the only one built around mobile apps.

| Company | Where the conversation happens |
|---|---|
| Reception | Inside a developer's iOS app |
| [Chatbase](/cases/chatbase/) | AI chatbot trained on a company's documents |
| [SiteGPT](/cases/sitegpt/) | AI chatbot trained on a company's website |

Our observation: Chatbase and SiteGPT automate answers with AI. Reception's listing focuses on the channel itself, reaching users inside the app before they churn or leave a review, and does not describe AI features, so we do not claim any.

## What can early founders learn from Reception?

- **Frame support as revenue.** "Make more money" sells better than "answer tickets".
- **Pick a sharp platform.** iOS app developers are a specific, reachable audience.
- **Verify early.** Stripe-verified numbers from week one give a public baseline.
- **Simple pricing helps.** One $25 monthly plan is easy to understand.
