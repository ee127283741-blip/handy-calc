## Origin of the idea
Osika's open-source GPT Engineer showed that a model could scaffold a whole codebase from a prompt. The commercial question was whether non-engineers would pay to build real web apps by chatting.

## How they started
The team turned the project into a hosted product, rebranded as Lovable and launched publicly in late 2024. It is venture-backed, not bootstrapped.

## Tools & tech stack
Frontier LLMs generating React front ends, with Supabase integration for databases and auth, GitHub sync and one-click deploys.

## Marketing
Viral demos on X and LinkedIn, an active community of "vibe coders", and templates people share.

## Sales & pricing
Freemium with monthly credit-based plans and team and business tiers.

## Monthly revenue
About **$8.3M/month**, implied from the $100M ARR reported in July 2025.

## Total revenue
Not disclosed.

## Founding date & timeline
- **2023:** Company started from GPT Engineer.
- **Late 2024:** Public launch as Lovable.
- **Jul 2025:** $100M ARR reported.
- **Nov 2025:** Nearly 8M users.

## Team size
Not disclosed with a dated primary source.

## Key takeaways
- Open source can be the top of the funnel for a paid product.
- Letting non-developers ship apps unlocked a huge new market.
- Credit-based pricing aligns cost with heavy model usage.
