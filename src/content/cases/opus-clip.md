## Origin of the idea
The team first built livestreaming tools that did not take off. Clipping highlights out of long streams was the feature users actually wanted, so they rebuilt the company around it.

## How they started
After the pivot in 2023 they launched a tool that finds the most engaging moments in a long video and cuts them into vertical shorts with captions.

## Tools & tech stack
Speech-to-text, LLMs that score moments for "virality", computer vision for reframing to vertical, and automatic captions.

## Marketing
Product-led growth among podcasters and YouTubers, plus creator partnerships and a free tier that watermarks output.

## Sales & pricing
Freemium with monthly credit-based plans by minutes of video processed.

## Monthly revenue
About **$1.7M/month**, implied from the ~$20M ARR reported in March 2025 (unverified: ARR Club repeats it from a newsletter we could not check).
