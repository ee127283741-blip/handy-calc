## Origin of the idea
Image-to-image diffusion models made it possible to keep the shape of a room while changing everything else. Levels saw an obvious paying audience: homeowners, renters, real-estate agents and interior designers who want to preview a style before spending money.

## How they started
He launched a simple upload-and-pick-a-style page in the autumn of 2022, shortly after Stable Diffusion became public, and shared results on X.

## Tools & tech stack
Same lightweight approach as his other products: a PHP and jQuery front end on a single server, with diffusion models run through a hosted GPU API.

## Marketing
Before/after images are the marketing. Levels posted them on X, and the product was listed on Product Hunt and many AI tool directories.

## Sales & pricing
Paid plans for a set number of renders per month. Professional users such as agents and stagers are the natural heavy users.
