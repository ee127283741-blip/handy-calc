WaLead AI picked one country and went deep. Instead of a global lead database, it sells prospecting built around Spanish companies and LinkedIn, priced from €165 a month. In November 2026 that focus showed $24,920 in MRR and $513,079 in all-time revenue ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/walead-ai)), roughly three years after its December 2023 founding.

## What does WaLead AI sell to Spanish sales teams?

WaLead AI sells a single go-to-market platform that finds, enriches, contacts and qualifies leads. The TrustMRR listing says the core problem is that these steps usually live in separate tools ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/walead-ai)). WaLead joins them into one product.

The Spanish-language homepage lists the parts: a B2B database, AI agents, LinkedIn automation, enrichment and a copilot ([founder-reported, WaLead homepage](https://walead.ai/)). It claims 16M+ B2B contacts in Spain, waterfall enrichment for verified emails and mobile numbers, and more than 1,000 B2B companies as customers. Trials come with 1,000 free credits and no card.

> "10x your sales on LinkedIn with AI" — WaLead AI listing ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/walead-ai))

WaLead AI has earned $513,079 in all-time revenue, with $24,920 in MRR from 271 active subscriptions in November 2026 ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/walead-ai)). Revenue in the last 30 days was $46,831, nearly twice MRR.

## Why did WaLead AI build around one country's data?

WaLead's bet is that local data beats global coverage for local buyers. Its homepage calls the database the best in Spain and shows examples built on Spanish companies and job titles ([founder-reported, WaLead homepage](https://walead.ai/)). Prompts such as finding SaaS CMOs in Madrid make the point.

A country focus changes the competition. Global tools cover Spain as one market among many, while WaLead can sell on depth, Spanish-language support and local events. The homepage promotes its own Day One event, a sign that community is part of the sales motion. The listing estimates about 1,000 users, which fits a product sold to teams rather than individuals.

Its entry price, from €165 a month, is higher than many self-serve tools, which matches its smaller subscriber count and higher revenue per account.

## What lessons does WaLead AI offer B2B founders?

- **A country can be a niche.** Spain-only data and Spanish-language marketing gave WaLead a clear position ([founder-reported, WaLead homepage](https://walead.ai/)).
- **Bundle the pipeline.** Finding, enriching, contacting and qualifying leads in one tool is the core pitch.
- **Watch the revenue mix.** 30-day revenue of $46,831 against $24,920 MRR shows non-recurring income matters ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/walead-ai)).
- **Free credits lower the barrier.** 1,000 credits with no card let buyers test the data before paying.
