Payout app revenue reached $20K a month 50 days after launch, for an app built in under two weeks. Connor Burd, 23, makes mobile apps without a computer science background by "vibe coding," describing what he wants to AI tools and letting them write the code. He told Starter Story that Payout, which helps people find class action settlements they may be owed, hit $20K a month 50 days in ([founder-reported, Starter Story](https://www.starterstory.com/stories/i-vibe-coded-a-20k-month-mobile-app-in-14-days)). It is one app in a portfolio he says earns more than a million dollars a year.

## What is the Payout app?

Payout helps users discover class action settlements they may qualify for. Many people are owed small amounts from settlements over data breaches, product defects or false advertising, but never claim them because they do not know the cases exist. Payout collects open settlements and shows users which ones may apply to them. Burd built it in under two weeks with AI coding tools such as Claude ([founder-reported, Starter Story](https://www.starterstory.com/stories/i-vibe-coded-a-20k-month-mobile-app-in-14-days)). Prices, team size and total revenue are not disclosed.

> "I'm 23 years old and my apps do over a million dollars in sales a year" — Connor Burd ([founder-reported, Starter Story](https://www.starterstory.com/stories/i-vibe-coded-a-20k-month-mobile-app-in-14-days))

## How did Payout make $20K a month so fast?

Payout reached $20K a month in 50 days through influencer videos, not ads. Burd said he launched with one niche creator, found the video format that worked, then repeated it with more creators ([founder-reported, Starter Story](https://www.starterstory.com/stories/i-vibe-coded-a-20k-month-mobile-app-in-14-days)). On The Superwall Podcast he later described Payout at about $45,000 a month, almost entirely from influencers ([founder-reported, The Superwall Podcast](https://www.buzzsprout.com/2522779/episodes/18406250-connor-burd-this-23-yr-old-genius-makes-1m-year-with-no-code-apps-here-s-how)). The two figures come from different dates.

## Why does Connor Burd build many small apps?

Burd treats apps as a portfolio: he builds quickly, keeps the ones that sell and moves on from the rest. He says his apps together bring in more than a million dollars a year ([founder-reported, Starter Story](https://www.starterstory.com/stories/i-vibe-coded-a-20k-month-mobile-app-in-14-days)). When an app takes two weeks instead of six months, failure is cheap, and the skill that matters is picking ideas and marketing them. The Superwall Podcast described his apps as built with no-code methods ([The Superwall Podcast](https://www.buzzsprout.com/2522779/episodes/18406250-connor-burd-this-23-yr-old-genius-makes-1m-year-with-no-code-apps-here-s-how)).

## How does Payout compare with STOPPR and PepAI?

Payout, [STOPPR](/cases/stoppr/) and [PepAI](/cases/pepai/) are all mobile subscription apps built quickly by founders without engineering backgrounds. STOPPR copied a proven app; PepAI picked a niche trending on TikTok; Payout found an overlooked consumer need, unclaimed settlement money. Payout grew the fastest of the three, and its founder is the most experienced, with a portfolio of earlier apps behind it.

Our reading: Payout's idea has a built-in hook, "you may be owed money," that suits short influencer videos. That made distribution easy. The risk is that settlement data is public, so competitors can copy the product as fast as Burd built it.

## What lessons does Payout hold for portfolio app builders?

- **Pick ideas with a hook.** "You may be owed money" sells itself in a 30-second video.
- **Treat apps as bets.** Two-week builds make it cheap to try many ideas and keep the winners.
- **Find the creator format, then repeat it.** Payout scaled by copying one successful influencer video across creators.
- **Speed cuts both ways.** If an app takes two weeks to build, a competitor can copy it just as fast.
