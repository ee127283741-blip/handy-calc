Flowmance Templates has a revenue shape that MRR alone hides. The Albanian template library, founded in January 2025, showed only $362 in MRR in November 2026, yet it had collected $74,493 all time ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/flowmance-templates)). The reason is its pricing: a yearly plan and a lifetime deal, neither of which shows up neatly as monthly recurring revenue.

## What does Flowmance Templates sell to web designers?

Flowmance Templates sells unlimited access to a library of 420+ premium Webflow, Framer and Figma templates plus 12,500+ ready-made sections ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/flowmance-templates)). Buyers are web designers, developers, agencies, freelancers and startups who want to launch a site without starting from scratch.

> "Choose from 420+ polished templates and 12,500+ ready-made sections for agencies, freelancers, studios, and SaaS teams that need to ship without starting from zero." — Flowmance Templates listing ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/flowmance-templates))

## Why does a lifetime deal shrink Flowmance's MRR?

A $499 lifetime purchase is revenue once, not every month, so it never adds to MRR ([founder-reported pricing, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/flowmance-templates)). A $199 yearly plan counts as about $17 a month. For a template business, that trade can make sense: designers buy when a client project starts, and a lifetime deal turns that moment into a large payment.

The cost is predictability. With 22 active subscriptions, almost all future revenue depends on new buyers finding the library through its blog, content marketing and X, the channels it lists. Its domain rating of 74 out of 100 suggests that content work has paid off in backlinks.

## What can digital product sellers learn from Flowmance Templates?

- **Pick the metric that fits the model.** Lifetime deals make MRR look small while all-time revenue grows.
- **Breadth is the product.** 420+ templates across three tools is a reason to buy access, not one template.
- **Content compounds.** A blog-led channel mix and a domain rating of 74 point to durable search traffic.
- **Lifetime pricing trades future for now.** It brings cash forward but leaves little recurring base.
