Lumoo ARR reached €700K in nine months, and the company was built by people who sell clothes, not software. Peter Thörngren and Henrik Skagerlind Fasth brought about 25 years of fashion and retail experience between them, and built their AI content platform on Lovable rather than hiring an engineering team ([founder-reported, Lovable](https://lovable.dev/blog/lumoo-ai-fashion-platform)). Lumoo lets brands create product images, virtual try-ons and translated content with AI. More than 15 major brands signed up in its first nine months.

## Who built Lumoo, and how?

Henrik Skagerlind Fasth started building with Lovable when the tool was still called GPT Engineer, experimenting with virtual clothing try-ons ([Lovable](https://lovable.dev/blog/lumoo-ai-fashion-platform)). With Peter Thörngren, now CEO, he turned those experiments into one platform that connects about 30 AI models to generate photorealistic product visuals, try-ons and localized content. Their advantage was knowing exactly what fashion marketing teams struggle with. Team size and prices are not disclosed.

## How did Lumoo reach €700K ARR?

Lumoo reached €700K ARR by selling to brands it already understood. Lovable's customer story reports €700K ARR nine months after launch, more than 15 major brands including Gant, Brothers and AWNR Group, and a target of €1M ARR ([founder-reported, Lovable](https://lovable.dev/blog/lumoo-ai-fashion-platform)). That is about €58K a month (calc). Total revenue is not disclosed.

## Why did Lovable's CEO invest in Lumoo?

Lumoo raised a SEK 6M (about €550K) angel round in December 2025, and Lovable CEO Anton Osika was one of the investors ([media-reported, Tech.eu](https://tech.eu/2025/12/05/lovable-built-lumoo-gets-funding-from-lovable-ceo/)). Like Plinq, Lumoo is a showcase for Lovable: domain experts building a business on its platform. Thörngren said the money would fund hiring and platform development.

> "This investment is a strong validation of the rapid growth we are experiencing" — Peter Thörngren ([founder-reported, Tech.eu](https://tech.eu/2025/12/05/lovable-built-lumoo-gets-funding-from-lovable-ceo/))

## How does Lumoo compare with Plinq and Photoroom?

Lumoo and [Plinq](/cases/plinq/) are both Lovable-built companies whose founders could not code, and Lovable's CEO invested in both. The difference is the buyer. Plinq sells one-off safety checks to consumers; Lumoo sells a business tool to fashion brands, which means fewer customers paying more each. [Photoroom](/cases/photoroom/) is the established competitor in AI product imagery, built by an engineering team and sold to millions of sellers.

Our reading: Lumoo's edge is industry knowledge. The founders did not need to discover what fashion marketers want, because they had been those marketers. Lovable let them turn that knowledge into software without the cost and delay of a development team, which is why they reached brand customers in months.

## What lessons does Lumoo offer industry experts?

- **Domain expertise is the moat.** Decades in fashion told the founders which problems brands would pay to solve.
- **AI builders let experts skip the dev team.** Lumoo was built on Lovable instead of with hired engineers.
- **Sell B2B for bigger tickets.** Fifteen brands produced €700K ARR, far fewer customers than a consumer app needs.
- **Orchestrate models, don't train them.** Lumoo connects about 30 existing AI models into one workflow.
