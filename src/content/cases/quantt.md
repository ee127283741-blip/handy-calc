Quant finance jobs are famously hard to get, and most of the prep material is books and videos. Quantt bets that people learn faster by building. Less than a year after its December 2025 founding, the UK platform had 149 active subscriptions and $37,788 in lifetime revenue ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/quantt)). The founder is not disclosed on the listing.

## How does Quantt teach quantitative finance?

Quantt teaches through code: every lesson is code the student writes and tests the student passes, with instant feedback across finance, maths and technology ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/quantt)). The company frames passive quant education as the problem it fixes.

> "Most quant education is passive. Quantt makes you build." — Quantt company statement ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/quantt))

The target learners are CS graduates, career changers and junior professionals breaking into quant roles. The stack is lean: Next.js on the frontend, a Neon database and Stripe for billing.

## Is Quantt's pricing high for an online course?

Quantt charges £39.95 a month or £249.95 a year, with custom Enterprise pricing and a 30-day money-back guarantee on all paid plans ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/quantt)). That is premium for a coding course, but the buyer is chasing a high-paying career, so the price is small next to the outcome. The annual plan works out to roughly half the monthly cost over a year, which nudges committed learners toward upfront payment.

Quantt, [Codédex](/cases/codedex/) and [DataExpert](/cases/dataexpert/) all sell technical education online, but Quantt is the most narrowly focused on one career track.

## What can edtech founders learn from Quantt?

- **Sell the career, not the course.** Quantt targets people breaking into quant jobs, which justifies premium pricing.
- **Active learning is the differentiator.** Code, tests and feedback set it apart from passive video courses.
- **A guarantee lowers risk on a pricey plan.** The 30-day money-back promise covers all paid tiers.
- **Niche can work fast.** 149 subscriptions in under a year is real traction for a specialised topic.
