SketchUp files only open in the version that made them or a newer one. Send a 2026 file to a colleague on SketchUp 2022, and it will not open. DownConvert sells the fix for that one annoyance. The tool, founded in December 2025 by Timothee Meyrieux, had $40,316 in lifetime revenue and $4,326 in last-30-day revenue by November 2026 ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/digital-natives-services-fz-llc)).

## What problem does DownConvert solve for SketchUp users?

DownConvert converts SketchUp files from newer versions into older ones, so a file saved in SketchUp 2026 can be opened in 2025, 2024 and back to 2017. Users can try it free and pay only if they need more ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/digital-natives-services-fz-llc)).

> "New versions of SketchUp can't be open on old one." — DownConvert listing on TrustMRR ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/digital-natives-services-fz-llc))

The listing frames the alternative as paying for a licence upgrade every year; it mentions $399 a year for SketchUp Pro ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/digital-natives-services-fz-llc)). The audience is both businesses and individuals.

## Why do DownConvert's own numbers disagree with the verified ones?

The company's own listing text is older than the verified figures. It says "475$ MRR", about $465 a month and $2,418 in total since May, all one-off with no subscriptions, and that the Lifetime Unlimited plan makes up 84% of revenue ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/digital-natives-services-fz-llc)).

The verified Stripe data now shows $40,316 all time and 57 active subscriptions. Either revenue grew sharply after that text was written, or recurring plans were added later. We treat the Stripe numbers as current and the listing text as an earlier snapshot; the date of that text is not disclosed.

## Where does DownConvert get its traffic?

DownConvert says it gets about 12,000 visitors a month and has about 22,000 users, with traffic coming 100% from organic Google search ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/digital-natives-services-fz-llc)). That fits the product: people search for exactly this problem, often with the version numbers in the query. The team is 2–5 people, and its website has a Domain Rating of 28.

## What lessons does DownConvert hold for niche tool builders?

- **Monetise a vendor's friction.** DownConvert earns from the gap between SketchUp versions.
- **Exact-match search can be the whole funnel.** The company reports 100% organic Google traffic.
- **Cheap one-time pricing converts.** $3.90 per file or $24.90 for unlimited is far below a licence upgrade.
- **Check old listing text against live data.** DownConvert's self-reported figures lag its verified Stripe totals.
