Jobhuntify has a puzzle on its TrustMRR page. It shows $250,444 in all-time revenue but only $1,997 in monthly recurring revenue in November 2026 ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/jobhuntify-com)). At today's pace, that total would take more than a decade to earn. The listing gives an April 2014 founding date, so this is an old business that has changed shape, not a new launch.

## What does Jobhuntify offer job seekers?

Jobhuntify bundles five tools: resume review, a job application tracker, interview prep, a resume builder and what it calls an authentic job board. The homepage says the review benchmarks a resume against real job descriptions and gives detailed feedback ([founder-reported, Jobhuntify homepage](https://jobhuntify.com/)). It also shows a map of 8,391 companies hiring across 592 cities.

The listing frames the problem as applicant tracking systems filtering out resumes that are not tailored to the job.

> "A job search that does not ghost you." — Jobhuntify homepage ([founder-reported, Jobhuntify](https://jobhuntify.com/))

## How much money does Jobhuntify make now?

Jobhuntify had $1,997 in MRR and 211 active subscriptions in November 2026, collected $2,086 in the prior 30 days and $250,444 all time ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/jobhuntify-com)).

## Why does Jobhuntify's listing tell two stories?

The TrustMRR description says the startup "offers various useful tools, calculators, and converters", while the insights and homepage describe a job search product ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/jobhuntify-com)). The domain rating shown for jobhuntify.com is 1 out of 100, which is very low for a site with this revenue history.

Our reading: the Stripe account likely carries revenue from earlier products, and Jobhuntify is the current one. The listing does not confirm this, so treat the all-time total as account history, not as Jobhuntify's own result.

## How does Jobhuntify compare with Rezi and AIApply?

All three sell AI help to job seekers, a crowded consumer market with high churn.

Our observation: Rezi won by being the best at one thing, resumes, and ranking for it. Jobhuntify spreads across five tools with almost no search authority. In a market where people search for a specific fix, breadth is harder to sell than depth.

## What are the takeaways from Jobhuntify's numbers?

- **Read all-time revenue with care.** $250,444 all time next to $1,997 MRR points to an older revenue history ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/jobhuntify-com)).
- **Low prices need volume.** About $9.46 per subscription needs a lot of users to add up.
- **Search authority matters here.** Job seekers search for tools, and a domain rating of 1 leaves that channel unused.
- **Focus beats bundles.** Rezi's single-product focus shows what depth can earn in the same market.
