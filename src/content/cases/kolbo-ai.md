A creator who wants an image from Midjourney, a song from Suno and a script from Claude usually pays three subscriptions. Kolbo.AI sells one. In November 2026 its subscriptions added up to $42,718 in MRR, yet it collected $95,034 in the past 30 days ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/kolbo-ai)). That gap between recurring and actual revenue is the most telling thing about the business.

## What does Kolbo.AI bundle into one workspace?

Kolbo.AI bundles more than 100 AI models for images, video, music, voice and chat. The homepage says users can create without switching platforms, and the TrustMRR listing names GPT, Claude, Midjourney and Suno among the models ([founder-reported, Kolbo.AI homepage](https://kolbo.ai)).

> "100+ models from the world's leading AI labs. One workspace." — Kolbo.AI homepage ([founder-reported, Kolbo.AI](https://kolbo.ai))

Beyond access, Kolbo.AI sells continuity. The homepage says the context stays with the work, and a "Creative Director" feature turns one concept into about 2 to 8 scenes. Teams pay for credits rather than per seat.

## Why did Kolbo.AI earn more in 30 days than its MRR?

Kolbo.AI collected $95,034 in the last 30 days against $42,718 in MRR ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/kolbo-ai)), more than twice its recurring base. Revenue outside subscriptions, such as annual plans paid upfront or credit packs, is the most likely explanation. The page does not break it down.

## How do Kolbo.AI's credits and plans work?

Kolbo.AI runs on credits on top of subscriptions. The TrustMRR listing shows a free plan, Lite at $27, Standard at $45 and Pro at $89 a month, up to 37% off with annual billing, and credit packs from $15 ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/kolbo-ai)).

The credit rules are generous. The homepage says subscription credits can build up to three monthly allocations, purchased packs never expire, and failed generations cost nothing. The average subscription is worth about $59.66 a month ([calc, from TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/kolbo-ai)), between Standard and Pro.

## How does Kolbo.AI compare with Magai and TypingMind?

Kolbo.AI, Magai and TypingMind all sell access to many AI models through one interface, but they monetise it in three different ways. That makes them a clean comparison of business models for "AI aggregator" apps.

| Company | How it charges | Revenue figure | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Kolbo.AI | Subscriptions plus credit packs | $42,718 MRR (Nov 2026) | [verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/kolbo-ai) |
| [Magai](/cases/magai/) | One subscription; plans differ by usage | Nearly $100K MRR | [founder-reported, Indie Hackers](https://www.indiehackers.com/post/tech/from-broke-to-100k-mrr-in-four-years-hS1wen0srjGNIWZxSnV9) |
| [TypingMind](/cases/typingmind/) | One-time licences, bring your own API key | Not disclosed | [media-reported, Lemon Squeezy](https://lemonsqueezy.com/case-study/typing-mind) |

Our observation: Magai and TypingMind started from chat, while Kolbo.AI leads with images, video and music, where each generation costs more. Credits suit that well, because heavy video users pay more and the subscription base stays lower. Kolbo.AI's numbers are the only Stripe-verified ones of the three.

## What lessons does Kolbo.AI hold for multi-model app builders?

- **Credits capture usage that MRR misses.** Kolbo.AI's 30-day revenue was more than double its MRR ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/kolbo-ai)).
- **Generous credit rules lower the risk of trying.** Rollover, no-expiry packs and free retries on failures answer the main fear of credit pricing.
- **Lead with expensive media, not chat.** Images, video and music justify higher spending than text alone.
- **Breadth is the pitch, not one model.** 100+ models in one place is easier to market than any single model's quality.
