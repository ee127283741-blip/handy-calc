Most companies on TrustMRR sell software. Ave Rides sells car rides in one small Florida town. Founded in June 2022 to serve Ave Maria, the bootstrapped company had $453,891 in lifetime revenue and $14,355 in the last 30 days by November 2026, all verified through Stripe ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/ave-rides-llc)). Its MRR, though, is just $215, which says a lot about how it earns.

## What does Ave Rides offer in Ave Maria, Florida?

Ave Rides offers safe, reliable and premium local transportation, plus rentals, for residents and visitors of Ave Maria and Southwest Florida ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/ave-rides-llc)). Rides use flat-rate pricing. The company was founded by Ave Maria University alum Savohn Hunt.

> "Reliable Ave Maria Transportation" — Ave Rides value proposition ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/ave-rides-llc))

Its iOS app holds a 4.7 out of 5 rating from 73 App Store ratings, according to TrustMRR.

## How does Ave Rides make $14,355 a month with $215 MRR?

Ave Rides makes most of its money from individual trips, not subscriptions. It collected $14,355 in the last 30 days while MRR from 12 active subscriptions was only $215 ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/ave-rides-llc)).

## Why does a one-town focus work for Ave Rides?

A single town gives Ave Rides a defined, reachable audience. The company reports about 3,777 users, and a local operator can focus on one community's routes and needs ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/ave-rides-llc)). The company is bootstrapped, with a team of 6 to 10 people.

## What lessons does Ave Rides teach local service founders?

- **Own a small geography.** One town and its university can support $453,891 in sales.
- **Read the right metric.** For per-trip businesses, 30-day revenue matters more than MRR.
- **An app helps a local service.** A 4.7 rating from 73 reviews builds trust with new riders.
- **Bootstrapping works offline too.** The company grew with no outside funding listed.
