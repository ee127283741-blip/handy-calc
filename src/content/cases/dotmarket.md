Buying a small online business is a leap of faith: the seller knows the numbers, and the buyer has to trust them. Dotmarket built a business on reducing that gap for French-speaking buyers. Founded by Kevin Jourdan in January 2020, it had earned $1,148,585 in lifetime revenue by November 2026 ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/dotmarket-eu)), making it one of the older and larger companies in this batch.

## What does Dotmarket do for buyers and sellers of web businesses?

Dotmarket is a marketplace for acquiring and selling digital businesses: SaaS products, media sites, e-commerce stores and web agencies. The company says it proposes more than 300 deals a year that it has verified and valued itself, and it calls itself the leading platform in France for this ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/dotmarket-eu)).

> "Dotmarket exists to reduce uncertainty and secure your investment decisions." — Dotmarket value proposition on TrustMRR ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/dotmarket-eu))

The audience is B2B: entrepreneurs and investors on both sides of a deal.

## Why is Dotmarket's lifetime revenue so large next to its MRR?

All-time revenue is about 92 times current MRR (calc, [from TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/dotmarket-eu)), while the company is roughly 82 months old. That ratio implies revenue beyond subscriptions, or past months that were stronger than today. For a marketplace, the most likely extra source is deal-related fees, but the split between memberships and other income is not disclosed.

The last 30 days also came in below MRR, at $6,995 versus $12,507 ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/dotmarket-eu)). Collections in a month can lag the subscription base, for example when annual plans renew at other times of year.

## What lessons does Dotmarket hold for marketplace founders?

- **Trust is the product.** Dotmarket sells verified, valued deals rather than raw listings.
- **A local market can be enough.** A French-language focus produced $1,148,585 in lifetime revenue.
- **Memberships smooth a lumpy business.** Deals close irregularly, while 381 subscriptions add steady income.
- **Longevity compounds.** Six-plus years of operation built a deal flow of 300+ opportunities a year.
