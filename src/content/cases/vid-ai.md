A faceless YouTube channel needs a script, a voice, footage, music and captions every single day. Vid.AI sells all five in one prompt. Two years after Priyam Raj founded it in September 2024, the tool had collected $1,667,672 in revenue and showed $51,121 in monthly recurring revenue ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/vidai-llc)). The more interesting number is the gap between those two.

## What does Vid.AI generate from a single idea?

Vid.AI turns a typed idea or script into a finished video. The homepage says it writes the script, adds a human-sounding voiceover, visuals, music and captions, and targets Shorts and long-form videos for YouTube, TikTok and Instagram ([founder-reported, Vid.AI homepage](https://vid.ai)).

> "Type an idea. Vid.AI writes the script, adds a human-sounding voiceover, visuals, music and captions" — Vid.AI homepage ([founder-reported, Vid.AI](https://vid.ai))

The homepage also lists 33 narrators, 12 image styles and 3M+ licensed stock videos. Videos run up to 20 minutes, or 60 minutes on the Plus and Pro plans, which pushes Vid.AI beyond the short-clip tools.

## How much revenue has Vid.AI earned in two years?

Vid.AI had earned $1,667,672 all time by November 2026, with $51,121 in MRR and $40,655 collected in the last 30 days ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/vidai-llc)). TrustMRR verifies these with a Stripe API key and ranked Vid.AI #73 on its list.

All-time revenue is about 33 times current MRR ([calc, from TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/vidai-llc)). Over 26 months, that means Vid.AI averaged far more than $51,121 a month at some point, so current MRR is likely below its peak. The page offers one clue: the founder's note describes a forward operating margin estimate made after pausing Meta ads.

## Why did Vid.AI pause its Meta ads?

Vid.AI's marketing ran on Meta Ads and influencers, according to the TrustMRR listing ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/vidai-llc)). The founder's note on the same page frames the business after pausing Meta ads, and stresses that the margin it gives is a forward estimate, not trailing profit, excludes owner distributions and is subject to reconciliation.

The reason for the pause is not disclosed. The pattern is familiar, though: paid acquisition can drive a large burst of sign-ups, and turning it off trades growth for margin. Thirty-day revenue below MRR is consistent with a business that is no longer buying new customers at the same pace.

## What does Vid.AI charge, and why do sources disagree?

Vid.AI's homepage lists Starter at $59, Plus at $99 and Pro at $199 a month, and says yearly plans save up to 22% ([founder-reported, Vid.AI homepage](https://vid.ai)). The TrustMRR listing still shows Starter at $39 ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/vidai-llc)), which suggests the entry price rose.

The average subscription is worth about $68.07 a month ([calc, from TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/vidai-llc)), between Starter and Plus. Eligible new subscribers also get 50% off the first month.

## How does Vid.AI compare with Faceless.video and StoryShort?

Vid.AI is the only one of these three faceless-video tools with Stripe-verified revenue, and its all-time total is the clearest number in the group. All three sell to people running channels without appearing on camera.

Our observation: this niche rewards paid acquisition and punishes it when it stops. Vid.AI's long-form support is a real difference from short-only rivals, but the revenue history points to marketing spend, not features, as the main growth lever.

## What are the takeaways from Vid.AI's numbers?

- **All-time revenue tells you what MRR hides.** $1,667,672 collected against $51,121 MRR shows a business that was once bigger than today's run rate ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/vidai-llc)).
- **Paid ads are a dial, not a foundation.** Vid.AI's own note frames current numbers around pausing Meta ads.
- **Raising the entry price is a lever for margin.** The Starter plan appears on TrustMRR at $39 and on the homepage at $59.
- **Bundle the whole workflow.** Script, voice, visuals, music and captions in one step is the product, not any single model.
