Coconote ARR reached $6.7M in under two years, and its founders never raised money or ran paid ads. Brett Bauman and Zack Hargett built Coconote, an AI app that turns lecture recordings into notes and quizzes, and grew it to 2 million users and 1 billion social views before Quizlet bought it ([founder-reported, The Superwall Podcast](https://www.buzzsprout.com/2522779/episodes/19010268-brett-bauman-zack-hargett-copy-this-ai-app-s-6-7m-yr-marketing-strategy)). Hargett has described himself as an app maker who is not especially technical. Growth came from people filming videos, not engineers.

## What does Coconote do for students?

Coconote turns audio, video, PDFs and links such as YouTube videos into organized notes, quizzes, study games and flashcards ([THE Journal](https://thejournal.com/articles/2026/02/13/quizlet-adds-ai-powered-tools-for-active-practice-notetaking.aspx)). A student records a lecture and gets study material back in seconds. Quizlet described it as a viral AI note-taker and study coach when it announced the acquisition. Pricing is not disclosed in the sources we used.

> "Under Quizlet, Coconote continues its mission to give learners superpowers" — Quizlet statement on the acquisition ([media-reported, THE Journal](https://thejournal.com/articles/2026/02/13/quizlet-adds-ai-powered-tools-for-active-practice-notetaking.aspx))

## Who built Coconote, and how technical are the Coconote founders?

Brett Bauman and Zack Hargett co-founded Coconote. On the Sub Club podcast, Hargett said Bauman's background is making apps and that his own is too, while describing himself as not especially technical ([Sub Club transcript](https://share.transistor.fm/s/07d90e73/transcript.txt)). That makes Coconote a mixed team: one co-founder builds, the other focuses on growth. Team size is not disclosed.

## How did Coconote grow to $6.7M ARR without paid ads?

Coconote reached $1M ARR four months after launch and later $6.7M ARR, with zero paid ads, the founders said ([founder-reported, Sub Club](https://getpodcast.com/podcast/sub-club2/bootstrapped-to-6-7m-arr-and-an-exit-to-quizlet-in-2-years-brett-bauma_0fee68e12f)). Their advice was to hire content creators, not influencers. The Superwall Podcast adds 2 million users and 1 billion social views, all bootstrapped, in under two years ([founder-reported, The Superwall Podcast](https://www.buzzsprout.com/2522779/episodes/19010268-brett-bauman-zack-hargett-copy-this-ai-app-s-6-7m-yr-marketing-strategy)). That ARR is about $558K a month (calc). Total revenue is not disclosed.

## Why did 40 million views earn Coconote only $25K?

Not all views convert. The Superwall Podcast highlights a case where 40M views earned Coconote only $25K, which the episode uses to argue about what kind of content actually converts ([The Superwall Podcast](https://www.buzzsprout.com/2522779/episodes/19010268-brett-bauman-zack-hargett-copy-this-ai-app-s-6-7m-yr-marketing-strategy)). The episode also covers onboarding experiments and retention. A billion total views mattered less than which videos brought in students who paid.

## How does Coconote compare with Turbo AI and Conch AI?

[Turbo AI](/cases/turbo-ai/) sells nearly the same product, AI notes, flashcards and quizzes from lectures, and its founders said it reached eight-figure ARR with only a small seed round. [Conch AI](/cases/conch-ai/) is another student AI tool whose non-technical co-founder drove growth through viral video. Coconote took a third path: bootstrapped, creator-led, then sold to an incumbent rather than staying independent. Like [Cal AI](/cases/cal-ai/), it ended with a larger consumer brand buying a viral AI app.

Our reading: in student AI, distribution through creators is the moat, and an incumbent like Quizlet may find buying that machine cheaper than rebuilding it.

## What are the lessons from Coconote's exit to Quizlet?

- **A growth co-founder can carry half the company.** Hargett is not especially technical; growth was his side.
- **Creators beat influencers for apps.** The founders recommend hiring content creators instead.
- **Views are not revenue.** One run of 40M views earned only $25K.
- **Bootstrapped apps can still exit.** Coconote sold to Quizlet without outside funding.
