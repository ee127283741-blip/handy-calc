WordPress still runs a large share of small business websites, and most of those owners do not want to learn it. 1clickwebsite.ai sells the shortcut: fill in a form, get a finished WordPress site. Seventeen months after its June 2025 start, the one-person company had $14,155 in monthly recurring revenue and $104,493 in all-time revenue ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/1clickwebsite-ai)).

## What does 1clickwebsite.ai actually generate?

1clickwebsite.ai generates a complete WordPress site from a short intake form. The TrustMRR listing says the output includes page text, images and SEO pages, so the customer receives a working site rather than a theme to configure ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/1clickwebsite-ai)). The listing files it under AI, no-code, design tools and content creation.

> "Generate SEO optimized Wordpress sites in 1 click." — 1clickwebsite.ai listing ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/1clickwebsite-ai))

The choice of WordPress matters. Many site builders lock customers into their own hosting. Output on WordPress is something agencies and freelancers already know how to host, edit and hand over to clients.

## What do Stripe-verified figures say about 1clickwebsite.ai revenue so far?

1clickwebsite.ai made $14,155 in MRR in November 2026, with $14,929 collected over the previous 30 days and 432 active subscriptions ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/1clickwebsite-ai)). All-time revenue was $104,493, checked through a Stripe API key.

## Which 1clickwebsite.ai plan fits which buyer?

1clickwebsite.ai has three monthly plans: Starter at $49, Professional at $97 and Expert at $197 ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/1clickwebsite-ai)). The listing does not spell out plan limits, so we cannot say how many sites each tier covers.

The price ladder is steep compared with a typical page builder. A $197 tier only makes sense for someone producing sites repeatedly, which points at agencies and freelancers rather than one-off business owners.

## Who builds 1clickwebsite.ai and with what stack?

The TrustMRR listing names Bennett Black as founder, the United States as location and a team of one ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/1clickwebsite-ai)). The stack is Next.js and TypeScript on the front end with Supabase and Stripe behind it. The founder's X account shows 161 followers, so this is not an audience-led launch. Marketing channels are not disclosed.

## How does 1clickwebsite.ai compare with SiteGPT and SEObot?

All three sell AI that produces web assets for small businesses, but they sit at different points of the funnel. [SiteGPT](/cases/sitegpt/) adds a chatbot to an existing site, [SEObot](/cases/seobot/) writes ongoing SEO content, and 1clickwebsite.ai creates the site itself.

Our observation: 1clickwebsite.ai has the most "one and done" job of the three, yet it still charges monthly. Holding 432 subscriptions on a product whose core output is delivered once suggests customers either keep building new sites or value ongoing hosting and edits. The listing does not say which.

## What lessons does the 1clickwebsite.ai story offer solo builders?

- **Build on a platform buyers already trust.** Generating WordPress, not a proprietary format, removes the lock-in objection.
- **Price for repeat users.** A ladder that tops out at $197 a month targets people who make sites for others ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/1clickwebsite-ai)).
- **An audience is optional.** With 161 followers on X, the founder reached $14,155 MRR without a big personal following.
- **Watch the average.** Revenue per subscription below the cheapest list price is a signal to check discounts and billing terms.
