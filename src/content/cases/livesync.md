A streamer opens Twitch, and the face on screen is not theirs. Livesync sells that trick as a cloud service, with no local GPU and no setup. Founded by Jinho Kim in February 2026, the US company had $28,179 in lifetime revenue and $2,098 in monthly recurring revenue by November 2026 ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/livesync)). The interesting part is the gap between a big free audience and a small paying one.

## What does Livesync do during a live stream?

Livesync swaps a user's face in real time while they stream or sit on a video call. The listing says it works on YouTube, Twitch, Zoom and more, with no setup needed, and targets streamers, VTubers, video call users, marketers and education platforms ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/livesync)).

> "25K+ active users transform their appearance instantly during live streams, video calls, and content creation." — Jinho Kim ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/livesync))

The tech stack listed is Next.js on the front end and Python with Stripe on the back end. Processing runs in the cloud, which is what removes the setup step.

## Why do so few Livesync users pay?

About 25,000 users against 91 subscriptions means well under 1% pay (calc, [from TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/livesync)). A free early-access offer pulls in curious visitors, and many of them want to try the effect once rather than stream with it every week. The company also says it ranks first on Google for "live face swap", so search traffic is likely a large part of that free top of funnel. Converting even a slightly bigger share of that audience is the clearest lever for growth.

## What are the takeaways from Livesync for AI video founders?

- **Real-time is the differentiator.** Livesync sells face swaps during live streams and calls.
- **Removing setup widens the funnel.** Cloud processing means no local install or GPU.
- **A free tier needs a paywall plan.** About 25,000 users but 91 subscriptions shows how much value stays unpaid.
- **One search phrase can carry a product.** Ranking for "live face swap" gives a small team steady demand.
