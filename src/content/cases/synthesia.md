Most AI video startups chase creators and advertisers. Synthesia went after the HR department. Its AI avatars present training videos, onboarding guides and internal updates, the kind of video nobody puts on social media. That unglamorous market got the London company past $100M in annual recurring revenue in April 2025 ([founder-reported, Synthesia](https://www.synthesia.io/post/100-million-revenue-adobe-investment)). By January 2026 its CFO put ARR at $150M ([founder-reported via CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/01/26/nvidia-alphabet-vc-arms-back-synthesia.html)), and investors valued Synthesia at $4B.

## Who founded Synthesia, and what did they bet on?

Synthesia was founded in 2017 by two business founders and two computer vision professors. Victor Riparbelli is CEO and Steffen Tjerrild is COO, and Prof. Matthias Niessner and Prof. Lourdes Agapito are co-founders, according to the company's [About page](https://www.synthesia.io/about). The professors brought the research on synthesising and reconstructing faces. The business founders turned it into a product companies would pay for.

The bet was about cost. Filming a person on camera needs a studio, a presenter and an editor, and the video has to be reshot every time the script changes. Synthesia replaces that with a script and an avatar.

> "Synthesia was founded on two core beliefs: first, that AI will bring the cost of content creation down to zero." — Victor Riparbelli ([founder-reported, Synthesia](https://www.synthesia.io/post/series-e-200-million-4-billion-valuation-future-work))

## How did Synthesia's ARR climb past $150 million?

Synthesia's ARR went from just over $100M to $150M in about nine months. The company announced the $100M milestone in April 2025, together with a strategic investment of undisclosed size from Adobe Ventures ([founder-reported, Synthesia](https://www.synthesia.io/post/100-million-revenue-adobe-investment)). In January 2026, CFO Daniel Kim told CNBC that ARR had reached $150M and that he expected it to pass $200M sometime in 2026 ([founder-reported via CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/01/26/nvidia-alphabet-vc-arms-back-synthesia.html)).

$150M ARR works out to about $12.5M a month ([calc, from CNBC's $150M](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/01/26/nvidia-alphabet-vc-arms-back-synthesia.html)). That is our arithmetic, not a reported monthly figure. The $200M mark is a forecast, and we found no report that Synthesia has reached it. Total revenue and profit are not disclosed. CNBC reported in January 2025 that more than half of annual revenue came from US customers and almost half from Europe ([media-reported, CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2025/01/15/ai-video-platform-synthesia-doubles-valuation-to-2point1-billion.html)).

## What is the Synthesia valuation after the GV-led round?

The Synthesia valuation is $4B, set by a $200M Series E that GV led in January 2026 ([founder-reported, Synthesia](https://www.synthesia.io/post/series-e-200-million-4-billion-valuation-future-work)). New investors Evantic and Hedosophia joined existing backers including NVentures, Accel, Kleiner Perkins and NEA. The valuation nearly doubled in a year. In January 2025, an NEA-led $180M Series D valued the company at $2.1B, more than double its $1B valuation from 2023 ([media-reported, CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2025/01/15/ai-video-platform-synthesia-doubles-valuation-to-2point1-billion.html)).

The round also included an employee secondary sale run with Nasdaq at the same $4B price ([media-reported, TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/01/26/synthesia-hits-4b-valuation-lets-employees-cash-in/)). TechCrunch put the team at over 500 people and said Synthesia plans to make AI agents a "core strategic focus." Riparbelli has kept his distance from the hype even while raising on it.

> "Of course, the hype cycle is beneficial to us," — Victor Riparbelli ([founder-reported, CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2025/01/15/ai-video-platform-synthesia-doubles-valuation-to-2point1-billion.html))

## Why do big companies buy Synthesia AI videos?

Big companies buy Synthesia because they make a lot of internal video that keeps going out of date. In April 2025 Synthesia said more than 70% of the Fortune 100 were customers, up from 40% two years earlier ([founder-reported, Synthesia](https://www.synthesia.io/post/100-million-revenue-adobe-investment)). By the Series E announcement in January 2026, it said more than 90% of the Fortune 100 used it ([founder-reported, Synthesia](https://www.synthesia.io/post/series-e-200-million-4-billion-valuation-future-work)). TechCrunch named Bosch, Merck and SAP as enterprise clients.

The self-serve plans are cheap, so the bigger contracts sit higher up. In November 2026 the pricing page listed a free Basic plan, Starter at $19 a month ($14 billed yearly), Pro at $89 a month ($59 billed yearly) and custom-priced Enterprise ([founder-reported, Synthesia pricing page](https://www.synthesia.io/pricing)). In our reading, the low tiers work as the way in, and Enterprise brings in most of the money.

## How does Synthesia compare with Arcads and Opus Clip?

All three sell AI video, but they sell it to different buyers, and that is a big part of why their scale differs. [Arcads](/cases/arcads/) makes UGC-style ad videos with AI actors for performance marketers. It reported $10M ARR with a team of 8 in November 2025 ([founder-reported via Latka](https://getlatka.com/interviews/arcads-romain-torres-2025)). Synthesia uses the same basic idea, a scripted avatar, for training and communications teams. At $150M ARR it was about 15 times larger two months later, with a team of more than 500.

[Opus Clip](/cases/opus-clip/) does the reverse: it cuts footage creators already have into short clips. It was reported at about $20M ARR in March 2025 ([unverified, ARR Club](https://www.arr.club/signal/opusclip-20m-arr-softbank-mar-2025)) and raised $20M from SoftBank Vision Fund 2 at a $215M valuation ([media-reported, Net Influencer](https://netinfluencer.com/opusclip-secures-20m-usd-from-softbank-vision-fund-2-launches-ai-video-search-tool)). Synthesia's $4B valuation reflects an enterprise customer base rather than a creator one. For the voice side of AI video, see [ElevenLabs](/cases/elevenlabs/).

## What can founders learn from Synthesia's revenue path?

- **Pick the boring budget.** Corporate training video is not glamorous, but it is recurring, and it took Synthesia into more than 90% of the Fortune 100.
- **Research founders plus operators.** Two professors and two business founders matched deep face-synthesis research with enterprise sales.
- **Let cheap plans feed enterprise deals.** Self-serve tiers start at $19 a month, while Enterprise pricing is custom.
- **Publish milestones as primary sources.** The $100M ARR post and the CFO's $150M figure give outsiders a dated growth curve, though total revenue and profit are still not disclosed.
- **Share the upside with the team.** The Series E came with an employee secondary sale at the full $4B valuation.
