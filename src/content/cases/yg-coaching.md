Fitness coaching is one of the oldest online businesses, and one of the least glamorous to track. YG Coaching is a small, honest data point. The UK service, founded in September 2025, had 8 active clients on subscription and $12,813 in lifetime revenue by November 2026 ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/yg-coaching)). It sits on a site full of AI apps as a reminder that people still pay people to keep them on track.

## What does YG Coaching offer its clients?

YG Coaching sells personalized nutrition and training programs built around a client's schedule. The target is busy professionals who want results that last rather than a short diet.

> "Sustainable results through personalized nutrition and training that fits your schedule." — YG Coaching company description ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/yg-coaching))

The listing links to the domain to10percentbodyfat.com, which names the goal in the URL. TrustMRR lists the market as Health & Fitness and Education, with Stripe as the only tool in its stack.

## How much does YG Coaching make from 8 clients?

YG Coaching had $1,161 in MRR from 8 active subscriptions in November 2026, collected $887 in the last 30 days and $12,813 all time ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/yg-coaching)). That works out to about $145 a month per client (calc: $1,161 / 8).

## Why does a three-month package suit YG Coaching?

A three-month block matches how long a body composition change takes to show, and it locks in revenue for a quarter. At £399 for 3 months, the listed price is about £133 a month ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/yg-coaching)), which is roughly consistent with the dollar MRR per client after currency conversion.

The trade-off is churn at each renewal point. With only 8 clients, one renewal more or less moves MRR by more than a tenth.

## How is YG Coaching different from Nutree and MamaSkin?

YG Coaching, [Nutree](/cases/nutree/) and [MamaSkin](/cases/mamaskin/) all serve health-minded consumers, but YG Coaching sells human time while the others sell apps.

Our observation: the apps earn about four times more MRR, but they need far more users to get there. YG Coaching earns about $145 per client a month, a figure a consumer app rarely reaches. The ceiling is the coach's hours, not the market.

## What lessons does YG Coaching hold for coaching businesses?

- **High price, few clients.** 8 clients produce $1,161 in MRR because each pays a premium.
- **Package the outcome.** A three-month program sells a result, not a monthly login.
- **A niche audience is enough.** Targeting busy professionals keeps the offer clear.
- **Time caps growth.** Without software leverage, MRR scales only with coaching hours.
