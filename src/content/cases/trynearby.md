TryNearby revenue comes from restaurants paying for TikTok-style videos made by people who live nearby. Its founder, Yousef Abdelfattah, co-founded FaZe Clan and spent about 15 years making content, but he is not an engineer. Replit presented TryNearby as a business a non-coder built with its AI tools ([media-reported, Creator Economy](https://creatoreconomy.so/p/how-non-coders-are-vibe-coding-100k-businesses-with-ai-amol-jain-replit)). Its Y Combinator page lists 120+ paying restaurants at $399 a month ([founder-reported, Y Combinator](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/trynearby)), about $48K a month in subscriptions (calc).

## What does TryNearby do for restaurants?

TryNearby matches restaurants with local social media creators who visit, film and post a review every month. A restaurant pays a flat subscription and gets a steady stream of short videos without managing influencers itself ([Y Combinator](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/trynearby)). Creators get free meals and paid work close to home. The company describes itself in plain terms.

> "subscription-based platform connecting local restaurants with nearby social media creators" — TryNearby ([founder-reported, Y Combinator](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/trynearby))

## How much revenue does TryNearby earn per restaurant?

TryNearby earns $399 a month per restaurant, and its YC page lists 120+ paying restaurants across Southern California ([founder-reported, Y Combinator](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/trynearby)). Multiplying the two gives about $47.9K a month (calc), though actual revenue depends on discounts and churn. Creators have posted 2,500+ videos with about 35M views. A Chinese-language profile reported about five people on the team ten months in ([media-reported, Woshipm](https://www.woshipm.com/operate/6457101.html)). Total revenue is not disclosed.

## Why did a FaZe Clan co-founder build TryNearby with Replit?

Yousef Abdelfattah's skill is content and creators, not software. Replit's head of product engineering, Amol Jain, cited TryNearby as one of three businesses built by non-coders with Replit, and argued that each founder's advantage was expertise or access rather than coding ([Creator Economy](https://creatoreconomy.so/p/how-non-coders-are-vibe-coding-100k-businesses-with-ai-amol-jain-replit)). For TryNearby, that advantage was knowing how creators work and how to recruit them.

## How does TryNearby compare with Creator Hunter and GenAIPI?

TryNearby, [Creator Hunter](/cases/creator-hunter/) and [GenAIPI](/cases/genaipi/) were all built by founders who are not engineers. Creator Hunter sells a creator database to startups; TryNearby sells done-for-you creator videos to restaurants, which is a service as much as software. GenAIPI, another Replit example, also mixes software with paid work for clients. All three charge business customers, which lets fewer customers produce meaningful revenue.

Our reading: TryNearby's software is the coordination layer, matching restaurants and creators and tracking visits, while the value is the network of creators. That is a business an AI-built app can support well, because the hard part is relationships, not code.

## What lessons does TryNearby offer creator-economy founders?

- **Sell outcomes, not tools.** Restaurants pay for videos posted, not for software to find creators.
- **Use your network as the product.** A FaZe Clan co-founder knows how to recruit and manage creators.
- **Flat pricing is easy to buy.** $399 a month is simple for a restaurant owner to approve.
- **Let AI build the coordination layer.** Replit handled the software for a founder who does not code.
