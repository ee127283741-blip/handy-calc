Search used to mean ten blue links. Now buyers ask ChatGPT, and brands want to know whether they are in the answer. Rank Prompt sells that answer. Founded in May 2025, it showed $44,701 in monthly recurring revenue and $338,595 in total revenue by November 2026 ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/rank-prompt)), riding the rise of "AEO", or answer engine optimisation.

## What does Rank Prompt measure in AI search?

Rank Prompt measures whether AI assistants recommend your brand. The homepage says it checks seven AI engines, including ChatGPT, Perplexity and Claude, to see whether they name your brand or a competitor when buyers ask, then suggests fixes for the gaps ([founder-reported, Rank Prompt homepage](https://rankprompt.com)).

> "Find out if ChatGPT recommends your business, and what to change so it does." — Rank Prompt homepage ([founder-reported, Rank Prompt](https://rankprompt.com))

TrustMRR describes the wider toolkit: AI search visibility tracking, competitor analysis, citation building and content creation from one dashboard. The audience is teams and agencies, and a free GEO course of 20 lessons doubles as marketing.

## Why are Rank Prompt's prices hard to pin down?

Rank Prompt's own pricing page shows two sets of prices. The plan cards list Starter at $39, Pro at $71, Agency at $119 and Agency Plus at $239 a month, billed annually, while the FAQ on the same page lists Agency at $149 and Agency Plus at $299 a month ([founder-reported, Rank Prompt pricing](https://rankprompt.com/pricing)). The likeliest reading is annual versus monthly billing, but the page does not say so outright.

## Where does Rank Prompt's traffic come from?

Rank Prompt grows through content. The TrustMRR listing names a blog, content marketing and a newsletter as its channels, and the site has a Domain Rating of 46 ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/rank-prompt)). A product about being found in AI answers markets itself by being found.

Social proof does the rest. The homepage claims 20,000+ marketers and agencies and a 5.0 rating on G2, and the TrustMRR listing names large brands such as Mastercard, LVMH and Costco as users ([founder-reported, Rank Prompt homepage](https://rankprompt.com)). The 20,000 figure far exceeds 216 paid subscriptions, so it likely counts free reports.

## What can AI-era SEO founders learn from Rank Prompt?

- **Sell the anxiety of a platform shift.** Brands do not yet know whether ChatGPT recommends them, and Rank Prompt sells the check ([founder-reported, Rank Prompt homepage](https://rankprompt.com)).
- **Agencies pay more per account.** An average near $206.95 a month shows where the revenue is.
- **A free first report is a strong funnel.** It lets 20,000+ users try the product before 216 subscriptions convert.
- **Keep the pricing page consistent.** Two price sets on one page create doubt for buyers comparing tools.
- **Verified numbers stand out in a hype market.** $44,701 MRR is checked against Stripe ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/rank-prompt)).
