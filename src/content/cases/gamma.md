## Origin of the idea
The founders saw that most of the time spent on slide decks goes into formatting, not ideas. They set out to build a flexible, web-native alternative to PowerPoint that lays itself out.

## How they started
Gamma started in 2020 as a non-AI document/presentation tool with modest traction. When generative AI arrived, it added "generate a deck from a prompt" and growth took off in 2023.

## Tools & tech stack
A web editor built around flexible "cards" instead of fixed slides, with text and image models from multiple providers generating content and layout.

## Marketing
Mostly product-led: every shared Gamma link shows the product to new people, supported by creator content on social platforms.

## Sales & pricing
Freemium with paid Plus and Pro plans, and team plans for companies.

## Monthly revenue
About **$8.3M/month**, implied from the $100M+ ARR announced in November 2025.

## Total revenue
Not disclosed.

## Founding date & timeline
- **2020:** Founded.
- **2023:** AI generation launched; rapid growth.
- **Nov 2025:** $100M+ ARR, $2.1B valuation, ~50 people.

## Team size
About 50 people at the $100M ARR milestone.

## Key takeaways
- An existing product can be reborn by one AI feature.
- Every shared document is an ad.
- Small teams can stay profitable at scale with AI-heavy workflows.
