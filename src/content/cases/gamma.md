Gamma spent its first years as an ordinary startup: a document and presentation tool founded in 2020, with modest traction. Then generative AI arrived, the team added one feature, "generate a deck from a prompt", and growth took off in 2023. By November 2025 Gamma announced more than $100M in ARR ([founder-reported, Business Wire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20251110805751/en)) with about 50 people, having raised only $23M before reaching that mark ([founder-reported, Gamma](https://gamma.app/insights/how-we-built-a-usd100m-business-differently)). It says it has been profitable for two years.

## Why did the founders start Gamma?

The founders, Grant Lee, Jon Noronha and James Fox, saw that most of the time spent on slide decks goes into formatting, not ideas. They set out to build a flexible, web-native alternative to PowerPoint that lays itself out. The pain is familiar to anyone who has nudged text boxes at midnight before a meeting: the tool demands design work when the person only wants to communicate.

## How does Gamma work?

Gamma is a web editor built around flexible "cards" instead of fixed slides. A user types a prompt or pastes notes, and text and image models from multiple providers generate content and layout. The result can be edited like a document and shared as a link, and the same approach produces presentations, documents and websites.

## How was Gamma built?

Gamma started in 2020 as a non-AI document and presentation tool with modest traction. The card-based editor already existed, so when generative AI arrived the team could add generation on top of a product built for flexible layouts. Using models from multiple providers kept it from depending on one. The team was about 50 people at the $100M ARR milestone ([media-reported, Business Wire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20251110805751/en)).

## How did Gamma market and price its product?

Growth was mostly product-led: every shared Gamma link shows the product to new people, supported by creator content on social platforms. A presentation is, by definition, shown to an audience, so each one is a demonstration.

Pricing is freemium with paid Plus and Pro plans, and team plans for companies.

## What challenges did Gamma face?

For its first years, the challenge was traction. The pre-AI product had modest traction, and many startups in that position run out of runway. Gamma survived long enough for a new capability to fit its product.

The second challenge is scale with a small team. Reaching $100M ARR with about 50 people meant relying on AI-heavy workflows and product-led growth instead of a large sales force, and the company says it has been profitable for two years.

## How much revenue does Gamma make?

Gamma announced $100M+ ARR in November 2025 ([founder-reported, Business Wire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20251110805751/en)), which implies about $8.3M a month. It raised at a $2.1B valuation in its Series B ([media-reported, Business Wire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20251110805751/en)). Total revenue is not disclosed.

The timeline: founded in 2020; AI generation launched in 2023 with rapid growth; $100M+ ARR, a $2.1B valuation and about 50 people in November 2025.

## Why did Gamma work?

An existing product can be reborn by one AI feature. Gamma's flexible card format was a better fit for AI-generated content than fixed slides, so the feature felt native rather than bolted on.

Every shared document is an ad. Product-led growth was built into the use case. And small teams can stay profitable at scale with AI-heavy workflows. [Formula Bot](/cases/formula-bot/) attacked another office pain, spreadsheet formulas, while [Lovable](/cases/lovable/) shows similar speed in app building with a venture-backed approach.

## What can founders learn from Gamma?

An existing product can be reborn by one AI feature. If you have a struggling product, ask what a new model makes newly possible inside it.

Every shared document is an ad. Products whose output is seen by others have free distribution.

Small teams can stay profitable at scale with AI-heavy workflows. Raising $23M before $100M ARR shows capital efficiency is possible even in a crowded category.
