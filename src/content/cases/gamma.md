Gamma spent its first years as an ordinary startup: a document and presentation tool founded in 2020, with modest traction. Then generative AI arrived, the team added one feature, "generate a deck from a prompt", and growth took off in 2023. By November 2025 Gamma announced more than $100M in ARR ([founder-reported, Business Wire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20251110805751/en)) with about 50 people, having raised only $23M before reaching that mark ([founder-reported, Gamma](https://gamma.app/insights/how-we-built-a-usd100m-business-differently)). It says it has been profitable for two years.

## What were Gamma's founders trying to fix about slides?

The founders, Grant Lee, Jon Noronha and James Fox, saw that most of the time spent on slide decks goes into formatting, not ideas. They set out to build a flexible, web-native alternative to PowerPoint that lays itself out. The pain is familiar to anyone who has nudged text boxes at midnight before a meeting: the tool demands design work when the person only wants to communicate.

## Why do Gamma's cards suit AI generation?

Gamma is a web editor built around flexible "cards" instead of fixed slides. A user types a prompt or pastes notes, and text and image models from multiple providers generate content and layout. The result can be edited like a document and shared as a link, and the same approach produces presentations, documents and websites.

Gamma started in 2020 as a non-AI document and presentation tool with modest traction. The card-based editor already existed, so when generative AI arrived the team could add generation on top of a product built for flexible layouts. Using models from multiple providers kept it from depending on one. The team was about 50 people at the $100M ARR milestone ([media-reported, Business Wire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20251110805751/en)).

## How does every shared Gamma link sell the product?

Growth was mostly product-led: every shared Gamma link shows the product to new people, supported by creator content on social platforms. A presentation is, by definition, shown to an audience, so each one is a demonstration.

Pricing is freemium with paid Plus and Pro plans, and team plans for companies.

## How close did Gamma come to running out of runway?

By late 2022 Gamma was close to running out. Co-founder Grant Lee wrote that the company had about 60,000 users, a little over a year of runway and a team of about a dozen people. They had to bet: go all-in on AI or watch Gamma slowly fade. They spent three months in a sprint, re-architecting the infrastructure, redesigning the interface and rethinking the whole approach ([founder-reported, Gamma](https://gamma.app/insights/how-we-built-a-usd100m-business-differently)).

The second challenge was staying small while growing. Gamma kept its team at about 50 people instead of the 200 Lee says would be typical, and chose profitability over vanity metrics. By November 2025 it had been profitable for two years.

> "This era of AI hype has seen companies raise massive rounds, burn through cash, and chase growth at any cost." — Grant Lee ([founder-reported, Gamma](https://gamma.app/insights/how-we-built-a-usd100m-business-differently))

## How much revenue does Gamma make, and is it profitable?

Gamma announced $100M+ ARR in November 2025 ([founder-reported, Business Wire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20251110805751/en)), which implies about $8.3M a month. It raised at a $2.1B valuation in its Series B ([media-reported, Business Wire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20251110805751/en)). Total revenue is not disclosed.

## How does Gamma compare with Formula Bot and Lovable?

[Formula Bot](/cases/formula-bot/) and Gamma both attack office work that people find tedious, spreadsheets for one and slides for the other. Formula Bot did it as a single-purpose tool run by one person with a day job. Gamma rebuilt a whole editor around AI and grew to about 50 people. Both rely on output that gets shared: a formula in a spreadsheet, a deck shown to an audience.

[Lovable](/cases/lovable/) is the closer match on numbers. Both passed $100M ARR in 2025, but on very different capital. Gamma says it reached $100M ARR with only $23M in initial funding and two years of profitability ([founder-reported, Gamma](https://gamma.app/insights/how-we-built-a-usd100m-business-differently)). Its story is a reminder that an AI pivot can rescue an existing product rather than requiring a new company.

## What should founders learn from Gamma?

- **One AI feature can rescue a struggling product.** Gamma bet its last year of runway on AI and it worked.
- **Pick a format that fits generation.** Flexible cards suited AI-made content better than fixed slides.
- **Shared output is free distribution.** Every presentation shown to an audience demonstrates the product.
- **Lean teams can scale profitably.** About 50 people and $23M raised before $100M ARR.
