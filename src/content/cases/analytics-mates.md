Analytics Mates has a revenue profile that looks lopsided at first glance. It had only 4 active subscriptions in November 2026, yet $716,503 in lifetime revenue and $23,786 collected in the last 30 days ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/analytics-mates)). The explanation is in the business itself: a services company for Google Analytics 4 that has also built its own AI software product, The Helm.

## What does Analytics Mates sell besides The Helm?

Analytics Mates sells hands-on GA4 support services alongside The Helm, according to its TrustMRR listing ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/analytics-mates)). The company says it combines automation with practical experience working across hundreds of brands, and serves agencies and enterprise clients. It reports a 5.0 rating on Clutch.

> "Most teams lose performance because the data underneath the dashboard is unreliable" — Analytics Mates company statement ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/analytics-mates))

That problem statement explains the pairing: fix the measurement first, then make the reporting readable.

## How does Analytics Mates make most of its revenue?

Most Analytics Mates revenue does not come from subscriptions. The company had $4,648 MRR from 4 active subscriptions, but collected $23,786 in the last 30 days and $716,503 all time by November 2026 ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/analytics-mates)).

The rest is consistent with project-based service work. Team size and the founders' names are not disclosed; the X account on the listing is the company's own.

## What does The Helm do with Google Analytics data?

The Helm is an AI-powered platform that helps teams reason about their Google Analytics data, surfacing clear insights and prioritising actions ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/analytics-mates)). Its pitch is reporting a team can read in seconds and defend in the boardroom. Prices are not disclosed on TrustMRR; the listing says self-serve plans can be reviewed inside the app.

Analytics Mates, [AEO Engine](/cases/aeo-engine/) and [Backlinker AI](/cases/backlinker-ai/) all sell to marketers, but Analytics Mates earns mainly from services while the other two report large recurring revenue.

Our observation: judging Analytics Mates by MRR alone would badly undercount it. Service revenue funds the software, and the agency experience shapes what The Helm automates.

## What takeaways does Analytics Mates offer service businesses?

- **Services can bankroll a product.** Most of the $716,503 all-time total appears to come from non-subscription work.
- **MRR is not the whole story.** Four subscriptions sit beside $23,786 collected in 30 days.
- **Productise what you already do by hand.** The Helm automates insight work the team did across hundreds of brands.
- **Social proof matters in services.** A 5.0 Clutch rating supports high-trust client work.
