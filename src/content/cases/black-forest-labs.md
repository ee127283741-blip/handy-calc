Black Forest Labs revenue went from zero to about $96M ARR in roughly a year. The German lab behind the FLUX image models launched in August 2024 and had about $96.3M in annual recurring revenue by August 2025, Bloomberg reported, citing a person familiar with the matter ([media-reported, Sifted](https://sifted.eu/articles/black-forest-labs-meta)). Its founders had already built one of the most famous image models, Stable Diffusion, at their previous job. This time they kept the business.

## Who founded Black Forest Labs?

Robin Rombach, Patrick Esser and Andreas Blattmann founded Black Forest Labs after helping develop the Stable Diffusion models, and Rombach is CEO ([Pulse 2.0](https://pulse2.com/black-forest-labs-300-million-series-b-at-3-25-billion-valuation-raised-to-advance-frontier-visual-intelligence/)). The company is based in Germany's Black Forest region. Its FLUX models generate and edit images, and companies pay to use them through an API or licenses. Black Forest Labs' plan prices and team size are not disclosed.

## How did Black Forest Labs make $96M ARR in a year?

Black Forest Labs made most of its money from businesses that build FLUX into their own products. The $96.3M ARR figure dates from August 2025, about $8M a month (calc) ([media-reported, Sifted](https://sifted.eu/articles/black-forest-labs-meta)). Bloomberg also reported that Meta agreed to pay about $140M over multiple years to use its image models ([media-reported, Bloomberg Law](https://news.bloomberglaw.com/private-equity/meta-to-pay-140-million-to-use-black-forest-labs-ai-for-images)). The company projected $300M ARR in fiscal 2026, a forecast we label unverified ([unverified, Sifted](https://sifted.eu/articles/black-forest-labs-meta)). Black Forest Labs declined to comment on revenue, and total revenue is not disclosed.

## What did Black Forest Labs raise in 2025?

Black Forest Labs raised a $300M Series B in December 2025 at a $3.25B post-money valuation, co-led by Salesforce Ventures and AMP, with a16z, General Catalyst and Nvidia among existing backers and Canva and Figma Ventures joining ([founder-reported, Pulse 2.0](https://pulse2.com/black-forest-labs-300-million-series-b-at-3-25-billion-valuation-raised-to-advance-frontier-visual-intelligence/)). Against the reported $96.3M ARR, that valuation is about 34 times revenue (calc). In July 2026 the company announced FLUX 3, trained across images, video, audio and actions.

> "We are only beginning to scratch the surface of versatile, capable, unified visual models" — Robin Rombach ([founder-reported, company release](https://norfolkdailynews.com/online_features/press_releases/black-forest-labs-unveils-flux-3-a-new-multimodal-frontier-model-for-visual-intelligence/article_5d86f39d-2be2-5e96-8fb6-d40ec6e53bec.html))

## How does Black Forest Labs compare with Midjourney and Photoroom?

Black Forest Labs sells models to businesses; [Midjourney](/cases/midjourney/) sells an image tool to individuals. Midjourney's revenue comes from monthly subscriptions paid by creators. Black Forest Labs earns from API usage and licenses, including large contracts like Meta's. [Photoroom](/cases/photoroom/) sits on the other side of that trade: an app company that sells finished image editing to sellers and can choose which models to use underneath.

Our reading: a model maker with a few very large licensees can grow ARR very fast, as Black Forest Labs did. The trade-off is concentration. When one buyer such as Meta can be a large share of revenue, renewals matter more than for a subscription app with millions of small customers.

## What are the takeaways from Black Forest Labs for model makers?

- **Research reputation opens doors.** The Stable Diffusion background helped the founders sign large licensees within a year.
- **License to the giants.** A multi-year Meta deal can add tens of millions in revenue in one contract.
- **Sell through APIs, not only apps.** FLUX reaches users inside other companies' products.
- **Big contracts concentrate risk.** A few large licensees make renewals the key number to watch.
