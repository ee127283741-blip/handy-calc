In a library full of AI support bots, Evergreen Support is a reminder that people still pay for people. The agency answers customer email for small online businesses, and in November 2026 it had $25,500 in monthly recurring revenue from only 23 active subscriptions ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/evergreen-support-llc)). All-time revenue was $839,808. That is a high-touch, high-ticket business hiding inside a SaaS-style revenue page.

## What does Evergreen Support do for small online businesses?

Evergreen Support runs a client's day-to-day support inbox with US-based specialists. The homepage lists four jobs: answer requests within 24 hours on weekdays, document support processes, spot customer trends and report them, and cover each other's vacations and sick days ([founder-reported, Evergreen Support homepage](https://evergreensupport.co/)). The listing describes the clients as online sellers and small online businesses.

> "Spend your time working on your business instead of in your business." — Evergreen Support homepage ([founder-reported, Evergreen Support](https://evergreensupport.co/))

The homepage says Emma and Ellis met while working at a startup in 2021 and started Evergreen Support when that startup closed. TrustMRR names Emma Fletcher as founder and July 2022 as the founding date.

## How does Evergreen Support make $25,500 a month from 23 clients?

Evergreen Support makes its money from a few large retainers. MRR of $25,500 across 23 subscriptions is about $1,109 per subscription ([calc, from TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/evergreen-support-llc)), far above typical software prices. The listing says onboarding starts at $1 and clients then pay recurring monthly or weekly charges ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/evergreen-support-llc)).

## Why is human support still a business for Evergreen Support in the AI era?

The pitch is the personal touch. The listing frames the problem as giving great support as a small business "without losing the personal touch" ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/evergreen-support-llc)), and the homepage says plainly that there is no fancy marketing lingo. The tech stack lists only Stripe.

Daily revenue in the snapshot arrives in lumps of $500 to $3,020 with many zero days ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/evergreen-support-llc)), which fits weekly or monthly invoices from a small client list. The trade-off is concentration: losing one client moves the total far more than losing one subscriber at a $9 app.

## Is Evergreen Support different from AI chatbots like Chatbase and SiteGPT?

Chatbase and SiteGPT sell AI chatbots that replace support work; Evergreen Support sells the work itself.

Our observation: [Chatbase](/cases/chatbase/) and [SiteGPT](/cases/sitegpt/) need many customers at software prices, while Evergreen Support reaches a similar monthly scale with two dozen accounts. The AI tools win on scale; the agency wins on revenue per customer and on clients who want a human answering.

## What takeaways does Evergreen Support offer service founders?

- **Few clients, high value.** About $1,109 per subscription means 23 accounts are enough for a $25,500 MRR business ([calc, from TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/evergreen-support-llc)).
- **Lower the first step.** A $1 onboarding offer turns a big service decision into a small trial.
- **Position against automation.** "Personal touch" is a clear reason to choose a human team while AI bots spread.
- **Steady beats spiky.** Revenue between about $19,000 and $28,000 a month for a year shows how sticky outsourced support can be.
