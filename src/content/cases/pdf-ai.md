## Origin of the idea
"Chat with your PDF" was one of the first clear consumer uses of GPT models. Chen, who already ran Testimonial.to, bet that a memorable exact-match domain would win search and word of mouth in a crowded category.

## How they started
He acquired the PDF.ai domain, shipped a version quickly and launched it to his X audience.

## Tools & tech stack
LLM APIs with retrieval over uploaded documents, wrapped in a simple upload-and-chat web app.

## Marketing
The domain did a lot of work: it is easy to remember and ranks for its own name. Early lifetime deals brought upfront cash and reviews.

## Sales & pricing
Monthly subscription with a free tier; lifetime deals were offered early on.

## Monthly revenue
**$25,070** in September 2023, with $17,600 profit (founder-reported); the 1,250+ paying users figure is unverified.

## Total revenue
Not disclosed.

## Founding date & timeline
- **Jun 2023:** Product launched on the PDF.ai domain.
- **Sep 2023:** $25K revenue in the month.

## Team size
Not disclosed.

## Key takeaways
- In a crowded category, brand and domain can be the edge.
- Existing audience plus fast launch beats a long build.
- Track profit, not only revenue: API costs are real.
