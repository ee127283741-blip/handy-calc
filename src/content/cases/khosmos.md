A plumber standing in a client's kitchen does not want to open a laptop. Khosmos bets that a phone app which drafts a quote in under a minute is enough to win that sale. In November 2026 the French app had $22,578 in monthly recurring revenue and $387,004 in all-time revenue ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/khosmos)), built for a niche most software founders ignore: builders and tradespeople.

## What does the Khosmos app do for French artisans?

Khosmos lets tradespeople create quotes, collect e-signatures on site or remotely, convert quotes to invoices in one click and take payment. The listing adds reduced-VAT certificates and job reports to that list, and says the target is micro-entrepreneurs and firms with 1 to 50 employees ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/khosmos)).

The homepage now leads with regulation: it tells visitors that electronic invoicing is mandatory and that Khosmos is compliant ([founder-reported, Khosmos homepage](https://khosmos.fr/)). Around the core app it has added phone tracking, a directory listing, scheduling, a marketplace and free tools such as a VAT calculator.

> "Create complete and professional quotes in less than 1 minute." — Khosmos listing ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/khosmos))

## How does Khosmos find tradespeople to sign up?

The listing names five marketing channels: SEO, app store optimization, Facebook, Instagram and TikTok ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/khosmos)). Free tools on the site, such as a quote generator and a company valuation estimate, fit the SEO side. A referral offer gives two months free to both the user and a colleague.

The stack is Flutter for the mobile app, Laravel on the back end and Stripe for payments. TrustMRR also counts 265.3K lines of code and 456 contributions in the last year, a sign of steady shipping.

## What lessons does Khosmos offer vertical SaaS builders?

- **A narrow niche can carry a business.** One trade in one country produced $387,004 in all-time revenue ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/khosmos)).
- **Regulation is a sales message.** The homepage leads with mandatory e-invoicing, not with features ([founder-reported, Khosmos homepage](https://khosmos.fr/)).
- **Build for the job site.** A phone-first app matches users who work away from a desk.
- **Widen around the core.** Phone tracking, scheduling and a marketplace give existing users more reasons to stay.
