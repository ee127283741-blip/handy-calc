Fyxer AI ARR grew thirtyfold in one year. The AI email assistant says it went from $1M to $30M ARR during 2025 and was adding about $1M of new ARR a week ([founder-reported, Fyxer press page](https://www.fyxer.com/press)). Unlike most AI startups here, Fyxer did not start as software. Brothers Richard and Archie Hollingsworth spent years running a human executive assistant business, then turned what their assistants did all day into a product.

## How did an assistant agency become Fyxer AI?

Fyxer AI came out of the founders' own staffing business. Fyxer says Richard and Archie Hollingsworth built what it calls Europe's largest tech-enabled executive assistant business, and decided the real fix for overloaded inboxes was better technology, not more people ([founder-reported, Fyxer](https://fyxer.com/about-fyxer)). With Matt Ffrench, they built Fyxer AI, which sorts email by priority, drafts replies in the user's voice and writes meeting notes. Fyxer says the product draws on the habits of real executive assistants. The company's founding date and prices are not disclosed.

> "the real cure wasn't more people, it was better technology" — Fyxer AI ([founder-reported, Fyxer](https://fyxer.com/about-fyxer))

## How fast did Fyxer AI ARR grow in 2025?

Fyxer AI ARR grew from $1M to $30M during 2025 ([founder-reported, Fyxer press page](https://www.fyxer.com/press)), about $2.5M a month at year end (calc). The steps in between come from different sources, so we list each with its own label. Richard Hollingsworth told Madrona that Fyxer went from $1M to $10M ARR in six months with about 30 employees ([founder-reported, Madrona](https://www.madrona.com/fyxer-ai-productivity-tools-for-email-and-meetings/)). Total revenue to date is not disclosed.

## How much has Fyxer AI raised?

Fyxer AI raised a $30M Series B in September 2025, led by Madrona with Lakestar participating, bringing total funding to $43M ([founder-reported, Fyxer statement](https://www.fyxer.ai/blog/series-b-statement)). At that point it reported more than $17M ARR and 90% three-month retention. Entrepreneur reported that the team grew from 7 to 56 people in 2025 as Fyxer expanded into the US ([media-reported, Entrepreneur UK](https://uk.entrepreneur.com/entrepreneurs/uk100-start-ups-fyxer-ai)).

## How does Fyxer AI compare with Otter.ai and Fireflies.ai?

Fyxer AI starts in the inbox; [Otter.ai](/cases/otter-ai/) and [Fireflies.ai](/cases/fireflies-ai/) start in meetings. Otter and Fireflies grew over most of a decade with free plans that spread as their bots joined calls. Fyxer reached $30M ARR in its first big year by selling to busy professionals who live in email, and it added meeting notes as a second feature. It is the only company in this library whose main product is the inbox itself.

Our reading: Fyxer's agency years were market research. The founders knew what an assistant does with an executive's email because they billed for it. That shows in the 90% three-month retention it reported at the Series B, which is the number we would watch more closely than ARR growth.

## What are the takeaways from Fyxer AI for service businesses?

- **Automate the service you already sell.** Fyxer turned an executive assistant business into software that does the same job.
- **Retention proves the product.** Fyxer reported 90% three-month retention alongside $17M+ ARR.
- **Start with the inbox.** Email is where professionals feel overload daily, which makes the value easy to see.
- **Grow the team after the revenue.** Fyxer reached $10M ARR with about 30 people and hired to 56 as ARR tripled.
