11x ARR is the cautionary number in this library. The AI sales rep startup said it was approaching $10M ARR two years after launch. Then TechCrunch reported in March 2025 that former employees said 11x counted trial-like contracts as ARR and that several companies shown as customers on its website were not customers ([media-reported, TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/03/24/a16z-and-benchmark-backed-11x-has-been-claiming-customers-it-doesnt-have)). Because the figure is disputed, we label it unverified. Two months later the founder was out as CEO.

## What does 11x sell?

11x sells "digital workers" for sales teams, led by an AI sales development rep that finds leads, researches them and sends outreach emails. Hasan Sukkar founded the company in 2022 ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/03/24/a16z-and-benchmark-backed-11x-has-been-claiming-customers-it-doesnt-have)). The pitch was that one AI rep could do the work of a human rep at a fraction of the cost. In September 2024 TechCrunch reported a $50M Series B led by a16z ([media-reported, TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2024/09/30/11x-ai-a-developer-of-ai-sales-reps-has-raised-50m-series-b-led-by-a16z-sources-say)). Its pricing and team size are not disclosed.

## Why is 11x ARR disputed?

11x ARR is disputed because of what it counted. According to TechCrunch, former and current employees said the company treated annual contracts with a roughly 90-day break clause as full ARR, even when customers left after the trial period ([unverified, TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/03/24/a16z-and-benchmark-backed-11x-has-been-claiming-customers-it-doesnt-have)). 11x told TechCrunch it reports contracted ARR to its board and that investors knew which metric it used. One employee cited churn of 70–80% among inbound customers; 11x said retention was 79%. Its monthly and total revenue are not disclosed.

## Which 11x customers were not real customers?

TechCrunch found that 11x had shown logos of companies that said they were not customers. ZoomInfo said it ran a one-month trial and that the product performed worse than its own human reps, and its lawyer threatened legal action; Airtable also said it was not a customer ([media-reported, TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/03/24/a16z-and-benchmark-backed-11x-has-been-claiming-customers-it-doesnt-have)). Other companies, such as Pleo and Rho, confirmed they used 11x. Sukkar publicly denied the allegations, and in May 2025 Bloomberg reported he had stepped down as CEO ([media-reported, Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2025-05-05/ceo-of-andreessen-horowitz-backed-ai-startup-11x-steps-down)).

## How does 11x compare with Artisan and Instantly?

11x, [Artisan](/cases/artisan/) and [Instantly](/cases/instantly/) all sell automated outbound sales, but they report numbers very differently. Instantly sells cold email software on monthly plans and its revenue is founder-reported in this library. Artisan sells an AI rep called Ava with a "stop hiring humans" ad campaign and reported about $5M ARR at its Series A ([founder-reported, TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/04/09/artisan-the-stop-hiring-humans-ai-agent-startup-raises-25m-and-is-still-hiring-humans)). 11x is the only one whose headline ARR is publicly disputed. [Clay](/cases/clay/) sits next to all of them as a data and enrichment tool rather than an AI rep.

Our reading: AI sales reps are easy to trial and hard to keep. Buyers sign to test a new idea, and many leave if the meetings do not come. Counting those trials as ARR makes growth look faster than it is, which is why the gap between contracted and retained revenue matters more in this category than most.

[Lindy](/cases/lindy/) made a similar AI worker promise, then retreated to a "Zapier of AI" pitch and reported high seven figures of revenue in July 2025 ([founder-reported, The SaaS Podcast](https://saasclub.io/podcast/lindy-flo-crivello-450/)).

## What are the lessons from 11x on reporting ARR?

- **Say which ARR you mean.** Contracted ARR that includes break clauses is not the same as retained recurring revenue.
- **Logos are claims.** Showing a company as a customer after a trial invites the exact scrutiny 11x received.
- **Churn is the real metric for AI reps.** Trials are easy to win; renewals show whether the product works.
- **Disclosure problems reach the founder.** The CEO change followed the reporting within about two months.
