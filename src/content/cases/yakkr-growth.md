Most people who start streaming never get past a handful of viewers. Yakkr Growth has sold tools to those streamers since January 2022, and over almost five years it has collected $354,975 in revenue ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/yakkr-growth)). Today its MRR is a much smaller $3,723, and founder Marcus Rowley is looking for a buyer. That contrast between a large lifetime total and a modest current run rate is the story here.

## What does Yakkr Growth give streamers?

Yakkr Growth is an all-in-one platform to help streamers get more viewers, increase engagement and monetise their streams ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/yakkr-growth)). It works across major social and streaming platforms, with AI features, collaboration tools and mentorship from successful Twitch Partners.

The audience is consumer: individual streamers trying to grow. The listed marketing channels are Meta Ads, Google Ads and TikTok, which means paid acquisition plays a bigger role than at most tools on this site.

## Why is Marcus Rowley looking for a new owner for Yakkr Growth?

The founder argues the product needs more reach than he can give it.

> "Yakkr has the potential to be an incredible tool for the millions of small streamers trying to be the next PewDiePie." — Marcus Rowley ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/yakkr-growth))

He says Yakkr has had thousands of paid users over the past few years and an extensive range of features, and that a creator, agency or media business could give it the audience and marketing attention it needs.

Our observation: Liinks and Taja AI each solve one task every creator repeats. Yakkr bundles many features for a narrower group, small streamers, and leans on paid ads. A single repeated task seems to retain users better than a broad growth toolkit.

## What can creator-tool founders learn from Yakkr Growth?

- **Lifetime revenue can hide decline.** $354,975 all time sits beside a much smaller current MRR.
- **Small streamers are a hard market.** Many start, few keep paying for growth tools.
- **Paid ads need strong retention.** Meta, Google and TikTok ads only work if subscribers stay.
- **Distribution may be worth more than features.** The founder's pitch to buyers is reach, not code.
