BetterPic's first test customer was a grandfather from Córdoba who wore his hat in every photo. The AI headshots were rough, but his delight told Miguel Rasero he had something. Rasero built BetterPic as his end-of-university project in 2023, and with co-founder Ricardo Ghekiere it grew from about $1,500 a month to $20,016 in gross revenue in July 2024 ([founder-reported, Starter Story](https://www.starterstory.com/stories/betterpic)).

## What did a Minecraft server teach BetterPic's builder?

At 14, Rasero bought a struggling Minecraft server for about €500, using his savings and a small loan from his father. Over four years he turned it into one of the top Spanish-speaking servers, with more than 2 million players and a team of 25+, learning DevOps, marketing, cybersecurity and even machine learning for anti-cheat.

On 27 February 2023 he tried a tool that made fantasy-style selfies, and the idea clicked: build the best solution for professional profile pictures. Google Trends showed growing demand. He spent three months on an AI crash course with Stable Diffusion and custom pipelines until faces looked right and strange textures were mostly gone.

> "Free product feedback can be deceiving - they might not value it the same if they were paying." — Miguel Rasero ([founder-reported, Starter Story](https://www.starterstory.com/stories/betterpic))

## How did BetterPic get from a slow start to a partnership?

Rasero launched in August 2023, just before a vacation and with some parts unfinished. August brought €343.24 and September €2,448.84 ([founder-reported, Starter Story](https://www.starterstory.com/stories/betterpic)). On 1 December 2023, Ricardo Ghekiere, a serial founder who ran the marketing agency saasmic.io, joined as co-founder after conversations about acquiring the business. At that point BetterPic averaged about $1,500 a month.

## How did one price change boost BetterPic's sales?

The founders noticed that over half of buyers chose the cheapest $25 package, which would never make the business profitable. Their theory was that the $10 gap to the $35 package felt too big. So they raised the basic package to $29 without changing what it included ([founder-reported, Starter Story](https://www.starterstory.com/stories/betterpic)).

The middle $35 package then saw a 400% increase in sales and became the best seller ([founder-reported, Starter Story](https://www.starterstory.com/stories/betterpic)). Ghekiere's broader point is that changing the product and offer moves growth more than tweaking bids on Google or targeting on Facebook.

With an average order of about $35, the team invested early in SEO: blog posts, programmatic "near me" pages for cities and free tools such as a LinkedIn AI picture analyser. They pushed "studio AI headshots" from position 43 to 8 on Google. Automated Pinterest posts of 100+ headshots, influencers and paid ads on Google and Reddit added more channels ([founder-reported, Starter Story](https://www.starterstory.com/stories/betterpic)).

## How much revenue do BetterPic AI headshots make?

July 2024 closed at $20,016 in gross revenue, up 90% from $10,555 in June, and was BetterPic's first profitable month excluding salaries ([founder-reported, Starter Story](https://www.starterstory.com/stories/betterpic)). The team had five people plus the two founders and had generated over 11 million headshots. Total revenue is not disclosed.

## How does BetterPic compare with HeadshotPro and Photo AI?

[HeadshotPro](/cases/headshotpro/) is BetterPic's most direct competitor: both turn selfies into professional headshots and both invested heavily in model quality. HeadshotPro's founder built a long chain of custom models; BetterPic's founders talk more about pricing psychology and SEO as growth levers, including a B2B offer for company headshots.

[Photo AI](/cases/photo-ai/) sells the broader version, photos of you in any setting, by subscription, run by one person. BetterPic sells packages per order, like HeadshotPro, which suits a purchase most people make once. The three cases show how many businesses one Stable Diffusion insight could support.

## Which lessons from BetterPic apply to pricing?

- **Anchor your cheapest plan.** Raising $25 to $29 made $35 look reasonable and lifted its sales 400% ([founder-reported, Starter Story](https://www.starterstory.com/stories/betterpic)).
- **Launch before it feels ready.** BetterPic shipped before a vacation and learned from the first few hundred euros.
- **Low order values need organic growth.** At about $35 an order, SEO had to carry acquisition.
- **Trust paying users' feedback most.** Free feedback is softer than feedback from customers.
