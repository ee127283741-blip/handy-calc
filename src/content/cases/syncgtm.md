Clay made data enrichment a hot category for sales teams, and a price point that leaves room underneath. SyncGTM, a two-to-five-person Australian company founded in June 2025, built its pitch on that room. By November 2026 it had $4,314 in MRR and $22,740 in all-time revenue ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/syncgtm)). Its plans start at $49 a month.

## What does SyncGTM automate for sales teams?

SyncGTM automates lead sourcing, email and phone finding, email verification and AI account research for SDRs, according to its listing ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/syncgtm)). Its audience is GTM engineers, SDRs, RevOps teams and agencies. The company says it has 50+ integrations and a GTM data MCP server with 50+ endpoints.

> "AI native GTM automation platform. Affordable alternative to Clay." — SyncGTM listing on TrustMRR ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/syncgtm))

## How much MRR does SyncGTM make?

SyncGTM made $4,314 in MRR from 36 active subscriptions in November 2026, with $5,282 collected in the last 30 days and $22,740 all time ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/syncgtm)).

## Why is SEO the engine behind SyncGTM?

SyncGTM's main traffic claim is organic search. The company reports about 9k high-intent website visits a month, about 30 organic sign-ups a day, and says it outranks Apollo and ZoomInfo on some high-intent queries ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/syncgtm)). Its domain rating was 54 out of 100.

The gap is conversion. About 3,500 users, or 4,000+ sign-ups by another field on the same page, against 36 paying subscriptions. The company says sign-ups from GitLab and Cloudflare are waiting to be activated, which shows where it sees upside.

## How does SyncGTM differ from Clay and Gojiberry AI?

SyncGTM is a small, cheaper take on the category that Clay leads, while Gojiberry AI focuses on buying signals on LinkedIn.

| Company | Focus | Revenue figure | Source |

| SyncGTM | Enrichment and GTM automation, from $49 | $4,314 MRR (Nov 2026) | [verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/syncgtm) |

| [Gojiberry AI](/cases/gojiberry-ai/) | LinkedIn buying-signal prospecting | $27K MRR (Dec 2025) | [founder-reported, Indie Hackers](https://www.indiehackers.com/post/tech/validating-via-services-before-building-a-27k-mrr-product-7FrT6FXChPOiUK4PbXFp) |

Our observation: naming the market leader in your tagline is a cheap way to borrow search demand. SyncGTM's "Clay alternative" pages sell to buyers already educated by Clay. Gojiberry AI took a different route, validating with services before building.

## What takeaways does SyncGTM leave for B2B SaaS founders?

- **Position under a category leader.** "Affordable alternative to Clay" explains the product in five words.
- **Comparison SEO can beat incumbents.** The company says it outranks Apollo and ZoomInfo on high-intent searches.
- **Sign-ups are not revenue.** Thousands of users have produced 36 paying subscriptions so far.
- **Tiered plans lift the average.** A $49 entry plan coexists with an average subscription near $120 a month.
