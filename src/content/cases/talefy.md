Choose-your-own-adventure books used to have a fixed number of endings. Talefy lets an AI write a new one every time. The US company, founded in May 2023, had earned $974,399 in lifetime revenue by November 2026 and was running at $17,775 in monthly recurring revenue ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/talefy)). It is closing in on its first million dollars without a public founder.

## What kind of game is Talefy?

Talefy is an AI story game: users create or explore interactive stories, and the narrative reacts to their choices. The listing pitches it to writers, readers, gamers and dreamers, and says it helps people beat writer's block with AI-generated prompts, plot twists and ideas ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/talefy)).

> "AI-Powered Interactive Story Games. Immerse Yourself in Endless Possibilities" — Talefy description on TrustMRR ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/talefy))

The product sits between a creative writing tool and a game, which lets it market to both audiences.

Talefy has earned $974,399 all time, and in November 2026 it showed $17,775 MRR from 590 active subscriptions, with $18,294 collected in the last 30 days ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/talefy)).

## Why is Talefy's average subscription worth $30?

The average Talefy subscription is worth about $30 a month (calc: $17,775 / 590, [from TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/talefy)), even though prices start at $1.99. The low entry price is a hook; heavy players likely move to larger plans or buy more generations, since every AI story turn costs compute. Plan details beyond the starting price are not disclosed, and neither is team size.

## What lessons does Talefy teach about AI entertainment products?

- **Structure turns chat into a game.** Talefy packages AI text as interactive stories with choices and twists.
- **A low entry price brings players in.** Prices from $1.99 still average out to about $30 per subscription.
- **Entertainment revenue can fade.** All-time revenue of $974,399 is high relative to today's $17,775 MRR.
- **A brand can outrun its founder.** Talefy approached seven figures in lifetime revenue without a named founder on its listing.
