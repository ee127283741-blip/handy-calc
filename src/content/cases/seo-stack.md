Google Search Console only keeps 16 months of data and shows 1,000 rows at a time. SEO Stack built a business on removing those limits. In November 2026 it showed $21,977 in MRR and $516,147 in all-time revenue ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/seo-stack)) from just 184 active subscriptions. That is a small customer list paying professional prices.

## What Search Console limits does SEO Stack remove?

SEO Stack removes the data caps that frustrate SEO professionals. The listing promises no 16-month data caps, no 1,000-row restrictions, unlimited exports and full historical access ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/seo-stack)). It describes the product as a rebuild of Google Search Console combined with Google Analytics.

The homepage says data keeps accumulating from the day you connect, with layered filters across all of it ([founder-reported, SEO Stack homepage](https://seo-stack.io/)). Around that warehouse sit 33 tools: rank tracking, site audits, keyword and intent analysis, content scoring, a title rewriter, client reporting and team task management.

> "Strong SaaS, good MOAT, established and as a data warehousing platform a lot easier to retain and scale client base." — Daniel Foley Carter ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/seo-stack))

## Why did SEO Stack add AI visibility tracking?

SEO Stack added AI visibility because search is no longer only Google's ten blue links. Its V3 release tracks whether ChatGPT, Gemini and Claude reference your brand or a competitor's, and adds prompt research and an AI assistant that answers questions about your own data ([founder-reported, SEO Stack homepage](https://seo-stack.io/)). The listing now pitches better results "in search engines and LLMs".

This is an extension, not a pivot. The warehouse remains the base, and AI visibility gives existing customers, mostly SEO professionals and agencies, another reason to stay. The founder's message stresses retention, and a data warehouse naturally gets more valuable the longer a customer stays connected.

## What lessons does SEO Stack teach about data tools?

- **Build on a platform's limits.** Search Console's 16-month and 1,000-row caps created SEO Stack's market ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/seo-stack)).
- **Stored history is a moat.** Data that accumulates from day one becomes harder to replace each month.
- **Few customers can be enough.** 184 subscriptions produced $21,977 in MRR ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/seo-stack)).
- **Extend into the next channel.** Tracking ChatGPT, Gemini and Claude mentions keeps an SEO tool relevant as search changes.
