Netflix has everything, which is exactly the problem for some parents. DivineTV sells the opposite: a narrow catalog with only Christian content. The app launched in January 2026 and had $112,642 in all-time revenue and $9,144 in MRR by November 2026 ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/divinetv)). That is more than $100,000 in its first eleven months or so.

## What is DivineTV and who watches it?

DivineTV is a Christian streaming service for families. It offers movies, series, documentaries, sermons and biblical stories in one app, and its listing says it avoids unwanted content and ads ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/divinetv)). The audience is Christian families, and about 50,000 people use it, according to the company.

> "Just like Netflix, but only Christian content for the whole family" — DivineTV listing on TrustMRR ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/divinetv))

## How much does DivineTV earn from subscriptions?

DivineTV earned $9,144 in MRR from 2,326 active subscriptions in November 2026, with $7,017 collected in the last 30 days and $112,642 all time ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/divinetv)). The numbers are verified through Superwall, the paywall tool in its stack.

## Why does DivineTV rely on Meta Ads?

DivineTV lists Meta Ads as its only marketing channel ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/divinetv)). Its domain rating was 3 out of 100, so the web plays little role. Facebook and Instagram let an advertiser reach people by interest, which suits a faith-based family product.

An annual price matters for paid acquisition. Collecting $39.99 up front helps cover the cost of an ad-driven install sooner than a small monthly fee would.

## What can consumer app founders learn from DivineTV?

- **A narrow catalog can be the feature.** "Only Christian content" is the reason to subscribe.
- **Borrow a famous comparison.** "Like Netflix, but" explains the product instantly.
- **Annual pricing supports paid ads.** Up-front cash helps fund Meta Ads acquisition.
- **Ratings build trust in a values-led niche.** 633 ratings averaging 4.7 back the family-safe promise.
