Maps know where a bar is and when it opens. They rarely know whether it will be packed at 10 p.m. on a Friday. BestTime sells that missing layer as an API, and in November 2026 it had $10,060 in monthly recurring revenue and $146,681 in all-time revenue ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/private-location-intelligence-api-platform)). The TrustMRR listing says one person runs it.

## What does the BestTime foot traffic API forecast?

BestTime forecasts hourly busyness for millions of public places, using anonymized GPS data to show visitor patterns and peaks. The company-written TrustMRR listing adds filters by country, city, neighborhood, venue type, rating and visit duration, plus an API for third-party apps ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/private-location-intelligence-api-platform)).

The homepage lists use cases: nightlife apps, city guides and travel, site selection, digital outdoor advertising, retail and hospitality operations, and tourism crowd management ([founder-reported, BestTime homepage](https://besttime.app/)). It also offers widgets and a demo tool called Radar.

> "Know When Places Are Busy" — BestTime homepage ([founder-reported, BestTime](https://besttime.app/))

## How does a one-person team sell BestTime to city builders?

The listing names only two marketing channels: SEO and a blog ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/private-location-intelligence-api-platform)). That fits a product for developers, who tend to search for "foot traffic API" when they need one rather than respond to ads. BestTime's domain rating of 48 is high for a small company and supports the SEO-first approach.

The tech stack is plain: JavaScript on the front end, Python, Docker and PostgreSQL behind it, and Stripe for billing. The company is bootstrapped and based in Singapore, founded in May 2025.

## What lessons does BestTime offer API founders?

- **Sell the missing dimension.** BestTime adds "when" to location data that already covers "where".
- **A free test key lowers the barrier.** Developers can try the data before paying, then move onto plans from $29 a month.
- **Wide price ranges need enterprise buyers.** A top plan near $2,999 a month only pays off if larger customers exist.
- **SEO suits developer tools.** BestTime lists just SEO and a blog as channels and still reached $10,060 in MRR.
