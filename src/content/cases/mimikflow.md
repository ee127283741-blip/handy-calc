LinkedIn prospecting is, as MimikFlow's own listing puts it, a second job. MimikFlow hands that job to an AI agent. Founded in France in March 2026, it had $11,191 in monthly recurring revenue and 81 active subscriptions by November 2026 ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/mimikflow)). Most of that arrived in the last four months, which makes it one of the faster climbers in the sales tools on this site.

## How does the MimikFlow AI setter work on LinkedIn?

MimikFlow runs the outbound sequence end to end. The homepage lists lead search, a personalised first message for every accepted connection, adaptive follow-ups, an "AI setter" that carries the conversation to a call, and meeting booking with reminders and no-show recovery ([founder-reported, MimikFlow homepage](https://mimikflow.com/)). It also offers WhatsApp replies and an MCP integration.

> "Your LinkedIn AI agent spots hot buying signals, convinces your prospects and books the call." — MimikFlow listing ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/mimikflow))

The buyer is B2B professionals who prospect on LinkedIn, and the listing estimates 2,647 users. The stack is Next.js on the front end with Python and Stripe behind it.

## How quickly has MimikFlow revenue grown since March 2026?

MimikFlow revenue took off in the summer. Verified monthly revenue was under $2,000 from April to June 2026, then rose to $3,455 in July, $7,342 in August, $9,522 in September and $10,986 in October ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/mimikflow)). The 30 days before the snapshot brought in $11,113, up 26.7%, and MRR rose 15.3%.

## What does MimikFlow's content strategy say about its growth?

MimikFlow's site is built like a content machine. Beyond the product pages, it publishes a LinkedIn prospecting guide, a tool comparison with prices it says are verified, free calculators and generators, a glossary and 2026 statistics drawn from 98,426 prospects ([founder-reported, MimikFlow homepage](https://mimikflow.com/)). It also runs an affiliate programme.

Traffic is modest but valuable. TrustMRR showed 1,749 visitors in the last 30 days and $6.53 of revenue per visitor ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/mimikflow)), measured through connected Google Analytics. A small, high-intent audience of salespeople converts well when the product promises booked meetings.

## What can sales-tool founders learn from MimikFlow?

- **Sell the meeting, not the message.** MimikFlow pitches booked calls, which is the outcome a salesperson is paid on.
- **Tiers lift the average.** About $138 per subscription against a €49 entry plan shows upgrades doing real work ([calc, from TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/mimikflow)).
- **Content can carry a young product.** Guides, comparisons and free tools give a seven-month-old company search traffic with $6.53 revenue per visitor.
- **Growth can arrive late.** Revenue was flat for three months before climbing, so early months are not a verdict.
