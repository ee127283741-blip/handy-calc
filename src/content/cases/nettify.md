Lovable and Base44 made AI site building a global race. Nettify picked one small country instead. The Norwegian AI website builder had $2,005 in MRR and 57 active subscriptions in November 2026, and it collected $5,414 in the last 30 days, up 90.4% ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/nettify)). Its seller says the local market was also the limit, and the business is listed for sale.

## What does Nettify build for Norwegian small businesses?

Nettify builds complete small-business websites from a description. Its homepage says a business describes itself and gets a finished site with hosting, SEO and a .no domain, free to try and with no code ([founder-reported, Nettify homepage](https://nettify.no/)). The site also sells online stores with Vipps payments, domain registration, web hosting and a monthly SEO service.

> "Selling to focus on other projects." — Nettify seller message ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/nettify))

The TrustMRR description adds an AI editor and a manual editor, ready-made templates, custom domains, built-in analytics, and lead capture and booking on every site ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/nettify)). The founders and team size are not disclosed.

## How does Nettify make money beyond its $2,005 MRR?

Nettify mixes subscriptions with one-time site sales. The seller describes revenue from recurring subscriptions plus one-time site sales ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/nettify)), which is why last-30-day revenue of $5,414 is more than twice MRR. MRR per subscription is about $35 ([calc, from TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/nettify)).

## Why is Nettify listed for sale at $199,000?

The seller says distribution was the bottleneck. The listing shows Nettify for sale since May 2026 at $199,000, a 3.1x revenue multiple, with 8 acquisition offers received ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/nettify)). The seller message calls the small Norwegian market the limit and pitches a buyer with larger reach.

The listed channels are SEO, Facebook, Instagram and Google Ads. The stack is React, Node.js and PostgreSQL on Hetzner, with Clerk for login, which the seller describes as low-cost to run. A buyer would get a working product with a local language moat and also a local ceiling.

Our observation: Lovable and Base44 sell to builders who want an app. Nettify sells to a plumber or salon that wants a site, a domain and leads, in Norwegian and with Vipps checkout. Localisation is its edge against global tools, but the same choice limits its market size.

## What lessons can founders learn from Nettify?

- **Local can be a moat.** Norwegian language, .no domains and Vipps payments give Nettify reasons to exist next to global AI builders ([founder-reported, Nettify homepage](https://nettify.no/)).
- **Bundle the boring parts.** Domains, hosting and SEO turn a site generator into a full small-business package.
- **One-time sales lift early revenue.** Site sales pushed 30-day revenue to $5,414, well above MRR.
- **Market size sets the exit.** The seller names a small market as the bottleneck, and the asking price reflects it.
