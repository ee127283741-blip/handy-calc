Numbers Game Limited has been selling football predictions since 2021, long before most AI tools on this site existed. The UK company had collected $549,984 in lifetime revenue by November 2026, while its current MRR stood at $4,593 from 379 subscriptions ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/numbers-game-limited)). That pairing, a large lifetime total and a modest present, is the most interesting thing about it.

## What does Numbers Game sell to football fans?

Numbers Game sells AI-powered football predictions through numbersgame.uk. The listing promises everything needed to make data-backed predictions quicker and easier, and says most punters bet on a whim because they lack the data ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/numbers-game-limited)). Its audience is football punters and enthusiasts.

> "Everything you need to make data-backed football predictions quicker, easier and have more fun whilst doing it." — Numbers Game value proposition on TrustMRR ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/numbers-game-limited))

Pricing is not disclosed; the listing says Contact Sales. The company tags itself under analytics, AI and games.

## Why does Numbers Game's revenue follow the football calendar?

Football prediction products depend on matches being played, so demand tends to rise and fall with the season. This is our reading, not a disclosed fact: the listing does not give monthly history. What it does show is that 30-day revenue ($4,776) and MRR ($4,593) were close in November 2026 ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/numbers-game-limited)), in the middle of the European season, which suggests mostly recurring billing right now.

The website has an Ahrefs Domain Rating of 9 out of 100, and no marketing channels are listed. With about 60,000 users and 379 subscriptions, roughly 0.6% of users pay (calc: 379 / 60,000).

## What lessons does Numbers Game teach about long-lived niche products?

- **Longevity compounds.** Five years of steady sales produced $549,984 in verified revenue.
- **Lifetime averages reveal the arc.** An average near $8,870 a month against $4,593 MRR shows an earlier, larger peak.
- **Free users dwarf paying ones.** About 0.6% of 60,000 users hold subscriptions.
- **Hidden pricing is a choice.** A Contact Sales listing for a $12-per-subscription product makes comparison harder for buyers.
