ConvertLabs sells to people who clean houses, mow lawns and wash driveways, not to other startups. That unglamorous market has produced $2,254,141 in all-time revenue, and the business showed $28,083 in MRR in November 2026 ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/convertlabs)). It is one of the older companies in this atlas, founded in April 2018, and its numbers show what a narrow vertical can add up to over several years.

## What does ConvertLabs do for cleaning and lawn care companies?

ConvertLabs is an all-in-one back office for home service businesses. Its homepage lists online booking with auto-charge, a scheduler, a CRM, invoicing, a website builder, email and SMS marketing, a mobile app and customer portal, and an Airbnb sync that turns reservations into cleaning jobs ([founder-reported, ConvertLabs homepage](https://convertlabs.io/)).

The industry pages cover residential and commercial cleaning, carpet cleaning, lawn care, pressure washing, roofing, handyman work, painting, pet walking and car washes. The homepage also promises that a website goes live in under a minute and that support replies in under an hour.

> "Run your home service business from one screen." — ConvertLabs listing ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/convertlabs))

## Why is ConvertLabs priced so far above consumer tools?

ConvertLabs charges business software prices because it replaces several tools a service company would otherwise pay for. The cheapest listed plan is $67 a month and the top plan is $397 ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/convertlabs)). A cleaning company that takes bookings, charges cards and pays staff through one system is buying operations, not a gadget.

The audience is small by design. The listing estimates about 1,000 users, all B2B home service owners. That fits the revenue: a few hundred paying accounts at business prices, rather than thousands of cheap consumer subscriptions. The homepage leans on customer outcomes, such as a testimonial claiming growth from zero to a large booking total in one year.

## What lessons does ConvertLabs offer vertical SaaS builders?

- **Boring markets pay.** Home cleaning and lawn care produced $2,254,141 in verified lifetime revenue ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/convertlabs)).
- **Bundle the whole workflow.** Booking, payments, websites and marketing in one product justify a $67 floor price.
- **Speak the trade's language.** Separate pages for cleaning, roofing, pet walking and car washes make one product feel specific to each.
- **Patience compounds.** A company founded in 2018 shows that steady MRR in a niche can add up to seven figures over time.
