Lawyers spend their days in meetings and court, and clients who call get voicemail. VoxNow turns those missed calls into a short written summary and an instant text back to the client. The Belgian startup showed $3,670 in monthly recurring revenue and $28,511 in all-time revenue in November 2026 ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/voxnow)). It is small, but it sells to one of the most conservative software buyers there is.

## What problem does VoxNow solve for Belgian lawyers?

VoxNow handles a law firm's voicemail. The listing says it transcribes each message, summarizes the key information, emails it to the lawyer and sends an automatic SMS reply to the caller ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/voxnow)). The audience is law firms, and the product is sold B2B.

The homepage has since widened the pitch. It calls VoxNow an AI legal assistant built for Belgian firms that catches missed calls, sorts email and prepares new case files, with a 30-day free trial ([founder-reported, VoxNow homepage](https://voxnow.be/)).

> "Your voicemail messages transcribed and summarized in seconds by email, with instant SMS responses to clients." — VoxNow listing ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/voxnow))

## Why does a niche like VoxNow work at €90 a seat?

VoxNow charges €90 per user a month, with custom plans for larger firms ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/voxnow)). For a law firm, one missed client call can matter more than a month of software, so the price is easy to justify if the tool works.

The niche is tight. The listing estimates about 20 users and a domain rating of 16, so VoxNow is not winning through SEO yet. It wins one firm at a time, in French, in one country, with a team of two to five people.

## What lessons does VoxNow offer legal tech founders?

- **Start with the admin, not the law.** VoxNow automates messages, not legal judgment, which lowers the risk for buyers ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/voxnow)).
- **Local language is a moat.** A French-speaking product for Belgian firms faces fewer direct rivals.
- **Seat pricing fits firms.** Charging per lawyer grows with the firm without new plans.
- **Widen from a wedge.** Voicemail came first; email sorting and case intake followed on the homepage.
