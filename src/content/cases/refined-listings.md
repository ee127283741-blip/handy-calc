Judge Refined Listings by MRR and it looks tiny: $1,343 a month from eight subscriptions. Judge it by cash and it looks very different: $14,339 in the 30 days before November 10, 2026, and $279,241 since it was founded ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/refined-listings)). The difference comes from how real estate agents buy. They pay per listing, not per month.

## What does Refined Listings sell to real estate agents?

Refined Listings sells virtual staging, digitally furnishing empty rooms in listing photos, along with marketing tools for agents and property managers. The company-written TrustMRR listing describes it as a virtual staging and marketing toolkit for that audience ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/refined-listings)).

> "Virtual staging and marketing toolkit for real estate agents and property managers." — Refined Listings listing ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/refined-listings))

TrustMRR tags it Real Estate, AI, Design Tools, Marketing and SaaS. The website is refinedlistings.com.

## How does Refined Listings make money if MRR is only $1,343?

Most Refined Listings revenue comes from credit packs, not subscriptions. The listing describes pay-as-you-go packages from $128 to $750 and an 18% repeat purchase rate ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/refined-listings)). Stripe-verified figures show $1,343 MRR, $14,339 in the last 30 days and $279,241 all time ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/refined-listings)).

Thirty-day revenue was about 10.7 times MRR ([calc, from TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/refined-listings)). That ratio is the real business model: one-off purchases tied to each new property. Profit is not disclosed, and neither is the founder.

## How does Refined Listings find buyers?

Refined Listings lists four marketing channels on TrustMRR: cold email, email marketing, a referral program and Google Ads ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/refined-listings)). These are channels for a defined professional buyer. Agents search for staging when a listing goes live, which suits Google Ads, and referrals travel well inside brokerages. The team is 2–5 people in the United States, and the owner says they are open to creative financing or negotiable acquisition terms.

Our observation: Interior AI sells inspiration to a broad audience on subscription. Virtual Staging AI and Refined Listings sell a work output to agents, priced around listings. Refined Listings goes furthest in that direction, bundling staging with marketing tools and charging by credit.

## What takeaways does Refined Listings offer vertical AI founders?

- **Price the way the buyer works.** Agents buy per listing, so credit packs beat a monthly plan.
- **Don't judge by MRR alone.** $1,343 MRR sat beside $14,339 in 30-day revenue.
- **Repeat buyers matter.** An 18% repeat purchase rate is the closest thing to retention in a credit model.
- **Vertical channels work.** Google Ads and referrals reach agents at the moment they need staging.
