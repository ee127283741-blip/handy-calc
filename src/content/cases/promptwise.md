New AI video models launch every few weeks, and few creators want five separate subscriptions. Promptwise sells one login for all of them. Founded in December 2025, per TrustMRR, it showed $67,135 in MRR and $310,515 in all-time revenue by November 2026 ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/promptwise)). That is an aggregator business, and its risks look different from a model maker's.

## What does Promptwise put in one workspace?

Promptwise puts image, video and audio models from several providers into a single workspace with shared credits. Its site names ChatGPT, Google, ElevenLabs, Kling, Flux and Seedance among the models, and adds its own tools on top: Flows, a visual canvas for chaining prompts and models; UGC Factory and Influencer Studio for AI creators in ads; and an assistant called Wise for writing prompts ([founder-reported, Promptwise](https://www.promptwise.com)).

The site also says Promptwise works inside Claude, ChatGPT or Cursor through MCP, and that all output carries a commercial license. Its FAQ promises speed on new models with the line "If it's new, we have it!"

## Who is Promptwise built for?

Promptwise is built for businesses that need a steady stream of content but do not want to make it. The TrustMRR listing tags the audience as SMBs, enterprises and direct consumers, and gives the problem in the company's own words.

> "Business don't want to predict, generate, post or analyze their content.. but we do." — Promptwise listing ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/promptwise))

The listing calls the product a "long horizon content agent" and names Slack communities as its marketing channel. It lists Mateo Starcevic Filipovic as founder, a United States location, a bootstrapped status and a team of 11-25 people ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/promptwise)).

## How much does Promptwise make on each plan?

Promptwise made $67,135 in MRR from 1,995 active subscriptions in November 2026, about $33.65 per subscription ([calc, from TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/promptwise)). Revenue in the last 30 days was higher, at $95,561.

| Plan or figure | Amount | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Starter | $19 a month, 275 credits | [founder-reported, Promptwise](https://www.promptwise.com) |
| Plus | $39 a month, 800 credits | [founder-reported, Promptwise](https://www.promptwise.com) |
| Pro | $79 a month, 1,800 credits | [founder-reported, Promptwise](https://www.promptwise.com) |
| Max | $149 a month, 3,800 credits | [founder-reported, Promptwise](https://www.promptwise.com) |
| MRR (Nov 2026) | $67,135 | [verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/promptwise) |
| All-time revenue (Nov 2026) | $310,515 | [verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/promptwise) |

The gap between 30-day revenue and MRR fits the listing's description of subscriptions plus top-ups, since credit top-ups are not recurring. The TrustMRR listing gives a price range of $9 to $349 ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/promptwise)), wider than the four plans on the site, and the page does not say what sits at either end. Profit is not disclosed.

## How does Promptwise compare with Arcads and Magai?

Promptwise sits between these two: like Magai it bundles other companies' models, and like Arcads it sells AI creators for ads. It is younger and smaller than both.

| Company | Model | Monthly figure | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Promptwise | Multi-model studio with UGC tools | $67,135 MRR (Nov 2026) | [verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/promptwise) |
| [Arcads](/cases/arcads/) | AI actors for video ads | ≈$833K a month, implied from $10M ARR | [founder-reported, Latka](https://getlatka.com/interviews/arcads-romain-torres-2025) |
| [Magai](/cases/magai/) | Many chat and image models, one login | Nearly $100K MRR (Feb 2026) | [founder-reported, Indie Hackers](https://www.indiehackers.com/post/tech/from-broke-to-100k-mrr-in-four-years-hS1wen0srjGNIWZxSnV9) |

Our observation: Magai took about four years to reach nearly $100K MRR, while Promptwise reached two-thirds of that in under a year. The difference may be the use case. Selling "one login for chat models" is a convenience; selling ad creative is tied to a marketing budget, which is where Arcads earns its larger number.

## What are the takeaways from Promptwise for AI wrapper founders?

- **Aggregation can sell fast.** A bundle of other providers' models reached $67,135 MRR within a year of founding ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/promptwise)).
- **Attach the bundle to a business job.** UGC ads and AI influencers give buyers a reason that "every model" alone does not.
- **Credits plus top-ups lift cash.** 30-day revenue ran well above MRR, which is how a credit model behaves.
- **Speed on new models is the moat, and it is thin.** A promise of "if it's new, we have it" must be kept every month.
