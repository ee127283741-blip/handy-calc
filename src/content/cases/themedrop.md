Dropshipping lives on speed: find a product, build a store, test ads, move on. ThemeDrop compresses the store-building step to about 30 seconds by generating a whole Shopify theme from an AliExpress link. The French tool, founded in April 2026, had $6,975 in lifetime revenue by November 2026, yet its MRR was only $388 ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/themedrop)). That split between cash and recurring revenue is the most telling number on the page.

## How does ThemeDrop build a Shopify theme from an AliExpress link?

ThemeDrop takes an AliExpress product link and generates a complete Shopify theme in about 30 seconds, along with marketing content for the store ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/themedrop)). The audience is Shopify store creators and dropshippers.

> "Generate a complete Shopify theme from an AliExpress link in 30 seconds." — ThemeDrop value proposition on TrustMRR ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/themedrop))

The original company description on TrustMRR is in French and calls it software for creating a Shopify theme in a few seconds. The listing tags it under e-commerce, SaaS, design tools and AI. The founder's name is not disclosed.

## What can e-commerce tool builders learn from ThemeDrop?

- **Match billing to the job.** A one-time task sold as a subscription shows up as cash, not MRR.
- **Speed is the product.** Thirty seconds from link to theme is the whole pitch for dropshippers.
- **Look past MRR.** $1,386 in 30-day cash against $388 MRR tells a different story than MRR alone ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/themedrop)).
- **Wide free use, narrow paid base.** About 3,000 users and 31 subscriptions show the conversion challenge.
