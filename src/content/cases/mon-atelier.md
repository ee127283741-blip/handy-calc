Many phone repair shops still track devices on post-its. MON ATELIER, a French software company run by one person, turned that mess into $2,936 of monthly recurring revenue within eight months of its March 2026 founding ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/mon-atelier)). Its edge is not AI or design. It is French regulation that foreign software ignores.

## What problem does MON ATELIER solve for repair shops?

MON ATELIER replaces notebooks, spreadsheets and generic invoicing apps with one ERP for repair tickets, quotes, invoices, stock, POS, online booking and automatic customer notifications ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/mon-atelier)). It serves phone, computer, appliance and bike repair shops in France and Switzerland.

> "Repair shops run on post-its and spreadsheets" — MON ATELIER company description on TrustMRR ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/mon-atelier))

The listing describes the pain points plainly: devices lost track of, parts forgotten on invoices, stock that never matches the shelf and a hand-written legal ledger.

## Why is French compliance MON ATELIER's moat?

Compliance is the feature competitors cannot copy quickly. MON ATELIER builds in NF525, 2026 e-invoicing via Pennylane, an automatic second-hand police ledger, FEC exports and one-click QualiRépar bonus claims ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/mon-atelier)). The listing calls this compliance the moat directly.

Generic invoicing tools ignore repair workflows, and foreign software ignores French rules. A shop that must meet those rules has few real options, which supports retention even at a €19 entry price. The company also reports 4.7/5 on Trustpilot, 99.9% uptime and zero marketing spend, with a 20-store franchise set to onboard soon. Its marketing channels are SEO, Facebook, Instagram and cold email.

## What can vertical SaaS founders learn from MON ATELIER?

- **Regulation can be a moat.** Built-in French compliance gives local shops a reason to choose a local tool.
- **Vertical beats generic.** Repair tickets, parts and stock are workflows generic invoicing apps miss.
- **A solo founder can serve 70 shops.** 72 subscriptions produced $2,936 MRR ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/mon-atelier)) with a team of 1.
- **Chains are the next lever.** A 20-store franchise is a bigger step than signing single shops one by one.
