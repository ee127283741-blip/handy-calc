Legal Tech's numbers do not add up at first glance, and that is the interesting part. The Bulgarian law firm platform showed $17,679 in monthly recurring revenue in November 2026, yet its all-time revenue was $16,652 and it collected only $910 in the last 30 days ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/legal-tech)). A business cannot have earned less in total than it books in a single month, so the MRR figure needs a closer look.

## What does Legal Tech do for Bulgarian law firms?

Legal Tech is a cloud practice management system for lawyers, law firms and in-house legal teams in Bulgaria. The homepage, written in Bulgarian, describes digital case files for clients, cases and deals, reminders for hearings and deadlines, automatic court and company documents, and lookups in the Commercial Register and the courts' e-justice portal ([founder-reported, Legal Tech homepage](https://legal-tech.bg/)).

> "Legal Tech is a cloud platform for managing law firms and legal departments" — Legal Tech listing ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/legal-tech))

The homepage also sells modules for anti-money-laundering checks and GDPR compliance, aimed at gambling operators, brokers and data protection officers as well as lawyers.

## Why is Legal Tech's MRR higher than its all-time revenue?

Legal Tech had $17,679 in MRR and 107 active subscriptions in November 2026, against $16,652 all time and $910 in the prior 30 days ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/legal-tech)). All three come from a Stripe API key.

MRR divided by subscriptions is about $165.22 ([calc, from TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/legal-tech)), roughly double the €79 top list price. Possible explanations include trials counted as paying, subscriptions recently moved to Stripe or annual billing outside the 30-day window, but none is confirmed, so the $910 cash figure is the more conservative signal. The listing does not explain the gap, and profit is not disclosed.

## How does Legal Tech price LT Solo, Standard and Pro?

The TrustMRR listing gives three monthly plans in euros: LT Solo at €19, LT Standard at €49 and LT Pro at €79 ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/legal-tech)). The homepage offers a free trial and a demo request.

The homepage claims 1,200+ users in Bulgaria, 64,500+ automated documents, 15,000 managed cases and 350,000 Commercial Register lookups ([founder-reported, Legal Tech homepage](https://legal-tech.bg/)). Those are usage claims, not revenue.

## How is Legal Tech different from Harvey and Legora?

| Company | Market | Focus |
|---|---|---|
| Legal Tech | Bulgarian solo lawyers and firms | Practice management, €19 to €79 a month ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/legal-tech)) |
| [Harvey](/cases/harvey/) | Large global law firms | Generative AI for legal work |
| [Legora](/cases/legora/) | Large European law firms | AI legal research and drafting |

Our observation: Harvey and Legora sell AI to elite firms at enterprise prices. Legal Tech goes the other way, selling cheap admin automation in one small country, where its edge is integration with local courts and registers that global tools do not cover.

## What are Legal Tech's lessons for local vertical SaaS?

- **Local integrations are a moat.** Links to Bulgarian courts and the Commercial Register are hard for global players to copy.
- **Verified does not mean simple.** $17,679 of MRR beside $16,652 all time shows why one metric is never enough ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/legal-tech)).
- **Small markets need adjacent buyers.** AML and GDPR modules widen the customer base beyond lawyers.
- **Low list prices fit solo practitioners.** A €19 entry plan targets the independent attorney.
