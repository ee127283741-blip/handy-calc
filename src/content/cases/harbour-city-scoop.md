Harbour City Scoop charges readers nothing, and its recurring revenue is a rounding error. Yet the Nanaimo newsletter collected $7,517 in the 30 days before November 2026 and $23,785 since it began ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/harbour-city-scoop)). It is a reminder that MRR is the wrong lens for a local media business like this one.

## What does Harbour City Scoop send its Nanaimo readers?

Harbour City Scoop sends a free weekly roundup of local events, stories and things to do in Nanaimo, British Columbia ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/harbour-city-scoop)). The listing says it emphasizes positive local stories, a deliberate contrast with the tone of much local news.

> "A local newsletter located In Canada" — Harbour City Scoop listing ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/harbour-city-scoop))

The listing tags it as news and magazines, community and entertainment.

## Why is Harbour City Scoop's MRR so low next to its revenue?

Harbour City Scoop had just $83 in MRR in November 2026, but $7,517 in revenue over the last 30 days ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/harbour-city-scoop)). The gap means almost all of its income arrives as one-off payments, not subscriptions.

## How does Harbour City Scoop find readers?

The listing names one marketing channel: Meta ads ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/harbour-city-scoop)). For a city newsletter that makes sense, because Facebook and Instagram ads can target people by location. Derian Thompson's X account shows 97 followers, so the founder's personal audience is not the engine.

Stripe is the only tool listed in the stack. Team size is not disclosed.

## How does Harbour City Scoop differ from TrendFeed and LocalRank?

[TrendFeed](/cases/trendfeed/) is also a content business, but it sells to a broad online audience. [LocalRank](/cases/localrank/) sells local search tools to businesses. Harbour City Scoop combines the two ideas: content as the product, local businesses as the likely payers.

Our observation: a geographic niche caps audience size, but it also gives the newsletter something national media cannot offer advertisers, namely a concentrated local readership.

## What lessons does Harbour City Scoop hold for local media builders?

- **Do not judge a newsletter by MRR.** $83 MRR hid $7,517 in monthly revenue ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/harbour-city-scoop)).
- **Free for readers can still pay.** The reader price is zero, and the business still earned $23,785 all time.
- **Geography is a targeting tool.** Meta ads aimed at one city match a city newsletter.
- **A tone can be a niche.** Choosing positive local stories differentiates it from standard local news.
