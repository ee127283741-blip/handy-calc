Most analytics dashboards tell a founder how many people visited. DataFast tries to answer a narrower question: which of those visitors paid. In November 2026 that pitch was worth $28,203 in monthly recurring revenue and $333,930 in all-time revenue ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/datafast)), and the listing put the team at one person. It is a small, focused business run by a founder with a very large audience.

## What problem does DataFast solve for founders?

DataFast connects traffic data to payment data, so a founder can see which marketing channel produced a paying customer rather than just a visit. The homepage describes three steps: install a script, link a payment processor, then let DataFast analyze the funnel ([founder-reported, DataFast homepage](https://datafa.st/)). It offers plugins for WordPress, Shopify and NPM.

The listing on TrustMRR frames the problem as "tracking revenue and optimizing marketing channels", with marketers and B2B buyers as the audience. Customer quotes on the homepage compare it with Google Analytics and PostHog, and one singles out revenue per visitor as the metric founders need.

> "Discover which marketing channels bring customers so you can grow your business, fast." — DataFast homepage ([founder-reported, DataFast](https://datafa.st/))

## How did Marc Lou grow DataFast without a team?

The listing gives the outline but not the playbook. TrustMRR names eight marketing channels for DataFast: SEO, word of mouth, X, YouTube, a newsletter, content marketing, LinkedIn and Reddit ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/datafast)). It also shows Marc Lou with 423.2k followers on X, which is the clearest distribution advantage on the page.

The business is bootstrapped and listed as a team of one, founded in July 2024 in Singapore. The tech stack is ordinary: Next.js and React on the front end, Node.js, MongoDB and Stripe behind it, hosted on Vercel and Render. The homepage claims 28,112 users ([founder-reported, DataFast homepage](https://datafa.st/)), while the TrustMRR listing says about 25,826, so the user count depends on which page you read and when.

DataFast is the smallest of these three by revenue, but it shares their shape: a solo or near-solo founder, a builder audience and a product sold to other makers.

Our observation: DataFast and Photo AI both lean on a founder with a large personal following on X, while Postiz grew through open source. DataFast is also the only one of the three whose product exists to measure marketing, so its customers are other founders trying to grow, which is the same crowd that follows its founder.

## What lessons does one-person DataFast hold for solo founders?

- **Answer one question well.** DataFast sells "which channel brought paying customers", not a full analytics suite ([founder-reported, DataFast homepage](https://datafa.st/)).
- **An audience is a channel.** A founder with 423.2k X followers starts with distribution most tools have to buy.
- **Low entry price, higher average.** A $9 starting plan sits beside roughly $20.92 of MRR per subscription, which points to upgrades doing real work.
- **Verify the numbers you sell on.** A revenue analytics product with Stripe-verified revenue on TrustMRR is its own proof point ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/datafast)).
