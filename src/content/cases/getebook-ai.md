An ebook used to mean weeks of writing, a designer and a formatting fight. GETebook.ai sells the shortcut: type an idea, get a finished file in minutes. Sixteen months after its July 2025 founding, it showed $11,067 in MRR and $163,809 in all-time revenue ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/getebook-ai)), with a team of one. The more striking number is the last 30 days, which came in at three times MRR.

## What does GETebook.ai turn ideas into?

GETebook.ai generates publish-ready ebooks and exports them to PDF, EPUB and Word. The company-written listing targets course creators, coaches, marketers and indie authors, and claims about 12,000 users ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/getebook-ai)). Its pitch is that the buyer needs no writing degree, no design skills and no ghostwriter.

> "Write it once. Let it earn forever." — GETebook.ai listing ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/getebook-ai))

That line sells the customer's outcome, a lead magnet or product that keeps earning, rather than the AI underneath.

## Why is GETebook.ai's 30-day revenue three times its MRR?

GETebook.ai collected $33,029 in the 30 days before the November 2026 snapshot, against $11,067 of MRR and 231 active subscriptions ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/getebook-ai)). All-time revenue was $163,809. TrustMRR checks these numbers through a Stripe API key.

## How does Bogdan Dragomir market GETebook.ai alone?

The listing names nine channels: a blog, SEO, Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, Google Ads, Meta Ads, TikTok Ads and email marketing ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/getebook-ai)). That is a paid-acquisition mix, unusual for a solo founder with 2,804 followers on X. The business is bootstrapped, and the stack is TypeScript, React, Tailwind CSS and Framer on the front end with Supabase, Vercel and Stripe behind it.

## How does GETebook.ai compare with Jenni AI and Rytr?

All three sell AI writing, but to different jobs.

| Company | Job | Buyer |
|---|---|---|
| GETebook.ai | Finished ebook files | Creators, coaches, marketers |
| [Jenni AI](/cases/jenni-ai/) | Academic writing help | Students and researchers |
| [Rytr](/cases/rytr/) | Short-form copy | Marketers and small businesses |

Our observation: GETebook.ai packages AI writing as a deliverable, a file you can sell or give away, rather than as a writing assistant. That framing lets it lean on paid social ads to creators, who already think in terms of products and lead magnets.

## What lessons does GETebook.ai offer founders selling AI-written ebooks?

- **Sell the artifact, not the model.** "Publish-ready ebook" is easier to buy than "AI writing".
- **Watch cash, not only MRR.** $33,029 in 30 days beside $11,067 MRR shows how much non-monthly billing can matter.
- **Paid ads can work solo.** The listing leans on Google, Meta and TikTok ads instead of a personal audience.
- **Fast to traction.** A July 2025 launch reached $163,809 all time by November 2026.
