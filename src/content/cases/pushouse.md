Email is where most store marketing lives. Pushouse bets on WhatsApp instead. The e-commerce automation tool was founded in January 2026, and by November 2026 it had $28,119 in monthly recurring revenue, 267 active subscriptions and $38,002 collected in the previous 30 days ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/pushouse)). For a ten-month-old company, that is a fast start built on a channel many Western tools treat as an afterthought.

## What can stores automate with Pushouse on WhatsApp?

Pushouse automates the messages a store sends around a purchase. The homepage lists welcome messages, first-purchase incentives, birthday wishes, cart recovery, back-in-stock alerts, order tracking, shipping updates, reorder reminders, win-back messages, chat flows and post-delivery surveys ([founder-reported, Pushouse homepage](https://pushouse.com/)). A second set of on-site modules adds spin-to-win games, pop-ups, shoppable photo tags, AI search and an AI sales assistant.

> "Boost your e-commerce sales effortlessly" — Pushouse listing ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/pushouse))

The listing says Pushouse is a Meta Business Partner, Meta Tech Partner and WhatsApp Solution Partner, and estimates about 1,500 users ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/pushouse)).

## How did Pushouse revenue climb in its first ten months?

Pushouse revenue rose quickly and then held in a band. Verified monthly revenue went from $7,584 in January 2026 to $35,909 in March, peaked at $43,184 in August, and was $37,609 in October ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/pushouse)). The 30 days before the snapshot were up 11.7%, and MRR grew 2.7%.

## How does Pushouse sell to e-commerce businesses?

Pushouse sells with a wide, mostly paid and outbound mix. The listing names Instagram, LinkedIn, Meta Ads, Google Ads, YouTube Ads, cold calling, cold email, events and conferences, and partnerships as channels ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/pushouse)). That is closer to a sales-led B2B company than to the indie, audience-led tools common on TrustMRR.

Pricing is modular. The homepage offers a 14-day trial per module with a card required ([founder-reported, Pushouse homepage](https://pushouse.com/)); module prices and team size are not disclosed. The stack is broad too, from Next.js and Remix to OpenAI and Anthropic models.

## How does Pushouse compare with Store Stock Checker and Postiz?

All three help businesses reach customers, but Pushouse is the only one built around a messaging channel the store owns.

Our observation: [Store Stock Checker](/cases/store-stock-checker/) sells to shoppers for a few dollars a month, while Pushouse sells to the stores at a much higher price per account. [Postiz](/cases/postiz/) posts to social feeds the business does not control; Pushouse messages a customer list directly, which is why cart recovery and order updates are its core.

## What takeaways does Pushouse offer e-commerce SaaS founders?

- **Pick the channel customers read.** Pushouse builds on WhatsApp, where order updates and offers land in a chat the customer already uses.
- **Partner badges can open doors.** Meta and WhatsApp partner status gives a young tool credibility with merchants ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/pushouse)).
- **Modules widen the menu.** Selling many small automations lets a store start with one and add more.
- **Outbound still works.** Cold calls, cold email and events took Pushouse past $300,000 in its first year.
