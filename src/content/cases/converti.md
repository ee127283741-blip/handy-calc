Plenty of agencies want to sell "AI automation" to clients. Few can build it. converti sells them the product to rebrand: AI agents that call, text and email leads under the agency's own name. Since June 2023 that has brought in $316,196 in all-time revenue, with $9,595 in MRR in November 2026 ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/converti)). The latest month, though, looks softer than the MRR suggests.

## What does converti let agencies resell?

converti is a no-code platform for launching AI agents under your own brand. The company-written listing says the agents call, text and email leads to automate follow-ups and book sales calls, for agencies and businesses that want to sell AI automation without technical skills ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/converti)). The audience is B2B.

> "stitch together 3-5 separate tools" — converti listing, on the problem it replaces ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/converti))

## Why is converti's last month below its MRR?

converti collected $4,228 in the 30 days before the snapshot, less than half its $9,595 MRR ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/converti)). MRR counts subscriptions that are active, not cash collected in a window, so the gap can come from billing timing, failed payments or recent churn. The listing does not say which. Founder Samin shows 2,390 followers on X; team size and marketing channels are not disclosed.

## How does converti compare with Retell AI and Instantly?

converti overlaps with two larger sales-automation cases on this site, but sells to a different buyer.

| Company | Product | Main buyer |
|---|---|---|
| converti | White-label AI agents for calls, texts and email | Agencies reselling AI |
| [Retell AI](/cases/retell-ai/) | Platform for AI voice agents | Companies building phone agents |
| [Instantly](/cases/instantly/) | Cold email software | Sales teams and agencies |

Our observation: converti wraps channels that Retell AI and Instantly each specialise in, then lets agencies put their own name on it. That makes the agency, not the end business, the customer, which explains the higher-priced Agency tier.

## What lessons does converti teach about white-label AI?

- **Sell picks and shovels to resellers.** Agencies want an AI product they can rebrand.
- **Bundle channels.** Calls, texts and email in one place replace 3-5 tools.
- **Read MRR beside cash.** $4,228 in 30 days against $9,595 MRR is worth watching.
- **Tiered pricing fits agencies.** Plans run from $75 to $291 a month.
