Sierra AI revenue took seven quarters to reach $100M and two more quarters to double. The customer service startup announced $100M in ARR in November 2025, 21 months after it started ([founder-reported via TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/11/21/bret-taylors-sierra-reaches-100m-arr-in-under-two-years/)). By late May 2026 it said it had added the next $100M ([founder-reported via TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/08/these-ai-startups-are-growing-revenue-at-faster-and-faster-rates/)). Most AI startups that grow this fast sell to developers or consumers. Sierra sells to the call-center budgets of companies like SiriusXM and ADT.

## Who are Bret Taylor and Clay Bavor, Sierra's founders?

Sierra was started in early 2024 by two senior tech executives, not first-time founders ([SiliconANGLE](https://siliconangle.com/2026/05/04/ai-agent-startup-sierra-valued-15b-new-950m-funding-round/)). Bret Taylor was co-CEO of Salesforce, which bought his earlier startup Quip, and he left in 2023. Clay Bavor spent 18 years at Google. TechCrunch says the two met at Google in 2005 ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/11/21/bret-taylors-sierra-reaches-100m-arr-in-under-two-years/)). Taylor is also chair of OpenAI's board.

That background explains the customer list. TechCrunch names Deliveroo, Discord, Ramp, Rivian, SoFi, Tubi, ADT, Bissell, Vans, Cigna and SiriusXM as customers. By May 2026 Sierra said nearly half of the Fortune 50 used it ([founder-reported, SiliconANGLE](https://siliconangle.com/2026/05/04/ai-agent-startup-sierra-valued-15b-new-950m-funding-round/)).

## How did Sierra's ARR grow so quickly?

Sierra's ARR went from zero to $100M in seven quarters, then from $100M to $200M in two. The second step was more than three times faster than the first.

The $150M and $200M figures were given only weeks apart, both by the company. Total revenue and headcount are not disclosed.

## Why does Sierra charge for outcomes instead of seats?

Sierra charges for completed work, which ties its price to the job it replaces. TechCrunch describes the model as outcomes-based: customers pay when an agent finishes a task, not a flat subscription ([media-reported, TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/11/21/bret-taylors-sierra-reaches-100m-arr-in-under-two-years/)). Sierra does not publish its rates. In our reading, this is why the revenue can grow so fast. A large customer that moves more of its support volume to Sierra pays more without signing a new contract, and the comparison it makes is with the cost of human agents, not with other software.

## Is Sierra's valuation in line with its revenue?

Investors pay a lot for each dollar of Sierra's revenue, but less than they did. TechCrunch noted that the $10B valuation from September 2025 was about 100 times the $100M ARR reached in November ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/11/21/bret-taylors-sierra-reaches-100m-arr-in-under-two-years/)). In May 2026 GV and Tiger Global led a $950M round at $15B, with Benchmark, Sequoia and Greenoaks also investing ([media-reported, SiliconANGLE](https://siliconangle.com/2026/05/04/ai-agent-startup-sierra-valued-15b-new-950m-funding-round/)). Against the $200M ARR announced weeks later, $15B is 75 times revenue (calc).

## How does Sierra compare with Decagon, Chatbase and My AskAI?

All four sell AI that answers customer questions, but at very different scales. [Decagon](/cases/decagon/), Sierra's closest rival, was valued at $4.5B in 2026 and has not disclosed revenue since late 2024 ([media-reported, TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/03/04/decagon-completes-first-tender-offer-at-4-5b-valuation/)). [Chatbase](/cases/chatbase/) was a self-serve chatbot builder that reached $64,000 MRR three months after a February 2023 launch ([founder-reported, Indie Hackers](https://www.indiehackers.com/post/how-a-college-student-reached-64-000-mo-in-6-months-by-being-an-ai-first-mover-ba7981f6e1)). [My AskAI](/cases/my-askai/) plugs into help desks like Intercom and reported about $40K MRR with two people in July 2025 ([founder-reported, Indie Hackers](https://www.indiehackers.com/post/tech/bootstrapping-to-40k-mrr-after-his-vc-backed-startup-failed-LF1CwRs1vL3oVLcuoIoE)).

In our view, the split is who buys. Chatbase and My AskAI sell plans to small teams that sign up online. Sierra and Decagon sell to big companies, priced against the cost of human agents. That market is smaller in customer count but much larger per customer, which is how Sierra's monthly revenue ended up hundreds of times higher than Chatbase's.

## What are the takeaways from Sierra's growth?

- **Founder reputation shortens enterprise sales.** Two well-known executives signed Fortune 50 customers within two years.
- **Price against labor, not software.** Outcome-based pricing lets Sierra charge for work that human agents used to do.
- **Growth sped up after $100M.** The second $100M of ARR took two quarters, against seven for the first.
- **Valuation multiples fell as revenue rose.** The multiple went from about 100 times ARR in 2025 to about 75 times in 2026 on our arithmetic.
