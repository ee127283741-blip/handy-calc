An AI that whispers answers during a job interview is one of the most argued-about product ideas of the past two years. Natively AI builds exactly that, as a one-person team in India, and it had earned $83,617 by November 2026, eight months after it was founded ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/natively-ai)). Its numbers are verified through Dodo Payments rather than Stripe. The founder's name is not disclosed; the listing shows only the product's own X account.

## What does Natively AI do during a live call?

Natively AI listens to an interview, meeting or call in real time and helps the user answer questions, take notes and summarize the conversation. It can use personal context such as resumes, job descriptions and uploaded files during the session ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/natively-ai)).

> "Natively takes perfect meeting notes and gives real-time answers, all while completely undetectable." — Natively AI value proposition ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/natively-ai))

The listing says it is source-available. The desktop app is built with Electron, React, TypeScript and Swift.

## Why is Natively AI free for personal use?

The listing says Natively AI is free for personal use, with an option to pay a small fee for the Natively API ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/natively-ai)). A free, source-available desktop app spreads through Reddit, YouTube and X, which are among its listed channels, and paying users buy convenience on top. About 3.7% of stated users hold a subscription (calc: 775 / 21,000).

## What can AI copilot builders learn from Natively AI?

- **Free and open can undercut a funded rival.** Source-available and free for personal use sets it apart.
- **Payment providers beyond Stripe can verify revenue.** Dodo Payments backs these numbers.
- **One-off sales matter.** 30-day revenue ran well above MRR.
- **Controversy cuts both ways.** "Undetectable" draws users and raises ethical questions.
