Second-hand selling on Vinted looks casual from the buyer's side. For a professional reseller it is a pile of chores: writing listings, bumping old ones, haggling in chat and tracking what was bought for how much. ControlResell sells software that does those chores, and in November 2026 it had $21,906 in monthly recurring revenue ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/controlresell)), less than two years after its January 2025 start in France.

## What chores does ControlResell automate for Vinted resellers?

ControlResell automates the repetitive parts of reselling: AI-generated listings, automatic relisting and negotiation with buyers, plus tracking of every sale and purchase ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/controlresell)). Its audience is active sellers on Vinted, Shopify and other platforms.

> "Automate the time-consuming tasks: AI listings, auto relisting, smart negotiation." — ControlResell value proposition on TrustMRR ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/controlresell))

The French homepage pitches it as an all-in-one system for professional resellers and promises to save 20 hours a week ([founder-reported, ControlResell](https://controlresell.com)). That time claim is the company's own marketing, not a measured figure. The listed tech stack includes React, React Native and Kotlin, which points to mobile apps as well as a web dashboard.

## Why does ControlResell price in euros and lean on YouTube?

ControlResell prices in euros because its brand is built in France: Growth costs €29.99, Business €59.99 and Enterprise €119.99 a month ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/controlresell)). The listing says the company has a YouTube channel with tens of thousands of views, a podcast and a recognised brand among French resellers, while noting that it is known in France only.

That is a classic niche-community playbook. Resellers learn from other resellers, and video content about sourcing and flipping is where they gather. The listing itself flags the US as the larger untapped market, which also tells us the current revenue is concentrated in one country.

## How does ControlResell compare with SoldComps and Store Stock Checker?

All three sell tools to people who make money from resale, but ControlResell charges the most per seat and targets full-time sellers. [SoldComps](/cases/soldcomps/) sells eBay sold-listing data through an API, and [Store Stock Checker](/cases/store-stock-checker/) sells cheap stock alerts.

Our observation: the three land in a similar MRR band from very different price points. ControlResell gets there with about 446 subscriptions, while low-priced alert tools need far more payers. A higher price works because ControlResell replaces hours of manual work for people whose income depends on it.

## What can founders learn from ControlResell's niche playbook?

- **Sell to people who earn money with your tool.** Professional resellers can justify €59.99 a month when the software saves selling time ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/controlresell)).
- **One country can be enough to start.** ControlResell reached $21,906 MRR while describing its brand as France-only.
- **Content is distribution in hobby-to-pro markets.** A YouTube channel and podcast give the company reach among resellers without a listed paid-ads channel.
- **Automation beats analytics for busy sellers.** The pitch is listings, relisting and negotiation done for you, not another dashboard.
