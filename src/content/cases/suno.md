## Origin of the idea
The four founders met building machine learning for finance at Kensho. They believed audio generation could let people who never learned an instrument make music.

## How they started
They first worked on speech and audio models, then launched a song generator that went viral through Discord and later its own app.

## Tools & tech stack
Proprietary audio generation models that produce vocals, lyrics and instrumentation from a text prompt, served through web and mobile apps.

## Marketing
Shareable songs and memes on social platforms, a Microsoft Copilot integration and word of mouth.

## Sales & pricing
Free daily credits, with paid monthly plans that add credits and commercial-use rights.

## Monthly revenue
About **$25M/month**, implied from the $300M annual revenue the CEO reported in February 2026.

## Total revenue
Not disclosed.

## Founding date & timeline
- **2022:** Founded in Cambridge, Massachusetts.
- **Nov 2025:** $250M Series C at $2.45B post-money; ~$200M annual revenue reported by the WSJ.
- **Feb 2026:** 2M paid subscribers and $300M ARR.

Not disclosed.

## Key takeaways
- Turning a skill into a prompt opens a mass consumer market.
- Fun, shareable output drives viral growth.
- Copyright disputes with labels are a real business risk.
