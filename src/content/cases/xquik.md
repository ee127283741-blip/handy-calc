AI agents that watch social media need a steady feed of X posts, and building scrapers for that breaks every few weeks. Xquik sells the feed instead. Burak Bayır started it in March 2026 and runs it as a one-person company; by November 2026 it had $42,886 in all-time revenue and $5,569 in the last 30 days alone ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/xquik)).

## What does the Xquik API pull from X?

Xquik searches and extracts public X data, including tweets, replies, profiles, followers and trends. It can monitor accounts and keywords every second and push new posts by webhook ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/xquik)). Access comes as one REST API, an MCP server for AI agents, SDKs for TypeScript, Python, Ruby, Go, Java, Kotlin, C# and PHP, a CLI and a Terraform provider.

> "charges only for delivered results" — Xquik company description ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/xquik))

The listing stresses that Xquik is an independent third-party service, not affiliated with X Corp.

## Why does Xquik sell credits that never expire?

Data buyers have uneven demand: a research project may pull millions of tweets one month and nothing the next. Xquik's pay-as-you-go credits start at $10 and never expire, and monthly plans at $20, $99 or $199 carry unused credits over ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/xquik)). That removes the main objection to a subscription, paying for capacity you do not use.

Distribution also runs through other platforms. The listing says Xquik runs 22 Apify Store Actors with more than 7,800 users and 5.6 million runs, which puts it in front of developers already shopping for scrapers.

## What lessons does Xquik hold for API founders?

- **Sell where agents shop.** An MCP server and Apify Actors meet AI builders inside the tools they already use.
- **Credits beat strict plans for bursty demand.** Non-expiring credits help explain why 30-day revenue exceeded MRR.
- **Breadth of SDKs is cheap reach.** Eight language SDKs plus a CLI and Terraform provider widen the funnel for a solo founder.
- **Platform risk is real.** Xquik depends on public X data and labels itself clearly as unaffiliated with X Corp.
