Professional investors pay analysts to read filings and build models. Mad Dog Alpha AI wants retail investors to get something similar from software. The US company, founded in May 2025, had $11,779 in lifetime revenue and $1,066 in MRR by November 2026 ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/mad-dog-alpha-ai)). It is early, with a large codebase relative to its revenue.

## What is Mad Dog Alpha AI building for retail investors?

Mad Dog Alpha AI is a cloud-based AI analytics SaaS that helps users process, visualise and research complex data streams automatically ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/mad-dog-alpha-ai)). The founder, listed as Valkla, frames it as an AI-run research shop.

> "An Equity Research Instituion build for retail investor, Drive by AI, Run by AI." — Valkla ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/mad-dog-alpha-ai))

The quote is reproduced exactly, typos included. The app runs at app.mdalpha.ai and users can start for free.

## Why does Mad Dog Alpha AI need such a large stack?

The product is heavy on data and AI, and the stack shows it. TrustMRR lists React, TypeScript and JavaScript on the frontend and Python, FastAPI, Anthropic, Firebase, Google Cloud, Cloudflare, Docker and GitHub Actions on the backend ([TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/mad-dog-alpha-ai)).

The listing shows 82,547 lines of code and 324 GitHub contributions in the last year. TrustMRR puts revenue per line of code at $0.14 ([TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/mad-dog-alpha-ai)), a reminder that a lot of engineering has gone in ahead of revenue.

## What lessons come from Mad Dog Alpha AI's first 18 months?

- **Code is not traction.** 82,547 lines of code sit behind $1,066 MRR.
- **Free entry needs a clear upgrade.** Starting free brings users; the paid step must be obvious.
- **Retail research is crowded.** Larger tools like AltIndex already serve the same investors.
- **Verified small numbers still help.** $11,779 all time is modest but fully checked through Stripe.
