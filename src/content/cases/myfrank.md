A restaurant chain or a group of clinics often has one problem online: customers who loved the visit never leave a review. MyFrank, a French SaaS, tackles that by turning staff into review collectors and crediting each review to the employee who asked for it. In November 2026 the tool had $10,595 in monthly recurring revenue and 187 active subscriptions ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/myfrank)), about 17 months after it was founded.

## How does MyFrank turn employees into Google review collectors?

MyFrank gives each employee a way to ask for a Google review and tracks which reviews came from whom. The company-written TrustMRR listing describes per-employee attribution through QR codes and a focus on businesses with several sites ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/myfrank)). That makes reviews something a manager can measure and reward, not just hope for.

> "MyFrank is a B2B SaaS platform that helps businesses collect and manage Google reviews by actively engaging their teams." — MyFrank listing ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/myfrank))

The website is myfrank.io, and the listing offers a free trial with no commitment.

## Who runs MyFrank, and why is it for sale?

Joseph Lecomte, the founder of MyFrank, lists the company in France with a June 2025 founding date ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/myfrank)). He states on the page that the business is for sale, but no asking price appears. No marketing channels or X account are listed.

The stack is plain and cheap to run: Vue.js on the front end, PHP and Stripe behind it. For a buyer, that means a small, understandable codebase attached to a recurring customer base of local and multi-site businesses.

## How does MyFrank compare with SEO Stack and DataFast?

All three help businesses get found in search, but they pull different levers.

Our observation: SEO Stack and DataFast both sell dashboards to marketers and founders who already have data and need it organised. MyFrank serves local and multi-site businesses, where reviews drive foot traffic. MyFrank's twist is operational: it changes what staff do at the counter, which is harder to copy than a dashboard.

## What lessons does MyFrank hold for local-business SaaS?

- **Measure a habit, then reward it.** Crediting each review to an employee makes asking for reviews part of the job.
- **Sell per site.** About $57 per subscription fits a tool priced for each location of a multi-site business.
- **Simple stacks sell well.** Vue.js, PHP and Stripe keep the business easy to hand over.
- **Small can still be sellable.** A $10,595 MRR SaaS less than two years old was on the market in November 2026.
