Zero G Trading has only 10 active subscriptions, yet it has collected $77,071 since September 2025 ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/zero-g)). The small US team sells retail traders a map of where options dealers are positioned, and a separate bot that trades futures for them. This case looks at how the business is priced and why its revenue swings; it is not a review of whether the tools make money for traders.

## What does Zero G Trading's Titan map show?

Titan is an options map. The listing says it shows where gamma and options flow will pin or move SPY, QQQ and single stocks, with live callouts that users can act on or automate to their broker ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/zero-g)). The problem it targets is trading blind into options walls and getting callouts too late to use.

> "Built by traders, for traders." — Zero G Trading listing on TrustMRR ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/zero-g))

Nebula, from the same team, automates NQ futures from TradingView. The backend is listed as Python, Pinecone and Stripe.

## How much money does Zero G Trading make from 10 subscribers?

Zero G Trading had $1,416 in MRR from 10 active subscriptions in November 2026, collected $4,284 in the last 30 days and $77,071 all time ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/zero-g)).

## Who buys Zero G Trading's tools?

The audience is options and day traders who want to see where dealers are positioned. The listing tags the company as both B2B and B2C and lists a wide set of channels: SEO, Instagram, LinkedIn, TikTok, X, cold DMs, cold email, email marketing and Discord ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/zero-g)).

That breadth fits a 2 to 5 person bootstrapped team that is still testing where traders gather. The website, zerogtrading.com, has an Ahrefs Domain Rating of 2 out of 100 on the listing, so search is not yet a strong channel.

## How does Zero G Trading compare with Heron Copier and AltIndex?

All three sell software to retail traders and investors, but they sit at different points between data and execution.

| Company | What it sells | MRR | Source |

| [AltIndex](/cases/altindex/) | Alternative data scores for US stocks | $16,340 (Nov 2026) | [verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/altindex-llc) |

Our observation: AltIndex and Heron Copier have hundreds of subscriptions at lower prices. Zero G Trading runs on a handful of customers paying up to $275 a month ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/zero-g)), which makes revenue lumpy: a few cancellations or one-off purchases move the numbers a lot.

## What lessons does Zero G Trading offer trading-tool founders?

- **Ladder the price by how much the tool does.** A $30 map, a $199 callout tier and a $275 automation product let users climb.
- **Few buyers means volatile revenue.** With 10 subscriptions, 30-day revenue ran about three times MRR.
- **Lifetime revenue can outpace MRR.** $77,071 all time from a business with $1,416 MRR shows money that did not stay recurring.
- **Try many channels, then narrow.** Nine channels for a tiny team signals that the best one is not yet clear.
