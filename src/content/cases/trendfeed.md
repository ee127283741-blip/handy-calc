TrendFeed revenue reached $12K in four weeks for a product that took four days to build. Sebastian Volkis, a non-technical founder in London, used AI coding tools to build TrendFeed. He reported £9,222 ($12K) in revenue over its first four weeks ([founder-reported, Indie Hackers](https://www.indiehackers.com/post/building-a-product-with-ai-in-four-days-and-hitting-12k-in-four-weeks-HxoUuTymcPNvhCNe8b6A)). It was not his first AI product. The audience he had built with an earlier app, ChatIQ, did much of the selling.

## Who is Sebastian Volkis?

Sebastian Volkis is a London founder who builds software without a computer science background, using AI coding tools to write the code. His earlier product, ChatIQ, an AI tool he built as a non-technical founder, reached more than 11,300 users ([founder-reported, Starter Story](https://www.starterstory.com/chatiq-breakdown)). Along the way he gathered an audience of people interested in building with AI, and he later sold a course teaching it. Team size and prices are not disclosed.

## How did ChatIQ set up TrendFeed's launch?

ChatIQ gave Volkis users, credibility and a list to sell to. Starter Story's breakdown reports that ChatIQ had 5,867 users and £3,498 MRR after 30 days, and 9,237 users and £7,225 MRR after 60 days ([founder-reported, Starter Story](https://www.starterstory.com/chatiq-breakdown)). When TrendFeed launched, Volkis ran a webinar and offered lifetime deals to that audience, bringing in about £5.5K on launch day ([founder-reported, Indie Hackers](https://www.indiehackers.com/post/building-a-product-with-ai-in-four-days-and-hitting-12k-in-four-weeks-HxoUuTymcPNvhCNe8b6A)).

## How much revenue did TrendFeed make in its first month?

TrendFeed made £9,222 ($12K) in its first four weeks ([founder-reported, Indie Hackers](https://www.indiehackers.com/post/building-a-product-with-ai-in-four-days-and-hitting-12k-in-four-weeks-HxoUuTymcPNvhCNe8b6A)). Much of that came from lifetime deals, which pay once instead of monthly, so the first month is not a recurring rate. TrendFeed's MRR is not disclosed. About £5.5K, more than half the first month (calc), arrived on launch day.

> "In our first four weeks, we've made £9,222 ($12k) in revenue." — Sebastian Volkis ([founder-reported, Indie Hackers](https://www.indiehackers.com/post/building-a-product-with-ai-in-four-days-and-hitting-12k-in-four-weeks-HxoUuTymcPNvhCNe8b6A))

## How does TrendFeed compare with FounderPal and Launch Fast?

TrendFeed, [FounderPal](/cases/founderpal/) and [Launch Fast](/cases/launch-fast/) all launched quickly by selling to an audience that already existed. Launch Fast's non-technical founder partnered with a coaching brand to reach its customers. FounderPal sells AI marketing tools to solo founders. TrendFeed sold to the community Volkis had built through ChatIQ and his content about AI coding. In each case, distribution came before the product, and AI made the product fast enough to build that the audience was still warm.

Our reading: four days to build and $12K in four weeks sounds like a story about AI tools, but the more important asset was the audience. Lifetime deals turned that audience into cash at launch. Whether TrendFeed keeps growing depends on monthly subscribers, a number Volkis has not shared.

## What can audience-first founders learn from TrendFeed?

- **Build the audience before the product.** ChatIQ's users and followers made TrendFeed's launch day possible.
- **AI shrinks build time to days.** A non-technical founder shipped a working product in four days.
- **Lifetime deals front-load revenue.** They bring cash at launch but are not recurring income.
- **Launch with an event.** A webinar turned attention into about £5.5K in sales on day one.
