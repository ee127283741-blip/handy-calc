## Origin of the idea
Shlomo, a repeat founder, wanted non-developers to build working internal tools and apps by describing them, with the database, auth and hosting already included so there is nothing to wire up.

## How they started
He built it alone as a side project, launched in early 2025 and grew it through word of mouth and posts on X and LinkedIn, without outside funding.

## Tools & tech stack
Large language models generate the app, running on a built-in backend (data, users, permissions and hosting) so generated apps work immediately.

## Marketing
Founder posts on LinkedIn and X, user showcases, and partnerships with communities of non-technical builders.

## Sales & pricing
Freemium with monthly plans based on message and usage credits.

## Monthly revenue
About **$8.3M/month**, implied from the ~$100M ARR described by Wix's CEO on the March 2026 earnings call.

## Total revenue
Not disclosed.

## Founding date & timeline
- **Early 2025:** Launch, bootstrapped.
- **Jun 2025:** Sold to Wix for $80M in cash.
- **Mar 2026:** ~$100M ARR per Wix.

## Team size
Not disclosed; Shlomo started solo and hired a small team before the sale.

## Key takeaways
- A solo founder can build a category product with AI tooling.
- "Batteries included" lowers the barrier for non-developers.
- Strategic buyers pay for momentum early.
