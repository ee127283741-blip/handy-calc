Launching a SaaS product means filling in the same form on dozens of directories. Fabu turns that chore into a browser workflow run by an AI agent. Listed on TrustMRR under the name axis-labv2026, the product had $53,042 in lifetime revenue and $3,217 MRR from 339 subscriptions in November 2026 ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/axis-labv2026)). At $9 a month, it is a volume play.

## What does Fabu automate for SaaS founders?

Fabu automates product submissions to 50+ platforms through a Chrome extension with AI-powered workflows ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/axis-labv2026)). It also lists trending SaaS products and lets founders review traffic, revenue and authority signals without building another marketing stack.

> "Discover trending SaaS products, or distribute yours to 50+ platforms with our AI Agent." — Fabu description ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/axis-labv2026))

Running in the browser matters: the extension works inside the founder's own session on each directory, instead of a server pretending to be the user.

## What lessons does Fabu teach about cheap founder tools?

- **Automate the boring launch step.** Directory submissions are tedious and repeated across products.
- **A browser extension can be the product.** It works inside the user's own sessions.
- **Low prices need a reason to stay.** A trending-products feed adds recurring value to a one-time job.
- **Sell to your own audience.** A founder with an X following sells easily to other founders.
