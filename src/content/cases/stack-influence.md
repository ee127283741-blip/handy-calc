On TrustMRR, Stack Influence looks like two companies. Its recurring revenue is a modest $27,442 a month, yet it collected $860,797 in the last 30 days and $29,332,912 since it started ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/stack-influence)). That all-time total ranked it fifth on the site in November 2026. The gap tells you how the business really earns: campaigns, not subscriptions.

## What is the Stack Influence micro-creator model?

Stack Influence connects e-commerce brands with micro creators who post about products they receive. The homepage lists product seeding, user-generated content with usage rights, content syndication, ambassador and affiliate programs and a managed service ([founder-reported, Stack Influence homepage](https://stackinfluence.com/)). It sells into Amazon, Walmart, Shopify and TikTok Shop sellers and says it was named to the top 15% of the Inc. 5000 list.

> "Automate authentic creator collaborations at scale without negotiating fees." — Stack Influence listing ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/stack-influence))

## How does Stack Influence earn $860K a month with $27K MRR?

Most of Stack Influence's revenue is not recurring. TrustMRR showed $860,797 collected in 30 days against $27,442 in MRR and 55 active subscriptions in November 2026 ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/stack-influence)). MRR was about 3% of that month's cash ([calc, from TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/stack-influence)).

The pricing explains the split. Campaign flat fees, platform fees and creator payouts arrive as one-off charges, while only the monthly plans count toward MRR. How much of the 30-day total passes through to creators is not disclosed, so revenue here is not the same as gross margin. Team size and profit are not disclosed either.

## What do Stack Influence's pricing tiers look like?

The listing describes a menu of fees rather than one plan. Per campaign, brands pay a $30 flat fee, a $500 flat fee plus platform fees, or a $100 flat fee plus an 8% payout when creators negotiate. Monthly plans are $0, $499, $1,799 or $6K a month on an annual contract ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/stack-influence)).

This structure lets a small brand test one campaign cheaply and a large one move to a managed contract. It also means revenue rises and falls with campaign volume.

## How does Stack Influence compare with Arcads and Creator Hunter?

All three help brands get creator-style content. They differ in whether real people or AI make it.

Our observation: Stack Influence and Arcads land at similar monthly scale from opposite bets. Arcads replaces the creator with AI; Stack Influence makes real creators cheap by paying them in product. Creator Hunter sells only the search step, which is why it is far smaller.

## What lessons does Stack Influence hold for marketplace founders?

- **MRR can hide a big business.** A $27,442 MRR line sits beside $29,332,912 in all-time revenue ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/stack-influence)).
- **Remove the negotiation.** Paying creators in product turns a slow, manual deal into a repeatable step.
- **Price for every brand size.** Fees from $30 per campaign to $6K a month let customers start small and grow.
- **Time compounds.** Founded in January 2020, Stack Influence had almost seven years to build its creator network.
