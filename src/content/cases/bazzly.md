Bazzly sells exactly one plan, and it is not cheap. At $199 a month ([founder-reported, Bazzly pricing](https://www.bazzly.ai/pricing)), it costs more than many full marketing suites, yet 189 subscribers paid it in November 2026. That added up to $34,931 in MRR and $100,346 in all-time revenue for a company TrustMRR dates to June 2025 ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/bazzly)).

## What does Bazzly automate on Reddit?

Bazzly automates the slow parts of Reddit marketing: finding threads, writing replies and sending DMs. The homepage says it scans Reddit around the clock for posts where people look for a product like yours, then replies, DMs high-intent posters and adds upvotes to push a reply toward the top ([founder-reported, Bazzly](https://www.bazzly.ai/)). A second pitch is search: Reddit threads rank on Google and get cited by AI tools, so a top comment can keep sending traffic.

> "Comment, DM and rank on Google to get leads and traffic from Reddit. All hands-off." — Bazzly website ([founder-reported, Bazzly](https://www.bazzly.ai/))

Customers choose how replies go out. Bazzly can post through its own aged, warmed-up accounts, or from the customer's account through a Chrome extension. The site markets the first option as "zero ban risk"; that is the company's claim, and we have not tested it. Reddit's own view of paid upvotes and third-party accounts is a risk buyers should weigh themselves.

## Why does Bazzly sell only one $199 plan?

Bazzly keeps pricing to one tier and meters the expensive actions with credits. The $199 plan includes 170 credits a month, opportunities, leads, reply autopilot, the browser extension, a public API and an MCP server ([founder-reported, Bazzly pricing](https://www.bazzly.ai/pricing)).

| Action | Credit cost | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Post via a warmed-up account | 50 credits | [founder-reported, Bazzly pricing](https://www.bazzly.ai/pricing) |
| Comment through a warmed-up account | 10 credits | [founder-reported, Bazzly pricing](https://www.bazzly.ai/pricing) |
| Upvote | 0.2 credits each | [founder-reported, Bazzly pricing](https://www.bazzly.ai/pricing) |
| AI draft (reply or DM) | 0.1 credits | [founder-reported, Bazzly pricing](https://www.bazzly.ai/pricing) |
| Autopilot AI drafts | Free | [founder-reported, Bazzly pricing](https://www.bazzly.ai/pricing) |

The pricing tells you where the cost sits. Drafting text is almost free, while posting from an aged account is the scarce resource. The trial gives 5 credits for warmed-account posting, so a free user can test the flow but not run a campaign.

## How fast did Bazzly's revenue grow after launch?

Bazzly reached $100,346 in lifetime revenue within about 17 months of its June 2025 founding date, and it now runs at $34,931 in MRR ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/bazzly)). Revenue in the last 30 days was $40,454, above MRR, which fits a product that also sells credit top-ups.

Two things stand out. MRR per subscription sits just under the $199 list price, so few customers seem to be on discounts. And the base is small: the homepage claims 2,324 founders use Bazzly ([founder-reported, Bazzly](https://www.bazzly.ai/)), but only 189 pay. Team size and profit are not disclosed. Filip Panoski, the founder of Bazzly, had 7,081 followers on X per TrustMRR.

## How does Bazzly compare with ReplyDaddy and Gojiberry AI?

Bazzly charges the most of these three social-selling tools and goes furthest into automation. [ReplyDaddy](/cases/replydaddy/) also mines Reddit for leads, and [Gojiberry AI](/cases/gojiberry-ai/) finds buying signals for outbound sales.

| Company | Approach | Revenue figure | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bazzly | Finds Reddit threads, drafts and posts replies, adds upvotes | $34,931 MRR (Nov 2026) | [verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/bazzly) |
| [ReplyDaddy](/cases/replydaddy/) | Reddit lead finding and reply suggestions | About $5,000 a month | [founder-reported, Starter Story](https://www.starterstory.com/businesses/replydaddy/founders) |
| [Gojiberry AI](/cases/gojiberry-ai/) | Buying-signal leads for outbound | $27K MRR (Dec 2025) | [founder-reported, Indie Hackers](https://www.indiehackers.com/post/tech/validating-via-services-before-building-a-27k-mrr-product-7FrT6FXChPOiUK4PbXFp) |

Our observation: the money in Reddit tools seems to follow how much work the tool takes off the customer's hands. ReplyDaddy's reported figure belongs to a tool that suggests; Bazzly posts. That extra step is also where the platform risk sits, because posting through accounts the customer does not own depends on Reddit tolerating it.

## What lessons does Bazzly hold for founders selling growth tools?

- **One high price can work for a narrow job.** Bazzly sells a single $199 plan and its average subscription is close to list ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/bazzly)).
- **Meter the scarce resource.** Text drafts cost 0.1 credits; posts from warmed-up accounts cost 50. Pricing follows real cost.
- **Free users are not paying users.** A claimed 2,324 users against 189 paid subscriptions shows how wide the gap can be.
- **Platform dependence is the real risk.** A business built on another network's threads, accounts and votes can change overnight if that network changes its rules.
