AI LinkedIn writers are one of the most crowded categories in software, and most of them are in English. Alchie picked French. Launched by Pierre Guilbaud in April 2026, it reached $10,555 in monthly recurring revenue and $68,221 in lifetime revenue within about seven months ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/alchie)). Its listing and tagline are written in French, aimed squarely at that market.

## What problem does Alchie solve for LinkedIn writers?

Alchie solves the blank-page problem: it takes raw, unpolished thoughts and turns them into finished LinkedIn posts. The listing says the target users are leaders and independents who struggle to articulate their ideas in writing ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/alchie)).

> "Transforms your raw thoughts into remarkable LinkedIn posts." — Alchie value proposition on TrustMRR ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/alchie))

The framing is about idea extraction, not just text generation. That is a sensible angle for busy executives, who usually have opinions but not time to draft.

## How much MRR does Alchie make after seven months?

Alchie made $10,555 in MRR in November 2026 from 181 active subscriptions, with $11,421 collected in the last 30 days and $68,221 all time ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/alchie)).

That is a mid-market price for a writing tool, which fits a B2C audience of professionals rather than students. The listed paid price itself is not disclosed; the listing only mentions 2 free posts.

## How was Alchie built and funded?

Alchie is bootstrapped and runs on a small, common indie stack: Next.js on the front end, Vercel hosting, Supabase for the backend and Stripe for billing ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/alchie)). Team size is not disclosed. Pierre Guilbaud has about 1,000 followers on X, so the founder's audience alone does not explain the growth; marketing channels are not disclosed either.

Our observation: WaLead AI sells LinkedIn as a lead channel, and Poppy AI sells a broad workspace. Alchie stays narrower than both, competing on language and personal voice. A non-English niche lowers the number of direct rivals at the cost of a smaller ceiling.

## What lessons does Alchie offer founders in crowded AI categories?

- **Language is a real niche.** Alchie's French positioning sets it apart from English-first LinkedIn writers.
- **Sell the outcome, not the model.** The pitch is turning raw thoughts into posts, not access to an AI.
- **A low-friction trial helps.** Two free posts with no commitment let users test their own voice before paying.
- **Seven months is enough to validate.** $10,555 MRR shows a narrow tool can reach real revenue fast.
