Abridge revenue comes from a job no doctor wants: writing up the visit after the patient leaves. The AI medical scribe listens to the conversation and drafts the clinical note. Its annualized recurring revenue reportedly reached $117M in the quarter before June 2025 ([media-reported, SiliconANGLE citing The Information](https://siliconangle.com/2025/06/24/medical-ai-startup-abridge-closes-300m-investment-5-3b-valuation/)). That month investors valued it at $5.3B. By June 2026 it was running in more than 300 health systems, including Johns Hopkins and Northwestern Medicine ([media-reported, Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/06/11/abridge-operating-system-medicine-nvidia-eli-lilly-artsight/)).

## What does Abridge's AI scribe actually do?

Abridge records a medical visit, transcribes it and turns the important details into a structured note in the hospital's electronic health record. SiliconANGLE describes the core as a Contextual Reasoning Engine that also drafts medical orders and generates ICD-10 and billing codes ([SiliconANGLE](https://siliconangle.com/2025/06/24/medical-ai-startup-abridge-closes-300m-investment-5-3b-valuation/)). Shiv Rao, a practicing cardiologist ([Fortune](https://fortune.com/2025/07/02/abridge-ceo-shiv-rao-on-raising-300-million-a-prospective-ipo-and-the-future-of-healthcare/)), cofounded the company in 2018 ([Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/06/11/abridge-operating-system-medicine-nvidia-eli-lilly-artsight/)). Fortune reports it now handles more than 100 million clinical conversations a year.

The customers are large health systems, not individual doctors. In July 2025 Rao cited 26,000 clinicians at Kaiser alone ([Fortune](https://fortune.com/2025/07/02/abridge-ceo-shiv-rao-on-raising-300-million-a-prospective-ipo-and-the-future-of-healthcare/)).

## How much ARR does Abridge make?

The only public revenue figure is $117M in annualized recurring revenue, reported by The Information for the quarter before Abridge's June 2025 round ([media-reported, SiliconANGLE](https://siliconangle.com/2025/06/24/medical-ai-startup-abridge-closes-300m-investment-5-3b-valuation/)). That works out to about $9.75M a month (calc). Abridge itself has not confirmed it or released a newer number. Pricing, current ARR and total revenue are not disclosed.

What the company does share is reach. It said it served more than 150 health systems in July 2025 ([founder-reported, Fortune](https://fortune.com/2025/07/02/abridge-ceo-shiv-rao-on-raising-300-million-a-prospective-ipo-and-the-future-of-healthcare/)). Fortune counted more than 300 live a year later. Health-system contracts are large and slow to sign, so doubling that count in a year points to strong revenue growth, though the size of it is not public.

## Why does Abridge call itself a revenue cycle company?

Abridge's notes do more than save time; they help hospitals bill. The same conversation that becomes a clinical note also produces billing codes, and SiliconANGLE reported that the 2025 funding would focus on that feature ([SiliconANGLE](https://siliconangle.com/2025/06/24/medical-ai-startup-abridge-closes-300m-investment-5-3b-valuation/)).

> "we're a revenue cycle company, along with the other things we do." — Shiv Rao ([founder-reported, Fortune](https://fortune.com/2025/07/02/abridge-ceo-shiv-rao-on-raising-300-million-a-prospective-ipo-and-the-future-of-healthcare/))

In our reading, this is the key to Abridge's pricing power. A tool that saves doctors time is a nice-to-have. A tool that helps the hospital capture revenue it would otherwise miss is something the finance team will pay for.

## How has Abridge's valuation changed?

Abridge's valuation nearly doubled in four months and then held. Fortune says the February 2025 Series D valued it at $2.75B. In June 2025 Andreessen Horowitz led a $300M Series E, with Khosla Ventures, at $5.3B ([founder-reported via SiliconANGLE](https://siliconangle.com/2025/06/24/medical-ai-startup-abridge-closes-300m-investment-5-3b-valuation/)). A $316M extension closed in April 2026 at the same $5.3B, bringing total funding to about $830M ([media-reported, Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/06/11/abridge-operating-system-medicine-nvidia-eli-lilly-artsight/)).

## How does Abridge compare with Freed and Harvey?

Abridge and [Freed](/cases/freed/) both sell AI scribes, but to opposite ends of medicine. Freed sells self-serve plans to small and independent practices at $39 to $119 a month ([founder-reported, Freed](https://www.getfreed.ai/blog/the-freed-story)). Sacra estimated Freed at $33M ARR in August 2026 ([third-party estimate, Sacra](https://sacra.com/c/freed/)), well below the $117M reported for Abridge a year earlier. [Harvey](/cases/harvey/) is the closest match in another field: an AI tool sold to large professional organizations, which reported more than $100M ARR in August 2025 ([founder-reported, Harvey](https://www.harvey.ai/blog/harveys-three-year-anniversary)).

In our view, the split is sales motion. Freed can sign a doctor in minutes with a credit card. Abridge has to win a hospital system, connect to its health record software and train thousands of clinicians. That is slower, but each contract is far larger, and the billing-code feature gives the buyer a financial reason to say yes. Harvey made a similar bet on big law firms.

[OpenEvidence](/cases/openevidence/) sells to the same doctors with a different model: it is free for clinicians and paid for by advertisers.

## What lessons does Abridge offer founders in vertical AI?

- **Tie the product to the buyer's revenue.** Abridge's billing codes turn a time-saver into something the hospital's finance team values.
- **Enterprise healthcare is slow but sticky.** Going from 150 to 300 health systems took a year, and each one is a large contract.
- **Domain founders help.** A cardiologist cofounder understands the note-writing burden from the inside.
- **Leaked numbers fill the gap.** Abridge's only revenue figure comes from The Information, not the company, so treat it as reported, not confirmed.
