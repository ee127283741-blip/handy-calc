In Brazil, many on-call shifts for doctors are posted in WhatsApp groups, reposted, duplicated and buried under spam. Caça Plantão sells a filter for that noise. Its AI reads the groups and sends each doctor only the shifts that match their city and specialty. In November 2026 it showed $1,914 in monthly recurring revenue, $3,984 all time and 88% month-over-month growth ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/cacaplantao)). It is tiny, but it solves a very specific, local problem.

## How does Caça Plantão turn WhatsApp groups into a shift feed?

Caça Plantão aggregates vacancies from WhatsApp groups and other sources, removes duplicates and spam, and matches each opportunity to a doctor's city and specialty ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/cacaplantao)). Doctors browse on the website, an app or WhatsApp, get personalised alerts, compare advertised pay with market medians and contact recruiters directly.

> "Medical on-call vacancies in your area delivered to you before they are filled." — Caça Plantão listing ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/cacaplantao))

The homepage says its AI has read 407,000 messages, which produced 697 unique vacancies in the last seven days alone ([founder-reported, Caça Plantão homepage](https://cacaplantao.com.br/)).

## Why does Caça Plantão hide recruiter contacts on the free plan?

The free plan shows the vacancy but not the contact. The homepage explains that free users see the recruiter's contact only after 21 days, by which time the shift is usually taken, while subscribers to the Radar plan can call the recruiter as soon as the vacancy appears ([founder-reported, Caça Plantão homepage](https://cacaplantao.com.br/)). Telemedicine vacancies are open only to subscribers.

The listing gives a free plan, Essential at 57.90 a month and an annual plan at 579.00 a year, and the homepage prices in reais. The team is 2–5 people, bootstrapped, with SEO as the named marketing channel.

## How is Caça Plantão different from Mercor and AIApply?

| Company | Who it serves | Matching approach |
|---|---|---|
| Caça Plantão | Brazilian on-call doctors | AI reads WhatsApp shift posts; $1,914 MRR ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/cacaplantao)) |
| [Mercor](/cases/mercor/) | Companies hiring experts | AI-led hiring marketplace |
| [AIApply](/cases/aiapply/) | Job seekers | AI applications and resumes |

Our observation: Mercor and AIApply work on formal job markets. Caça Plantão works on an informal one that lives in chat groups, so its main asset is not a model but access to the messages and a time advantage that doctors will pay for.

## What lessons does Caça Plantão offer founders in informal markets?

- **Informal channels hide paid problems.** Shift posts scattered across WhatsApp groups became a subscription product.
- **Sell time, not access.** The free plan shows vacancies; the paid plan shows them first with the contact.
- **Small can grow fast.** $1,914 of MRR with 88% month-over-month growth ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/cacaplantao)) is an early but real signal.
- **Local language is a moat.** A Portuguese product for Brazilian doctors faces few global competitors.
