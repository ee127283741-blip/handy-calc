Google Analytics is free, which makes paid web analytics a hard sell. Simple Analytics sold it anyway, by promising to count visitors without tracking them. Eight years after its 2018 launch, it had collected $1,942,406 and showed $37,790 in monthly recurring revenue ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/simple-analytics)), with a team its about page puts at three people.

## Why does Simple Analytics refuse to use cookies?

Simple Analytics avoids cookies so it can measure traffic without consent banners or personal data. The about page states the rule plainly ([founder-reported, Simple Analytics about page](https://www.simpleanalytics.com/about)).

> "No cookies, no personal data, no fingerprinting. Ever." — Simple Analytics about page ([founder-reported, Simple Analytics](https://www.simpleanalytics.com/about))

The TrustMRR listing frames the benefit for businesses: it measures visitors who reject consent, without identifying them and without relying on Google's Consent Mode modelling. It also lists GDPR and SOC 2 compliance, EU ownership, and that it works alongside GA4 rather than only replacing it.

## What does Simple Analytics charge per user?

Simple Analytics charges $20 a month for its Self-serve plan, with each extra user another $20 a month, for 100k to 2.5M pageviews based on an estimate the customer sets ([founder-reported, Simple Analytics pricing](https://www.simpleanalytics.com/pricing)). A free plan covers hobby sites with one user, five websites and one month of history, but requires a badge. Enterprise is priced after a call.

| Plan | Price | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Free | $0, badge required | [founder-reported, Simple Analytics](https://www.simpleanalytics.com/pricing) |
| Self-serve | $20 a month per user | [founder-reported, Simple Analytics](https://www.simpleanalytics.com/pricing) |
| Enterprise | Custom | [founder-reported, Simple Analytics](https://www.simpleanalytics.com/pricing) |

The average subscription is worth about $30.02 a month ([calc, from TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/simple-analytics)), a little above one Self-serve seat. The pricing page also says "We don't do Black Friday", a small signal of the brand's no-tricks stance.

## How does a three-person Simple Analytics team serve agencies?

Simple Analytics runs a B2B business with a very small team. The about page lists three people ([founder-reported, Simple Analytics about page](https://www.simpleanalytics.com/about)), and TrustMRR names its audience as analysts at agencies, enterprise teams and white-label partners.

White-label partners matter here. Agencies can resell privacy-first analytics under their own name, which brings many client sites through one relationship. A Domain Rating of 81 on TrustMRR also shows how much the brand is linked to across the web, a result of years of writing about privacy and analytics.

## How does Simple Analytics compare with Postiz and Post Bridge?

Simple Analytics is the oldest of three bootstrapped marketing tools on this site with Stripe-verified numbers, and the only one that sells measurement rather than publishing. Comparing them shows how age and growth trade off.

Our observation: the newer social tools passed Simple Analytics' MRR within two years, riding creator demand. Simple Analytics instead built a durable, slow business on a regulatory tailwind, privacy law, and has collected close to two million dollars doing it. Its 30-day revenue matching MRR suggests lower volatility than fast-growing peers.

## What lessons does Simple Analytics teach about privacy as a product?

- **A principle can be the product.** "No cookies, no personal data, no fingerprinting" is both the feature list and the marketing ([founder-reported, Simple Analytics about page](https://www.simpleanalytics.com/about)).
- **Regulation can be a tailwind.** Consent rules created a gap that free analytics leaves open: visitors who reject tracking.
- **A tiny team can run a long-lived SaaS.** Three people and $1,942,406 in lifetime revenue ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/simple-analytics)).
- **Work alongside the incumbent.** Positioning next to GA4, not only against it, lowers the cost of switching.
