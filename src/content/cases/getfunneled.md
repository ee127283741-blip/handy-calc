Most AI startups sell software and let the customer do the work. Get Funneled flips that. It sells a finished post-click funnel to direct-to-consumer brands and claims AI does 97% of the labour behind it. In November 2026 that productized service brought in $18,403 in monthly recurring revenue from 22 subscriptions ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/getfunneled)), and its founder had put it up for sale.

## What is a post-click funnel, and what does Get Funneled build?

A post-click funnel is what a shopper sees after clicking an ad: the landing page, offer and checkout path. Get Funneled builds that experience for D2C brands as a done-for-you service ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/getfunneled)). TrustMRR tags it Marketing, E-commerce, SaaS and Analytics.

> "AI Native post click funnel experience for D2C brands" — Get Funneled listing ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/getfunneled))

The same listing says it is a productized service that AI does 97% of the work for, which explains how a small team can serve paying brands at a monthly retainer.

## How much revenue does Get Funneled make per client?

Get Funneled had $18,403 MRR, $18,363 in revenue over the last 30 days and $112,245 all time in November 2026 ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/getfunneled)). The monthly and 30-day numbers are almost identical, which is what a clean retainer business looks like.

## Why is James Claridge selling Get Funneled?

James Claridge, the founder of Get Funneled, gives a personal reason on the TrustMRR page: he is selling because his partner is pregnant and he wants to spend time with his family ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/getfunneled)). No asking price is shown. The company was founded in January 2025 in Australia.

The stack is heavier than most small services: Angular, Svelte and TypeScript on the front end; AWS, Docker, Redis, Cloudflare and PostgreSQL behind it; and both Anthropic and OpenAI models. That is real software behind a service wrapper, which may matter to a buyer.

Our observation: AEO Engine shows how far a done-for-you AI service can scale. DataFast shows the self-serve path, with many cheap subscriptions. Get Funneled is closer to AEO Engine: few clients, higher fees and AI used to keep delivery costs down rather than to replace the service.

## What takeaways does Get Funneled offer service founders?

- **Productize the agency.** A fixed monthly offer let Get Funneled bill 22 clients like subscriptions.
- **Use AI for delivery, not the pitch.** The claim is that AI does 97% of the work, so the margin is in the process.
- **Retainers make clean revenue.** MRR and 30-day revenue were almost identical in November 2026.
- **Life decides exits too.** The stated reason for selling is family, not a stalled business.
