Anything app builder revenue reached $2M ARR in the first two weeks after launch, TechCrunch reported in September 2025 ([media-reported, TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/09/29/vibe-coding-startup-anything-nabs-a-100m-valuation-after-hitting-2m-arr-in-its-first-two-weeks)). That got the company an $11M round at a $100M valuation. Anything was not its founders' first try. Dhruv Amin and Marcus Lowe, both former Google product leaders, had already built an AI app builder called Create. They relaunched with a new agent, a new name and a focus on apps that make money.

## How did Create become Anything?

Anything is the second version of the founders' app builder. Dhruv Amin and Marcus Lowe first built Create, and in August 2025 they released Anything with a new coding agent that builds, tests and publishes web and mobile apps from a prompt ([The Register](https://www.theregister.com/2025/08/14/anything_vibe_coding_platform_released/)). The pitch is that non-developers can go from an idea to a published app with payments, logins and a database without writing code. Anything's founding date, team size and plan prices are not disclosed in sources we could check.

## How did Anything make $2M ARR in two weeks?

Anything reached $2M ARR in its first two weeks, about $167K a month (calc), per TechCrunch ([media-reported, TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/09/29/vibe-coding-startup-anything-nabs-a-100m-valuation-after-hitting-2m-arr-in-its-first-two-weeks)). The relaunch did not start from zero: the founders already had users and a product from Create. Footwork led the $11M round, with Uncork, Bessemer and M13 joining. Anything's current ARR and total revenue are not disclosed.

## Why did Anything spend $2M on a domain?

Anything bought anything.com for $2M in December 2025 ([founder-reported, Anything release](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20251210824314/en)), roughly a fifth of the $11M it had raised three months earlier (calc). A generic one-word name is hard to find in search, and owning the exact domain fixes that. The release framed it as part of a plan to let anyone make money on the internet with apps they build.

> "Buying our domain was a small first step," — Marcus Lowe ([founder-reported, Anything release](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20251210824314/en))

## What happened when Apple pulled Anything from the App Store?

Apple removed Anything's iPhone app from the App Store twice, and the company said it would shift to a desktop companion app and other workarounds ([media-reported, AI Directory](https://aidirectory.com/news/anything-desktop-companion-app-after-app-store-removal)). Dhruv Amin told TechCrunch the iPhone app mainly let users preview the iOS apps they were building, and that Apple raised security concerns about app builders being used to make harmful software. For a company selling mobile apps to non-developers, losing its own iPhone app was a direct hit to the build-and-preview loop.

## How does Anything compare with Lovable and Emergent?

Anything is much smaller than the leaders in its category. [Lovable](/cases/lovable/) reported $100M ARR about eight months after launch, and [Emergent](/cases/emergent/) reported a $120M run rate in July 2026 ([founder-reported, TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/15/indian-ai-coding-startup-emergent-becomes-a-unicorn-just-over-a-year-after-launch/)). Anything's $2M ARR came in two weeks, a fast start, but it has not published a later number. Its focus on mobile apps puts it closer to Emergent, which also launched a mobile app, and makes it more exposed to Apple's rules than web-first builders like [Bolt.new](/cases/bolt-new/).

[Shipper](/cases/shipper/) is a smaller rival built by two brothers who describe themselves as non-technical.

## What are the lessons from Anything for vibe-coding founders?

- **A relaunch can beat a fresh start.** Anything reused its founders' users and lessons from Create and hit $2M ARR in two weeks.
- **Platform rules are a business risk.** Apple removing the iPhone app twice hurt a product built around mobile apps.
- **Brand assets can be worth real money.** Anything spent $2M on its exact-match domain to fix a generic name.
- **Publish the second number.** A fast first milestone gets attention; a later one shows whether growth lasted.
