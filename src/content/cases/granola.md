Most AI meeting tools solve note-taking by sending a bot into your call. Granola went the other way. Its app sits quietly on your laptop, listens to the audio, and waits for you to jot a few words before it writes anything. Users and companies kept signing up anyway. In March 2026, less than three years after it was founded, Granola raised $125M at a $1.5B valuation ([media-reported, TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/03/25/granola-raises-125m-hits-1-5b-valuation-as-it-expands-from-meeting-notetaker-to-enterprise-ai-app/)). It still has not said how much money it makes.

## Why did a former Socratic founder start Granola?

Chris Pedregal had already sold one AI company. His earlier startup, Socratic, was an AI tutoring app that taught students how to solve a homework problem from a photo. It sold to Google, and he stayed at Google for a couple of years ([media-reported, TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2024/05/22/granola-debuts-an-ai-notepad-for-meetings/)). After working with GPT-3, he built several prototypes, including an AI journaling app. That work convinced him that large language models would change the tools people use at work, especially tools for spoken language.

He met co-founder Sam Stephenson, who had worked at the note-taking app Ideaflow, through a meetup group about tools for meetings. Both lived in London. They founded Granola in March 2023 ([media-reported, TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2024/05/22/granola-debuts-an-ai-notepad-for-meetings/)). They spent about a year testing it with a closed group of beta users before the public launch in May 2024 ([founder-reported, Granola blog](https://www.granola.ai/blog/series-a)).

## How does Granola take meeting notes without a bot?

Granola connects to your calendar and transcribes the Mac's audio directly. No bot joins the call, and at launch it worked with Zoom, Google Meet, Teams, Slack and WebEx ([media-reported, TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2024/05/22/granola-debuts-an-ai-notepad-for-meetings/)). During the meeting it behaves like a plain notepad. When the meeting ends, it combines what you typed with what it heard and writes fuller notes. It also looks at who attended and what kind of meeting it was, so a sales call and a job interview get different structure.

> "Granola is a deceptively simple notepad for meetings." — Chris Pedregal ([founder-reported, Granola blog](https://www.granola.ai/blog/announcement))

Two design choices stand out. Your own words appear in black and the AI's additions in gray, and a "Zoom In" button shows the exact transcript quotes behind each line ([founder-reported, Granola blog](https://www.granola.ai/blog/announcement)). Pedregal called this a steering wheel for LLMs: the user points the AI at what matters instead of handing their judgment to it.

## How fast did Granola's user base grow?

Granola has never published a total user count, but it has shared growth rates. In October 2024 it said weekly users had grown 6x since launch. It also claimed very high retention: half of the people who try Granola still use it 10 weeks later, and they average 6 meetings a week ([founder-reported, Granola blog](https://www.granola.ai/blog/series-a)). Over half of its users were in leadership roles, according to the same post. Partners at firms such as Benchmark, Sequoia and Accel used it to take pitches.

By May 2025, Pedregal told TechCrunch that the user base had grown 10% every week since launch, mostly through word of mouth among VCs and founders. He did not say how many users that meant ([founder-reported, TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/05/14/ai-note-taking-app-granola-raises-43m-at-250m-valuation-launches-collaborative-features/)). People also started using the app for personal notes, keeping it open all day.

## Does Granola disclose its revenue or ARR?

Granola's revenue is not disclosed. It has not published MRR, ARR, a paying-customer count or a team size. What is public is its price and its funding. At launch, Granola was free for the first 25 meetings and then cost $10 a month, with team or company plans planned for later ([media-reported, TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2024/05/22/granola-debuts-an-ai-notepad-for-meetings/)). By March 2026 it had business and enterprise plans, and its new APIs were tied to those tiers ([media-reported, TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/03/25/granola-raises-125m-hits-1-5b-valuation-as-it-expands-from-meeting-notetaker-to-enterprise-ai-app/)). Prices for those plans are not in our sources.

## What went wrong when Granola locked down its local database?

Granola's move into enterprise software came with a public stumble. A group of users, including an a16z partner, had built on-device AI agent workflows on top of Granola's local data. When the company locked down its local database, those workflows broke, and the users were angry ([media-reported, TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/03/25/granola-raises-125m-hits-1-5b-valuation-as-it-expands-from-meeting-notetaker-to-enterprise-ai-app/)). Pedregal said Granola did not intend to lock down data. Its local cache simply was not designed for AI workflows, so the company changed how it stored the data.

He promised APIs for bulk access, and Granola shipped them with the Series C: a personal API on business and enterprise plans, and an enterprise API for admins. These followed a Model Context Protocol (MCP) server introduced in February 2026. The company also added Spaces, shared team workspaces with access controls, and named customers such as Vanta, Gusto, Asana and Cursor.

## What lessons does Granola teach about building AI tools?

- **Remove the thing users dislike most.** TechCrunch argues that people dislike visible bots in meetings but don't mind an app on someone's computer, and calls that the core reason for Granola's popularity ([media-reported, TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/03/25/granola-raises-125m-hits-1-5b-valuation-as-it-expands-from-meeting-notetaker-to-enterprise-ai-app/)).
- **Keep the human in the loop as a product feature.** Black-versus-gray text and quote-level sourcing turned "you stay in control" from a slogan into something users can see ([founder-reported, Granola blog](https://www.granola.ai/blog/announcement)).
- **Seed the right early users.** Granola spread among founders and VCs by word of mouth, and its funding rounds grew alongside that adoption. Its raises grew from a $4.25M seed ([media-reported, TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2024/05/22/granola-debuts-an-ai-notepad-for-meetings/)) to a $125M Series C ([media-reported, TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/03/25/granola-raises-125m-hits-1-5b-valuation-as-it-expands-from-meeting-notetaker-to-enterprise-ai-app/)).
- **Treat power users' workarounds as a roadmap.** Locking the local database broke agent workflows. Granola recovered by shipping official APIs and an MCP server, but only after a public backlash.
- **Plan for the core feature becoming a commodity.** Granola itself says AI meeting notes are becoming a commodity, so it is betting on team context and integrations rather than transcription alone.
