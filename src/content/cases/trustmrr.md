Almost every revenue figure in the latest batch of cases on this site comes from one source: TrustMRR. So it is fair to ask how TrustMRR itself makes money. Its own listing answers in the same verified format it applies to everyone else: $31,653 in the last 30 days, $13,491 in MRR and $409,043 all time in November 2026 ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/trustmrr)). The gap between those first two numbers is the most interesting part of the story.

## What is TrustMRR and how does it verify revenue?

TrustMRR is a public database of startup revenue in which each number comes from the startup's payment provider. Its methodology note says revenue, MRR, customers and churn are calculated from payment records retrieved through an API key or an equivalent owner-authorised credential, and are not screenshots or self-reported claims ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/trustmrr)). Profile text, descriptions and seller messages are user-generated.

> "The $1.2B database of verified startup revenues. Accidentally a startup marketplace." — TrustMRR listing ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/trustmrr))

The listing names Marc Lou as founder, October 2025 as the founding date, Singapore as the location and a team of one person. Pricing is not disclosed on the listing.

## Why is TrustMRR revenue so much higher than its MRR?

Because most TrustMRR revenue is not recurring. The listing shows only nine active subscriptions and $13,491 MRR, but $31,653 collected in the last 30 days ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/trustmrr)). The difference points to one-time payments, which fits the marketplace side: other listings on the site carry paid "Growth" and "Scale" sale tiers. TrustMRR does not break down its revenue by product, so this reading is ours.

Revenue over the last 12 months was $388,665 ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/trustmrr)). The 30-day snapshot was down 34.1%, and MRR was down 45.5%, so the November picture is weaker than the summer peak. The listing reported a 90% profit margin, a company-provided figure.

## How does TrustMRR get 400,000 visitors a month?

Mostly through search and its founder's audience. TrustMRR showed 404,588 visitors and 674,401 Google Search impressions in the last 30 days, measured through connected DataFast and Google Search Console ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/trustmrr)). Every startup profile is its own indexable page, often ranking for "<company> revenue" searches.

The listing names eight channels: SEO, word of mouth, X, YouTube, a newsletter, content marketing, LinkedIn and Reddit. Marc Lou had 423,171 X followers. Revenue per visitor was just $0.10 ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/trustmrr)), which shows the traffic is mostly readers, not buyers.

## What lessons does TrustMRR teach about data-led products?

- **Trust can be the product.** TrustMRR's value is that its numbers come from payment APIs, not screenshots ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/trustmrr)).
- **Every record can be a landing page.** Thousands of startup profiles turn into search traffic, with 674,401 impressions in 30 days.
- **Marketplaces earn in lumps.** With nine subscriptions, most revenue arrives as one-time payments, so monthly totals swing.
- **Free readers fund paid sellers.** A $0.10 revenue per visitor is fine when the audience itself is what sellers pay to reach.
