Live sports apps live or die on data that arrives in seconds. SportsApi Pro sells that data as an API, and by November 2026, eleven months after it was founded, it had 107 paying subscriptions and $5,287 in monthly recurring revenue ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/sportsapi-pro)). Its founder has also put it up for sale, which makes the numbers a snapshot of a business changing hands.

## What does SportsApi Pro sell to developers?

SportsApi Pro sells one API that covers real-time scores, match events, lineups, standings, historical data and odds. The TrustMRR headline says 20+ sports and 15,000+ competitions; the value proposition on the same page says 25+ sports and 25,000+ competitions ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/sportsapi-pro)).

> "Provides a unified API for real-time sports data" — SportsApi Pro company description ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/sportsapi-pro))

The audience is B2B: developers building live sports products. The listing names React and TypeScript on the frontend and Dodo Payments for billing.

## Why is the SportsApi Pro founder selling?

The founder says the reason is time, not trouble. In a message on TrustMRR, the founder calls SportsApiPro.com a fully built sports data API SaaS with low maintenance and clear subscription opportunities, and says they are focusing on other projects ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/sportsapi-pro)).

For a buyer, the attraction is a running subscription base in a niche where developers rarely switch once their app depends on a data feed.

## How does SportsApi Pro differ from Apiframe and Image-Charts?

SportsApi Pro, [Apiframe](/cases/apiframe/) and [Image-Charts](/cases/image-charts/) are all paid APIs for developers, but they wrap very different inputs.

| Company | What the API wraps | MRR (Nov 2026) | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| SportsApi Pro | Live sports data | $5,287 | [verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/sportsapi-pro) |
| [Apiframe](/cases/apiframe/) | AI image and video models | $35,032 | [verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/apiframe) |
| [Image-Charts](/cases/image-charts/) | Chart images from a URL | $6,278 | [verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/image-charts) |

Our observation: Image-Charts is the closest match in size and in being a durable utility. Apiframe grew faster by riding AI model demand. SportsApi Pro sits in between: its data is always in demand, but the market already has large incumbents, so a small player competes on breadth and price.

## What lessons does SportsApi Pro offer API builders?

- **Breadth is the pitch.** One API for many sports and competitions is the core promise.
- **Keep copy consistent.** The same listing quotes both 20+ and 25+ sports, which a buyer will notice.
- **Low maintenance sells.** The founder leads with low upkeep, a key point for anyone acquiring a side project.
- **Small ticket, sticky base.** About $49 per subscription across 107 customers is modest but recurring.
