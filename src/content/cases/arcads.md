## Origin of the idea
Performance marketers need a steady stream of fresh "person talking to camera" ads, and hiring creators for each variant is slow and expensive. AI video and voice models made it possible to generate those variants from a script.

## How they started
The founders launched a simple script-to-video product aimed at app and digital-product advertisers, starting from about $5K a month in early 2024.

## Tools & tech stack
Generative video, lip-sync and voice models behind a web app where marketers pick an AI actor, paste a script and render many variants.

## Marketing
They use their own product: paid social ads made with Arcads, plus influencer marketing and outbound sales to agencies.

## Sales & pricing
Self-serve monthly plans based on the number of videos, with higher tiers for agencies.

## Monthly revenue
About **$833K/month**, implied from **$10M ARR** the founder reported to Latka in November 2025, with 6,000+ customers. Earlier figures: $5M ARR on the Let's Talk Ads podcast and $6M ARR attributed to a May 2025 Latka interview (unverified: the Latka page no longer shows it).

## Total revenue
Not disclosed.

## Founding date & timeline
- **Early 2024:** Launch, ~$5K/month.
- **Apr 2025:** $5M ARR.
- **May 2025:** $6M ARR, ~4,000 paying customers (unverified).
- **Nov 2025:** $10M ARR, 6,000+ customers, team of 8 (founder-reported via Latka).

## Team size
A team of 8 as of November 2025 (founder-reported via Latka).

## Key takeaways
- Sell to buyers who measure ROI daily: performance marketers.
- Dogfooding your ad tool doubles as proof.
- Most customers sold digital products, not physical goods.
