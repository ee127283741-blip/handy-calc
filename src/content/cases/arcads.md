A performance marketer running ads on social platforms needs a constant supply of new creative: a person talking to camera about a product, in dozens of variations, every week. Hiring a creator for each variant is slow and expensive. Arcads replaced the creator with an AI actor and the shoot with a script. Launched in early 2024, the company reported $10M ARR, 6,000+ customers and a team of just 8 by November 2025 ([founder-reported via Latka](https://getlatka.com/interviews/arcads-romain-torres-2025)).

## Why did the founders start Arcads?

Performance marketers need a steady stream of fresh "person talking to camera" ads, and hiring creators for each variant is slow and expensive. AI video and voice models made it possible to generate those variants from a script. Founders Romain Torres and Dylan Fournier saw a buyer with a recurring, measurable need: ad creative wears out, and testing new versions is how performance marketing works.

## How does Arcads work?

Arcads is a web app where marketers pick an AI actor, paste a script and render many variants. Behind it, generative video, lip-sync and voice models produce a video of the actor delivering the script in a user-generated-content style. Because each render is cheap, a marketer can test many hooks and scripts instead of a few.

## How was Arcads built?

The founders launched a simple script-to-video product aimed at app and digital-product advertisers. Torres told Latka the service did about $5K in MRR in its first week after launching in January 2024 ([founder-reported via Latka](https://getlatka.com/blog/arcads-revenue-ceo)). The product combines several generative models behind one interface rather than training a single proprietary model. Outside funding is not disclosed.

## How did Arcads market, sell and price its product?

Arcads uses its own product: paid social ads made with Arcads, plus influencer marketing and outbound sales to agencies. Dogfooding means every ad they run is also a demonstration of what customers will get.

Sales are mostly self-serve monthly plans based on the number of videos, with higher tiers for agencies. Agencies are a natural upsell because they produce creative for many clients at once. Most customers sold digital products, not physical goods, which fits the original focus on app and digital-product advertisers.

## What challenges did Arcads face?

The main difficulty for an outside reader is the shifting set of growth figures. The founder gave $5M ARR on the Let's Talk Ads podcast ([founder-reported, Let's Talk Ads](https://lets-talk-ads.simplecast.com/episodes/zero-to-5m-arr-in-1-year-generation-ai-with-romain-torres-co-founder-of-arcads-KlCZVasZ)); the case timeline places that milestone in April 2025. A $6M ARR figure and about 4,000 paying customers were attributed to a May 2025 Latka interview, but they are unverified because the Latka page no longer shows them ([unverified, Latka](https://getlatka.com/interviews/arcads-romain-torres-2025)).

The market challenge is competition: once AI actors became possible, many tools offered them. Arcads' answer was focus on performance marketers and agencies, and proving the product in its own ad accounts.

## How much revenue does Arcads make?

Arcads had $10M ARR in November 2025, which implies about $833K a month ([founder-reported via Latka](https://getlatka.com/interviews/arcads-romain-torres-2025)). At that point it reported 6,000+ customers ([founder-reported via Latka](https://getlatka.com/interviews/arcads-romain-torres-2025)) and a team of 8 ([founder-reported via Latka](https://getlatka.com/interviews/arcads-romain-torres-2025)). Total revenue is not disclosed.

Put on a timeline: about $5K in MRR in the first week after the January 2024 launch (founder-reported via Latka); $5M ARR around April 2025 (founder-reported on the podcast); $6M ARR and about 4,000 paying customers in May 2025 (unverified); $10M ARR, 6,000+ customers and 8 people in November 2025 (founder-reported via Latka).

## Why did Arcads work?

It sold to buyers who measure ROI daily. Performance marketers can see within days whether an ad works, so a tool that produces more winning ads proves itself quickly, and churn is decided by results rather than opinions.

Dogfooding the ad tool doubled as proof. The company's own paid social campaigns are both its acquisition channel and its demo reel. And a team of 8 at $10M ARR shows how far a focused product can go with few people. Compare [OpusClip](/cases/opus-clip/), which sells AI video to creators, and [Tweet Hunter](/cases/tweet-hunter/), which sold content tools to people growing audiences.

## What can founders learn from Arcads?

Sell to buyers who measure ROI daily: performance marketers. A customer who can measure your value will pay for it quickly.

Dogfooding your ad tool doubles as proof. If your product makes marketing, use it to market yourself.

Know your customer mix. Most Arcads customers sold digital products, not physical goods, and that knowledge shapes the actors, scripts and agencies you target.

And track your numbers carefully in public. Arcads' milestones come from several interviews; only some can still be checked.
