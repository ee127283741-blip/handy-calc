AI video clipping is one of the most crowded corners of creator software, with well-funded players already in it. AutoClip entered anyway in February 2026, and nine months later it showed $11,773 in monthly recurring revenue ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/autoclip)). Its listing names one marketing channel, TikTok, which is also where its customers post their clips.

## What does AutoClip do with a long video?

AutoClip cuts a long video into short vertical clips and then posts them for you. The company-written listing says it turns long videos into viral shorts and auto-posts them to all your social platforms in one click ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/autoclip)). The audience is creators, marketers, agencies and content teams, and the listing counts about 630 users.

> "AutoClip turns long videos into viral shorts, and auto-posts them to all your social platforms in one click." — AutoClip listing ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/autoclip))

The posting step matters. Many clippers stop at the export, so the user still has to upload to each network by hand.

AutoClip had $11,773 MRR and 378 active subscriptions in November 2026, with $12,446 collected in the last 30 days and $46,921 all time ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/autoclip)). ## Why does AutoClip run on Whop and Convex?

The stack is built for speed. The listing names Next.js on the front end and Convex plus Whop on the back end ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/autoclip)). Whop is a payments and storefront platform popular with creator-economy sellers, so it fits a product sold to creators. Convex handles the database and backend logic without a separate server.

Distribution is equally narrow: TikTok is the only listed marketing channel. For a tool that makes TikTok clips, showing the output on TikTok is a natural demo.

## How does AutoClip compare with Opus Clip and Submagic?

AutoClip competes directly with [Opus Clip](/cases/opus-clip/), the best-known AI clipper, and sits beside [Submagic](/cases/submagic/), which focuses on captions for short videos. Both are older and larger.

Our observation: AutoClip does not try to out-feature them. It bundles clipping with auto-posting and bills through Whop, a channel those tools do not lean on. Its plans start at $19.99 a month ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/autoclip)), so it is priced as a cheaper alternative for solo creators rather than a studio tool.

## What lessons does AutoClip offer for crowded AI markets?

- **A crowded market can still pay.** AutoClip reached $11,773 MRR within nine months despite well-known rivals ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/autoclip)).
- **Own the last step.** Auto-posting removes the manual upload that other clippers leave to the user.
- **Sell where the output lives.** One channel, TikTok, is also the place customers publish.
- **Payments can follow the audience.** Billing through Whop puts the product inside a platform creators already use.
