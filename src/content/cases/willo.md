AI app builders promise a working website in minutes. Willo, launched in March 2026, promises the next step too: once the site is live, it keeps running the business. Eight months in, that pitch showed $6,922 in monthly recurring revenue, 119 paying subscriptions and $32,144 in all-time revenue ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/willo)). It is a small number in a category dominated by giants, which makes its positioning worth a look.

## What does Willo build in the first 10 minutes?

Willo turns a description of a business into a working setup. The homepage walks through a first 10 minutes: competitor research, brand positioning, a site built and deployed, a 20-task roadmap, a contact form and an email signup form ([founder-reported, Willo homepage](https://willo.ai/)). Users then change things by chatting, and edits go live straight away.

> "Build, run, and grow your business. Then it keeps working for you." — Willo homepage ([founder-reported, Willo](https://willo.ai/))

Hosting, SEO for Google and AI search, analytics, forms, sign-ins and payments are bundled, so the user does not stitch tools together. A beta lets users migrate an existing site.

## How much does Willo make eight months after launch?

Willo had $6,922 in MRR and 119 active subscriptions in November 2026, and it collected $7,196 in the 30 days before the snapshot ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/willo)). All-time revenue was $32,144 since the March 2026 founding.

## Who is Willo built for?

The TrustMRR listing names entrepreneurs and business creators who want to automate and grow a business, and frames the problem as ideas that "die in spreadsheets and to-do lists" ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/willo)). The homepage offers starting points such as an e-commerce store, a SaaS landing page, a personal website, a reporting dashboard and an onboarding portal, plus a section for trades businesses.

TrustMRR shows founder Jason Francis with about 537 followers on X. The listing does not name marketing channels, so how Willo found its first 119 subscribers is not disclosed.

## How does Willo differ from Lovable and Base44?

| Company | What the user describes | What happens after launch |
|---|---|---|
| Willo | A business idea | Willo keeps doing content, marketing and support; $6,922 MRR ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/willo)) |
| [Lovable](/cases/lovable/) | An app | The user keeps building |
| [Base44](/cases/base44/) | An app | The user keeps building |

Our observation: Lovable and Base44 compete on how well they build software. Willo competes on what happens after the build, aiming at owners who want a business, not an app. That is a narrower but less crowded promise, and it fits a small player that cannot outspend the leaders.

## What lessons can small AI builders take from Willo?

- **Pick the step after the giants.** Willo sells running the business, not just building it.
- **Bundle the boring parts.** Hosting, forms, payments and SEO in one place remove setup work for non-technical owners.
- **Early numbers are honest numbers.** $6,922 of MRR after eight months ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/willo)) is a real baseline, not a projection.
- **Show the first 10 minutes.** A timed walkthrough on the homepage makes an abstract AI promise concrete.
