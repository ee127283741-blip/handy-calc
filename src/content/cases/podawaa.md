A LinkedIn post with zero likes in its first hour rarely recovers. Podawaa sells the fix: early likes and comments that push a post into more feeds. "Never publish a LinkedIn post with 0 likes again," its site promises ([founder-reported, Podawaa](https://www.podawaa.com)). In November 2026 that promise showed $60,510 in MRR and $1,255,133 in all-time revenue ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/podawaa)).

## How does Podawaa boost a LinkedIn post?

Podawaa boosts a post by sending likes and comments to it over several hours after it is published. A user enters a LinkedIn profile URL once, publishes as usual, and Podawaa detects the new post and starts the boost. The site offers two modes. Community mode is free and runs through a Chrome extension: your account engages with other members' posts, and theirs engage with yours. Private mode is paid, needs no install, and your account only receives engagement.

The site claims over 10,000 active creators and 100,000 likes delivered daily ([founder-reported, Podawaa](https://www.podawaa.com)). Those are company figures, not independently checked.

## What does Podawaa charge for each plan?

Podawaa charges in euros, from free to €159 a month, with limits on likes and comments per post. Yearly billing saves 17%, and agencies pay extra per added account.

| Plan | Price a month (excl. VAT) | Likes and comments per post | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Community | €0 | Up to 20 likes, 1 comment | [founder-reported, Podawaa](https://www.podawaa.com) |
| Scale | €59 | Up to 50 likes, 10 comments | [founder-reported, Podawaa](https://www.podawaa.com) |
| Accelerate | €89 | Up to 100 likes, 20 comments | [founder-reported, Podawaa](https://www.podawaa.com) |
| Dominate | €159 | Up to 250 likes, 50 comments | [founder-reported, Podawaa](https://www.podawaa.com) |

The pricing reads like a menu of reach: more money, more engagement per post.

## How much revenue do Podawaa boosts bring in?

Podawaa had $60,510 in MRR from 579 active subscriptions in November 2026, and $54,610 in revenue over the last 30 days ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/podawaa)). That works out to about $104.51 of MRR per subscription ([calc, from TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/podawaa)), above the €89 Accelerate price. The page does not explain the difference; the listed prices exclude VAT and are in euros.

TrustMRR dates Podawaa to June 2024 and lists it as bootstrapped and based in France. Reaching $1,255,133 in total revenue in about two and a half years implies steady, not explosive, sales. Profit and headcount are not disclosed.

## Who founded Podawaa, Tom Benattar or Stephen Zambaux?

Podawaa's two sources name different people. TrustMRR lists Tom Benattar as the founder, while the Podawaa site presents Stephen Zambaux as founder and CEO and quotes him directly.

> "We built Podawaa for our own posts." — Stephen Zambaux ([founder-reported, Podawaa](https://www.podawaa.com))

Both may be co-founders, but neither page says so, so we list both and note the gap. The site's safety message has a similar tension. It says Podawaa never publishes, edits a profile or sends a message from your account, while its FAQ says it cannot rule out that LinkedIn limits an account or a boosted post's reach.

## How does Podawaa compare with Postiz and Tweet Hunter?

Podawaa earns less per month than Postiz but sells something neither of these tools sells: engagement itself, rather than help creating or scheduling posts.

| Company | What it sells | Monthly figure | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Podawaa | LinkedIn likes and comments | $60,510 MRR (Nov 2026) | [verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/podawaa) |
| [Postiz](/cases/postiz/) | Social media scheduling | $373,501 MRR, Stripe only (Nov 2026) | [verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/postiz) |
| [Tweet Hunter](/cases/tweet-hunter/) | X growth and writing tools | Not disclosed | [Our Tweet Hunter case](/cases/tweet-hunter/) |

Our observation: Postiz and Tweet Hunter help users make better content and post it on time. Podawaa sits after the post and buys it an audience. That makes it easy to explain and easy to measure, a like count, but it also depends on a platform that may not welcome it. Its own FAQ admits that risk, which is rarer than the claims of zero risk common in this niche.

## What takeaways does Podawaa offer social media founders?

- **Sell the metric people already watch.** Likes on a LinkedIn post are visible within minutes, and Podawaa built $1,255,133 in revenue around them ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/podawaa)).
- **A free mutual tier feeds the paid one.** Community mode needs members to engage, and Private mode sells the same result without the work.
- **Platform risk belongs in the FAQ.** Podawaa states that LinkedIn may limit reach, and buyers should weigh it.
- **Keep founder records consistent.** Two sources naming two founders leaves readers unsure who runs the company.
