Most businesses on TrustMRR sell software to thousands of small accounts. Indexsy does the opposite. In November 2026 it showed $34,642 in monthly recurring revenue from just 26 active subscriptions, and $2,748,111 in all-time revenue ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/indexsy-consulting-ltd)). It is a link building and SEO agency that has been running since 2015, and it shows what a decade of selling one service to search-hungry companies can add up to.

## What does Indexsy sell to its B2B clients?

Indexsy sells SEO and link building as a service. The company-written TrustMRR listing describes it as "comprehensive link building and SEO solutions for sustainable growth", aimed at businesses that want better online visibility and search rankings ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/indexsy-consulting-ltd)). The audience is tagged B2B, and the listing links to a services page on indexsy.com.

> "Buy seo services from us" — Indexsy listing ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/indexsy-consulting-ltd))

The tagline is blunt, and that fits the model: clients buy outcomes, not a dashboard. The listing does not describe how AI is used in delivery, so we do not claim it.

## Why does Jacky Chou's long run matter for Indexsy?

Indexsy was founded in December 2015 in Canada, so the $2,748,111 all-time figure ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/indexsy-consulting-ltd)) reflects years of client work rather than a sudden launch. The listing describes Jacky Chou as "buying online businesses up to $1m" and shows 63.2k followers on X. Indexsy's own domain has a Domain Rating of 65 out of 100 on the listing, which matters for an agency selling links: its site is its portfolio.

## How does Indexsy compare with SEO Stack and Backlinker AI?

Indexsy sells the same outcome as several SEO cases on this site, but through people and retainers rather than self-serve software.

| Company | Model | What the buyer gets |
|---|---|---|
| Indexsy | Agency retainers, 26 subscriptions | Done-for-you SEO and link building |
| [SEO Stack](/cases/seo-stack/) | Software | SEO tooling the buyer runs |
| [Backlinker AI](/cases/backlinker-ai/) | Software | Automated backlink building |

Our observation: software tools in this space chase volume at low prices, while Indexsy reaches a similar MRR band with a handful of high-value accounts. That trades scale for concentration risk, since losing one client moves the number far more than it would for a tool.

## What lessons does Indexsy offer service founders?

- **Few clients can carry real MRR.** Twenty-six subscriptions produced $34,642 of MRR in November 2026.
- **Longevity compounds.** A business founded in 2015 shows $2,748,111 all time on Stripe.
- **Your own site is the sales deck.** An SEO agency with a strong Domain Rating proves its craft before a call.
- **Mix recurring and one-off work.** Thirty-day revenue above MRR points to extra project income, though the split is not disclosed.
