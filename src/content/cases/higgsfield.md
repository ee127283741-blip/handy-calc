Higgsfield revenue grew about 3.5x in seven months. The AI video app reported a $200M annualized run rate in January 2026 ([founder-reported, Reuters](https://ca.finance.yahoo.com/news/ai-video-startup-higgsfield-hits-120257371.html)) and $700M in August 2026 ([founder-reported, Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/technology/ai/articles/higgsfield-raises-400-million-series-133532074.html)). Higgsfield's pitch is not one famous model. It packages video and image models into presets, camera moves and effects that social media marketers can use to make ads and short clips without a film crew.

## Who founded Higgsfield, and what does it sell?

Higgsfield was founded by Alex Mashrabov, the former head of generative AI at Snap. Snap had bought his earlier startup, AI Factory, in 2020 for $166M ([media-reported, Reuters](https://ca.finance.yahoo.com/news/ai-video-startup-higgsfield-hits-120257371.html)). When Higgsfield launched in 2024, Mashrabov told TechCrunch that social media and social media marketing would be its main money-making niche ([TechCrunch via Yahoo News](https://ca.news.yahoo.com/former-snap-ai-chief-launches-152822487.html)). The app sells subscriptions to creators who want stylized videos fast. Its plan prices, founding date and team size are not disclosed in sources we could check.

> "Our target audience is creators of all types," — Alex Mashrabov ([founder-reported, TechCrunch via Yahoo News](https://ca.news.yahoo.com/former-snap-ai-chief-launches-152822487.html))

## How much revenue does Higgsfield make in 2026?

Higgsfield reported $700M in annualized revenue in August 2026, about $58M a month (calc) ([founder-reported, Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/technology/ai/articles/higgsfield-raises-400-million-series-133532074.html)). There is an important caveat on the January figure. Reuters added a correction saying the $200M annualized run rate was a projection that the company said is not recognized revenue ([founder-reported, Reuters](https://ca.finance.yahoo.com/news/ai-video-startup-higgsfield-hits-120257371.html)). Neither figure is audited, and total revenue is not disclosed.

## Why did Higgsfield's valuation quadruple in 2026?

Revenue grew faster than the price. The January 2026 Series A extension valued Higgsfield at over $1.3B ([founder-reported, Reuters](https://ca.finance.yahoo.com/news/ai-video-startup-higgsfield-hits-120257371.html)). The August Series B, led by DST Global, valued it at $5.4B ([founder-reported, Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/technology/ai/articles/higgsfield-raises-400-million-series-133532074.html)). That is about 4x the valuation on 3.5x the run rate (calc), so investors paid roughly the same multiple of revenue in August as in January.

## How does Higgsfield compare with Runway and Synthesia?

Higgsfield is the fastest-growing video company in this library, and it sells to a different buyer. [Runway](/cases/runway/) builds its own frontier video models and courts film and studio work. [Synthesia](/cases/synthesia/) sells AI avatar video to enterprises for training and internal communication, with longer contracts. Higgsfield sells monthly subscriptions to marketers and creators who need many short, eye-catching clips. [HeyGen](/cases/heygen/) is closest in buyer, with avatar video for marketing.

Our reading: Higgsfield's advantage is distribution and packaging, not one model. It can add whichever video model is best this month as a preset. The risk is the same: if a model maker sells the same presets directly, Higgsfield has to win on workflow and community instead.

## What lessons does Higgsfield offer AI app founders?

- **Package models for one buyer.** Higgsfield turned general video models into presets for social media marketers.
- **Read the fine print on run rates.** Reuters had to note that Higgsfield's January $200M figure was a projection, not recognized revenue.
- **Founder history speeds fundraising.** Mashrabov had already sold a startup to Snap, which helped investors move fast.
- **Model-agnostic is a strategy.** An app that can switch models keeps up with each new release without training its own.
