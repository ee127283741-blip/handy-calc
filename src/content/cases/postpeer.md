Every social network has its own API, its own review process and its own way of breaking. PostPeer wraps seven of them behind one endpoint and sells access to developers and AI agents. Founded in April 2026 by Jonathan Geiger, it had $6,270 MRR and $31,228 in lifetime revenue by November 2026 ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/postpeer)). Its last 30 days were its strongest yet on the page.

## What does PostPeer replace for developers?

PostPeer replaces separate integrations for each platform with one API for X, Instagram, YouTube, TikTok, Facebook, Threads and Pinterest ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/postpeer)). Beyond posting and scheduling, it covers analytics, messaging and comment management.

> "Unified Social Media API for Developers and AI Agents" — PostPeer value proposition ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/postpeer))

The stack is Next.js, Node.js, AWS, Redis and MongoDB, with Polar handling billing. TrustMRR verifies the revenue through that Polar account.

## Why does PostPeer target AI agents?

AI agents that run marketing need a way to act, not just write. A single posting endpoint lets an agent publish to seven networks without the developer building seven integrations ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/postpeer)). The listing serves both B2B and B2C buyers, and credits suit usage-based demand, where an agent might post rarely or in bursts.

Our observation: Post Bridge sells the same job to people who click buttons; PostPeer sells it to code. Apiframe shows that an API wrapper around messy third-party systems can grow large once developers trust its reliability.

## What lessons does PostPeer offer API founders?

- **Wrap the painful integrations.** Seven social APIs behind one endpoint is a clear time saver.
- **Name AI agents as customers.** Agents need tools to act, and say so in the positioning.
- **Free credits lower the first step.** Developers can test before paying.
- **Recent momentum shows in 30-day revenue.** About 30% of lifetime revenue came in one month.
