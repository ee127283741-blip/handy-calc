## Origin of the idea
Dinh used ChatGPT daily and was frustrated by the official interface: no search across chats, no prompt library, no way to use his own API key. He built the interface he wanted.

## How they started
He shipped a first version within days of the ChatGPT API opening in March 2023 and announced it on X, where he already had an audience from earlier products such as DevUtils and Xnapper.

## Tools & tech stack
A front-end-heavy web app that talks directly to model APIs with the user's own key, also available as a macOS app and a self-hosted version. License keys are handled by Lemon Squeezy.

## Marketing
Building in public on X, frequent feature launches, and an affiliate programme run through the payment provider.

## Sales & pricing
One-time license purchases instead of a subscription at launch, because users pay model providers for usage themselves. Team and self-hosted licenses came later.

## Monthly revenue
Not disclosed from a primary source with a date.

## Total revenue
**$200K+** within about four months (payment provider case study, Aug 2023) and **$500K+** after about a year (founder-reported, Feb 2024).

## Founding date & timeline
- **Mar 2023:** Launch, days after the ChatGPT API release.
- **Aug 2023:** $200K+ revenue reported.
- **Feb 2024:** $500K+ total revenue reported.

## Team size
Four people as of August 2023 (per the Lemon Squeezy case study).

## Key takeaways
- Wrap a popular tool with the features power users are missing.
- Bring-your-own-key keeps margins near 100%.
- Speed to market mattered more than polish.
