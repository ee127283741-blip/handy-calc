Every static website eventually needs a contact form, and a contact form needs somewhere to send its data. Web3Forms is that somewhere. Started in December 2020 in India, it had around 412,000 users and $44,178 in monthly recurring revenue by November 2026 ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/web3forms)). It is a near-pure example of a freemium developer utility.

## What does Web3Forms do for a static website?

Web3Forms forwards HTML form submissions to the site owner's email. A developer points a form at the Web3Forms endpoint, and messages arrive in the inbox without any server, database or backend code ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/web3forms)).

> "No server or backend code needed. It's free!" — Web3Forms description on TrustMRR ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/web3forms))

That pitch fits the way sites are built now. Frameworks such as Next.js and Astro output static pages that deploy to hosts like Vercel or Cloudflare, and a form handler is one of the few pieces still missing. Web3Forms lists its own stack as Next.js, Tailwind CSS, Vercel, Hetzner and Cloudflare.

## How many Web3Forms users actually pay?

Roughly 0.8% of Web3Forms users pay, based on 3,208 subscriptions among about 412,000 users ([calc, from TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/web3forms)). The average subscription is worth about $13.77 a month. Exact paid plan prices are not disclosed on the listing, which only says "paid plans available" above the free 250 submissions a month.

A low conversion rate is normal for a generous free tier. The free plan is the marketing: every site using Web3Forms is a developer who already trusts it when they hit the limit or need a feature. TrustMRR also shows a Domain Rating of 87, which is very high for a small company and points to many backlinks from tutorials and sites using the service.

## How does Web3Forms compare with Supabase and TypingMind?

Web3Forms is a narrow backend utility, while Supabase is a full backend platform; both make money by letting front-end developers skip server work. TypingMind adds a contrast from the solo-developer side: a small tool sold differently.

Our observation: Web3Forms and Supabase both rely on a free tier that developers adopt before they ever talk about money. The difference is scope. Supabase replaces a whole backend; Web3Forms replaces one endpoint, so it earns less per user but needs almost no sales effort.

## What lessons does Web3Forms hold for utility builders?

- **Solve one missing piece of a popular stack.** Static sites lack form handling, and Web3Forms fills exactly that gap ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/web3forms)).
- **A free tier can be the whole marketing plan.** About 412,000 users feed 3,208 subscriptions without a listed ad channel.
- **Low prices need very high volume.** At about $13.77 per subscription, reaching $44,178 MRR took thousands of payers.
- **Patience can pay off.** Most of the $366,094 lifetime revenue came years after the 2020 launch.
