Travel agents juggle clients, suppliers and itineraries, often in spreadsheets and inboxes. JourneyFuse sells them a CRM built for that job, and it grew fast. Founded in March 2026, it showed $11,898 in MRR and $71,870 in all-time revenue by November 2026 ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/journeyfuse)). Who runs it is not disclosed.

## Who is the JourneyFuse CRM built for?

JourneyFuse is built for travel agents and travel agencies, according to its TrustMRR listing ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/journeyfuse)). The listing tags it as travel, SaaS, productivity and customer support software. It does not list individual features, so we will not guess at them.

> "We develop CRM solutions for Travel Agents and agencies" — JourneyFuse listing ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/journeyfuse))

A vertical CRM wins when general tools feel like too much setup. Independent agents have a specific workflow, and a product named for them is easier to say yes to.

## How fast did JourneyFuse revenue grow after launch?

JourneyFuse reached $11,898 in MRR with 581 active subscriptions about eight months after its March 2026 start ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/journeyfuse)). It collected $21,607 in the 30 days before the snapshot, well above its MRR.

## What does JourneyFuse charge travel agents?

JourneyFuse lists a single price of $25 a month ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/journeyfuse)). MRR divided by subscriptions comes to about $20.48 ([calc, from TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/journeyfuse)), a little under list price, which fits some customers paying annually or on discount.

One plan keeps the buying decision simple. A solo agent does not need to compare tiers before trying the product.

## What remains unknown about the JourneyFuse team?

Most of the company is not disclosed. TrustMRR shows no founder name, no team size and no marketing channels for JourneyFuse ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/journeyfuse)). The only stack detail is Stripe for payments. That makes it hard to say how the company found 581 paying subscriptions so quickly.

## How does JourneyFuse compare with Plutio and Calendesk?

[Plutio](/cases/plutio/) and [Calendesk](/cases/calendesk/) also sell business software to small service providers, but both are broader. Plutio covers freelancers generally and Calendesk focuses on bookings. JourneyFuse narrows the audience to one trade.

Our observation: the narrow focus may explain the speed. A product named for travel agents can be found by travel agents, while horizontal tools must fight for generic search terms. The trade-off is a smaller total market.

## What lessons does JourneyFuse hold for founders selling to travel agencies?

- **Name the trade.** A CRM described for travel agents is easier to find and trust than a general CRM.
- **One price is enough at first.** A single $25 plan reached 581 subscriptions in eight months ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/journeyfuse)).
- **Read 30-day revenue beside MRR.** A large gap points to non-monthly payments.
- **Verified numbers can stand alone.** Even with the founder not disclosed, Stripe-checked revenue gives readers a reliable picture.
