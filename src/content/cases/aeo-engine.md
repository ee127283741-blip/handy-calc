Search engine optimization used to mean ranking on page one. AEO Engine sells the next version: being the answer an AI gives. In November 2026 the agency showed $190,113 in MRR and $2,718,751 in all-time revenue ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/aeo-engine)) from just 64 active subscriptions. That is an agency's customer count with a software company's dashboard.

## What does AEO Engine sell to brands?

AEO Engine sells done-for-you "answer engine optimization", content and technical work meant to get a brand cited by AI search tools. The company's TrustMRR line states the goal plainly.

> "The AI SEO agency that makes you the answer in AI Overviews, ChatGPT, and Google." — AEO Engine listing ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/aeo-engine))

Its [About page](https://aeoengine.ai/about) claims 40+ AI agents, 7 to 20 long-form articles a week and 50+ brands served worldwide ([founder-reported, AEO Engine](https://aeoengine.ai/about)). It also lists client results such as +306% organic traffic for a dental brand. These are company claims, and we have not seen them verified by clients or a third party.

## Who runs AEO Engine, and how old is it?

Vijay C. Jacob, the Founder and CEO, runs AEO Engine with Dan Ashburn, Co-Founder and CMO, according to the About page. The company is older than its name. The page says the business dates to 2018, with roots in FosterFBA, an Amazon seller service, and was formalized as AEO Engine LLC in January 2026. TrustMRR lists June 2018 as the founding date, which matches the older lineage.

Jacob's other ventures include ProductScope AI and UGC Engine, per the same page, and the company is based in Manvel, Texas. Total headcount is not disclosed. That history matters for the revenue below: the AEO brand did not start from zero customers.

## How much revenue does AEO Engine make from 64 clients?

AEO Engine had $190,113 in MRR from 64 active subscriptions in November 2026, or about $2,971 per subscription ([calc, from TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/aeo-engine)). Revenue over the last 30 days was $169,548.

The average lands almost exactly on the Scale price, which suggests most clients sit in the middle tiers. Because the Stripe account may include revenue from the pre-2026 business, we would not read the all-time figure as AEO-only income.

## How does AEO Engine compare with ChatSEO and Leadmore AI?

AEO Engine earns more than six times the monthly revenue of either tool, because it sells labor-backed retainers rather than self-serve software. All three target the same fear, losing traffic as search changes, but price it very differently.

| Company | Model | Monthly figure | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| AEO Engine | Agency retainers from $1,597 | $190,113 MRR (Nov 2026) | [verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/aeo-engine) |
| [ChatSEO](/cases/chatseo/) | Self-serve AI SEO product | $30K MRR (Oct 2026) | [founder-reported, Indie Hackers](https://www.indiehackers.com/post/tech/focusing-on-audience-first-and-hitting-30k-mrr-in-8-months-ootKyp98moigKLdaOON4) |
| [Leadmore AI](/cases/leadmore-ai/) | AI marketing product | Over $30K MRR (Dec 2025) | [founder-reported, Indie Hackers](https://www.indiehackers.com/post/tech/hitting-30k-mrr-with-an-ai-marketing-product-n59ORJCYjnZC61Q096UL) |

Our observation: the software tools need hundreds of paying users to reach $30K, while AEO Engine needs only dozens of clients to pass six figures. The trade-off is margin and scale. A service with 40+ AI agents still promises weekly articles and results, and each new client adds delivery work that a pure software product does not carry.

## What takeaways does AEO Engine offer service founders?

- **Rename the service for the new channel.** An agency with 2018 roots repositioned around AI answers and showed $190,113 MRR ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/aeo-engine)).
- **High tickets need few customers.** 64 subscriptions averaging about $2,971 carry the business.
- **Tiered retainers work like SaaS plans.** Named tiers from $1,597 to $10,000 make an agency easy to buy.
- **Treat client case figures with care.** The growth percentages on the About page are self-reported, and buyers should ask for their own baseline.
