A coloring book or a gratitude journal has almost no words, yet thousands of them are sold on Amazon by people who never draw. Low Content AI sells those people the pages. In November 2026 the tool had $14,849 in monthly recurring revenue and $656,778 in all-time revenue ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/low-content-ai)), from a user base the listing puts at about 1,000.

## What does Low Content AI make for Amazon KDP sellers?

Low Content AI generates images and text for journals, planners and coloring books, formatted to publish on Amazon KDP. The homepage lists AI image generation, AI text generation and templates in every plan, and promises books "in minutes, not hours or days" ([founder-reported, Low Content AI homepage](https://lowcontent.ai/)).

The TrustMRR listing names the audience as self-publishers, KDP entrepreneurs and content creators, a business-to-consumer market. The customer is not the book reader but the seller, who uses the tool to fill a catalog faster.

> "Effortlessly Create Low-Content Books with AI" — Low Content AI listing ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/low-content-ai))

## How much revenue does Low Content AI make on yearly plans?

Low Content AI had $14,849 in MRR and 336 active subscriptions in November 2026, and $656,778 in all-time revenue ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/low-content-ai)). TrustMRR checks these figures through a Stripe API key. The last-30-day figure did not appear on the page we read.

| Plan | Credits per year | Yearly price | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Basic | 480,000,000 | €390 | [founder-reported, pricing page](https://lowcontent.ai/) |
| Pro | 720,000,000 | €490 | [founder-reported, pricing page](https://lowcontent.ai/) |
| Star | 1,320,000,000 | €990 | [founder-reported, pricing page](https://lowcontent.ai/) |

MRR divided by subscriptions is about $44.19 per subscription ([calc, from TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/low-content-ai)). That is close to what a €490 yearly plan works out to per month, so the average customer likely sits near the Pro tier, though the plan mix is not disclosed. Team size and profit are not disclosed either.

## Why does Low Content AI price in euros per year?

The pricing page leads with yearly billing and says paying yearly saves 16.6% against monthly ([founder-reported, pricing page](https://lowcontent.ai/)). Yearly prices of several hundred euros are high for a hobby tool but reasonable for a seller who treats KDP as a business.

The page sells credits in very large round numbers, from 480 million to 1.32 billion a year. Only the top Star plan includes API access, which suggests the heaviest users want to automate book production rather than click through templates.

## How does Low Content AI compare with SoldComps and DataFast?

All three sell tools to people running small online businesses, but Low Content AI earns the most per subscription of the three.

| Company | Customer | MRR (Nov 2026) | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Low Content AI | Amazon KDP self-publishers | $14,849 | [verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/low-content-ai) |
| [SoldComps](/cases/soldcomps/) | eBay resellers | $25,931 | [verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/soldcomps) |

Our observation: SoldComps and Low Content AI both serve marketplace sellers whose income depends on a platform they do not control. If Amazon changes KDP rules on AI content, Low Content AI's customers feel it first.

## What takeaways does Low Content AI hold for niche AI tools?

- **Sell to the seller, not the reader.** Low Content AI's customer is the person building a KDP catalog, who pays for speed.
- **Annual pricing fits business users.** Plans from €390 a year only work because buyers expect to earn the money back.
- **A small user base can carry real revenue.** About 1,000 users and 336 subscriptions sit behind $14,849 in MRR.
- **Platform risk is shared.** A tool built for one marketplace inherits that marketplace's policy changes.
