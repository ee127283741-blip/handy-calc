Every vending machine needs a spot, and the best spots are hard to find. VendPlacer sells that search. Seven months after its April 2026 founding, the marketplace showed $3,753 in MRR, $9,217 collected in the last 30 days and $47,338 in all-time revenue ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/vendplacer)). Its founder is not disclosed.

## What two sides does VendPlacer connect?

VendPlacer connects vending machine operators with property owners worldwide ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/vendplacer)). Operators use it to find high-traffic locations, and property owners use it to earn passive income from space they already have. The listing tags it as a marketplace and e-commerce business.

> "connects vending machine operators with property owners worldwide." — VendPlacer listing ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/vendplacer))

The operator side is the paying side in the listing, which fits the pattern where the party hunting for supply pays for access.

## How does VendPlacer make money from vending operators?

VendPlacer charges $69 a month and had 152 active subscriptions and $3,753 in MRR in November 2026 ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/vendplacer)). MRR per subscription is about $24.69 ([calc, from TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/vendplacer)), far below the list price.

## Why might VendPlacer's growth be front-loaded?

VendPlacer reached $47,338 in all-time revenue within seven months of its founding ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/vendplacer)). Vending has become a popular side-hustle topic, and new operators look for locations right after buying machines. A product that answers that first need can collect early payments that do not recur. This is our reading of the numbers, not a claim from the company.

## What is not disclosed about VendPlacer?

The founder, team size and marketing channels are not disclosed on the TrustMRR listing ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/vendplacer)). The stack lists only Stripe. We also do not know how many property owners are on the platform, which matters for a two-sided marketplace.

## How does VendPlacer compare with TryNearby and Store Stock Checker?

[TryNearby](/cases/trynearby/) and [Store Stock Checker](/cases/store-stock-checker/) both help people find something physical in the real world. VendPlacer applies the same idea to commercial space: it helps operators find places to put a machine.

Our observation: location-finding tools are easy to explain and pay off quickly for the user. The harder part for VendPlacer is keeping operators subscribed once they have placed their machines.

## What lessons does VendPlacer hold for marketplace founders?

- **Charge the side that is searching.** Operators looking for locations pay $69 a month ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/vendplacer)).
- **Ride a side-hustle wave.** Demand for vending locations can arrive in bursts.
- **Watch revenue against MRR.** $9,217 in 30 days next to $3,753 MRR shows how much is non-recurring.
- **Retention is the next test.** Once a machine is placed, the reason to keep paying weakens.
