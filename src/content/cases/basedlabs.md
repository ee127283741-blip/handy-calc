When Andrew Adams and his co-founder Michael started BasedLabs in December 2023, both were unemployed with about two to six months of personal runway. That gave them roughly five months to make it work. Four months in, BasedLabs had $10K MRR and about 150,000 signups, and they had spent less than $500 on marketing ([founder-reported, Starter Story](https://www.starterstory.com/stories/basedlabs-ai-based-ai-image-video-creator)).

## Where did the idea for BasedLabs come from?

From a dead end. The two had spent weeks building a tool to create short-form videos automatically with AI, and could not find high-quality AI b-roll footage to put in them. Around the same time Stability AI released Stable Video Diffusion, an open-source image-to-video model. That opened a chance to be among the first image-to-video websites.

The founders fit the idea. Michael had 15 years of development experience and had just built a CapCut competitor. Adams had about five years in product and marketing on Web3 and AI projects. Their only validation was the hype on Twitter and Reddit every time a new AI image or video model came out.

## How fast did BasedLabs ship its first version?

Three days. Michael built an MVP with an image-to-video generator, Google sign-in and an endless scrolling landing page of users' creations. They spent $100–$200 in the first month and used Replicate's API to spin up new AI tools fast ([founder-reported, Starter Story](https://www.starterstory.com/stories/basedlabs-ai-based-ai-image-video-creator)).

There was no launch event. As soon as people could sign up and make videos, the founders spent whole days posting content on Twitter, Reddit, Medium, TikTok, Instagram, YouTube Shorts and their blog. It took about two months to get steady traffic.

## What turned BasedLabs' traffic on?

One small paid tweet. A creator with about 200–300 followers shared a video she made on BasedLabs; the founders paid her $100, the tweet went viral and traffic that week jumped to around 10,000 visits ([founder-reported, Starter Story](https://www.starterstory.com/stories/basedlabs-ai-based-ai-image-video-creator)).

After that, SEO became the main growth engine. BasedLabs built free tools people search for, such as Face Swap, an AI Character Generator, an AI Art Generator and a Character Voice Generator, and those pages bring about 5,000 visits a day from Google.

> "Do not underestimate the power of SEO and to even adapt your business or tool to rank well for SEO." — Andrew Adams ([founder-reported, Starter Story](https://www.starterstory.com/stories/basedlabs-ai-based-ai-image-video-creator))

## How much MRR did BasedLabs AI hit in four months?

Four months after starting, BasedLabs reported $10K MRR and about 150,000 signups ([founder-reported, Starter Story](https://www.starterstory.com/stories/basedlabs-ai-based-ai-image-video-creator)). It was fully bootstrapped by two founders with no employees. Total revenue and pricing are not disclosed.

## How does BasedLabs compare with neural frames and Interior AI?

[neural frames](/cases/neural-frames/) built on the same Stability AI models but chose one audience, musicians, and one job, music videos. BasedLabs went wide with many tools, each one a search landing page. Both reached five-figure MRR; the narrow route gave neural frames a clear roadmap, while the wide route gave BasedLabs many ways to be found on Google.

[Interior AI](/cases/interior-ai/) shows a third option: one vertical, rooms, where a single before-and-after image sells the product. All three rent or reuse open models instead of training their own, which keeps costs low but means distribution decides who wins.

## What are the key takeaways from BasedLabs?

- **Ride model releases.** Stable Video Diffusion gave a three-day MVP a head start.
- **Free tools are SEO assets.** Each free generator is a page people search for.
- **Small paid boosts can spark big spikes.** A $100 tweet did more than weeks of posting ([founder-reported, Starter Story](https://www.starterstory.com/stories/basedlabs-ai-based-ai-image-video-creator)).
- **A short runway forces speed.** Five months of savings meant shipping in days, not months.
