RapidNative has a funnel most indie apps would envy: about 349,857 users, by the company's count ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/shaper-studio-inc)). Only 182 of them held a paid subscription in November 2026, worth $5,278 in MRR ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/shaper-studio-inc)). The gap between those two numbers is the central story of prompt-to-app tools in 2026.

## What does RapidNative generate from a prompt?

RapidNative generates mobile app code. A user describes an idea and the tool produces a React Native and Expo app, which the company says is a real full-stack app ready to publish.

> "Go from idea to a real full-stack mobile app, ready to publish, from a prompt." — RapidNative company description ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/shaper-studio-inc))

The listing names founders, product managers, UX designers and developers as the audience, both B2B and B2C. The problem it targets is letting non-developers build working apps without deep technical knowledge.

## How much revenue has RapidNative made since March 2025?

RapidNative had made $80,217 all time by November 2026, with $7,162 in the last 30 days and $5,278 in MRR from 182 subscriptions ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/shaper-studio-inc)). The 30-day figure is higher than MRR, which suggests some one-off or annual payments on top of monthly plans.

## Why does RapidNative convert so few of its users?

The free funnel is huge and the paid base is small. 182 paying subscriptions out of about 349,857 reported users is roughly 0.05% (calc, [from TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/shaper-studio-inc)). Prompt-to-app tools attract many curious visitors who try one idea and leave.

Distribution is not the bottleneck. Sanket Sahu has 14.6k followers on X, and the rapidnative.com domain has an Ahrefs Domain Rating of 58, high for a site this size ([TrustMRR listing](https://trustmrr.com/startup/shaper-studio-inc)).

## How does RapidNative stack up against Capgo and 1clickwebsite.ai?

RapidNative, [Capgo](/cases/capgo/) and [1clickwebsite.ai](/cases/1clickwebsite/) all help people ship apps or sites faster, at different stages.

| Company | Job | MRR (Nov 2026) | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| RapidNative | Generate a mobile app from a prompt | $5,278 | [verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/shaper-studio-inc) |
| [Capgo](/cases/capgo/) | Live updates for shipped Capacitor apps | $33,747 | [verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/capgo) |
| [1clickwebsite.ai](/cases/1clickwebsite/) | Generate a website | $14,155 | [verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/1clickwebsite-ai) |

Our observation: Capgo earns six times more by serving teams whose apps are already live and who pay to keep shipping. RapidNative sells the first step, where intent is weakest. 1clickwebsite.ai shows that generation tools can earn more when the output, a website, is something a small business needs right away.

## What takeaways does RapidNative offer AI builder founders?

- **Top-of-funnel is not revenue.** About 349,857 users produced 182 subscriptions.
- **Price tiers climb fast.** Plans run from $20 to $199 a month but the average sits near the bottom.
- **Audience helps, but does not close.** A 14.6k X following and strong domain rating still yield modest MRR.
- **Sell to the shipped, not only the curious.** Tools for live apps, like Capgo, earn more per customer.
