Most lead tools start with a database of contacts. Traxy starts with behaviour: who liked, commented on or shared posts in your market this hour. Ten months after its January 2026 founding, that idea showed $52,376 in monthly recurring revenue and $169,067 collected in total ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/traxy-ai)). For a company of 2–5 people, that is a fast start in one of software's most crowded categories.

## What does Traxy actually do with LinkedIn engagement?

Traxy turns LinkedIn activity into a lead list. The homepage says the product scans LinkedIn engagement across your market every hour, checks which people fit your target buyer, and routes the new leads into a CRM, an outreach tool or Slack, together with the signal that triggered each lead ([founder-reported, Traxy homepage](https://www.traxy.ai)).

> "Tell traxy who you're after. It finds them, qualifies them and starts the conversation." — Traxy homepage ([founder-reported, Traxy](https://www.traxy.ai))

The pitch is timing rather than volume. A person who just engaged with a post about your category is, in Traxy's framing, a buyer already active in the market. The TrustMRR listing describes the audience as B2B sales teams and the product as "social media listening".

## Why is the average Traxy customer paying more than the Pro plan?

The average Traxy subscription is worth about $214.66 a month, above the listed $149 Pro price ([calc, from TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/traxy-ai)). That figure is MRR divided by 244 active subscriptions. The gap suggests some customers are on the Enterprise tier, which TrustMRR lists as contact-sales pricing, or pay for more than one seat or workspace.

The homepage does not print prices at all. It sends visitors to a 7-day free trial or a demo, and claims more than 1,000 B2B teams use the product ([founder-reported, Traxy homepage](https://www.traxy.ai)). That user count is larger than the 244 paying subscriptions, so it likely includes trial users; the company does not break it down.

Traxy sits next to two other outbound cases on this site, and it is the only one with Stripe-verified numbers. The three products attack the same job, booking sales meetings, from different ends: who to contact, and how to send at scale.

Our observation: Traxy and Gojiberry AI both sell "signals" instead of contact lists, and both landed in the tens of thousands of MRR within roughly a year. Instantly is the delivery layer such tools feed into. Traxy's homepage says leads can be routed to an outreach tool, so in practice it complements a sender like Instantly rather than replacing it.

## What can founders learn from Traxy's first ten months?

- **Sell a fresh signal, not a static list.** Traxy's core promise is hourly scans of engagement, which is harder for buyers to get from a one-off database export ([founder-reported, Traxy homepage](https://www.traxy.ai)).
- **One public price can anchor a higher average.** Traxy lists one $149 plan, yet the average subscription works out to about $214.66.
- **Integrate into existing workflows.** Routing leads to a CRM, outreach tool or Slack lets Traxy sit beside the tools sales teams already pay for.
- **Small teams can move quickly in crowded markets.** A 2–5 person, bootstrapped team reached $52,376 MRR within ten months of founding ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/traxy-ai)).
