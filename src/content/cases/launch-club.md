Eighteen customers. That is the number of active subscriptions behind Launch Club in November 2026, and together they supported $34,348 in monthly recurring revenue ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/launch-club)). Most small SaaS businesses need hundreds or thousands of subscribers to reach that level. Launch Club gets there with a handful of members paying a lot, which makes it look more like a high-ticket club than a typical self-serve tool.

## What does Launch Club teach about Reddit and AI search?

Launch Club sells Reddit marketing as a route into AI search results. The company-written listing on TrustMRR describes a SaaS that helps business owners learn how to promote their products, plus tools and content creation for members ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/launch-club)). The bet is simple: AI assistants often quote Reddit threads, so a brand that shows up in the right threads may show up in AI answers.

> "Reddit marketing to improve AI search." — Launch Club listing ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/launch-club))

The site is launchclub.ai, and TrustMRR files it under Marketing, SaaS and Social Media.

## How much revenue does Launch Club make from 18 subscriptions?

Launch Club had $34,348 MRR in November 2026, $55,928 in revenue over the previous 30 days and $1,330,272 all time ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/launch-club)). TrustMRR checks these numbers through a Stripe API key.

MRR divided by 18 subscriptions is about $1,908 per subscription ([calc, from TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/launch-club)). The last 30 days brought in more than MRR, which suggests one-off payments on top of subscriptions, though the split is not disclosed. Pricing and team size are not disclosed either.

## Where does Ken Savage find Launch Club members?

Ken Savage, the founder of Launch Club, does not rely on a big audience. The listing shows 6,077 followers on X ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/launch-club)), a modest count for a business at this revenue. Instead it lists four paid or direct channels: Meta Ads, cold email, cold DMs and a referral program.

That mix fits a high-ticket offer. When each member is worth well over a thousand dollars a month, paying for ads and sending cold outreach can make sense in a way it rarely does for a cheap tool. The tech stack is listed as JavaScript on the front end and Stripe behind it.

## How does Launch Club compare with AEO Engine and Rank Prompt?

All three sell visibility in AI search, but they package it differently.

Our observation: Launch Club sits between an agency and a tool. Like AEO Engine it charges high prices to few customers, but it teaches members to do the work rather than doing it for them. Rank Prompt measures visibility; Launch Club focuses on one channel, Reddit, that feeds it.

## What lessons does Launch Club offer high-ticket founders?

- **Few customers can be enough.** Eighteen subscriptions carried $34,348 MRR in November 2026.
- **Price decides the channel.** At roughly $1,908 per subscription, Meta Ads and cold outreach become affordable.
- **Ride a new search habit.** Framing Reddit work as AI search work ties an old tactic to a new demand.
- **Concentration cuts both ways.** With 18 accounts, losing a few members would move MRR sharply.
