A football coach can draw the perfect play and still lose the game because one receiver ran the wrong route. Play Designer Pro sells a fix for that gap between the whiteboard and the field. The tool, founded in the United Kingdom in June 2025 by Aaron Butler, had 244 active subscriptions and $5,458 in MRR by November 2026 ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/play-designer-pro)). A British founder selling American football software is the first surprise; a quiz engine as the core feature is the second.

## How does Play Designer Pro turn a playbook into quizzes?

Play Designer Pro lets an offensive coach design plays and then automatically builds quizzes from them. Players answer the quizzes to learn the playbook, and the coach can see who understands which plays ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/play-designer-pro)).

> "Helps teach and track player understanding of playbooks, which most tools don’t do." — Play Designer Pro on TrustMRR ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/play-designer-pro))

That second half is the pitch. Plenty of tools let coaches draw plays. The listing positions tracking player understanding as the part competitors skip.

## Why is Play Designer Pro's revenue seasonal?

Football has a season, and a coaching tool follows it. The last-30-day revenue of $4,299 sits below the $5,458 MRR in November 2026 ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/play-designer-pro)), which can reflect the timing of renewals rather than a drop in customers. TrustMRR does not give a monthly history, so we cannot confirm a seasonal curve.

The pricing field lists only a 14-day free trial. A trial long enough to cover a couple of practice weeks lets a coach test the quizzes on a real roster before paying.

Our observation: Chessigma and Verbi sell to the learner directly. Play Designer Pro sells to the coach, who then brings a whole roster into the product. One buyer, many users, is a strong shape for a niche tool, and it fits a sport where the coach controls what the team uses.

## What can niche tool builders learn from Play Designer Pro?

- **Find the step competitors skip.** Drawing plays is common; checking that players learned them is the hook.
- **Sell to the person who controls the team.** One coach subscription reaches a full roster of players.
- **Low price, many accounts.** About $22 per subscription across 244 accounts still adds up to $5,458 MRR ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/play-designer-pro)).
- **Geography does not limit the niche.** A UK founder built a business on American football coaching.
