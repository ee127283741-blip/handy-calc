Juggling multiple banking apps just to know how much money is left this month is the problem Pluma was built to remove. The Brazilian startup, founded in August 2025, sells one consolidated screen instead. By November 2026 it had 208 active subscriptions and $14,771 in lifetime revenue ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/pluma)), with plans priced in Brazilian reais.

## What does the Pluma app do with bank data?

Pluma connects to a user's banks and cards, categorises each transaction with AI, tracks subscriptions and credit cards, and shows how much is left to spend in a consolidated overview ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/pluma)). It is aimed at individuals, families and small business owners who want money management with little effort.

> "Connect your banks and cards in minutes." — Pluma company statement ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/pluma))

The listing says Pluma is made in Brazil and compliant with LGPD, the country's data protection law.

## Why is Pluma priced in reais?

Pluma prices its plans in Brazilian reais, from R$ 14,16 to R$ 63,05 a month ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/pluma)), which fits an app built in Brazil for Brazilian bank users. Local pricing keeps the app affordable, but it also means many subscribers are needed for meaningful revenue. The company describes itself as profitable and growing; TrustMRR does not publish its costs, so that claim is founder-reported.

## How does Pluma differ from BreadSync and AltIndex?

Pluma, [BreadSync](/cases/breadsync/) and [AltIndex](/cases/altindex/) are all finance tools with Stripe-verified revenue, but Pluma is the only one managing everyday household money.

Our observation: BreadSync and AltIndex charge for an edge in betting or investing, while Pluma charges for convenience. Pluma's low revenue per subscriber, about $6 a month, reflects that positioning.

## What takeaways does Pluma offer fintech founders outside the US?

- **Local pricing fits local markets.** Plans in reais keep the app within reach of Brazilian users.
- **Compliance is part of the pitch.** Stating LGPD compliance answers the first trust question about bank data.
- **Low prices need volume.** 208 subscriptions add up to only $1,308 MRR.
- **Separate claims from verified data.** "Profitable" is founder-reported; the revenue is Stripe-verified.
