Jim Løining did not build BambooVPN from scratch. He bought it, rebuilt it, and says he now spends less than 2 hours a week on it. By November 2026 the mobile VPN app had $9,817 MRR, 1,493 active subscriptions and $178,915 in lifetime revenue ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/bamboovpn)). It is a case study in buying an app instead of launching one.

## What does BambooVPN promise users?

BambooVPN promises global servers, strong privacy and zero logs ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/bamboovpn)). The listing pitches it against VPNs that throttle and snoop, and calls it fast, private and drama-free. It is a consumer app with about 500,000 users, according to the company, and billing runs through Superwall.

## Why is the BambooVPN owner selling?

He says he lacks time, not faith in the app. In his message, growth has been consistent and 100% organic, and macOS, Windows and Chrome extension versions are built but not yet released ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/bamboovpn)).

> "Selling because I'm out of time, not out of belief, too many other projects." — Jim Løining ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/bamboovpn))

He also lists untouched levers: email marketing, paid ads and upsells, none of which he uses today. For a buyer, that list is the growth plan: each lever adds revenue to a product that already sells without marketing spend.

Our observation: FaceKit and Nutree depend on new content or features to stay fresh. A VPN is a utility people keep paying for while they need it, which is how BambooVPN can grow while its owner works on it a couple of hours a week.

## What can app buyers learn from BambooVPN?

- **Buying beats building for speed.** An acquired, rebuilt app reached $178,915 in revenue.
- **Utilities need little upkeep.** Under 2 hours a week is the owner's own estimate.
- **Check which stores are connected.** The verified figures exclude the 20% from the Play Store.
- **Unused channels are upside.** Email, ads and upsells are all untried.
