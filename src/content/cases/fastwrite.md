Students already write their papers in Microsoft Word, so fastwrite put its AI there instead of asking them to open another tab. Eighteen months after its May 2025 founding, the German startup showed $12,278 in monthly recurring revenue and $212,825 in all-time revenue ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/fastwrite-io)). Its growth runs through student-heavy channels like TikTok and Instagram.

## Why does fastwrite live inside Microsoft Word?

fastwrite lives inside the document. The company-written listing describes AI-powered writing tools for students and leads with one promise ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/fastwrite-io)):

> "Make Academic Writing Simple - Directly in Microsoft Word." — fastwrite listing ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/fastwrite-io))

The target users are students at leading universities, and the listing claims about 30,000 users. Working in Word means citations, formatting and drafts stay in the file a student hands in, instead of being pasted back and forth from a chat window.

All-time revenue is more than 17 times current MRR, even though the company is only 18 months old. That points to a higher peak earlier, possibly tied to the rhythm of university terms. Monthly history and profit are not disclosed.

## How does fastwrite reach students?

The listing names five channels: TikTok, Instagram, YouTube, Reddit and email marketing ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/fastwrite-io)). Four of the five are social platforms where students spend time, and none is paid search. The team is 2-5 people based in Germany.

The tech stack is unusual for a web SaaS: Swift on the front end, with Appwrite, Stripe, Google Cloud, Vercel and C# behind it. C# is a common choice for Office add-ins, which matches the Word-first product.

## How does fastwrite compare with Jenni AI and Textero?

fastwrite sells to the same students as [Jenni AI](/cases/jenni-ai/) and [Textero](/cases/textero/), two AI academic writing tools covered on this site. Both of those run as web editors; fastwrite runs inside Word.

Our observation: the Word integration is fastwrite's main difference, and its euro pricing and German base suggest a European student market first. Jenni AI is the larger, older brand in the category. For a small team, choosing a different surface rather than a different feature list is a cheap way to stand apart.

## What are the takeaways from fastwrite's Word-first approach?

- **Go where the work already happens.** fastwrite builds into Microsoft Word instead of asking students to switch tools.
- **Student revenue is seasonal.** All-time revenue of $212,825 against $12,278 MRR suggests earlier peaks ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/fastwrite-io)).
- **Social channels fit a young audience.** TikTok, Instagram, YouTube and Reddit carry the marketing.
- **A small team can ship an add-in.** Two to five people built and sell the product.
