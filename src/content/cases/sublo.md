A €2.89 subscription does not sound like a business. Multiply it by 362 active subscribers and Sublo reached $1,506 in MRR by November 2026 ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/sublo)). The German Chrome extension shows two subtitle tracks at once, so a viewer can watch a show in one language while reading another, and it got there without spending on marketing.

## What does Sublo do for language learners?

Sublo translates subtitles and shows dual subtitles in all languages, with the listing citing 50+ languages for streaming services ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/sublo)). Its audience is language learners, film enthusiasts and ESL teachers, all consumers rather than businesses.

> "Watch with subtitles in any language." — Sublo company description on TrustMRR ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/sublo))

The product is a browser extension, so it works on top of streaming sites instead of competing with them. The stack listed is Supabase and Stripe.

## How did Sublo grow with zero marketing?

Sublo grew through distribution it did not pay for. The company says it launched in April, has done zero marketing so far, and got all users through the Chrome Web Store and SEO ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/sublo)).

That matters for a €2.89 product. At that price, paid ads would struggle to earn back their cost, so free channels are not just convenient but necessary. People search the Chrome Web Store for exactly this kind of tool, which puts Sublo where the intent already is.

## What lessons does Sublo hold for extension builders?

- **App stores are a channel.** All of Sublo's users came from the Chrome Web Store and SEO, with zero marketing.
- **Sit on top of big platforms.** An extension can serve streaming viewers without licensing any content.
- **Cheap plans need volume.** 362 subscriptions add up to only $1,506 MRR ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/sublo)).
- **A free tier feeds the paid one.** The free-forever plan lets users try dual subtitles before paying €2.89.
