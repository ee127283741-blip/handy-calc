Character chat apps are usually discussed in billions of messages and huge funding rounds. Embraces.AI is a much smaller bet on the same habit. Founded in May 2026, it showed $10,030 in monthly recurring revenue and $44,316 in all-time revenue by November 2026 ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/embraces-ai)). That is fast for a bootstrapped team of two to five people.

## What is the Embraces.AI companion app?

Embraces.AI is an AI companion that users text like a friend. The company-written TrustMRR listing says it offers realistic companions and lets users chase romantic storylines with characters from their favourite games and shows, for an audience of roleplayers and romantasy fans ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/embraces-ai)).

> "A companion you text like your best friend." — Embraces.AI listing ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/embraces-ai))

The listing marks it as a business-to-consumer product with about 6,000 users. Its domain rating is 1 out of 100, so search traffic is not yet a meaningful channel.

## How fast did Embraces.AI reach $10K MRR?

Embraces.AI had $10,030 in MRR and 410 active subscriptions in November 2026, with $44,316 in all-time revenue ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/embraces-ai)). TrustMRR checks these numbers through a Stripe API key. With a May 2026 founding date, the business reached that level in about six months.

| Tier | Price | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Crush | $12 | [founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/embraces-ai) |
| Partner | $25 | [founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/embraces-ai) |
| Soulmate | $50 | [founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/embraces-ai) |

MRR divided by subscriptions is about $24.46 per subscription ([calc, from TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/embraces-ai)), close to the middle Partner tier. About 410 of roughly 6,000 users pay, around one in fifteen. The billing period of each tier and profit are not disclosed.

## Who builds Embraces.AI and on what stack?

The founder is not disclosed; the TrustMRR listing shows a dash in the founder field and a team of 2–5 people ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/embraces-ai)). The company is bootstrapped and based in Singapore.

The tech stack listed is JavaScript on the front end, Python and PostgreSQL behind it, hosting on Google Cloud managed with Terraform, and Stripe for billing. No marketing channels are listed, so how Embraces.AI found its first 6,000 users is not disclosed.

## How does Embraces.AI compare with Character.AI and DataFast?

Embraces.AI sits in the same category as Character.AI but at a tiny fraction of its scale, and its revenue is closer to a solo indie tool such as DataFast.

Our observation: Character.AI built a broad, free-first platform. Embraces.AI narrows the audience to romance roleplay and charges from the start, with its top Soulmate tier. A narrow niche with high willingness to pay can produce revenue quickly without a large user base.

## What takeaways does Embraces.AI offer AI companion builders?

- **Niche beats breadth at the start.** Embraces.AI targets roleplayers and romantasy fans rather than everyone.
- **Tiered intimacy is a pricing ladder.** Crush, Partner and Soulmate tiers name the relationship and raise the price.
- **Speed is possible without SEO.** A domain rating of 1 did not stop the app from reaching $10,030 in MRR within about six months.
- **Anonymous teams limit trust signals.** With the founder not disclosed, verified revenue is the main public proof.
