Screen Movie shows why MRR can be the wrong number to judge an indie app. The Hungarian macOS screen recorder, launched in August 2026, reports just $18 in MRR, yet it had collected $7,938 by November 2026 ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/screen-movie)). Almost all of that came from one-time lifetime licences, sold by a founder with a large audience on X.

## What can you make with the Screen Movie app?

Screen Movie records the screen, captures screenshots, edits multiple scenes, animates text, images and captions, adds music and exports product videos in minutes ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/screen-movie)). It targets macOS users who want polished screencasts.

> "Create cinematic-looking screen recordings and videos with ease." — Screen Movie listing on TrustMRR ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/screen-movie))

The front end is built with SwiftUI, the website with Framer, and Polar handles payments. Team size is not disclosed. The listing places it in utilities, productivity and design tools.

## Why does Screen Movie sell a one-time licence?

Mac utility buyers are used to paying once, and a single $39 price ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/screen-movie)) removes the subscription objection. The cost is that revenue resets every month: each new sale must come from fresh attention, which is where founder Solt Wagner's X following, listed at 25.9k, becomes the main channel.

Our observation: subscription peers look far bigger on MRR, but Screen Movie's lifetime model is a deliberate choice. Its revenue should be judged by monthly sales, not recurring revenue.

## What should indie Mac developers learn from Screen Movie?

- **Lifetime pricing hides traction from MRR.** All-time and 30-day revenue tell the real story.
- **An audience replaces a marketing budget.** A large X following can sell a paid Mac app from day one.
- **A clear output sells the tool.** "Product videos in minutes" is a concrete promise.
- **One-time sales need constant launches.** Without recurring revenue, every month starts near zero.
