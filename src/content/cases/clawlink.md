AI agents are only as useful as the apps they can reach, and wiring an agent into Gmail or Slack still means OAuth screens, API keys and token refreshes. ClawLink, a one-person company in Sri Lanka, sells that wiring for $6 a month. By November 2026 it had 261 paying subscriptions and $8,522 in lifetime revenue ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/clawlink)). It is a small, clean example of a business built on top of someone else's agent platform.

## What does ClawLink connect OpenClaw agents to?

ClawLink connects OpenClaw and Hermes Agent to Gmail, Calendar, Slack, Notion, GitHub, Shopify and more than 100 other apps through one plugin. The user installs it, pairs an account and lets the agent use those apps from chat ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/clawlink)).

> "No OAuth setup. No API keys. No custom MCP config." — ClawLink company description on TrustMRR ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/clawlink))

The listing's problem field goes further and mentions one-click connections to over 1,100 apps, while the description says 100+. We report both as the company states them. The product is open source and the front end runs on Next.js.

## Why does a free open-source plugin still get paid users?

ClawLink is open source, yet 261 people pay for it ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/clawlink)). The value is the hosted part: someone has to register OAuth apps, store credentials and refresh tokens. Self-hosting is possible, but a $6 subscription is cheaper than an afternoon spent on OAuth consoles.

The company reports about 14,214 users, which puts paid conversion near 2% (calc: 261 / 14,214). That is a typical open-source funnel, where a wide free base feeds a small paying layer.

ClawLink's model is volume on a platform wave; its biggest risk is that OpenClaw or Hermes ships the same integrations natively.

## What are the takeaways from ClawLink's first year?

- **Build where a new platform has gaps.** ClawLink fills the integration gap in OpenClaw and Hermes Agent.
- **Hosting is the paid layer of open source.** Credentials and token refreshes are what 261 subscribers pay $6 a month to avoid ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/clawlink)).
- **Low prices need wide funnels.** About 14,214 users support a small paid base.
- **Platform risk is real.** A business built on another product's gaps can shrink if those gaps close.
