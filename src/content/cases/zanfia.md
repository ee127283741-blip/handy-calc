Zanfia is a five-year-old business that most English-speaking founders have never heard of. The Polish platform lets creators sell courses and memberships under their own brand, and in November 2026 it showed $10,178 in monthly recurring revenue from just 208 subscriptions ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/zanfia)). Its all-time revenue had passed a quarter of a million dollars.

## What does Zanfia give creators?

Zanfia gives creators one place to build, sell and run a community. The company-written listing describes it as AI-first and aimed at online creators, course authors, newsletter writers and community builders ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/zanfia)).

> "The AI-first sales and community platform for creators. Build, sell, and grow under your own brand." — Zanfia listing ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/zanfia))

The phrase "under your own brand" is the pitch. Marketplaces take a cut and own the customer relationship; a white-label platform lets the creator keep both.

## How does Zanfia earn $10K a month from 208 customers?

Zanfia had $10,178 MRR and 208 active subscriptions in November 2026, with $9,821 collected in the last 30 days and $273,273 all time ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/zanfia)). The figures are verified through a Stripe API key.

## How long did it take Zanfia to get here?

Zanfia was founded in May 2021 in Poland and is run by a team of 2-5 people ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/zanfia)). That is a slow, steady build, not a launch-week spike. Marketing channels are not listed, and the founder's X account has about 2,000 followers, which is modest.

The only stack item named is Stripe. Creator platforms live or die on payments, so Stripe handling checkout for both Zanfia and its creators' customers is the core of the product.

## How does Zanfia compare with Postiz and Plutio?

Zanfia sells to creators, like [Postiz](/cases/postiz/), which schedules social posts, and it is an all-in-one business tool, like [Plutio](/cases/plutio/), which bundles projects, invoices and client portals for freelancers.

Our observation: all three sell "one tool instead of five" to independent operators. Zanfia's difference is that its customers make money through it, selling courses and memberships, so the platform is tied to the creator's income rather than to their admin. That kind of product tends to be hard to leave once a creator's students and subscribers live inside it.

## What are the lessons from Zanfia's five-year build?

- **Slow can be fine.** Five years after founding, Zanfia showed $273,273 in all-time revenue ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/zanfia)).
- **Few customers, higher plans.** About $49 per subscription against a $29 entry price shows upgrades carry the revenue.
- **Own-brand is a selling point.** Creators pay to keep their customer relationship off marketplaces.
- **Local markets can launch global tools.** A small Polish team built a product with an English-language pitch.
