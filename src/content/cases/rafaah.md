Mohammad Hassan has 52.7K followers on X, yet his wealth tracking app RAFAAH Wealth had just 15 paying subscriptions in November 2026 ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/rafaah)). That contrast is the story. A large audience can open the door, but it does not automatically turn followers into subscribers for a finance product priced in dirhams. The company still brought in $7,382 all time, every dollar verified through Stripe.

## What does the RAFAAH Wealth app track for investors?

RAFAAH Wealth tracks an investor's total wealth in one app. The listing names the problem as jumping between different apps to see total wealth, and the audience as investors in the GCC and global markets who have outgrown spreadsheets ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/rafaah)).

> "We build tools to help companies and individuals manage their assets and make better financial decisions." — RAFAAH Wealth company description on TrustMRR ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/rafaah))

The company is based in the United Arab Emirates and was founded in May 2025. It describes itself as bootstrapped, with a team of 2 to 5 people.

## How much revenue does RAFAAH Wealth earn from 15 subscribers?

RAFAAH Wealth had $1,337 in MRR in November 2026 from 15 active subscriptions, with $1,496 collected in the last 30 days and $7,382 all time ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/rafaah)).

## Why does RAFAAH Wealth price in dirhams?

RAFAAH Wealth prices its plans at 99 AED and 299 AED a month, billed monthly or yearly ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/rafaah)). Pricing in the local currency matches the stated audience in the GCC, where the founder also markets through X, YouTube and Instagram.

The tech stack is small: JavaScript and Next.js on the front end, PostgreSQL and Stripe on the back end. The listing adds a mission line about AI-powered financial freedom, but it does not describe which AI features the app has, so we do not list any.

Clarity products tend to be stickier but harder to sell, which may explain why a 52.7K-follower founder has 15 subscribers rather than hundreds.

## What are the takeaways from RAFAAH Wealth so far?

- **An audience is a channel, not a conversion rate.** 52.7K followers on X produced 15 verified subscriptions by November 2026 ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/rafaah)).
- **Local currency signals a local focus.** AED pricing tells GCC investors the product is built for them.
- **Yearly billing smooths early cash.** Last-30-day revenue above MRR fits the monthly-or-yearly model.
- **A small stack is enough to start.** Next.js, PostgreSQL and Stripe carried the product to $7,382 all time.
