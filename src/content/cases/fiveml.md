LinkedIn outreach tools are usually sold by teams with sales staff and support desks. FiveML is run by one person in Cyprus. In November 2026 it had 23 active subscriptions, $5,182 in monthly recurring revenue and $108,635 collected since April 2025 ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/fiveml-ltd)). Each customer pays a premium price, which is how a tiny customer list adds up.

## What does FiveML automate on LinkedIn?

FiveML automates the repetitive steps of LinkedIn prospecting. The company-written TrustMRR listing says it visits profiles, sends connection requests and runs personalized follow-ups to generate leads ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/fiveml-ltd)). The company is listed as FiveML LTD, with the website at fivemedialab.com.

> "a LinkedIn automation platform that helps businesses generate leads by automating profile visits" — FiveML listing ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/fiveml-ltd))

TrustMRR tags it Sales, SaaS and Marketing.

## How much revenue does FiveML make at $149.99 a seat?

FiveML had $5,182 MRR in November 2026, $5,849 in revenue over the last 30 days and $108,635 all time ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/fiveml-ltd)). Pricing is $149.99 per user a month, with discounts for extra users and prepayments.

## How does LN Santoro sell FiveML alone?

LN Santoro, the founder of FiveML, lists two marketing channels: LinkedIn and cold email ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/fiveml-ltd)). Using LinkedIn to sell a LinkedIn tool is a neat loop, since every campaign doubles as a demo. The team is one person, the stack shown is Stripe, and the founder says the business is for sale so he can focus on other projects. No asking price is shown.

## How does FiveML compare with WaLead AI and Gojiberry AI?

All three sell LinkedIn prospecting, but at very different scales.

| Company | Approach | Monthly figure | Source |

| [Gojiberry AI](/cases/gojiberry-ai/) | LinkedIn buying signals | $27K MRR (Dec 2025) | [founder-reported, Indie Hackers](https://www.indiehackers.com/post/tech/validating-via-services-before-building-a-27k-mrr-product-7FrT6FXChPOiUK4PbXFp) |

Our observation: Gojiberry AI sells signals about who to contact, and WaLead AI sells a broader prospecting workflow. FiveML sells the plain automation layer at a high per-seat price. That works with a few loyal accounts, but it is the most exposed to LinkedIn's own limits on automation.

## What takeaways does FiveML offer solo sales-tool founders?

- **Premium seats keep a solo business viable.** At $149.99 per user, 23 subscriptions carried $5,182 MRR.
- **Sell where the product works.** LinkedIn outreach for a LinkedIn tool shows the product in action.
- **Check the all-time ratio.** $108,635 lifetime against current MRR hints at a stronger past.
- **Platform risk is real.** A tool that automates someone else's network depends on that network's rules.
