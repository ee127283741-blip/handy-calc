Move abroad, switch SIM cards, and your bank's verification code goes to a phone number you no longer have. Tether Mobile sells the fix: a number that keeps receiving texts and forwards them to email or chat. The Canadian one-person company had $2,340 in MRR in November 2026, while its all-time revenue was only $3,189 ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/tether-mobile)). That ratio says most of its revenue arrived very recently.

## What problem does Tether Mobile solve for travellers?

Tether Mobile keeps SMS messages, including two-factor codes from home, reachable after someone moves or changes SIM. The listing names digital nomads, travellers, expats and teams as the audience, and also privacy-focused users ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/tether-mobile)).

> "A phone number that forwards its texts to your email or messaging apps." — Tether Mobile value proposition ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/tether-mobile))

A key detail: its US numbers are real mobile numbers, not VoIP, for services that reject VoIP numbers. Users can also port in an existing US number for free.

## Who pays Tether Mobile and through which plans?

Pricing starts at $10 a month per number in Canada and $15 a month in the US, UK and the Netherlands ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/tether-mobile)). Business plans add Slack, Microsoft Teams, webhooks, Zapier, Make, n8n and MCP, which lets teams route codes into shared tools or AI agents. The listed marketing channels are broad: content marketing, SEO, Instagram, Facebook, Reddit, X, events, word of mouth and a referral program. The stack is Laravel, Cloudflare and Stripe.

## What lessons does Tether Mobile hold for solo SaaS builders?

- **Painful edge cases make good products.** Losing 2FA codes abroad is rare but urgent.
- **Technical details are the moat.** Real mobile numbers, not VoIP, solve rejections that cheaper options cannot.
- **Per-unit pricing is easy to understand.** One number, one monthly fee.
- **Integrations open a business tier.** Webhooks, Zapier and MCP turn a consumer tool into team infrastructure.
