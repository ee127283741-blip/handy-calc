Most faceless video tools sell software and leave the work to the creator. White Glove Content sells the finished video. Founded in April 2026, it reached $61,394 in monthly recurring revenue and 491 active subscriptions by November 2026 ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/white-glove-content)). That is one of the fastest climbs we have recorded for a service business, and the owners had already put it up for sale.

## What does White Glove Content actually deliver to subscribers?

White Glove Content delivers ready-to-upload faceless videos for YouTube, TikTok, Instagram Reels, Facebook and X. The company-written listing says each subscription covers scripting, AI voiceover, editing and thumbnails, with an optional Auto Uploader that publishes to accounts the client connects ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/white-glove-content)). Clients pay monthly or yearly, there are no contracts, and clients keep their ad revenue.

The homepage adds SEO titles, tags and descriptions and a channel-unique AI narrator voice, and says the team works across more than 100 niches ([founder-reported, White Glove Content homepage](https://www.whiteglovecontent.com/)).

> "We make it. We post it. You earn." — White Glove Content homepage ([founder-reported, White Glove Content](https://www.whiteglovecontent.com/))

## How fast did White Glove Content revenue grow after launch?

Very fast, then it levelled off. Verified monthly revenue went from $2,142 in April 2026 to $68,310 in September and $68,372 in October ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/white-glove-content)). In the 30 days before the November snapshot it collected $59,835, and TrustMRR showed revenue down 20.7% and MRR down 14.2% over that window.

## Why is White Glove Content listed for sale at $2.5 million?

The listing does not give a reason. It shows White Glove Content listed for sale since September 2026 at an asking price of $2,500,000, a 3.5x revenue multiple, with 21 acquisition offers received ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/white-glove-content)). The team size is given as two to five people, and the founders are not disclosed on the page.

The timing is worth noting for a buyer. The sale listing went up in the same month revenue peaked, and the 30-day trend since then has been negative. Recurring video work also depends on people doing the editing, so a buyer would be taking on an operation, not just code.

That makes its margin and its growth ceiling depend more on how efficiently a small team can produce videos. Another faceless video business, Vid.AI, was also listed for sale on TrustMRR in the same period ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/vidai-llc)).

## What lessons does White Glove Content hold for AI service founders?

- **Sell the outcome, not the tool.** White Glove Content charges for finished videos, so customers who do not want to learn a generator still buy.
- **Productised tiers make a service feel like SaaS.** Fixed plans from $54 to $284 a month replace custom quotes ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/white-glove-content)).
- **Speed can come from a hot niche.** Going from $2,142 to over $68,000 a month in five months shows how much demand faceless content had in 2026.
- **Watch the trend, not only the peak.** A 20.7% drop in 30-day revenue is a signal any buyer at a 3.5x multiple will study.
