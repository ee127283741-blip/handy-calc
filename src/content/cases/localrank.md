Local SEO is the work of getting a plumber or a dentist into the map results for their city. LocalRank turns that agency service into software, and now adds tracking of how AI assistants recommend local businesses. In November 2026 it showed $18,841 in MRR and $742,937 in all-time revenue, less than two years after its February 2025 founding ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/localrank-so)).

## What problem does LocalRank solve for local SEO agencies?

LocalRank helps agencies rank clients higher on Google without hiring more staff, according to its TrustMRR listing ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/localrank-so)). The website lists the tools that replace manual work: a local rank tracker across a city grid, a citations builder that keeps name, address and phone consistent across directories, Google Business Profile post scheduling and review management, and a leads database ([founder-reported, LocalRank](https://localrank.so)).

> "All-in-one AI Local SEO Software." — LocalRank, company description ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/localrank-so))

The newer features target AI search. An AI recommendation monitor tracks how ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and Perplexity recommend a business, and an LLM citation builder places businesses in "best X in city" roundups.

## How did Jacky Chou grow LocalRank through education?

LocalRank's founder on TrustMRR is Jacky Chou, listed under his Indexsy handle. On an October 2025 episode of In the Pit with Cody Schneider, the description says the talk covered how LocalRank grows by educating and productizing services, plus a chapter on community-led growth and seeding new agencies ([founder-reported, In the Pit](https://share.transistor.fm/s/341f46a6)). Team size and funding are not disclosed.

That route fits the buyer. Agencies first want to learn a repeatable local SEO offer, then buy the tool that runs it.

## How much revenue does LocalRank make in 2026?

LocalRank made $18,841 in MRR and $14,551 over the last 30 days in November 2026, from 186 active subscriptions ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/localrank-so)). All-time revenue was $742,937.

All-time revenue is about 39 times current MRR ([calc, from TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/localrank-so)), although the company is only 21 months old. That suggests revenue was once higher, or that large one-time payments such as annual plans or academy sales came in earlier. The listing does not explain it. The site's claim of 500+ paying agencies is also well above the 186 active subscriptions.

## How does LocalRank compare with ChatSEO and SiteGPT?

[ChatSEO](/cases/chatseo/) sells SEO advice through a chatbot, and [SiteGPT](/cases/sitegpt/) builds chatbots trained on a site's content. LocalRank sells neither advice nor chat: it runs the repetitive local SEO tasks an agency bills clients for.

Our reading: LocalRank's buyer resells its output, so a $297 plan becomes part of a much larger client retainer. That makes high tiers easier to sell than they would be to an end business. The AI-search features are a hedge, in case map rankings lose traffic to assistants.

## What takeaways does LocalRank offer SaaS founders?

- **Productize a service you already sell.** LocalRank turns agency tasks into software ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/localrank-so)).
- **Teach first, sell second.** Education and a community feed agencies into the tool.
- **Price for resellers.** Agencies pay up to $2,997 a month because the cost is passed on.
- **Track the next search surface.** LocalRank now monitors AI assistant recommendations.
