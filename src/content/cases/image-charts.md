Email clients cannot run JavaScript, so a live chart in a newsletter or PDF has to arrive as a picture. Image-Charts has sold that picture since January 2017. By November 2026 it had earned $544,024 all time and had $6,278 in monthly recurring revenue from 50 active subscriptions ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/image-charts)). It is a long-running, quiet developer API that started before the current AI wave.

## How does the Image-Charts API turn a URL into a chart?

Image-Charts returns an image for a URL. The homepage says your first API call is a URL with no sign-up needed: paste it into a browser, an img tag or an email, and the chart comes back as an image ([founder-reported, Image-Charts homepage](https://www.image-charts.com/)). It supports bar, line, pie, radar, bubble, sparkline and Chart.js charts, plus QR codes, and connects to Zapier and Make.

> "Instantly create beautiful charts." — Image-Charts homepage ([founder-reported, Image-Charts](https://www.image-charts.com/))

The listing targets marketers, business owners, developers and "low-code lovers", and the site carries a guide on migrating from Google Image Charts, the older Google API that worked the same way.

## Why is Image-Charts revenue so uneven month to month?

Because Image-Charts sells yearly plans. The listing gives Business at $89 a month and Enterprise at $224 a month, both paid yearly ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/image-charts)). Annual renewals land in single months, so verified revenue jumps to $18,673 in January 2026 and $17,007 in April, then drops near $2,300 in quieter months ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/image-charts)).

## What has kept Image-Charts selling for almost ten years?

A narrow job that does not go away. The listing frames the problem as skipping "the hours of trying to find a way to embed charts across apps and browsers" and the complexity of a server-side setup ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/image-charts)). Reports, emails and chatbots still need static images, whatever charting library is in fashion.

Francois-Guillaume Ribreau, the founder of Image-Charts, had 5,036 followers on X. The company is bootstrapped and based in France, and its homepage leans on integrations with Google Sheets, Google Forms, Pipedrive and Dropbox through Zapier and Make rather than on a founder audience.

Image-Charts is often wired into a report or email once and left running, which keeps churn low but also keeps growth slow. Its higher price per account partly makes up for a smaller customer count.

## What lessons can API founders learn from Image-Charts?

- **A URL is the simplest API.** Image-Charts needs no SDK or sign-up for the first call, which lowers the barrier to try it ([founder-reported, Image-Charts homepage](https://www.image-charts.com/)).
- **Ride a gap left by a big company.** A migration guide from Google Image Charts targets users of an older Google service.
- **Annual billing smooths cash, not charts.** Yearly plans bring $17,000+ months followed by quiet ones, so read MRR, not monthly revenue.
- **Niche tools can last.** Nearly ten years and $544,024 all time show the durability of a single, focused job.
