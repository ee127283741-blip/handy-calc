Anyone who has collected 40 invoices or CVs from Gmail knows the routine: open, download, rename, upload, repeat. Save Bulk Gmail Attachments turns that into one action. The Czech utility, founded in May 2025, had $6,469 in lifetime revenue and $725 in MRR by November 2026 ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/save-bulk-gmail-attachments)). It is about as small and specific as a paid product gets.

## What does Save Bulk Gmail Attachments automate?

Save Bulk Gmail Attachments saves attachments from many Gmail emails at once, either to Google Drive or a ZIP file ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/save-bulk-gmail-attachments)). The target users are bookkeepers, accountants, recruiters and ops staff, who deal with attachments in volume.

> "Save every Gmail attachment to the right drive folder — one click." — Save Bulk Gmail Attachments value proposition ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/save-bulk-gmail-attachments))

The listing also says the tool is Google CASA Tier 2 security verified, which matters for any app that asks for Gmail access.

## Why does a Google security review matter for Save Bulk Gmail Attachments?

Gmail access is the whole product, and Google gates that access. Apps that read user mail need to pass Google's security assessment, and the listing highlights the CASA Tier 2 verification as a trust signal ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/save-bulk-gmail-attachments)).

For a small tool, that review is also a moat. A copycat would need to go through the same process before it can ask business users for inbox access.

## What can extension makers learn from Save Bulk Gmail Attachments?

- **Boring chores are products.** Downloading attachments one by one is a real, paid pain.
- **Name the product after the search.** The name is exactly what users type into Google.
- **Platform approval builds trust.** CASA Tier 2 verification answers the security question up front.
- **Low prices need many users.** At about $5 per subscription, scale is the only route to bigger revenue.
