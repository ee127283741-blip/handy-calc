Anyone who has tried to rent a villa in Bali for months rather than nights knows the routine: scroll Facebook groups, message agents, repeat. Bali Villa Hub built a business out of replacing that search. By November 2026 it showed $6,101 in MRR, 99 active subscriptions and $57,126 in all-time revenue ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/bali-villa-hub)), about 17 months after a June 2025 founding.

## What does Bali Villa Hub do for long-term renters?

Bali Villa Hub aggregates long-term villa listings in one place. The company-written listing says it compares 3,000+ Bali villa rentals from 100+ verified agencies, agents and owners, for a B2C audience ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/bali-villa-hub)). Its categories are real estate, travel and marketplace.

> "Tired of searching through Facebook groups?" — Bali Villa Hub listing ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/bali-villa-hub))

That question names the incumbent precisely: not another platform, but informal social groups.

## Who pays Bali Villa Hub if bookings are free?

The listing advertises $0 booking fees ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/bali-villa-hub)), yet Stripe shows 99 active subscriptions. The listing does not say who holds them. The 100+ agencies, agents and owners are the natural paying side of a free-to-renter marketplace, but that is our reading, not a disclosed fact. The founder, team size and marketing channels are not disclosed either; the listing says only that it has been featured in multiple publications.

## How does Bali Villa Hub compare with Interior AI and Virtual Staging AI?

All three sell into property, but at different points.

| Company | Where it sits in property |
|---|---|
| Bali Villa Hub | Finding a long-term rental |
| [Interior AI](/cases/interior-ai/) | Redesigning a room from a photo |
| [Virtual Staging AI](/cases/virtual-staging-ai/) | Staging listing photos for agents |

Our observation: the AI tools sell software to people who already have a property. Bali Villa Hub sells inventory, which is slower to build but harder to copy. Its moat is the 100+ supplier relationships, not code.

## What lessons does Bali Villa Hub offer marketplace founders?

- **Pick one city and one rental length.** Long-term Bali villas is narrow enough to own.
- **Name the real competitor.** For renters it is Facebook groups, not another startup.
- **Free for one side can still mean MRR.** $0 booking fees sit beside 99 active subscriptions.
- **Inventory is the moat.** 3,000+ listings from 100+ suppliers take time to gather.
