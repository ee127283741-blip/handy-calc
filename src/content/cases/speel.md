Speel's dashboard tells two stories at once. In November 2026 the AI ad-video tool showed $64,958 in MRR from 1,086 subscriptions, and only $2,826 in revenue over the previous 30 days ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/speel-co)). Both numbers come from the same Stripe connection. Understanding why they differ is more useful than either one alone.

## What does Speel generate for advertisers?

Speel generates user-generated-content style videos and images, the casual, phone-shot look of a real customer talking to camera, for ads and social posts. The company's listing puts it in one line.

> "Use AI to generate realistic UGC-style videos and images for ads and socials." — Speel listing ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/speel-co))

UGC ads are popular because they look less like ads. Hiring real creators costs time and money per video, and Speel replaces that with AI actors. The listing tags Speel as B2B and claims about 100,000 users ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/speel-co)). We could not load the product site itself, so details of the workflow beyond the listing are not disclosed here.

## Who is behind Speel, and when did it launch?

Speel is run by a founder listed only as Yann, based in France and linked on TrustMRR to Prosp.ai, another startup. TrustMRR dates Speel to August 2025, and the listing says it launched in October and went "from 0 to 1M ARR in 3 months", a claim we could not check against the Stripe history shown.

That claim matters for reading the numbers. A run to $1M ARR would mean MRR near $83K, above the November 2026 reading of $64,958 ([calc, from TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/speel-co)). If the claim is accurate, MRR has since come down from a peak. If it was a projection, it was never reached. The listing does not say which.

## Why is Speel's 30-day revenue so far below its MRR?

Speel's 30-day revenue of $2,826 is about 4% of its $64,958 MRR ([calc, from TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/speel-co)), and the listing gives no reason. MRR counts the monthly value of active subscriptions, while 30-day revenue counts money actually collected. A gap this wide usually means billing has slowed sharply: failed payments, paused billing, refunds, or most customers on annual plans that renewed earlier.

## How does Speel compare with Arcads and Vidon.ai?

Speel sells the same kind of output as Arcads, AI people in ad videos, at a far smaller scale, and it is much larger than early Vidon.ai. The three show how the AI video ad market grew over a few years.

| Company | What it makes | Monthly figure | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Speel | UGC-style ad videos and images | $64,958 MRR (Nov 2026) | [verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/speel-co) |
| [Arcads](/cases/arcads/) | AI actors for video ads | ≈$833K a month, implied from $10M ARR | [founder-reported, Latka](https://getlatka.com/interviews/arcads-romain-torres-2025) |
| [Vidon.ai](/cases/vidon-ai/) | Videos from blog posts | $6K MRR, five months in (Oct 2022) | [founder-reported, Starter Story](https://www.starterstory.com/stories/vidon-ai) |

Our observation: Arcads proved that marketers will pay for AI UGC, which made it easier for followers like Speel to sell the same idea. Being second is cheaper on education but harder on retention, because a buyer can switch tools between campaigns. Speel's collected revenue in the last 30 days hints at how fragile that position can be.

## What should founders learn from Speel's numbers?

- **MRR is a promise; collected revenue is the proof.** Speel's $64,958 MRR next to $2,826 collected shows why both belong on a dashboard ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/speel-co)).
- **Fast followers can sell quickly.** A market opened by others let Speel reach 1,086 subscriptions within about a year of founding.
- **Growth claims need dates.** "0 to 1M ARR in 3 months" is hard to judge without the month it refers to.
- **Campaign tools face switching risk.** Ad creative is bought per campaign, so loyalty has to be earned each time.
