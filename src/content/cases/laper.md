Writers are among the loudest critics of generative AI, which makes them an odd market for an AI writing tool. Laper sells to them anyway, by promising less AI, not more. Fourteen months after its September 2025 founding, the Hong Kong company showed $52,477 in monthly recurring revenue and 1,618 active subscriptions ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/laper)).

## Why does Laper pitch AI to writers who dislike it?

Laper's whole positioning is built around writers' distrust of AI. The homepage headline offers "AI Novel & Screenplay Writing", and the copy argues the tool should explain and organise a script, not write it for you ([founder-reported, Laper homepage](https://laper.ai)).

> "Laper knows why writers and screenwriters hate generative AI." — Laper homepage ([founder-reported, Laper](https://laper.ai))

The TrustMRR listing uses a sharper tagline, "AI Screenwriting Software, Without the AI Aftertaste", and describes Laper as a creative OS for screenwriters, directors and storyboard artists. It pitches itself as "Cursor, CapCut, and Figma for visual storytelling", which signals a scope wider than a word processor.

Laper had $52,477 in MRR in November 2026, $55,240 in revenue over the last 30 days and $210,429 in total since launch ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/laper)). TrustMRR verifies these with a Stripe API key.

## What do Laper subscribers pay on average?

Laper's average subscription is worth about $32.43 a month ([calc, from TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/laper)). That is MRR divided by 1,618 active subscriptions. Actual list prices are not disclosed: the homepage links to a pricing page but shows no amounts, and the TrustMRR listing leaves pricing blank.

The audience explains the price point. TrustMRR tags Laper as B2C, aimed at individual professional screenwriters, writers, directors and producers, not studios buying seats. An average in that range is a consumer-style subscription, closer to writing apps than to enterprise production software.

## How has Laper grown with such a small team?

Laper grew on a long list of channels, run by a team of 2–5 people with no outside funding ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/laper)). The listing names SEO, content marketing, a blog, a newsletter, X, Google Ads, X Ads, cold email, email marketing, Hacker News and partnerships.

Speed of shipping is the other claim. In a founder's letter on the homepage, ChunXiang Zhao, CEO of Laper, Corp., says the team ships around twenty improvements almost every week ([founder-reported, Laper homepage](https://laper.ai)). The stack listed on TrustMRR includes React, TypeScript, Go, Supabase, PostgreSQL and Docker, with Stripe for payments.

## How does Laper compare with Jenni AI and Textero?

Laper is the only one of these three writing tools built for scripts and stories rather than academic text. All three sell AI writing help to individuals on subscriptions, which makes the comparison useful.

Our observation: the academic tools sell speed, while Laper sells restraint. Jenni AI's scale shows how large the student writing market is, but a screenwriter's tolerance for generated text is lower, and Laper's "only helps where they actually want it" message is designed around that. Laper's figure is also the only Stripe-verified one in this table.

## What lessons does Laper offer niche AI builders?

- **Turn your market's objection into the pitch.** Laper leads with writers' dislike of generative AI instead of hiding from it ([founder-reported, Laper homepage](https://laper.ai)).
- **A narrow professional niche can still be large enough.** A tool for screenwriters reached 1,618 paying subscriptions in about 14 months.
- **Consumer pricing works for professionals working alone.** An average near $32.43 a month fits writers paying from their own pocket.
- **Ship visibly and often.** Around twenty improvements a week gives a small team a story to tell on X, Hacker News and the newsletter.
- **Verified numbers make small companies credible.** The $52,477 MRR figure is checked against Stripe ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/laper)).
