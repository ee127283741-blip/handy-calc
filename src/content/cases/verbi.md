Verbi's pitch is a dig at the biggest names in language apps: talk, don't drill. A small bootstrapped team founded it in April 2026, and by November it had $23,601 in MRR, 5,006 active subscriptions and $270,039 in all-time revenue ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/verbi)). That lifetime total is large for seven months, which hints at a fast start.

## Why does Verbi teach through conversation instead of flashcards?

Verbi centres learning on spoken practice with an AI tutor. The listing says learners hold real conversations, learn vocabulary along the way and track progress across every skill that matters ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/verbi)). Its stated problem is memorizing words you'll never use.

The audience is practical: people preparing for travel, an exam or a career move. Verbi runs on both iOS and Android, built with React Native and a Node.js backend, with RevenueCat handling subscriptions.

> "The fastest path to confidence is speaking, not endless flashcards." — Verbi listing ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/verbi))

## Why is Verbi rated lower on Google Play than on the App Store?

Verbi's ratings split by platform: 4.6 from 404 ratings on the App Store and 3.9 from 556 ratings on Google Play ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/verbi)). The listing gives no reason. A cross-platform React Native app can behave differently on each store, and Android reviewers may meet the paywall or voice features differently.

What the split does show is that Verbi has more Android raters than iPhone raters. A team of 2–5 people shipping on both stores in its first months is a deliberate bet on reach.

## What are the lessons from Verbi's first seven months?

- **Position against the default.** "Speaking, not endless flashcards" defines Verbi by what it is not ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/verbi)).
- **Cross-platform widens reach.** React Native let a small team ship on iOS and Android at once.
- **Ratings vary by store.** A 4.6 versus 3.9 split is a signal to watch, not a footnote.
- **A proven category lowers risk.** Speak's scale shows the demand before Verbi had to prove it.
