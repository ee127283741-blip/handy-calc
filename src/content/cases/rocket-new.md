Rocket.new ARR reached $4.5M within months of launch, from a team in Surat, India, not Bangalore or San Francisco. CEO Vishal Virani said in September 2025 that the platform had more than 400,000 users in 180 countries and over 10,000 paid subscribers ([founder-reported, AI Magazine](https://aimagazine.com/news/rocket-new-the-vibe-coding-startup-with-us-15m-in-funding)). The same month it raised a $15M seed led by Salesforce Ventures. Rocket.new is a vibe-coding app builder, and its founders had already spent years on an earlier code-generation tool.

## How did DhiWise become Rocket.new?

Rocket.new is the second product from Vishal Virani, Rahul Shingala and Deepak Dhanak. Their earlier company, DhiWise, turned designs into code for developers ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/09/22/rocket-new-one-of-indias-first-vibe-coding-startups-snags-15m-from-accel-salesforce-ventures)). With Rocket.new they moved to prompts: users describe an app and Rocket builds the web or mobile version. Virani said about 45% of users build mobile apps and 55% build websites. The founding date, team size and plan prices are not disclosed.

> "Our underlying architecture is completely different from what Lovable, Bolt, and everyone is doing," — Vishal Virani ([founder-reported, TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/09/22/rocket-new-one-of-indias-first-vibe-coding-startups-snags-15m-from-accel-salesforce-ventures))

## How did Rocket.new make $4.5M ARR so quickly?

Rocket.new made its first $4.5M ARR by charging a small share of a large free user base. More than 10,000 of its 400,000+ users paid, about 2.5% (calc), for $4.5M ARR, or about $375K a month (calc) ([founder-reported, AI Magazine](https://aimagazine.com/news/rocket-new-the-vibe-coding-startup-with-us-15m-in-funding)). Users came from 180 countries. Virani told TechCrunch he aimed for $20–25M ARR by the end of 2025 and $60–70M by June 2026 ([unverified, TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/09/22/rocket-new-one-of-indias-first-vibe-coding-startups-snags-15m-from-accel-salesforce-ventures)). Those are targets, and we found no later sourced revenue figure. Total revenue is not disclosed.

## Who invested in Rocket.new's seed round?

Salesforce Ventures led Rocket.new's $15M seed in September 2025, with Accel and Together Fund participating ([media-reported, TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/09/22/rocket-new-one-of-indias-first-vibe-coding-startups-snags-15m-from-accel-salesforce-ventures)). TechCrunch described it as one of India's first vibe-coding startups. A $15M seed is large for India, and it came on the strength of paid users rather than a demo.

## How does Rocket.new compare with Emergent and Lovable?

Rocket.new and [Emergent](/cases/emergent/) are both Indian vibe-coding companies selling mostly to users abroad, but they are at very different scales. Emergent reported a $120M run rate by July 2026 ([founder-reported, TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/15/indian-ai-coding-startup-emergent-becomes-a-unicorn-just-over-a-year-after-launch/)); Rocket.new's last sourced figure is $4.5M ARR in September 2025. [Lovable](/cases/lovable/), from Stockholm, reported $100M ARR in July 2025. Rocket.new's mix of mobile and web apps is closest to Emergent's, and both bet that people outside Silicon Valley will pay to build software.

Our reading: Rocket.new's paid conversion, about 2.5% of users, is typical for a freemium tool. Its stated targets would need that share or the price per user to rise sharply. Until it publishes a newer number, the September 2025 figures are the only ones we can stand behind.

## What lessons does Rocket.new offer vibe-coding founders?

- **A previous product is a head start.** DhiWise gave the founders code-generation experience before prompts took over.
- **Build anywhere, sell everywhere.** A team in Surat reached users in 180 countries.
- **Targets are not results.** Rocket.new's $20–25M goal for 2025 is a plan; only the $4.5M figure is reported.
- **Mobile apps are a big share of demand.** About 45% of Rocket.new users build mobile apps, not just websites.
