One day before his vacation, Fernando Pessagno heard he might lose his job. As an Argentinian product designer living in Sweden on a work visa, that was his worst fear. Instead of relaxing, he set himself a public challenge on X and YouTube: ship something in the 10 days of his holiday. The result was aiCarousels, which reached $5,000 MRR about six months later, with 95% of it profit ([founder-reported, Starter Story](https://www.starterstory.com/stories/aicarousels)).

## What problem do LinkedIn carousels create?

Carousel posts were popular on LinkedIn, and Pessagno had made them himself for startup marketing teams. Even for an experienced designer they took a long time. Templates look great until you change the fonts, colours and text, and then non-designers get lost in too many choices. Competing head-on with template giants like Canva or Envato was not realistic.

So he did the opposite: fewer design choices, locked element positions and automatic font sizing, so carousels look good enough quickly.

> "What I’m selling is time, not just designs." — Fernando Pessagno ([founder-reported, Starter Story](https://www.starterstory.com/stories/aicarousels))

## What can one designer ship in 10 days?

A working SaaS, if the plan is tight. He built without fancy frameworks, used Outseta for authentication, billing and support tickets, and called OpenAI's API for the writing.

| Days | What Pessagno built |
|---|---|
| 1–3 | Carousel editor, navigation, font auto-resize, download, slide controls and a settings sidebar |
| 4–5 | Save and load, backgrounds, colour palettes, font pairs and slide types |
| 6 | AI content generator that writes slides from a topic |
| 7 | Monetisation with Outseta and a paywall |
| 8 | Logo and landing page |
| 9–10 | Product Hunt preparation and launch |

The AI generator has since grown to work from free text, a website URL or a YouTube video. His view is that AI should be a thin layer on top of a product that stands on its own.

## How did aiCarousels find customers after launch?

The Product Hunt launch went better than he feared: ninth product of the day with 154 upvotes and 64 comments. Five people paid on launch day, which meant $50 MRR. Small, but proof that people would pay ([founder-reported, Starter Story](https://www.starterstory.com/stories/aicarousels)).

Early users hit lots of bugs, so he answered support tickets fast, then used the conversation to ask for feedback and connect on LinkedIn. He sends a weekly, first-person summary of updates and fixes, and never hides that he is a one-man team. Being the first AI carousel generator brought shares from blogs and newsletters, and with a good domain name and those backlinks, SEO became the biggest traffic source. He also posts build-in-public updates to about 3,000 LinkedIn followers and on Twitter, and his Medium post about the build reached almost 20,000 views.

## What MRR did aiCarousels reach in six months?

Pessagno set a goal of $2,500 MRR within 12 months. Six months in, aiCarousels was at $5,000 MRR, 95% of it profit, and still growing ([founder-reported, Starter Story](https://www.starterstory.com/stories/aicarousels)). Total revenue and pricing details are not disclosed. The Starter Story page header shows $10K a month, but the founder's own text gives $5,000 MRR, which this case uses.

The business let him leave his 9-to-5 and plan to stay in Sweden on a partner visa. He also runs a second product, ResumeMaker.Online. Next on his list: affiliates, blog posts and free tools such as an AI LinkedIn summary generator.

## How does aiCarousels compare with Arcads and FounderPal?

[Arcads](/cases/arcads/) and aiCarousels both sell marketing content creation, but at opposite ends. Arcads generates video ads with AI actors for performance marketers and earns six-figure contracts. aiCarousels helps individual creators make simple slide posts for social feeds at a low price. Both win by producing "good enough" content fast for formats that burn through creative.

[FounderPal](/cases/founderpal/) shares aiCarousels' playbook of free tools and SEO to attract solopreneurs. Both founders built in public and used small free tools to feed a paid product, which keeps acquisition cheap for a one- or two-person team.

## What can solo builders learn from aiCarousels?

- **Short sprints limit the downside.** Ten days of work is easy to walk away from if nobody cares.
- **Fewer choices can be the feature.** Locked layouts saved non-designers time against template giants.
- **First movers get free backlinks.** Being the first AI carousel generator turned launch buzz into lasting SEO.
- **Talk to users like a person.** Fast support and casual weekly updates turned bug reports into loyalty.
