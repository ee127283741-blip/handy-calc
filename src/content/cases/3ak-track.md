3AK Track revenue started with an audience, not an app. Braylin Byrd and Christian Jones are Division I track athletes with more than 150,000 followers who wanted training advice. In January 2026 they launched an AI training app built in Rork, an AI tool that creates mobile apps from prompts, without writing code. It made $10K in its first month ([founder-reported, Rork](https://rork.com/blog/3ak-case-study)), and Starter Story cited about $17,460 in a recent 28 days from RevenueCat data ([founder-reported, Starter Story](https://www.starterstory.com/businesses/3ak-track-field/sources)).

## Who built 3AK Track, and how?

Braylin Byrd runs track at Texas Tech and Christian Jones runs at Texas Southern; they were teammates in high school ([Starter Story](https://www.starterstory.com/businesses/3ak-track-field/sources)). They built 3AK Track entirely in Rork with no hand-written code, using Supabase for the backend and RevenueCat for subscriptions ([Rork](https://rork.com/blog/3ak-case-study)). The app offers AI stride analysis, sprint training plans and run tracking. Team size and plan prices are not disclosed.

> "The #1 Track & Field Training App — Built for Athletes Who Want Real Results." — 3AK Track App Store listing ([founder-reported, App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/3ak-track-field/id6756290066))

## What revenue has 3AK Track reported so far?

3AK Track earned $10K in its first month and $45K in its first two months ([founder-reported, Starter Story](https://www.starterstory.com/businesses/3ak-track-field/sources)). Starter Story cited about $17,460 in a recent 28-day period, verified by RevenueCat, the subscription tool the app uses. Stork later reported about $70K in total over six months and 70,000 users ([founder-reported, Stork](https://www.stork.ai/blog/ai-built-their-app-they-made-70k)). ## Why did 3AK Track's audience matter so much?

3AK Track's founders had distribution before they had a product. More than 150,000 followers already watched their training content, so the app had customers on day one ([Starter Story](https://www.starterstory.com/businesses/3ak-track-field/sources)). The first month's $10K came from turning that audience into subscribers. For athletes and creators, an AI app builder now turns a following into a software business without hiring developers.

## How does 3AK Track compare with Wrestle AI and Hockey AI?

3AK Track, [Wrestle AI](/cases/wrestle-ai/) and [Hockey AI](/cases/hockey-ai/) are all sports training apps built in Rork by young founders who could not code. Wrestle AI reached about $17K a month ([founder-reported, Starter Story](https://rosetta.to/u/starterstory/how-i-grew-my-mobile-app-to-17k-per-month)); Hockey AI, built by two brothers aged 14 and 17, was at about $1K a month ([founder-reported, Rork](https://rork.com/blog/eli-hockey-ai-case-study)). 3AK Track's advantage is its founders' audience of 150,000+, which the other two did not start with.

Our reading: Rork's sports apps show a repeatable pattern, an athlete turning knowledge of a sport into an AI coach. What separates the outcomes is distribution. Founders with an existing audience start at thousands of dollars a month; founders without one have to build it.

## What lessons does 3AK Track offer athlete-founders?

- **Your audience is your launch.** 150,000+ followers turned into $10K in the first month.
- **AI builders remove the developer step.** Two college athletes shipped an app with no hand-written code.
- **Use verified revenue tools.** RevenueCat data made 3AK Track's numbers checkable.
- **Niche sports have paying fans.** Track athletes pay for training plans built for their event.
