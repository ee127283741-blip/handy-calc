Most AI SEO tools sell content at volume. Backlinker AI sells links, the slower and harder half of SEO, by sending AI-written pitches to reporters and editors. In November 2026 it showed $19,750 in MRR and $567,492 in all-time revenue from only 45 active subscriptions ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/backlinker-ai)). That ratio, a few customers paying a lot, sets it apart from most small AI tools.

## What does Backlinker AI automate for SEO teams?

Backlinker AI automates the outreach that earns press and editorial links. Its TrustMRR listing describes AI-powered outreach to reporters and editors, built for agencies, founders and SEO teams ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/backlinker-ai)).

> "Backlinker AI automates backlink acquisition through AI-powered reporter and editorial outreach." — Backlinker AI, company description ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/backlinker-ai))

Link building has long been done by hand or bought from agencies. Journalists ask for expert sources, someone has to write a relevant answer quickly, and only a few answers get used. An AI system can draft and send many more pitches than a person.

## Who started Backlinker AI, and when?

TrustMRR lists the founder as Bennett, with no surname, and gives July 2023 as the founding date and the United States as the country. The team size and funding are not disclosed. The listing counts about 200 users with a B2B audience, which is far more than the 45 active subscriptions, so many users are likely team seats, trials or past customers. The page does not explain the gap.

## Why does Backlinker AI charge $300 a month?

Backlinker AI prices like a service, not an app. The company-written listing gives a price of $300 a month ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/backlinker-ai)), which is closer to a cheap link-building retainer than to a typical AI subscription.

## How much does Backlinker AI make per customer?

Backlinker AI made $19,750 in MRR and $18,910 over the last 30 days in November 2026 ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/backlinker-ai)). All-time revenue was $567,492, and the listing claims growth of roughly 180% year over year ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/backlinker-ai)).

MRR divided by 45 subscriptions is about $438.89 per account ([calc, from TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/backlinker-ai)), above the $300 list price. Some customers probably pay for more volume or several sites. The risk is concentration: losing a handful of agencies would cut revenue sharply.

## How does Backlinker AI compare with ChatSEO and Topical Map AI?

All three sell SEO with AI, but at different points in the funnel. [ChatSEO](/cases/chatseo/) packages an SEO expert's knowledge into a chatbot, and [Topical Map AI](/cases/topical-map-ai/) plans what content a site should publish. Backlinker AI works after the content exists, by getting other sites to link to it.

Our reading: links are the part of SEO that buyers find hardest to do themselves, so they will pay more for it. That shows in the per-account figure. The tradeoff is reputation risk, because outreach at scale can annoy the reporters it depends on.

## What lessons does Backlinker AI hold for B2B AI founders?

- **Automate the expensive, manual job.** Link outreach was a retainer business before Backlinker AI made it software ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/backlinker-ai)).
- **Price against the service you replace.** A $300 plan looks cheap next to an agency.
- **Few customers can be enough.** 45 subscriptions carried almost $20K in MRR.
- **Watch concentration.** Each lost account costs hundreds of dollars a month.
