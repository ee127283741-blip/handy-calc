Many insurance agents still send quotes as screenshots and messy PDFs, then chase signatures by phone. QuoteDeck packages that into one professional presentation. Founded by Drew Hodges in November 2025, it had $20,530 in all-time revenue a year later, yet only $700 in monthly recurring revenue from two subscriptions ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/quotedeck-llc)). That mismatch is the most interesting thing about it.

## What does QuoteDeck replace in an insurance agent's workflow?

QuoteDeck lets agents and agencies create and present health and life insurance quotes in one platform. The company describes the status quo bluntly: quotes sent as screenshots, messy emails and jumbled PDFs, client details gathered over the phone, and signatures and compliance handled however agents can ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/quotedeck-llc)).

> "It's a mess, and it costs you deals and your time." — QuoteDeck company description ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/quotedeck-llc))

It is built with React, Next.js and TypeScript on the front end and Node.js, Supabase, AWS and Stripe on the back end.

## How does QuoteDeck find insurance agents?

QuoteDeck lists more marketing channels than most tiny SaaS: Facebook, Instagram, Meta Ads, cold email, email marketing, LinkedIn, affiliates, word of mouth and forums ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/quotedeck-llc)). Insurance agents are a defined profession with their own communities, which makes affiliates and forums practical.

The listing also shows 352 code contributions in the last year and about 930K lines of code, a sign of steady solo building.

## What takeaways does QuoteDeck offer vertical SaaS founders?

- **Describe the mess precisely.** Screenshots, jumbled PDFs and phone calls are a pain any agent recognises.
- **Agencies can pay above list price.** $350 average per subscription suggests larger deals than the public tiers.
- **Lifetime revenue can hide a thin base.** $20,530 all time sits on just two current subscriptions ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/quotedeck-llc)).
- **Professional niches have reachable channels.** Affiliates, forums and cold email fit a defined trade like insurance.
