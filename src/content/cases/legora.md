Legora ARR doubled from $100M to $200M in less than six months. Max Junestrand, Legora's CEO, wrote that getting from $1M to $100M took 18 months, the second hundred took less than six, and the company had just passed $200M ([founder-reported, Max Junestrand on X](https://x.com/MaxJunestrand/status/2102368939896361182)). Legora started in Stockholm in 2023 and competes head-on with Harvey for the world's large law firms. In October 2026 it raised $550M at an $8.8B post-money valuation.

## Who founded Legora, and what does it sell?

Max Junestrand, Sigge Labor and August Erséus founded Legora in Stockholm in 2023 ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/03/10/legora-reaches-5-55-billion-valuation-as-ai-legaltech-boom-endures/)). Legora sells a legal AI platform that drafts and reviews documents, runs research and works through large sets of contracts in tables, plus agents that carry out multi-step legal work. Its customers are law firms and in-house legal teams. Junestrand wrote that 130,000 lawyers use it every month across 2,100 firms and legal teams in more than 80 countries ([founder-reported, Max Junestrand on X](https://x.com/MaxJunestrand/status/2102368939896361182)). Prices and team size are not disclosed.

## How fast did Legora ARR grow?

Legora's ARR grew from about $1M to $100M in 18 months, then to more than $200M in under six months more. The company announced the $100M mark in April 2026, with more than 1,000 customers ([founder-reported, Legora release](https://legora.com/newsroom/legal-teams-adoption-of-ai-propels-legora-past-100-million-in-annual-recurring-revenue)); The Next Web reported the 18-month timeline ([The Next Web](https://thenextweb.com/news/legora-just-hit-100-million-in-revenue-it-took-18-months)). At $200M ARR that is about $16.7M a month (calc). Total revenue to date is not disclosed.

## What did Legora's 2026 funding rounds value it at?

Legora's valuation rose from $5.55B in March 2026 ([media-reported, TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/03/10/legora-reaches-5-55-billion-valuation-as-ai-legaltech-boom-endures/)) to $8.8B post-money in October 2026, when Atreides Management led a $550M Series E ([media-reported, TechCrunch](https://finance.yahoo.com/technology/ai/articles/legal-ai-startup-legora-raises-185156489.html)). At $200M ARR, the October price is about 44 times ARR (calc). Legora said it would use the money to expand across North America, Europe and Asia Pacific.

## How does Legora compare with Harvey and EvenUp?

Legora is the challenger to [Harvey](/cases/harvey/), and the gap depends on which Harvey number you trust. Harvey reported $190M ARR in January 2026 ([founder-reported, CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/03/25/legal-ai-startup-harvey-raises-200-million-at-11-billion-valuation.html)), close to Legora's later $200M. A September 2026 report put Harvey above $400M ARR ([unverified, SaaS Sentinel](https://saassentinel.com/2026/09/10/harvey-raises-550m-at-15-5b-valuation-as-legal-ai-arr-tops-400m/)), but that figure is not in Harvey's own announcement. Harvey was founded a year earlier, in 2022, and was valued at $15.5B in September 2026 ([founder-reported, Harvey](https://www.harvey.ai/blog/harvey-raises-dollar550m-at-a-dollar155b-valuation-to-help-legal-teams-own-their-intelligence)), against Legora's $8.8B. [EvenUp](/cases/evenup/) is a different kind of legal AI company: it sells one workflow, demand letters, to personal injury firms and does not disclose ARR.

Our reading: investors still pay more per dollar of ARR for the category leader. Legora's case is speed: it reached $200M ARR about three years after founding, starting from Europe.

## Which lessons from Legora apply to other legal AI startups?

- **A second mover can catch up.** Legora started a year after Harvey and passed $200M ARR about three years after founding.
- **Home market first, then the US.** Legora built from Stockholm and is now using its Series E to expand across North America, Europe and Asia Pacific.
- **Publish the timeline, not only the total.** "$1M to $100M in 18 months" tells investors more than one ARR number.
- **Seats scale with lawyers.** 130,000 monthly users across 2,100 firms shows the product spreading inside each customer.
