## Origin of the idea
Postma had already sold an AI copywriting tool (Headlime). After Stable Diffusion's release he tried an AI stock-photo site, dropped it over quality concerns, and built ProfilePicture.AI in about 30 hours. Business headshots turned out to be the use case people paid for most reliably.

## How they started
He launched HeadshotPro alongside another image product, watched which one grew, and then focused on headshots.

## Tools & tech stack
Rather than a single fine-tuned model, the company says it chains dozens of open-source and custom models: a vision-language model filters bad or unsafe uploads, and a face-restoration model cleans artefacts. It spent about a year on its own models.

## Marketing
Postma is known for SEO: landing pages for many headshot searches (by job, style and use) plus comparison pages. Team plans give companies a reason to buy for everyone at once.

## Sales & pricing
One-off packages rather than subscriptions, with team ordering for companies. The pitch is price and speed compared with booking a photographer.

## Key takeaways
- Run several small bets and double down on the winner.
- Quality filtering of inputs is part of the product.
- One-off pricing fits a purchase people make once a year.
