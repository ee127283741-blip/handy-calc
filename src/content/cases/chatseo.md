Paul Vengeons built a tool designed to replace his own job. He had worked his way from freelance SEO writer to Paris agency strategist to one of France's best-known SEO consultants, with a large following on YouTube. ChatSEO packages his methods into a chatbot. It launched in December 2025 with paying customers from day one and reached $30K MRR by October 2026 ([founder-reported, Indie Hackers](https://www.indiehackers.com/post/tech/focusing-on-audience-first-and-hitting-30k-mrr-in-8-months-ootKyp98moigKLdaOON4)).

## Why would an SEO consultant build a tool to replace himself?

Vengeons did not want a startup. He expected it to take too much time and energy. But he saw too many green flags to ignore: AI had become good enough to do real SEO work, and thousands of people already trusted his methods.

ChatSEO is a chatbot that guides users to SEO results on their own sites. It knows the business model, competitors, tone of voice and past SEO results, can pull official data such as search volumes to recommend keywords, and is trained on the methods he refined by hand for years. For a site about search visibility, it is a neat example of AI being used to do SEO itself.

## How did three partners build ChatSEO?

Vengeons partnered with Nicholas Dulait, who brings his own audience and growth skills, and Léo, a developer they found after about ten calls. Léo started as a freelancer and was then offered a co-founder stake. They wrote a simple business plan and invested about $10K ([founder-reported, Indie Hackers](https://www.indiehackers.com/post/tech/focusing-on-audience-first-and-hitting-30k-mrr-in-8-months-ootKyp98moigKLdaOON4)).

| Layer | What ChatSEO uses |
|---|---|
| Front end | Next.js |
| API | TypeScript on Hono |
| Data | Postgres and Redis |
| AI | LLMs through Vercel's AI SDK |
| SEO data | DataForSEO and Google Search Console |

They did little validation; the launch itself was the test, and they have shipped improvements every week since.

## Why did ChatSEO charge from day one?

Because paying customers tell the truth. ChatSEO offers a free trial of five prompts with no credit card, then three plans: Starter at €29, Pro at €49 and Ranker at €79 a month, with 20% off yearly plans ([founder-reported, Indie Hackers](https://www.indiehackers.com/post/tech/focusing-on-audience-first-and-hitting-30k-mrr-in-8-months-ootKyp98moigKLdaOON4)). Plans differ by credits and the number of connected sites, and heavy users can buy extra credits, which grows revenue inside each account.

> "Paying customers give you much more honest feedback than free users." — Paul Vengeons ([founder-reported, Indie Hackers](https://www.indiehackers.com/post/tech/focusing-on-audience-first-and-hitting-30k-mrr-in-8-months-ootKyp98moigKLdaOON4))

Most growth came from audiences the founders already had: Vengeons on YouTube, LinkedIn, TikTok and their French podcast, and Dulait on his own social channels. An affiliate program adds more. Paid ads have been humbling: X ads brought signups at about €10 each, and none became paying customers.

## How much MRR does ChatSEO make?

ChatSEO made about €7K in its first month, almost all from Vengeons' French audience. By June 2026 monthly revenue had tripled to about €22K, and by October 2026 it reported $30K MRR ([founder-reported, Indie Hackers](https://www.indiehackers.com/post/tech/focusing-on-audience-first-and-hitting-30k-mrr-in-8-months-ootKyp98moigKLdaOON4)). Total revenue is not disclosed.

Churn was the hardest number. In spring 2026 ChatSEO lost about 20% of subscribers a month, so it needed many new customers just to stay flat. By August churn was down to 10%, while paying subscribers grew from about 350 in April to about 600. About 71% of customers are French, so expanding into English-speaking markets is the next step.

## How does ChatSEO compare with Magai and Leadmore AI?

[Magai](/cases/magai/) shows the same audience-first pattern: an email list of nearly 100,000 people meant customers on day one. ChatSEO did it with a YouTube and podcast audience in French. Both founders spent years building trust before building the product.

[Leadmore AI](/cases/leadmore-ai/) sells another form of organic visibility, on Reddit, and its founder was preparing a GEO product for AI search. ChatSEO focuses on classic Google SEO with live data. Together they show demand for AI tools that help small businesses get found, whether by search engines, communities or AI assistants.

[Topical Map AI](/cases/topical-map-ai/) is an SEO planning tool built with AI by blogger Pete McPherson, who does not have a programming background.

## What takeaways does ChatSEO offer expert-founders?

- **Build the audience before the product.** Thousands of people who trusted the founder bought in month one.
- **Charge from day one.** Paying users gave honest feedback that free users would not.
- **Watch churn before acquisition.** At 20% monthly churn, growth only kept the business flat.
- **Partners can cover your gaps.** An expert, a growth marketer and a developer each brought what the others lacked.
