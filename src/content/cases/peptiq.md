One person, ten months, $135,581. That is the verified record of PeptIQ, a health and fitness mobile app that Kris Chase founded in the United States in January 2026 ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/peptiq)). By November 2026 the app had 1,482 paying subscribers and about 40,000 users. The listing says little about the product itself, so this case focuses on what the numbers and the stack do show.

## What do we know about the PeptIQ app?

PeptIQ is a mobile app in the Health Fitness and Mobile Apps markets, serving both B2B and B2C users, according to its TrustMRR listing ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/peptiq)). The page carries no product description, value proposition or pricing, so what the app does in detail is not disclosed there.

> "1 person" — PeptIQ team size field ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/peptiq))

The stack is listed: Expo, React Native and Next.js on the front end, with Supabase, Node.js, Cloudflare, Vercel, Resend and RevenueCat behind it.

## Which marketing channels does PeptIQ use?

PeptIQ lists one of the widest channel mixes on TrustMRR: app store optimization, SEO, a blog and newsletter, organic Instagram, Facebook, TikTok and X, paid Google, Meta and TikTok ads, plus affiliates, partnerships and influencers ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/peptiq)). For a one-person team, that breadth points to heavy use of paid acquisition and automation, though spend is not disclosed.

## What are the takeaways from PeptIQ for app builders?

- **Niche health apps can pay fast.** PeptIQ passed $135,581 within its first year.
- **A cross-platform stack fits a solo team.** Expo and React Native cover both app stores.
- **Distribution is broad, not single-channel.** The listing names more than a dozen channels.
- **Thin public detail limits analysis.** Pricing and the product description are not disclosed.
