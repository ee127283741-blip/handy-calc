Most founders on revenue leaderboards sell software to thousands of customers. uhoh sells IT support to a few dozen. In November 2026 it showed $37,489 in monthly recurring revenue from just 37 active subscriptions ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/uhoh-com)). Each customer is worth about as much as a hundred typical SaaS subscriptions, and the reason is that uhoh sells people, not software.

## What does uhoh do differently from a classic IT provider?

uhoh delivers IT support through Slack, staffed by humans, without tickets. The homepage pitches a managed service provider and IT help desk "for Growing Startups, Delivered by Humans, in Slack", promising no ticketing hassle ([founder-reported, uhoh homepage](https://uhoh.com)).

> "We're replacing clunky IT with clean, fast, and flexible support." — uhoh homepage ([founder-reported, uhoh](https://uhoh.com))

The TrustMRR listing calls it "the first IT help desk that's free to try and priced by you". The homepage adds a 30-day trial, a 30-day money-back guarantee and a three-step onboarding that starts with a 15-minute call.

uhoh made $37,489 in MRR in November 2026, $45,636 over the last 30 days and $472,131 since its June 2025 start ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/uhoh-com)). TrustMRR verifies these with a Stripe API key.

All-time revenue is also high relative to 17 months of operation, so earlier months or one-off projects likely contributed. Team size is not disclosed.

## Where does uhoh's first customer base come from?

uhoh's homepage points to a network of founders. Its testimonials come from founders and CEOs of startups and small businesses, and it names Jon Sturgeon as co-founder of uhoh and CEO of Dingus & Zazzy ([founder-reported, uhoh homepage](https://uhoh.com)). The TrustMRR listing places the company in Canada and lists the audience as B2B.

That pattern is common for services. The first few dozen customers often come through the founder's existing company and contacts, not through ads. With a Domain Rating of 17 on TrustMRR, search is clearly not yet the main channel ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/uhoh-com)).

## How does uhoh compare with Chatbase and SiteGPT?

uhoh sits in this site's customer support category with Chatbase and SiteGPT, but it is the opposite bet. The two AI tools replace support staff with chatbots; uhoh sells human staff, reached through a chat tool.

Our observation: uhoh needs 37 customers to reach revenue that a chatbot company needs hundreds or thousands for. The trade-off is scale. Software margins grow with each customer, while a human help desk adds labour cost with every new account.

## What are the takeaways from uhoh's model?

- **Few customers can mean real revenue.** 37 subscriptions produced $37,489 in MRR ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/uhoh-com)).
- **Meet buyers where they already work.** Delivering IT support in Slack removes the ticketing step startups dislike.
- **Flexible pricing lowers the barrier.** An average near $1,013 a month against a $3k list price shows "priced by you" in practice.
- **Risk reversal sells services.** A 30-day trial and money-back guarantee make a large monthly fee easier to accept.
