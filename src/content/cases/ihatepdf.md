Most free PDF sites ask you to upload a contract or a passport scan to a server you know nothing about. ihatepdf.cv does the work inside the browser instead. That privacy angle drew about 350,000 users by November 2026, according to the founder, yet the product earned just $1,798 in its first year ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/ihatepdf-cv)). The gap between reach and revenue is the story here.

## What can ihatepdf.cv do with a PDF?

ihatepdf.cv offers more than 60 PDF tools for editing, converting, compressing, merging, splitting, signing and OCR. All core processing runs locally in the browser, it works offline, and it needs no sign-up and adds no watermark ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/ihatepdf-cv)).

> "files never leave the user's device" — ihatepdf.cv company description ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/ihatepdf-cv))

The listing names React on the front end and Dodo Payments for billing. It serves both businesses and consumers.

## Why is a free PDF tool a hard business for ihatepdf.cv?

A free tool with no account wall gives users little reason to pay. Privacy and offline use are real benefits, but they are also delivered on the free tier, so the paid offer has to be something else. The listed marketing channels are SEO, X, LinkedIn, Reddit and a blog ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/ihatepdf-cv)), which are good at bringing traffic and weaker at converting it.

The handful of paying accounts suggests the money comes from a few larger buyers rather than from the consumer crowd.

## How does ihatepdf.cv differ from Web3Forms and Image-Charts?

All three are single-purpose utilities, but the other two charge for usage that grows with the customer.

Our observation: Web3Forms and Image-Charts run on servers and meter something, submissions or images, so heavy users naturally upgrade. ihatepdf.cv runs on the user's own machine, which is great for privacy and costs, but leaves nothing to meter. Its 350,000 users are an audience; the business model is still open.

## What can tool makers learn from ihatepdf.cv?

- **Local processing is a strong hook.** "Files never leave your device" is a clear reason to choose one PDF site over another.
- **Free everything caps revenue.** When the core value is free, paid plans need a separate, obvious reason to exist.
- **Audience is an asset.** About 350,000 users and a founder with a large X following give the product room to test paid features.
- **A few big buyers can carry early MRR.** Three subscriptions produced all of the $747 MRR ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/ihatepdf-cv)).
