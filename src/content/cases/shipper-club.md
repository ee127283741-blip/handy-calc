Shipper Club sells membership the way some concerts sell tickets: the earliest seats were cheapest, and every new batch costs more. The developer creator OrcDev opened the club in March 2026, and by November 2026 it had collected $39,532 ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/shipper-club-diamond-sponsor)). Almost none of that is recurring, and that is by design.

## What do members get from Shipper Club?

Shipper Club is a private community where builders push each other to ship faster, build with taste and turn ideas into products ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/shipper-club-diamond-sponsor)). Its audience is "modern builders" who care about what works right now, especially with AI tools.

The listing frames the problem as noise: too many tools and too much generic advice. The homepage adds member perks, including partner discounts it says add up to over $1,470 ([founder-reported, Shipper Club](https://shipper.club)).

## How does Shipper Club make money without subscriptions?

Shipper Club makes most of its money from one-time memberships, plus a small recurring stream from Diamond sponsors. TrustMRR showed $39,532 all time, $2,430 in the last 30 days and $1,250 MRR from four active subscriptions in November 2026 ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/shipper-club-diamond-sponsor)). Payments run through Creem, not Stripe.

> "$149 one-time payment, no subscription" — Shipper Club pricing on TrustMRR ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/shipper-club-diamond-sponsor))

The four subscriptions average about $312 a month each ([calc, from TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/shipper-club-diamond-sponsor)), consistent with sponsor slots rather than member fees. Team size is not disclosed.

## Why do Shipper Club tiers get more expensive?

Shipper Club sells membership in ten numbered tiers whose price rises as each batch fills. The Founder tier sold out at $99 and the Legend tier at $129; the open Shipper tier costs $149, and later tiers climb to $399 ([founder-reported, Shipper Club](https://shipper.club)).

Rising tiers reward early joiners and give the next buyer a reason not to wait. The homepage showed 246 members and 54 spots left in the current batch.

## How does Shipper Club compare with FounderPal and Launch Fast?

Shipper Club, [FounderPal](/cases/founderpal/) and [Launch Fast](/cases/launch-fast/) all sell to indie founders, but only Launch Fast charges a monthly subscription. FounderPal, like Shipper Club, built revenue from one-time payments.

| Company | What it sells | Model | Revenue figure | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Shipper Club | Builder community | One-time, $149 tier | $39,532 all time (Nov 2026) | [verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/shipper-club-diamond-sponsor) |

Our observation: one-time pricing turns an audience into cash quickly, but revenue then depends on a steady flow of new buyers. Shipper Club's sponsor subscriptions are its attempt to add a recurring base.

## What are the takeaways from Shipper Club's launch?

- **An audience can fund a community fast.** OrcDev's following on X helped Shipper Club reach $39,532 in about eight months ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/shipper-club-diamond-sponsor)).
- **Price ladders create urgency without discounts.** Each sold-out tier makes the next one look like the best remaining deal.
- **Sponsors can supply the recurring layer.** Four Diamond sponsors provide all of the reported MRR.
- **Read MRR carefully for one-time businesses.** $1,250 MRR understates a club that sells mostly lifetime access.
