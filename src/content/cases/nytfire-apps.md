Eight customers. That is the entire subscription base behind Nytfire Apps, and it was enough for $1,536 in monthly recurring revenue in November 2026 ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/nytfire-apps)). The business does not sell software to thousands of users. It sets up a CRM for plumbers, roofers and coaches who do not want to touch one themselves.

## What does Nytfire Apps build for local businesses?

Nytfire Apps sells a configured CRM and marketing automation, not a blank tool. The company-written listing names HVAC, plumbing, roofing, coaching and real estate businesses as customers ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/nytfire-apps)).

> "Done-For-You CRM & Marketing Automation for Trades, Coaches & Real Estate" — Nytfire Apps listing ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/nytfire-apps))

The listing also says the company builds apps in several niches and does real estate land and notes deals, so the CRM is one line of a broader small business.

## Why build Nytfire CRM on GoHighLevel?

GoHighLevel is a white-label CRM platform that agencies resell under their own name. The listing says Nytfire is built on it ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/nytfire-apps)), so the founder does not maintain core CRM software. The work is configuration, automations and support for each client.

Founded in July 2025 in the United States, the company lists no marketing channels. The founder, Daniel Esteban Martinez, has about 3,200 followers on X.

## How does Nytfire Apps compare with Instantly and Plutio?

[Instantly](/cases/instantly/) sells cold email software to thousands of self-serve customers, and [Plutio](/cases/plutio/) sells an all-in-one business tool for freelancers. Both are product companies. Nytfire Apps is a service wearing a SaaS invoice.

Our observation: the service model trades scale for price. Eight subscriptions would be tiny for Instantly, but at $297 to $400 a month ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/nytfire-apps)) each Nytfire client is worth many self-serve users. The ceiling is the founder's time, since each new client needs a build.

## What are the lessons from Nytfire Apps' service model?

- **Done-for-you sells to non-technical buyers.** Trades businesses pay to skip setup work.
- **Resell a platform, add the labour.** Building on GoHighLevel removes the need to write a CRM.
- **Setup fees change the cash picture.** $4,637 in 30 days against $1,536 MRR shows how much one-off work adds ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/nytfire-apps)).
- **Service revenue scales with hours.** Every new client needs a build, so growth needs people or templates.
