Calorie tracking apps usually fail at the same moment: when users get tired of logging every meal. Nutree tries to take that work away. Launched in April 2026, the app had 2,080 active subscriptions and $37,490 in lifetime revenue by November 2026, with $9,424 collected in the last 30 days alone ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/nutree)). It is a small app growing on paid social, not on a founder brand.

## What does Nutree automate for calorie trackers?

Nutree automates the parts of nutrition tracking people give up on. It adjusts daily goals by itself, suggests meals and tracks macros, so users do not have to recalculate targets by hand ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/nutree)).

> "Nutree solves the problem of manually tracking calories and macros by automatically adjusting goals and suggesting meals." — Nutree listing on TrustMRR ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/nutree))

The app supports 7 languages and is built with Flutter on the front end and Python on the backend, according to the listing.

## How much revenue does Nutree make per subscriber?

Nutree made $4,798 in MRR in November 2026 from 2,080 active subscriptions, with $9,424 in the last 30 days and $37,490 all time ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/nutree)). That is about $2.31 of MRR per subscription (calc: $4,798 / 2,080).

## Which channels does Nutree use to grow?

Nutree lists eight marketing channels: Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, Meta Ads, TikTok Ads, email marketing, SEO and influencers ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/nutree)). Its Domain Rating is 0, so search traffic to the website is not the engine; growth comes from social video and paid ads that send users straight to the app stores.

That mix is common for consumer health apps. The founder's name is not disclosed, and the team is 2–5 people, bootstrapped.

## What lessons does Nutree hold for health app builders?

- **Remove the chore people quit over.** Nutree automates goal changes and meal suggestions instead of adding more logging.
- **Annual plans front-load cash.** Last-30-day revenue of $9,424 was nearly twice the $4,798 MRR.
- **Paid social can replace SEO.** With a Domain Rating of 0, the app still built 2,080 subscriptions.
- **Low price per user needs scale.** About $2.31 of MRR per subscription means growth depends on volume.
