Traders who run several accounts across different platforms need every trade mirrored on each one, fast. TradeCopier.cloud says it does that in the cloud, with no VPS to rent and keep online. The Indonesian company, founded in January 2026, had 37 active subscriptions and $1,032 in MRR by November 2026, with $6,784 collected since launch ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/tradecopier-cloud)). Its price, $8 per account, is built for traders with many accounts.

## What does TradeCopier.cloud copy between trading platforms?

TradeCopier.cloud copies trades from one account to others across MT4, MT5, cTrader, MatchTrader and TradeLocker. The listing says it does this without a VPS or any additional software ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/tradecopier-cloud)).

> "Copy trades across multiple platforms with ease, without the need for VPS or any additional software." — TradeCopier.cloud value proposition on TrustMRR ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/tradecopier-cloud))

The company description is one line: a cloud-based trade copier. The stack listed is Next.js on the front end, with Express, Kubernetes and Paddle on the back end.

## Why does per-account pricing suit a trade copier?

Per-account pricing grows with the customer. A trader copying from one master account to many follower accounts pays $8 for each, so a customer who scales up their accounts pays more without a sales conversation ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/tradecopier-cloud)).

It also keeps the entry price low. A trader can test with one or two accounts before moving more of them over. The company reports about 1,234 users against 37 paid subscriptions, so most users have not yet paid.

## What lessons does TradeCopier.cloud offer fintech builders?

- **Remove the infrastructure the user hates.** No VPS is the core of the pitch.
- **Charge per unit of value.** $8 per account lets revenue grow as traders add accounts ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/tradecopier-cloud)).
- **Support every platform the customer uses.** MT4, MT5, cTrader, MatchTrader and TradeLocker cover a multi-broker trader.
- **Proven niches leave room for newcomers.** Heron Copier's results show demand that a cloud-only entrant can target.
