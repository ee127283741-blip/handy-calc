Retell AI ARR grew more than fivefold in about ten months while the team stayed near 20 people. The voice agent platform had $7.2M ARR with seven people in March 2025 ([media-reported, Lean AI Leaderboard](https://twitter.com/henrythe9ths/status/1900582696251703425)) and said it passed $40M ARR in January 2026 ([founder-reported, Retell AI release](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/01/29/3228780/0/en/Upgraded-Retell-AI-Voice-Platform-Enables-Corporate-Call-Centers-to-Deploy-Infinite-AI-Sales-and-Support-Agents-Across-Voice-Chat-Email-and-SMS-Company-Revenue-Now-Exceeds-40M-ARR.html)). Retell AI sells the plumbing for AI phone agents to developers and call centers, and its revenue grows as calls go through.

## What does Retell AI build?

Retell AI builds a platform for AI voice agents that answer and make phone calls: appointment booking, sales qualification, customer support and call center overflow. It came out of Y Combinator's Winter 2024 batch, founded in 2023 by Bing Wu (CEO), Zexia Zhang, Evie Wang, Weijia Yu and Todd Li ([Y Combinator](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/retell-ai)). Developers connect a language model, a voice and a phone number, and Retell handles the hard real-time parts, such as turn-taking and interruptions. Its January 2026 release said agents now also work across chat, email and SMS.

## How did Retell AI ARR grow so fast?

Retell AI ARR grew with call volume. The company says it launched in 2024 and grew to $40M ARR in 2025, with more than 40 million real-time AI phone calls a month ([founder-reported, Retell AI release](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/01/29/3228780/0/en/Upgraded-Retell-AI-Voice-Platform-Enables-Corporate-Call-Centers-to-Deploy-Infinite-AI-Sales-and-Support-Agents-Across-Voice-Chat-Email-and-SMS-Company-Revenue-Now-Exceeds-40M-ARR.html)). At $40M ARR that is about $3.3M a month (calc). Plan prices and total revenue are not disclosed in sources we could check.

## How lean is the Retell AI team?

Very lean. Henry Shi's Lean AI Leaderboard counted 21 full-time employees at $36M ARR in November 2025 ([media-reported, Lean AI Leaderboard](https://x.com/henrythe9ths/status/1991180919970161001)), or about $1.7M of ARR per employee (calc). In March 2025 it listed seven people at $7.2M ARR, about $1M each (calc). Those counts come from the leaderboard, which collects company-provided numbers, so we label them media-reported.

## How does Retell AI compare with Decagon and Sierra?

Retell AI sells the platform; [Decagon](/cases/decagon/) and [Sierra](/cases/sierra/) sell finished customer service agents to large brands. Decagon and Sierra sell to large companies through enterprise sales. Retell AI lets developers and call centers build their own voice agents and grows as their call volume grows. [ElevenLabs](/cases/elevenlabs/) is another neighbor, selling AI voices to developers.

Our reading: a self-serve developer platform can reach $40M ARR with a team of about 20 because customers do the integration work. The trade-off is control. Retell AI does not own the end customer relationship the way Sierra does, and each voice model or telephony provider it depends on is also a possible competitor.

## What can lean AI founders learn from Retell AI?

- **Sell picks and shovels.** A platform that others build voice agents on grows with every customer's call volume.
- **Self-serve scales without sales staff.** Retell AI reached about $1.7M of ARR per employee at 21 people.
- **Solve the hard real-time part.** Turn-taking and latency are what make a phone agent feel human, and that is the product.
- **Publish milestones in the release headline.** Retell AI put "$40M ARR" in its press release title, which made the figure easy to cite.
