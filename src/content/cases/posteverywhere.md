Social media schedulers are one of the most crowded software categories there is. PostEverywhere found a fresh angle anyway: build the scheduler for AI agents, not just for people clicking through a calendar. Fifteen months after its August 2025 founding, the bootstrapped UK company had 463 paying subscriptions and $65,516 in lifetime revenue ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/posteverywhere)).

## Why build PostEverywhere around AI agents instead of a calendar?

PostEverywhere exposes full CLI, MCP and API access so Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor, Grok or any other AI agent can manage a brand's social media end to end, according to its TrustMRR listing ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/posteverywhere)). The bet is that more marketing work will be delegated to assistants, and those assistants need a reliable place to publish.

> "PostEverywhere is a Social Media Scheduling Tool built for AI Agents." — PostEverywhere company description ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/posteverywhere))

The audience is broad: content creators, business owners, social media managers and entrepreneurs.

## How does a low price shape PostEverywhere's growth?

PostEverywhere competes partly on price, with plans from $9 a month and a listing that calls it one of the most affordable tools in its niche ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/posteverywhere)). Low prices need volume, and roughly 2,831 users against 463 subscriptions shows a wide free or trial funnel feeding paid plans. The site also carries an Ahrefs Domain Rating of 64 on TrustMRR, high for a company this young, which points to search as a channel.

## How is PostEverywhere different from Bazzly and aiCarousels?

PostEverywhere, [Bazzly](/cases/bazzly/) and [aiCarousels](/cases/aicarousels/) all sell AI-era marketing software, but PostEverywhere is the only one designed to be operated by another AI.

Our observation: aiCarousels makes content, while PostEverywhere distributes it. Being the publishing endpoint an agent calls could make PostEverywhere stickier than a single-format creation tool.

## Which takeaways from PostEverywhere matter for scheduling tools?

- **Reposition a crowded category for a new buyer.** "For AI agents" turns a commodity scheduler into a distinct product.
- **MCP and API access are distribution.** Every assistant that can call the tool is a potential channel.
- **Cheap plans demand a big funnel.** About 2,831 users support 463 paid subscriptions.
- **Bootstrapped can still be fast.** The company reached $65,516 in lifetime revenue in about 15 months without outside funding.
