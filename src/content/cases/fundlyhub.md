Most startups on TrustMRR are judged by one number: monthly recurring revenue. Fundlyhub breaks that habit. The fundraising platform showed just $2 in MRR in November 2026, yet $11,691 moved through its Stripe account in the previous 30 days and $70,773 since it started ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/fundlyhub)). It is a reminder that the MRR column can badly misdescribe a payments business.

## What kind of platform is Fundlyhub?

Fundlyhub is an online fundraising platform. Anyone can start a campaign for a person or cause, and donors give through the site. TrustMRR describes it as a fundraising platform with one hub for every cause, and lists its market as Community.

> "Fundraising platform - one hub for every cause" — Fundlyhub company description ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/fundlyhub))

The company website calls FundlyHub "the trust network for online fundraising" and says it is a DBA of CYTY Inc. Featured campaigns on the homepage are personal medical and family appeals, several written in Russian ([founder-reported, FundlyHub](https://fundlyhub.org)).

## How much money does Fundlyhub process each month?

Fundlyhub took in $11,691 in the last 30 days before November 9, 2026, and $70,773 all time, according to Stripe data on TrustMRR ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/fundlyhub)). Its MRR was $2 from a single active subscription.

## How does Fundlyhub earn money with a 0% platform fee?

Fundlyhub says it does not take a platform fee. The website states that a donation goes to the person being helped minus the card fee, and that tips to FundlyHub are optional ([founder-reported, FundlyHub](https://fundlyhub.org)). Optional tips are the only revenue source the site names.

That model trades a cut of every donation for trust. A donor who sees no fee is more likely to give, and a campaign owner has a reason to pick it over platforms that take a percentage. The cost is that the company's own take depends on donor goodwill.

Our observation: BreadSync and TrustMRR can be valued on MRR. Fundlyhub cannot. Its 30-day volume is close to BreadSync's monthly figure, but a buyer or reader would need to know the tip share before calling it a revenue business of the same size.

## What can marketplace founders learn from Fundlyhub?

- **MRR is the wrong lens for payments.** Fundlyhub shows $2 MRR beside $70,773 of verified volume.
- **A zero fee is a trust signal.** The 0% platform fee is the headline on its homepage.
- **Volume and income are different numbers.** Without a tip breakdown, gross volume overstates what the company keeps.
- **Community campaigns can travel.** Featured appeals in Russian suggest a diaspora audience, not only US donors.
