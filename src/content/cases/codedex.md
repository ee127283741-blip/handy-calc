Codédex looks like a Game Boy cartridge and teaches Python. Founder Sonny Li, who describes going from a failing computer science major to teaching code ([Product Hunt](https://www.producthunt.com/@sonnynomnom)), wrapped beginner lessons in pixel art and quests. In November 2026 the site showed $80,532 in MRR and $1,521,108 in all-time revenue ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/codedex)), most of it from small monthly payments.

## How does Codédex turn coding lessons into a game?

Codédex turns lessons into an 8-bit adventure, where learners move through chapters and exercises like levels. The company's own pitch is about tone more than syllabus.

> "The most fun and beginner-friendly way to learn to code." — Codédex listing ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/codedex))

The TrustMRR listing tags Codédex as education, dev tools, AI and games, and lists a stack of TypeScript, React, Vercel, Google Cloud and Stripe. The free tier offers 250+ hours of interactive lessons. Paid learners join the Club, which starts at $9.99 a month ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/codedex)).

## Who built Codédex before it launched?

Sonny Li built Codédex after teaching code for years. Li's Product Hunt bio says they taught computer science at Columbia and NYU ([Product Hunt](https://www.producthunt.com/@sonnynomnom)), and the same profile lists the title Founder & CEO. TrustMRR dates the company to November 2022.

That background shows in the product. Codédex aims at people who have never coded, and it spends its effort on making the first hour feel easy rather than on advanced tracks. Team size and funding are not disclosed on the pages we used.

## How much money does Codédex earn per learner?

Codédex earns about $12.53 a month per active subscription, based on $80,532 MRR over 6,429 subscriptions ([calc, from TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/codedex)). That sits just above the $9.99 Club starting price, which fits a mix of monthly and higher plans.

## How does Codédex compare with Speak and Coconote?

Codédex is far smaller than both, and its price is lower too. All three are consumer education subscriptions, but Speak and Coconote use AI as the core product, while Codédex uses game design as the hook.

Our observation: Codédex's free tier is unusually generous, 250+ hours, which builds a huge audience but a low paid share. Speak and Coconote put more value behind the paywall. Codédex's choice looks closer to a community strategy, where a large free crowd brings word of mouth and a small slice pays.

## What can edtech founders learn from Codédex?

- **Style is a real differentiator.** A pixel-art coding school reached $80,532 MRR in a crowded category ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/codedex)).
- **Teachers make credible founders.** Sonny Li's classroom background shaped a product built around the beginner's first hour.
- **A big free tier needs a cheap upgrade.** At $9.99 to start, the Club asks little of a learner who already likes the lessons.
- **Count paying share, not just users.** Two million claimed users and 6,429 subscriptions tell different stories, and both matter.
