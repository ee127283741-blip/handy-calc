ShiftNex ARR reached about $1M within five months, according to a Kenyan news outlet, and its founder did not write the code. Allan Njoroge, who grew up in Eldoret, Kenya, built ShiftNex, a US platform that matches healthcare workers with open shifts, on Lovable despite having no formal coding background. Mwakilishi reported KSh 129M in annual recurring revenue within five months and more than 5,000 healthcare professionals using it ([media-reported, Mwakilishi](https://mwakilishi.com/amp/node/374137)). Njoroge's edge was not software; it was years in healthcare staffing.

## What did Allan Njoroge use to build ShiftNex?

Allan Njoroge built ShiftNex with Lovable, the AI app builder, instead of hiring an engineering team ([Mwakilishi](https://mwakilishi.com/amp/node/374137)). Before ShiftNex he founded Actriv, a healthcare staffing agency in Washington State, so he knew how facilities fill shifts and where agencies charge the most. ShiftNex lets facilities post shifts and nurses, nursing assistants and other professionals pick them up directly. Team size and prices are not disclosed.

> "smart care delivery platform that helps healthcare facilities manage their workforce, reduce agency dependency, and collaborate with local professionals" — ShiftNex on Product Hunt ([founder-reported, Product Hunt](https://www.producthunt.com/products/shiftnex-network/launches/shiftnex-ai-network))

## What ARR has ShiftNex reported?

ShiftNex reached KSh 129M in annual recurring revenue within five months, Mwakilishi reported in April 2026 ([media-reported, Mwakilishi](https://mwakilishi.com/amp/node/374137)). At roughly 129 Kenyan shillings to the US dollar, that is about $1M, or about $83K a month (calc). More than 5,000 healthcare professionals used the platform. The article is the only source for these figures, and it did not give a US-dollar number, so the conversion is ours. Total revenue is not disclosed.

## Is ShiftNex really five months old?

Not exactly. The five-month figure refers to the Lovable-built platform. Business listings such as G2 show ShiftNex serving customers since 2022 from Tacoma, Washington ([G2](https://www.g2.com/products/shiftnex-ai)), which fits Njoroge's earlier staffing work in Washington State. The likely reading is that an existing staffing business moved to a new AI-built platform, with existing relationships helping the fast ARR. We report both facts and do not combine them.

## How does ShiftNex compare with Plinq and Lumoo?

ShiftNex, [Plinq](/cases/plinq/) and [Lumoo](/cases/lumoo/) are Lovable-built businesses whose founders brought industry experience rather than engineering. Plinq's founder was a growth marketer; Lumoo's founders came from fashion retail; Njoroge came from healthcare staffing. ShiftNex reports the largest ARR of the three, and its customers, healthcare facilities, have urgent and recurring staffing needs.

Our reading: the strongest non-coder businesses are not new ideas from outsiders, but insiders rebuilding their own industry's software. Njoroge knew the customers, the pain and the pricing before Lovable wrote a line of code.

## What lessons does ShiftNex offer industry insiders?

- **Rebuild the software you know you need.** A staffing founder built the staffing tool he wanted.
- **Existing relationships speed revenue.** Prior industry work likely helped ShiftNex reach large ARR fast.
- **Check the timeline.** A "five-month" platform can sit on a business that is years old.
- **Convert currencies carefully.** KSh 129M is about $1M only at the exchange rate used.
