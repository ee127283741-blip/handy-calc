Getting an AI agent to post on X usually means applying for a developer account and paying for API access. OpenTweet skips that step and sells itself as the posting layer agents plug into. Started in Serbia in October 2025 and run by one person, it reached 240 active subscriptions and $30,358 in all-time revenue by November 2026 ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/opentweet)).

## How does OpenTweet let AI agents post to X?

OpenTweet automates posting to X, Bluesky and LinkedIn through an API, an MCP server and integrations with agents such as OpenClaw and Hermes. Its stated problem is posting to X and other networks without needing a developer account ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/opentweet)).

> "The posting layer for X, built for AI agents" — OpenTweet value proposition ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/opentweet))

The audience is developers and AI agents, and the listed stack is Next.js, MongoDB, Auth0 and Docker, with Paddle handling payments.

## Why does OpenTweet start at $11.99 after a free week?

A low price fits a crowded market and a technical buyer who tries before paying. OpenTweet gives 7 days free, then starts at $11.99 a month ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/opentweet)). Of about 14,000 reported users, 240 hold active subscriptions, so the trial is a wide funnel with a narrow paid end.

The founder's own following of about 20.9k on X is a natural launch channel for a tool about posting to X.

## How is OpenTweet different from Post Bridge and SuperX?

All three automate social posting, but they target different users.

| Company | Main user | MRR (Nov 2026) | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| OpenTweet | Developers and AI agents | $4,504 | [verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/opentweet) |
| [Post Bridge](/cases/post-bridge/) | Creators posting to many platforms | $49,650 | [verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/post-bridge) |
| [SuperX](/cases/superx/) | People growing an X audience | $18,945 | [verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/superx) |

Our observation: Post Bridge and SuperX sell dashboards to humans. OpenTweet sells plumbing to software, which is a smaller market today but one that grows with every agent framework. If agents become the main users of social APIs, an API-first product is in the right place.

## What lessons does OpenTweet offer API-first founders?

- **Remove a gatekeeper.** Posting without an X developer account is the core value.
- **Build for agents, not only people.** MCP and agent integrations put OpenTweet inside new workflows.
- **A cheap entry tier still averages up.** Revenue per subscription is above the $11.99 starting price.
- **A solo founder can serve 240 subscribers.** A one-person team built $30,358 lifetime revenue in about a year ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/opentweet)).
