When AI chatbots and voice agents became a service that agencies could sell, those agencies hit a practical problem. Their clients needed somewhere to log in, see results and pay. ChatDash sells that missing layer as a white-label portal. In November 2026 it showed $18,470 in MRR and $602,356 in all-time revenue from 104 active subscriptions ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/chatdash-llc)).

## What gap in the AI agency market does ChatDash fill?

ChatDash gives agencies a branded interface for the AI agents they build for clients. The TrustMRR listing describes a white-label AI assistant dashboard where clients manage their own knowledge bases, with Voiceflow and OpenAI Assistant API integrations ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/chatdash-llc)). The agency builds the agent with one of those tools, and ChatDash becomes the face the client sees.

That is a classic picks-and-shovels business. ChatDash doesn't need to win the agent market, it only needs agencies selling agents to keep growing.

## How has ChatDash moved toward voice agents?

The current website leans into voice. It says ChatDash works with agents built on Retell, Vapi or ElevenLabs, and splits the product into automation, delivery and billing ([founder-reported, ChatDash](https://chat-dash.com)). Automation is a visual workflow builder that triggers on events such as call start and call end. Delivery is the client portal with per-client permissions. Billing connects to Stripe for usage-based, subscription, setup-fee and outcome-based charges.

> "Trusted by 600+ Agencies" — ChatDash, homepage claim ([founder-reported, ChatDash](https://chat-dash.com))

Outcome-based billing, such as charging per booked appointment, lets an agency price on results instead of hours.

## How much MRR does ChatDash earn per agency?

ChatDash earned $18,470 in MRR and $15,455 over the last 30 days in November 2026, from 104 active subscriptions ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/chatdash-llc)). All-time revenue was $602,356 in about 31 months.

MRR divided by subscriptions is about $177.60 per agency ([calc, from TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/chatdash-llc)). All-time revenue is about 33 times current MRR, which suggests revenue was at least as high in earlier months. The 600+ agencies claim on the homepage is far above 104 paying subscriptions, so it likely counts past or trial accounts.

## How does ChatDash differ from Chatbase and Retell AI?

[Chatbase](/cases/chatbase/) sells a custom chatbot directly to a business, and [Retell AI](/cases/retell-ai/) sells the voice-agent platform that developers build on. ChatDash sits between them and the end client, selling to the agency that resells the agent.

Our reading: that position is both safe and exposed. It is safe because ChatDash gains whenever any agent platform grows. It is exposed because platforms such as Retell can add their own white-label portals, and the move from Voiceflow toward voice shows ChatDash has to follow where agencies go.

## What lessons does ChatDash hold for AI infrastructure founders?

- **Serve the reseller.** ChatDash sells to agencies, not end businesses ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/chatdash-llc)).
- **Own the client-facing layer.** The portal is what the agency's customer sees every day.
- **Build billing in.** Usage and outcome-based charges make the portal hard to replace.
- **Follow the agent platforms.** ChatDash moved its pitch toward Retell, Vapi and ElevenLabs.
