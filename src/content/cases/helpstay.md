Most startups on revenue leaderboards are a year or two old. HelpStay.com started in Ireland in April 2014, long before the current AI wave, and has quietly collected $440,728 from travellers and hosts since ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/helpstay-com)). It is a useful reminder of what a small, steady online marketplace looks like after a decade.

## What does HelpStay offer travellers and hosts?

HelpStay is a work exchange platform: hosts get help, and travellers get food and accommodation in return ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/helpstay-com)). The listing says it offers volunteer stays in more than 100 countries, and the homepage advertises more than 2,500 free volunteering stays in 2026 ([founder-reported, HelpStay](https://helpstay.com)).

> "Give a little help, stay for free" — HelpStay value proposition on TrustMRR ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/helpstay-com))

The problem it lists is affordable travel through volunteering. The stay itself is free; HelpStay earns from the subscriptions that give access to the platform.

## Why is HelpStay's revenue so seasonal?

The last 30 days brought in $1,336, about half the $2,529 MRR figure ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/helpstay-com)). A gap like that is typical when customers pay yearly: MRR spreads each annual payment across 12 months, while cash arrives in bursts when people plan trips.

November is the low season for northern-hemisphere travel planning, so a quiet month is not surprising. The listing gives no monthly history, so we cannot show the yearly cycle directly.

## What lessons does HelpStay's decade teach marketplace founders?

- **Charge for access, not the transaction.** HelpStay keeps stays free and sells subscriptions ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/helpstay-com)).
- **Longevity compounds.** Twelve years of small payments added up to $440,728.
- **Read MRR with the seasons in mind.** Annual memberships make cash and MRR diverge in quiet months.
- **Supply is the moat.** More than 2,500 listed stays across 100+ countries is what travellers pay to reach.
