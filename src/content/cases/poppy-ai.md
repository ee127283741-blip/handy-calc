In September 2024, Naz Dumanskyy and his co-founder Qazi were two months away from bankruptcy. They had quit everything, moved to San Francisco and spent six months building tools that all failed. Then a TikTok about their latest product went viral, and in 10 days they made $100K. By May 2025, Poppy AI was making about $400K a month ([founder-reported, Starter Story](https://www.starterstory.com/stories/poppyai-write-emails-that-sell)).

## Why did two YouTubers build an AI whiteboard?

Five years earlier, the two had started a YouTube channel together and grown it to 1 million subscribers. They hated running it. In 2024 they left everything behind with one goal: build software they would use themselves every single day.

The idea came from Qazi. They disliked ChatGPT because it felt linear and restricted, but they loved whiteboard tools like Miro and Whimsical. Poppy AI combines the two: a collaborative AI whiteboard where creators can drop in Instagram posts, TikToks, Facebook ads, YouTube and LinkedIn videos, and use AI to model new viral content and ads from them.

## What failed before Poppy AI found its buyers?

Selling was the hard part. They made YouTube videos, ran webinars for content creators and copywriters and even tried selling on the street. For three months the product barely sold.

What finally worked was a creator who made a video about Poppy AI that brought in $200K+ over two months ([founder-reported, Starter Story](https://www.starterstory.com/stories/poppyai-write-emails-that-sell)). The founders realised they had been selling to the wrong audience, and that different platforms do different jobs.

> "We learned that on short form people learn about the product, and on youtube people buy the product." — Naz Dumanskyy ([founder-reported, Starter Story](https://www.starterstory.com/stories/poppyai-write-emails-that-sell))

## How do affiliates power Poppy AI's growth?

Poppy AI doubled down on what worked: creators and affiliates making content about it. The affiliate program is designed so creators can earn real money, and the founders built community into the product itself.

| Growth lever | What Poppy AI does |
|---|---|
| Affiliates | One creator earned $40K in commissions in a month; another about $10K a month |
| Referrals | 30% of revenue comes from customers referring others |
| Community | All customers in a Telegram group, with weekly community calls |
| Onboarding | One-on-one onboarding for every customer; 30 calls a day for two weeks during one rush |
| Product updates | Weekly releases based on customer feedback |
| Source | [founder-reported, Starter Story](https://www.starterstory.com/stories/poppyai-write-emails-that-sell) |

## How much money does Poppy AI make a month?

In May 2025, Poppy AI reported about 5,000 creators and about $400K a month in revenue, with two founders and ten employees ([founder-reported, Starter Story](https://www.starterstory.com/stories/poppyai-write-emails-that-sell)). Total revenue and pricing are not disclosed.

The path was steep: six months of failed tools in 2024, a viral TikTok in September 2024 that brought $100K in 10 days, then a creator video that brought $200K+ in two months, and about $400K a month by May 2025 ([founder-reported, Starter Story](https://www.starterstory.com/stories/poppyai-write-emails-that-sell)).

## How does Poppy AI compare with Arcads and Leadmore AI?

[Arcads](/cases/arcads/) also sells AI to people who make ads, but generates finished video ads with AI actors. Poppy AI is a thinking and drafting space where creators model new content on what already works. Both grew through the same formats they help customers make: short videos and creator content.

[Leadmore AI](/cases/leadmore-ai/) sells growth on one platform, Reddit. Poppy AI is platform-agnostic, pulling content from every major network into one board. Where Leadmore AI grows through conversations and a private community, Poppy AI grows through affiliates who are paid well enough to make Poppy AI content their job.

## What can creator-founders learn from Poppy AI?

- **Build the tool you use every day.** Poppy AI came from the founders' own frustration with linear AI chat.
- **Find the right audience before scaling.** Three months of failed selling ended when they found the creators who cared.
- **Short video creates awareness; long video sells.** That split shaped where they put effort.
- **Make affiliates rich and they will sell for you.** Big commissions turned creators into Poppy AI's sales team.
