Dashla sells Tesla owners an extra screen made from a device they already carry: their iPhone. Founded in March 2026, the iPhone app had 1,564 active subscriptions and $88,912 in all-time revenue by November 2026 ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/dashla)). It is a narrow product for one car brand, and that narrowness is the point.

## What does the Dashla app show Tesla drivers?

Dashla turns an iPhone into a live driving display for Tesla owners. The company-written listing on TrustMRR describes it as a mobile dashboard app for Tesla drivers that shows real-time driving data on the phone ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/dashla)). It is distributed through the [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6760206753) rather than a separate website.

> "Dashla is a mobile dashboard app for Tesla drivers that turns an iPhone into a real-time driving display," — Dashla listing ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/dashla))

TrustMRR tags it Utilities, IoT Hardware and Mobile Apps, with a consumer audience.

## How much revenue does Dashla make from in-app purchases?

Dashla had $4,151 MRR in November 2026, $6,995 in the last 30 days and $88,912 all time ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/dashla)). The figures come from a RevenueCat API key rather than Stripe, which is normal for App Store subscriptions.

| Plan or metric (Nov 2026) | Value | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Premium Monthly | $2.99 | [founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/dashla) |
| Premium Annually | $29.99 | [founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/dashla) |
| Premium Lifetime | $139.99 | [founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/dashla) |
| MRR | $4,151 | [verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/dashla) |
| Active subscriptions | 1,564 | [verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/dashla) |

Lifetime purchases help explain the numbers. A $139.99 one-off payment adds to revenue but not to MRR, which is why the last 30 days brought in more than MRR and why all-time revenue is large for an app founded in March 2026 ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/dashla)). The split between plans is not disclosed.

## Who builds Dashla, and how big is the team?

The founder of Dashla is not disclosed on its TrustMRR page, and neither are the team size, location or marketing channels ([TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/dashla)). The only stack item listed is RevenueCat for subscriptions. That leaves the product and its numbers to speak for themselves.

## How does Dashla compare with Brawler and Stoppr?

Dashla is a consumer iPhone app with a subscription paywall, like two other app cases on this site.

Our observation: Brawler and Stoppr serve broad audiences around fitness and habits. Dashla serves only Tesla owners, and its listing does not mention AI. Its ceiling is lower, but its buyer is easy to find, and a lifetime plan lets enthusiasts pay once and generously.

## What lessons does Dashla hold for niche app makers?

- **Pick a buyer you can name.** Tesla owners are a clear, reachable group with a shared complaint.
- **Offer a lifetime plan.** A $139.99 option pulls revenue forward that MRR alone does not show.
- **Use the phone people already have.** Dashla replaces a hardware accessory with software.
- **Small MRR can hide a real business.** $88,912 all time within about eight months is solid for a one-brand utility.
