Growing on X is a game of volume and timing, and SuperX sells the tooling for both. It finds posts that already work in a niche, drafts new ones in the user's voice, schedules them and measures what happened. In November 2026, about 15 months after its founding, it showed $18,945 in MRR and $266,915 in all-time revenue ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/superx)).

## What does SuperX do for people growing on X?

SuperX covers the full loop of posting on X. Its website lists discovery of high-performing posts and trends, AI drafts and rewrites in the user's voice, a scheduler that picks times when the audience is active, a feed of posts worth replying to, and automations such as auto-DMs and auto-retweets ([founder-reported, SuperX](https://superx.so)). A Chrome extension adds analytics on profiles and posts inside X itself.

> "Create, schedule posts, and engage strategically." — SuperX, value proposition ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/superx))

## Who is behind SuperX?

TrustMRR lists Rob Hallam as the founder, with about 68,200 followers on X, and gives France as the country and August 2025 as the founding date ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/superx)). That audience matters: a tool for X growth is easier to sell when its founder visibly uses it. Team size and funding are not disclosed.

The site claims more than 11,294 creators, 400K+ posts and replies sent, and 5B+ impressions by its users. Those are the company's own numbers.

## How has SuperX priced its Pro, Advanced and Ultra tiers?

SuperX uses three tiers based on connected accounts, posts, AI credits and automations ([founder-reported, SuperX](https://superx.so)).

| Plan | Price (Nov 2026) | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Pro | $49 a month | [founder-reported, SuperX](https://superx.so) |
| Advanced | $49 a month launch price, normally $99 | [founder-reported, SuperX](https://superx.so) |
| Ultra | $199 a month | [founder-reported, SuperX](https://superx.so) |

Setting Advanced at the same launch price as Pro pushes most new buyers to the middle tier. Ultra adds 10 accounts, 3,000 posts a month and API access, which points at agencies and power users.

## How much money does SuperX make from subscriptions?

SuperX had $18,945 in MRR and $17,497 in revenue over the last 30 days in November 2026, from 451 active subscriptions ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/superx)). All-time revenue was $266,915.

MRR divided by subscriptions is about $42.01 ([calc, from TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/superx)), a little below the $49 entry price. Older plans or discounts probably explain the gap. The listing claims about 11,294 users, so only about 4% pay ([calc, from TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/superx)), a typical ratio for a tool with a free extension.

## How does SuperX stack up against Tweet Hunter and Creator Buddy?

[Tweet Hunter](/cases/tweet-hunter/) built the AI growth-on-X category and was sold to Lempire in 2022. [Creator Buddy](/cases/creator-buddy/) is another AI tool for X, launched with a large founder audience. SuperX enters a market both have proved, and adds automations and a Chrome extension for analytics.

Our reading: the category rewards founders with audiences, and all three cases lean on that. The product features are close to each other, so distribution and price decide most sales. SuperX's $49 launch price for its middle tier is an aggressive move in that contest.

## What can creator-tool founders learn from SuperX?

- **Sell to a channel you already win on.** SuperX's founder has a large X following ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/superx)).
- **Use a free extension as the top of the funnel.** Analytics inside X brings users who may later pay.
- **Anchor the middle tier.** A launch price equal to Pro steers buyers to Advanced.
- **Expect a small paying share.** About 4% of claimed users paid in November 2026.
