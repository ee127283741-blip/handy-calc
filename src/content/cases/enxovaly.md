Expecting parents juggle checklists, clothing sizes and gifts from relatives who all want to help. Enxovaly, a Brazilian app, puts that whole preparation into one place. Founded in June 2026, it had 3,058 active subscriptions and $18,258 in revenue over the last 30 days by November 2026 ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/enxovaly)), a fast start for a five-month-old consumer app.

## What does the Enxovaly app help parents organise?

Enxovaly keeps a baby registry, clothing, gifts and pregnancy preparation in one app ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/enxovaly)). Parents build a checklist, sort clothes by size, save products from any store and share a gift registry so family members avoid buying duplicate presents.

> "Plan your baby checklist, organize clothes by size, save products from any store, and share a gift registry with family." — Enxovaly company description ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/enxovaly))

The listing also mentions gift reservation tracking, a pregnancy timeline and a community for mothers.

## Why does Enxovaly offer so many Pro plans?

Enxovaly lists weekly, monthly, quarterly and annual Pro subscriptions, from $2.99 a week to $19.99 a year ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/enxovaly)). A pregnancy lasts months, not years, so parents may prefer a short commitment, while cheap annual plans capture families who want to keep the registry after birth. The app is free to download, with in-app purchases, and holds a 5.0 rating from 3 App Store ratings on the listing.

## What lessons does Enxovaly teach consumer app builders?

- **Solve one stressful life stage end to end.** Checklist, wardrobe, registry and timeline sit in one app.
- **Shared features spread the product.** A family gift registry invites more people in.
- **Match plan length to the life stage.** Weekly to annual options fit a time-bound need.
- **Read collected revenue next to MRR.** $18,258 in 30 days versus $5,650 MRR shows how annual plans shape the numbers.
