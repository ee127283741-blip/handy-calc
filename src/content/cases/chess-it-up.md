Chess engines are free, but understanding what they say is not. Chess It Up wraps the Stockfish engine in a web app that explains your games, and it gives most of that away. The bootstrapped US platform, founded in October 2025, had earned $2,540 all time by November 2026, and $1,612 of it arrived in the last 30 days ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/chess-it-up)). Most of its lifetime revenue came in a single month.

## What does the Chess It Up analyzer do?

Chess It Up lets players load a game and run it through Stockfish to see strategy insights and move accuracy, according to its listing ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/chess-it-up)). It targets chess players and enthusiasts and is built with Next.js, Tailwind CSS, Supabase and Stripe.

> "Free Online Chess Analysis Tool - Stockfish Game Analyzer" — Chess It Up listing on TrustMRR ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/chess-it-up))

The value proposition reads like a search query: the kind of words a player might type after a lost game.

## Where does Chess It Up find chess players?

Chess It Up lists six social channels: Facebook, Instagram, Reddit, TikTok, YouTube and X ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/chess-it-up)). That is a wide spread for a young bootstrapped product. Its domain rating was 8 out of 100, so search backlinks were still thin.

Chess content travels well on short video and Reddit, where players share puzzles and blunders. A free analyzer gives that content a natural link destination.

## What lessons does Chess It Up offer bootstrappers?

- **Give the engine away, sell the depth.** Stockfish is free; the paid tier sells extra insight on top.
- **Name the product after the search.** "Stockfish Game Analyzer" matches what players actually type.
- **A strong month can dwarf the history.** 63% of lifetime revenue arrived in 30 days.
- **Copying a proven niche is fine.** Chessigma shows the demand exists; execution and channels decide the rest.
