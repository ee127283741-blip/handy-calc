A logo saved as a PNG looks fine on screen and useless to a laser cutter. VectoSolve fixes that gap for crafters, and by November 2026 it had earned $23,081 doing so ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/vectosolve)). The tool was founded in France in October 2025, is run by one person, and has 1,892 paying customers across 98 countries, according to the company.

## What problem does VectoSolve solve for Cricut and laser users?

VectoSolve rebuilds raster images as vectors and exports them in the file format a cutting, laser or embroidery machine expects. The listing explains that a raster logo blurs when scaled and that these machines cannot read it at all ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/vectosolve)).

> "VectoSolve turns PNG, JPG and WEBP images into clean, editable SVG vectors." — VectoSolve company description ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/vectosolve))

Exports include SVG, DXF, EPS, AI, PDF, PNG, G-code and the embroidery formats DST, PES and JEF, with a local centerline engine for line art.

## How is VectoSolve priced?

VectoSolve mixes one-off and recurring payments. Users get one free watermarked preview per day without signing up, then can buy credit packs from $6.99 or subscribe from $7.99 a month or $49 a year ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/vectosolve)). The company says the product also ships as a Chrome and Firefox extension. Marketing runs on SEO and a blog.

## What can tool builders learn from VectoSolve?

- **Serve a machine's file format.** Supporting DST, PES and G-code opens a maker audience.
- **Credits suit occasional users.** One-off packs push 30-day revenue above MRR.
- **A free daily preview lowers friction.** No signup is needed to try it.
- **Small but broad.** 1,892 paying customers in 98 countries, run by one person.
