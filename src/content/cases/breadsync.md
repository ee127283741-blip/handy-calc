Sportsbooks hand out welcome bonuses to win new customers. BreadSync sells bettors the math to turn those bonuses into cash, and it charges $197 a month for it. Eight months after a March 2026 founding, the tool showed $6,281 in monthly recurring revenue and $57,046 in all-time revenue ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/breadsync)) from only 60 subscriptions.

## What does BreadSync do with sportsbook bonuses?

BreadSync is a sportsbook bonus tool for bettors in the US and Canada. The homepage says it shows what each welcome offer in your state is worth, sizes the hedge bets that turn bonus bets into withdrawable cash, compares odds across 130+ sportsbooks to find low-hold and arbitrage pairs, and tracks every bet, deposit and withdrawal ([founder-reported, BreadSync homepage](https://breadsync.com/)). It also runs a Discord community.

The original TrustMRR listing describes it more plainly, as a cloud analytics dashboard for portfolio performance, profit and loss and transaction history.

> "Work the Bonuses. Track What You Keep." — BreadSync homepage ([founder-reported, BreadSync](https://breadsync.com/))

## Why can BreadSync charge $197 a month?

BreadSync prices against the value of the bonuses, not against other software. Its homepage calculator shows a sample $500 deposit worth $1,865 in offers ([founder-reported, BreadSync homepage](https://breadsync.com/)), so a user who works several sportsbooks can see the fee as small next to the payout. The free tracker and calculators draw people in, and Pro adds the strategy layer.

The risk is the same as the appeal. Bonus offers are finite: once a bettor has used up the welcome deals in their state, the reason to keep paying shrinks. That may explain why last-30-day revenue came in below MRR.

## What lessons does BreadSync offer niche tool builders?

- **Price against the payoff.** A $197 plan reads as cheap beside a sample $1,865 in bonus value ([founder-reported, BreadSync homepage](https://breadsync.com/)).
- **Give the tracker away.** Free tools build the habit; the paid tier sells the strategy.
- **Finite value means churn.** Welcome bonuses run out, so revenue below MRR is a warning to watch.
- **Few customers can be enough.** 60 subscriptions produced $6,281 MRR within eight months ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/breadsync)).
