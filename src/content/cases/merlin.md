## Origin of the idea
Switching tabs to an AI chat breaks your flow. The founders put the assistant where people already work: a shortcut on any web page to summarise, reply or rewrite.

## How they started
They shipped a Chrome extension soon after ChatGPT launched, riding the first wave of "ChatGPT in your browser" searches on the Chrome Web Store.

## Tools & tech stack
A browser extension that calls several third-party models (GPT, Claude, Gemini and others) and can read the current page, YouTube transcripts or email threads.

## Marketing
Chrome Web Store search and ratings, plus press releases about user milestones and a Forbes 30 Under 30 Asia listing for a co-founder.

## Sales & pricing
Freemium: a limited daily free allowance and a paid Pro plan with higher limits and premium models.
