ElevenLabs revenue went from a startup figure to a scale-up figure in nine months. The voice AI company said it closed 2025 with more than $330M in annual recurring revenue ([founder-reported, ElevenLabs](https://elevenlabs.io/blog/series-d)). By September 2026, co-founder and CEO Mati Staniszewski told TechCrunch it was at $600M ARR ([founder-reported, TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/24/twenty-minutes-with-the-ceo-of-elevenlabs-now-reportedly-valued-at-22-billion/)). Over the same months its valuation doubled to $22B. It all started with two teenagers in Poland laughing at badly dubbed films.

## How did bad movie dubbing in Poland lead to ElevenLabs?

ElevenLabs started from a familiar annoyance: foreign films dubbed in one flat voice. The company says Mati Staniszewski and Piotr Dabkowski are high school friends who grew up together in Poland and were "often amused by the 'rough' dubbing of American movies they watched" ([founder-reported, ElevenLabs press page](https://elevenlabs.io/press)). Staniszewski worked at Palantir before becoming CEO, and Dabkowski, now CTO, worked at Google. TechCrunch called them childhood friends and named the same dubbing problem as their inspiration ([media-reported, TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2023/06/20/voice-generating-platform-elevenlabs-raises-19m-launches-detection-tool/)). The company was founded in 2022 and is based in London ([media-reported, CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/02/04/nvidia-backed-ai-startup-elevenlabs-11-billion-valuation.html)).

> "We started by building a voice that could sound human - and we did." — Piotr Dabkowski ([founder-reported, ElevenLabs](https://elevenlabs.io/blog/series-d))

## How quickly did ElevenLabs find its first users?

It found them within months. ElevenLabs launched in beta in late January 2023, and by its June 2023 Series A it said it had more than a million registered users ([media-reported, TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2023/06/20/voice-generating-platform-elevenlabs-raises-19m-launches-detection-tool/)). That $19M round was co-led by Nat Friedman and Daniel Gross alongside Andreessen Horowitz, and a person familiar with the deal put the post-money valuation at about $99M. By January 2025 the company said employees at more than 60% of Fortune 500 companies used its tools, and that its headcount had grown from 30 to 120 in a year ([founder-reported, ElevenLabs](https://elevenlabs.io/blog/series-c)).

## Who pays for ElevenLabs voices today?

Two groups pay: individual creators on self-serve plans, and large companies. In November 2026 the pricing page listed a free plan, then Starter at $6, Creator at $22, Pro at $99, Scale at $299 and Business at $990 a month, with custom Enterprise pricing ([founder-reported, ElevenLabs pricing page](https://elevenlabs.io/pricing)). At the enterprise end, CNBC said end-of-2025 revenue was driven by customers including Deutsche Telekom and Revolut ([media-reported, CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/02/04/nvidia-backed-ai-startup-elevenlabs-11-billion-valuation.html)). The company also pays out to some users. By January 2025, creators had shared more than 5,000 voices in its Voice Library and earned more than $2M in rewards ([founder-reported, ElevenLabs](https://elevenlabs.io/blog/series-c)).

## How did ElevenLabs revenue climb from $330M to $600M ARR?

ElevenLabs revenue grew about 80% in roughly nine months, using the company's own figures. It closed 2025 with more than $330M ARR ([founder-reported, ElevenLabs](https://elevenlabs.io/blog/series-d)). A May 2026 post gave a slightly different end-of-2025 figure, $350M, and said the company passed $500M ARR in the first four months of 2026 ([founder-reported, ElevenLabs](https://elevenlabs.io/blog/500m-arr-and-new-investors)). In September 2026 Staniszewski put it plainly:

> "We are now $600 million in ARR." — Mati Staniszewski ([founder-reported, TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/24/twenty-minutes-with-the-ceo-of-elevenlabs-now-reportedly-valued-at-22-billion/))

That works out to about $50M a month ([calc, from TechCrunch's $600M](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/24/twenty-minutes-with-the-ceo-of-elevenlabs-now-reportedly-valued-at-22-billion/)). This is our arithmetic, not a reported monthly figure. Total revenue, profit and gross margin are not disclosed. Staniszewski declined to go into margins in detail, but said he was fine with them falling if that helped win market share.

## Why did ElevenLabs' valuation double in seven months?

Investors paid for growth that kept speeding up. Sequoia led the $500M Series D in February 2026 at an $11B valuation, which the company said brought total funding to $781M across five rounds ([founder-reported, ElevenLabs](https://elevenlabs.io/blog/series-d)). The May 2026 close added BlackRock, Wellington, D.E. Shaw, Schroders and NVIDIA's NVentures, plus a $100M tender ([founder-reported, ElevenLabs](https://elevenlabs.io/blog/500m-arr-and-new-investors)). In September 2026, a $300M employee tender co-led by Wellington and T. Rowe Price valued ElevenLabs at $22B, and the company said ARR for its ElevenAgents product had more than tripled since February ([founder-reported, ElevenLabs](https://elevenlabs.io/blog/tender-22bn)). On a public listing, Staniszewski said the decision "will depend on the time and place" ([founder-reported, TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/24/twenty-minutes-with-the-ceo-of-elevenlabs-now-reportedly-valued-at-22-billion/)).

## How does ElevenLabs compare with Suno and Submagic?

All three sell generated media to people who are not studio professionals, but they took very different routes. [Suno](/cases/suno/) is the closest match. It also trains its own audio models and reported $300M in annual revenue in February 2026 ([founder-reported, Unite.AI](https://www.unite.ai/suno-reaches-2-million-paid-subscribers-and-300m-arr-in-two-years)), about the level ElevenLabs reached at the end of 2025. Suno's $2.45B post-money valuation in November 2025 ([media-reported, Unite.AI](https://www.unite.ai/suno-reaches-2-million-paid-subscribers-and-300m-arr-in-two-years)) is a fraction of ElevenLabs' $11B three months later. Our reading is that the enterprise customers explain much of that gap. Suno sells mainly to consumers, while ElevenLabs also sells to telecoms and banks.

[Submagic](/cases/submagic/) is the opposite in money terms. It reached $8M ARR with 13 people and $0 in outside funding by June 2025 ([founder-reported, Latka](https://getlatka.com/interviews/submagic-david-zitoun-2025)). ElevenLabs raised $781M and has more than 800 staff. [Synthesia](/cases/synthesia/) is another London AI media company worth reading alongside this one.

## What lessons can founders take from ElevenLabs?

- **Start from a problem you have lived with.** Growing up with rough dubbing in Poland gave the founders a clear, concrete use case before voice AI was a category.
- **Serve creators and enterprises with one product.** Plans from $6 to $990 a month sit beside deals with companies like Deutsche Telekom, so both groups pay for the same models.
- **Publish your numbers yourself.** Almost every milestone here comes from ElevenLabs' own blog, which makes its growth curve easy to cite.
- **Watch for small inconsistencies.** The end-of-2025 ARR appears as $330M+ in one company post and $350M in another, and total revenue is not disclosed.
- **Use tenders to keep staff.** Two employee tenders in about a year, at $6.6B and then $22B, let staff sell shares without an IPO ([founder-reported, ElevenLabs](https://elevenlabs.io/blog/tender-22bn)).
