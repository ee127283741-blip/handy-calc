Most AI image tools want you to open their app. CreativeClaw goes the other way: it plugs a stack of image, video and audio models into the chat assistant you already use. Seven months after an April 2026 founding, that idea showed $14,540 in monthly recurring revenue and $249,644 in all-time revenue ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/creativeclaw)), with a team of one person listed.

## What does CreativeClaw add to Claude and ChatGPT?

CreativeClaw is a remote MCP server that gives an AI assistant access to many generation models at once. The homepage lists Claude, Claude Code, ChatGPT, Codex and Grok among supported clients, and names models such as Nano Banana 2, FLUX 3, Kling 4.0, Seedance 2.5 and ElevenLabs v4 ([founder-reported, CreativeClaw homepage](https://creativeclaw.co/)).

The TrustMRR listing pitches it as a way to stop switching between separate creative tools. Its built-in skills generate several variants across models, then let the assistant compare and pick one.

> "Every top AI model for images, video, audio & 3D. One MCP. Go." — CreativeClaw listing ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/creativeclaw))

## Why does CreativeClaw sell credits instead of plans?

CreativeClaw charges per generation. The homepage says $10 buys 1,000 credits, credits never expire, there is no subscription and new users get 100 free credits ([founder-reported, CreativeClaw homepage](https://creativeclaw.co/)). Each model costs a different number of credits, so heavy video work burns through a pack faster than still images.

This model matches its costs: every generation calls a paid upstream model, so usage pricing keeps margins tied to usage. The homepage also claims more than 148,000 finished generations delivered and offers a done-for-you brand theme as an add-on.

Our observation: CreativeClaw is the youngest of the three and the only one that has no interface of its own. It rides on the assistants people already pay for. Its MRR is the lowest of the group, but its credit sales mean MRR understates how much money moves through it.

## What can founders learn from CreativeClaw's first seven months?

- **Go where users already are.** CreativeClaw built no new app; it added skills to Claude and ChatGPT through MCP ([founder-reported, CreativeClaw homepage](https://creativeclaw.co/)).
- **Read MRR in context.** Last-30-day revenue far above MRR shows that credit packs, not plans, carry this business.
- **Price like your costs.** Paying per generation matches upstream model bills that also scale per generation.
- **Small can move fast.** A one-person, bootstrapped team reached $249,644 in all-time revenue within about seven months ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/creativeclaw)).
