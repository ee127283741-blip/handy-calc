## Origin of the idea
Anderson, who had already built the viral dating-reply app RizzGPT, noticed the "looksmaxxing" trend among young men on TikTok. An app that rates a face and suggests changes fit that trend exactly.

## How they started
He built the app quickly, reportedly with much of the code written by ChatGPT, and launched in December 2023.

## Tools & tech stack
A mobile app that sends front and side selfies to vision models (partly OpenAI) and returns scores for features such as jawline and skin.

## Marketing
Almost entirely organic social: users post their Umax score screens in their own videos, which acts as free advertising. Anderson claims over a billion impressions.

## Sales & pricing
Weekly subscription of $3.99, with a referral option to skip the first fee.

## Monthly revenue
About **$500K/month** in subscription revenue (founder claim to Fortune, July 2024; Fortune could not verify it). A Chinese industry analysis later suggested it fell during 2024.

## Total revenue
Not disclosed.

## Founding date & timeline
- **Dec 2023:** Launch.
- **Jul 2024:** 7M+ downloads; founder claims ~$500K/month.

## Team size
Not disclosed.

## Key takeaways
- Ride an existing cultural trend instead of creating demand.
- A shareable result screen is the growth engine.
- Products rating appearance draw ethical scrutiny; Fortune raised mental-health concerns.
