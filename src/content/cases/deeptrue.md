A sales call between Seoul and São Paulo used to need an interpreter or a shared second language. Deeptrue sells a third option: speak your own language and let AI translate in the meeting. The US company, founded in February 2025 by Jinho Kim, had $27,019 in all-time revenue and $2,549 in MRR by November 2026 ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/deeptrue)). Its listing is short, which makes the verified numbers the main story.

## How does Deeptrue translate meetings in real time?

Deeptrue works as a translation copilot inside the meeting tools people already use. It supports 30+ languages on Zoom, Google Meet and Microsoft Teams, according to its listing ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/deeptrue)). The stack listed is Next.js on the front end, Python on the back end and Stripe for billing.

> "Real-time AI translation copilot for global meetings." — Deeptrue company description on TrustMRR ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/deeptrue))

## Why does Deeptrue's MRR sit close to its monthly cash?

Deeptrue's last-30-days revenue of $2,252 is slightly below its $2,549 MRR ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/deeptrue)). That is the profile of a mostly monthly subscription business: cash in the month tracks the recurring base rather than spiking from annual or lifetime deals.

For a meeting tool that suits the use case. Teams that run international calls every week keep paying; there is little reason to buy a one-off. The listing names no marketing channels, and its domain rating was 2 out of 100, so search is not yet its source of customers.

Our observation: Voicy and Talknotes work after the fact, on recordings. Deeptrue works live, in the call, which raises the bar on latency but also ties it to a recurring weekly habit.

## What takeaways does Deeptrue offer meeting-tool builders?

- **Plug into existing platforms.** Zoom, Meet and Teams support means no new meeting app to adopt.
- **Recurring use makes recurring revenue.** Monthly cash close to MRR reflects steady subscriptions.
- **A thin listing can still prove traction.** Verified numbers say more than a long pitch.
- **Distribution is the open question.** With no listed channels and a low domain rating, growth depends on reaching global teams directly.
