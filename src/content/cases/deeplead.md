Cold outreach tools usually ask customers to bring a lead list, a set of inboxes and a sequence. Deeplead asks for one thing, a website URL, and does the rest. Founded in February 2026, it showed $22,227 in MRR and $81,191 in all-time revenue by November ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/deeplead-io)). That is a fast start for a bootstrapped tool from Germany.

## How does Deeplead turn a website into outreach?

Deeplead reads a business's website to learn what it sells, who it is for and what sets it apart. It then builds campaigns, writes a personal email for each prospect and sends them from its own warmed-up inboxes ([founder-reported, Deeplead](https://www.deeplead.io)). Replies are forwarded to the customer's email, and follow-ups stop once someone answers. It also sends LinkedIn connection requests and Instagram DMs to local businesses.

The site says emails are verified twice, and each sending domain must reach at least 80% inbox placement with a provider before sending to it. Those controls matter, because deliverability is where cheap outreach tools usually fail.

## Who does Deeplead sell to?

TrustMRR lists the audience as small and midsize businesses, local businesses and agencies, and tags the company as bootstrapped in Germany ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/deeplead-io)). The founder is Julian M. Wagner. Team size is not disclosed.

The website shows client results as proof, such as Placify's 215 positive replies from 6,245 prospects. These are the company's own claims, and we have not verified them.

Deeplead sells one plan, with a short trial and a guarantee on time to first lead ([founder-reported, Deeplead](https://www.deeplead.io)).

The site defines a first lead as a real human reply. Auto-replies, bounces and unsubscribes don't count.

## How fast did Deeplead's MRR grow after launch?

Deeplead reached $22,227 in MRR and 551 active subscriptions in November 2026, about nine months after its February founding ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/deeplead-io)). Revenue over the last 30 days was higher, at $26,855, and all-time revenue was $81,191.

MRR divided by subscriptions is about $40.34 ([calc, from TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/deeplead-io)), a little above the $37 plan, which fits customers buying credit top-ups. The 30-day revenue above MRR also points to top-ups or annual payments. Most of the all-time total must have come in recent months, which suggests growth was steep.

## How does Deeplead compare with Instantly and Gojiberry AI?

[Instantly](/cases/instantly/) is the bigger cold-email platform, where the customer runs the campaigns. [Gojiberry AI](/cases/gojiberry-ai/) finds buyers through LinkedIn buying signals. Deeplead takes more of the job off the customer: it picks targets, writes the messages and owns the inboxes.

Our reading: Deeplead sells to a less technical buyer, such as a local business owner, who would never set up domains and sequences. The low price fits that buyer. The risk is that one product now carries the deliverability of every customer at once.

## What takeaways does Deeplead offer outbound founders?

- **Ask for less input.** A website URL replaces a lead list and an email sequence ([founder-reported, Deeplead](https://www.deeplead.io)).
- **Guarantee the outcome buyers care about.** Deeplead promises a time to first reply, not a number of emails.
- **Protect deliverability in the product.** Inbox-placement checks are part of the pitch.
- **A low price can grow fast.** 551 subscriptions in nine months came at about $40 each ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/deeplead-io)).
