Cold calling is supposed to be dead, yet a solo French founder has built a profitable business selling phone numbers to people who still dial. coldcaller.pro, founded in April 2026, had $99,043 in lifetime revenue and $14,998 in the last 30 days by November 2026 ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/coldcallerpro)). Its listed MRR is far smaller, which makes the numbers worth a closer look.

## What does coldcaller.pro give its users?

coldcaller.pro delivers qualified cold-calling leads enriched with the business owner's direct mobile and email, inside a simple interface for tracking calls and results ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/coldcallerpro)). It sells to solopreneurs, salespeople and agencies, and reports about 600 users.

The problem it targets is manual prospecting: no more scraping, cleaning data or hunting for decision-makers' contacts. Users search, get owner-level contacts and start dialing. The backend is written in Python, with Stripe handling subscriptions. Founder Aboubakr runs it alone and bootstrapped.

## Why is coldcaller.pro profitable with no ads?

The founder says the business is cheap to run and grows by referral.

> "Coldcaller is profitable from day one" — Aboubakr, founder of coldcaller.pro ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/coldcallerpro))

The company description cites a 93% margin, about 9% visitor-to-paid conversion with no free trial, and 100% community-driven acquisition through word of mouth ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/coldcallerpro)). He is selling to focus on his other SaaS companies and agency.

## What can sales tool founders learn from coldcaller.pro?

- **Unfashionable channels still pay.** Cold callers need direct owner numbers, and they buy them.
- **A narrow geography is a moat.** French B2B data serves a market global tools cover less closely.
- **No free trial can still convert.** The founder reports about 9% visitor-to-paid conversion.
- **Watch MRR and cash together.** Thirty-day revenue runs far above listed MRR here.
