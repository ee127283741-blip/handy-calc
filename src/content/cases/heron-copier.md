Heron Copier's current MRR looks modest. Its history does not. The combined trading journal and trade copier showed $7,331 in MRR in November 2026 but $504,704 in all-time revenue ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/heron-copier)), the result of more than three years of selling software to traders from a small company based in Indonesia.

## What does Heron Copier do for traders?

Heron Copier combines a trading journal with a trade copier that works across multiple platforms, in the cloud or on a local machine ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/heron-copier)). A trade copier mirrors trades from one account to others, and a journal records them for review.

> "A trading journal and multi-platform cloud-based and local-based trade copier." — Heron Copier listing ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/heron-copier))

The listing does not name the supported trading platforms, so we do not list them. Heron Copier is a tool, not investment advice, and this page does not assess trading results.

## How did Heron Copier earn over $504K with a $7K MRR?

Heron Copier earned $504,704 in all-time revenue between June 2023 and November 2026, while current MRR was $7,331 and last-30-day revenue was $6,473 ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/heron-copier)). All-time revenue is about 69 times current MRR ([calc, from TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/heron-copier)).

## What does Heron Copier charge?

Heron Copier offers a 7-day free trial, then $19 a month or $179 a year ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/heron-copier)). The annual plan equals about $14.92 a month. MRR per subscription is about $18.85 ([calc, from TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/heron-copier)), so most subscribers likely pay monthly.

## What stack and payment setup does Heron Copier use?

Heron Copier runs on Kubernetes and Express and takes payments through Creem, not Stripe ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/heron-copier)). TrustMRR verified its revenue through a Creem API key. The founder's X account shows 34 followers, and marketing channels and team size are not disclosed.

## How does Heron Copier compare with Hebbia and SoldComps?

[Hebbia](/cases/hebbia/) sells AI research software to finance professionals, and [SoldComps](/cases/soldcomps/) sells pricing data to resellers. Heron Copier is closer to SoldComps in scale and buyer: a small tool sold to individuals trying to make money in a market.

Our observation: tools for individual traders can earn a lot in good markets and shrink when activity fades. Heron Copier's large lifetime total next to a smaller current MRR fits that cyclical pattern, though the listing does not explain it.

## What takeaways does Heron Copier offer niche tool builders?

- **Lifetime revenue tells a different story than MRR.** $504,704 all time sits beside $7,331 MRR ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/heron-copier)).
- **Bundle the workflow.** A copier plus a journal covers both doing and reviewing trades.
- **Stripe is not the only option.** Heron Copier runs on Creem and still gets verified numbers.
- **Expect cycles.** Products tied to trading activity should plan for uneven months.
