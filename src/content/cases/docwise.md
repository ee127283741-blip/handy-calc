Healthcare AI headlines are dominated by medical scribes raising hundreds of millions. Docwise works the other end of the clinic: the front door. Founded in July 2025, it helps private clinics get found online and get patients booked, and it had $42,658 in lifetime revenue by November 2026 ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/docwise)). Salman Mustafa is listed as founder.

## Which clinic problems does Docwise target?

Docwise targets the small gaps that cost private clinics patients: visibility, response time, systems and follow-up ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/docwise)). Its value proposition covers the whole patient journey, from being found to being booked to coming back.

> "Every Clinic Will Be Powered by AI. We help yours to lead!" — Docwise company description ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/docwise))

The listing also notes integrations with major EHRs, the record systems clinics already run on.

## How does Docwise reach clinic owners?

Docwise lists a wide mix of marketing channels on TrustMRR: email marketing, cold email, a referral program, a blog, content marketing and SEO ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/docwise)). For a B2B product sold to private clinics, cold email and referrals are direct routes to owners. The stack includes React, Webflow, WordPress, FastAPI on Hetzner, Anthropic models and Stripe.

## How does Docwise compare with Freed and Abridge?

Docwise, [Freed](/cases/freed/) and [Abridge](/cases/abridge/) all sell AI to healthcare providers, but Freed and Abridge automate clinical notes, while Docwise focuses on patient acquisition and follow-up.

Our observation: the scribe market is crowded with well-funded players, while clinic marketing and booking is a quieter niche where a small company can still compete.

## What lessons does Docwise hold for healthcare AI founders?

- **Pick the job the giants ignore.** Docwise sells growth, not documentation.
- **Integrations earn trust.** EHR connections make an outside tool fit the clinic's workflow.
- **Watch run rate against lifetime totals.** $42,658 all time against $1,105 MRR shows revenue has not stayed recurring.
- **Many channels, one buyer.** Cold email, referrals and SEO all aim at private clinic owners.
