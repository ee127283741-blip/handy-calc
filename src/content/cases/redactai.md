Writing a LinkedIn post every day is hard work, and a whole category of AI tools grew up to do it for you. RedactAI is one of the earlier ones. Nicolas Pamart started it in France in May 2023, and it has since earned $340,260 in total ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/redactai)). By November 2026, though, its MRR was $2,976, a fraction of what that lifetime total implies it once was.

## What does RedactAI do for LinkedIn creators?

RedactAI is an all-in-one platform to create content on LinkedIn ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/redactai)). Features include post generation, viral hooks, post scheduling, creator discovery, competitor analysis, statistics and idea generation.

> "Grow faster on LinkedIn with AI posts, viral hooks, engaging stories." — RedactAI company description ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/redactai))

The listing says it saves users hours of writing and creator's block by producing posts in the user's own voice, tuned for LinkedIn's algorithm. Its audience spans creators, marketing directors, sales leaders, coaches and co-founders.

## How much revenue has RedactAI collected since 2023?

RedactAI has earned $340,260 all time, and in November 2026 it had $2,976 MRR from 126 active subscriptions, with $1,353 collected in the last 30 days ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/redactai)).

## Why did RedactAI attract over 44,000 users?

A free start and a clear, popular job brought users in. RedactAI reports about 44,160 users against 126 active subscriptions ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/redactai)). LinkedIn ghostwriting was one of the first obvious uses of AI writing tools, and RedactAI arrived early in 2023.

Its Ahrefs Domain Rating of 44 on TrustMRR is high for a small SaaS, a sign that years of content and mentions have built search visibility.

## How does RedactAI compare with Liinks and Post Bridge?

All three sell social media tools to creators, but their revenue paths split.

| [Post Bridge](/cases/post-bridge/) | Multi-platform social scheduler | $49,650 MRR (Nov 2026) | [verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/post-bridge) |

Our observation: Post Bridge and Liinks sell infrastructure, posting and links, that does not get cheaper as AI improves. RedactAI sells AI writing on one platform, which general chatbots now also do. Its large lifetime total next to a small MRR fits that pressure, though the page itself does not explain the decline.

## What takeaways does RedactAI offer AI writing startups?

- **Being early pays, for a while.** $340,260 all time came from an early AI writing niche.
- **Commodity AI erodes pricing power.** Post writing is now something many general tools do.
- **Users are not subscribers.** About 44,160 users produced 126 active subscriptions.
- **Workflow features defend better.** Scheduling and analytics are harder to replace than text generation.
