Taja AI has already crossed a line many AI startups only talk about: more than $1 million in lifetime revenue. TrustMRR showed $1,036,313 all time and $13,410 in monthly recurring revenue in November 2026 ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/taja-ai)). The gap between those two numbers tells the story of a tool that started in May 2023, early in the AI video wave, and now earns far less each month than its history suggests.

## What does Taja AI do with a long-form video?

Taja AI repurposes one long video into many short pieces. The homepage says a user uploads a YouTube video or a local file, the AI learns the channel's niche and brand voice, then it produces shorts, clips with captions, thumbnails and text posts ([founder-reported, Taja AI homepage](https://taja.ai/)). It claims about 27 outputs for a typical video and 3.8 weeks of content on average from one click.

> "Taja helps you repurpose your long-form video content into ready-to-share clips, shorts, and social posts for every platform." — Taja AI homepage ([founder-reported, Taja AI](https://taja.ai/))

Scheduling is built in for YouTube, X, Instagram, Facebook, TikTok and LinkedIn.

## How did Taja AI earn $1M in total but only $13K MRR now?

Taja AI had $13,410 in MRR, 340 active subscriptions and $11,551 in revenue for the last 30 days in November 2026 ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/taja-ai)). Its $1,036,313 all-time figure is about 77 times current MRR ([calc, from TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/taja-ai)), across roughly three and a half years.

## Why does Taja AI target realtors and congregations?

The TrustMRR listing names three audiences: solopreneurs, realtors and congregations ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/taja-ai)). Churches that stream sermons and agents who film property tours both produce long videos regularly but rarely have an editor to cut them into shorts.

Marketing channels on the listing include SEO, a blog, a newsletter, Facebook, Instagram, Google Ads, cold email, word of mouth and affiliates. The homepage claims 256,000+ businesses integrated and more than 2.6 million hours saved. Plan prices are not disclosed on the listing, which only says "Start Free Trial", and team size is not disclosed.

## How does Taja AI compare with OpusClip and Submagic?

| Company | Starting point | Notable focus |
|---|---|---|
| Taja AI | Long video to shorts plus scheduling | Realtors, churches, solopreneurs; $13,410 MRR ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/taja-ai)) |
| [OpusClip](/cases/opus-clip/) | Long video to viral clips | Creators and businesses at scale |
| [Submagic](/cases/submagic/) | Short video captions and edits | Short-form creators |

Our observation: OpusClip and Submagic grew by winning creators. Taja AI picked less glamorous niches where users care more about saving time than going viral. That focus may explain why it survived a crowded category, even as its monthly revenue came down from earlier levels.

## What lessons does Taja AI's $1M lifetime offer AI video founders?

- **Lifetime revenue hides the trend.** $1,036,313 all time sits beside $13,410 of MRR ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/taja-ai)).
- **Boring niches can be loyal niches.** Realtors and congregations publish long video every week.
- **Bundle scheduling with generation.** Taja keeps users inside one tool from upload to posting.
- **Margins matter in AI video.** The listing claims a 40% profit margin on a compute-heavy product.
