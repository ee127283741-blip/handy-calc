A recruiter with 200 applicants cannot phone all of them, so most get filtered out by a CV scan. Hirevire offers another option: send every applicant a short list of questions and let them answer on video. Founded in India in December 2021, the bootstrapped company had $13,766 in monthly recurring revenue in November 2026 ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/hirevire)).

## What does Hirevire replace in a hiring process?

Hirevire replaces the first-round phone screen with structured async video interviews ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/hirevire)). Candidates record responses when it suits them and do not need to create a login. The product supports 21 languages, generates AI scorecards and can auto-disqualify applicants.

> "Watch every applicant answer your questions." — Hirevire value proposition on TrustMRR ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/hirevire))

The homepage describes Hirevire as one-way video interview software that collects video, audio and text answers, and says hiring can be managed from Claude or ChatGPT ([founder-reported, Hirevire](https://hirevire.com)). That last detail shows a small team adding AI-assistant integrations instead of building a heavier recruiting suite.

## Why does Hirevire exclude rejected candidates from plan limits?

Hirevire lets auto-disqualified candidates fall outside plan limits, so rejected applicants do not count against what a customer pays for ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/hirevire)). It is a small pricing detail with a clear purpose. Usage-based limits often punish a company for posting a popular job, and this rule removes that worry for the applicants a recruiter never wanted to see.

Combined with no-login recording, the design aims to keep both sides of the funnel low-friction: candidates do not drop off at a sign-up wall, and buyers do not hit a limit because of spam applications.

## How does Hirevire differ from AiApply and Welya?

Hirevire sits on the employer side of hiring, while [AiApply](/cases/aiapply/) sells to job seekers. [Welya](/cases/welya/) is another async video business, but it uses recorded video to sell through webinars rather than to screen candidates.

Our observation: B2B buyers pay Hirevire several times more per account than consumers pay AiApply. Hirevire needs far fewer customers for each dollar of revenue, but it also has to win a business purchase decision each time.

## What lessons does Hirevire offer bootstrapped HR software founders?

- **Replace one painful step, not the whole stack.** Hirevire targets the first-round phone screen and nothing more ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/hirevire)).
- **Remove friction for the person who does not pay.** No-login recording matters because candidates owe the employer nothing.
- **Design limits that feel fair.** Not counting rejected applicants against a plan removes a common pricing complaint.
- **A tiny team can carry a B2B product for years.** A 2–5 person bootstrapped team has built $283,166 in lifetime revenue.
