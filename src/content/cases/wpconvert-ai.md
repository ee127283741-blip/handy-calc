AI site builders made it easy to generate a website in minutes. They did not make it easy to hand that site to a client who runs everything on WordPress. WPConvert.ai fills that gap. Jose Montero, the solo founder, launched it in October 2025, and by November 2026 it had $50,060 in lifetime revenue ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/wpconvert-ai)). It is a business built on the side effects of other companies' growth.

## What does WPConvert.ai convert, and into what?

WPConvert.ai turns websites built with Lovable, Cursor, v0, Replit, Base44 and other AI tools into production-ready WordPress sites, keeping design and functionality ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/wpconvert-ai)). It supports dynamic WordPress features, WooCommerce, blogs, forms and custom post types, plus an API, CLI and MCP for developers.

> "Turn AI-built websites into production-ready WordPress sites in minutes." — WPConvert.ai value proposition ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/wpconvert-ai))

Its audience is web agencies, freelancers, business owners and WordPress professionals. Elementor and Gutenberg support is listed as coming soon.

WPConvert.ai had $3,016 MRR from 51 active subscriptions in November 2026, with $3,734 collected in the last 30 days and $50,060 all time ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/wpconvert-ai)).

## Why does WPConvert.ai depend on AI site builders growing?

Every new Lovable or v0 site is a potential WPConvert.ai customer. The product exists because AI builders output modern frontend code, while many agencies and clients still run on WordPress, and the listing says the manual rebuild between the two is what it removes ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/wpconvert-ai)).

Marketing is broad for a solo founder: SEO, Reddit, TikTok, YouTube, email, content and word of mouth. TrustMRR shows an Ahrefs Domain Rating of 68, high for a product this young.

## How does WPConvert.ai differ from 1clickwebsite.ai and Lovable?

All three produce websites with AI, but they sit at different points in the workflow.

| Company | Role | Revenue figure | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| WPConvert.ai | Converts AI sites to WordPress | $3,016 MRR (Nov 2026) | [verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/wpconvert-ai) |
| [1clickwebsite.ai](/cases/1clickwebsite/) | Generates WordPress sites from a form | $14,155 MRR (Nov 2026) | [verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/1clickwebsite-ai) |
| [Lovable](/cases/lovable/) | AI app and site builder | ≈$8.3M/month, implied from $100M ARR | [media-reported, The Next Web](https://thenextweb.com/news/vibe-coding-platform-lovable-becomes-historys-fastest-growing-software-startup) |

Our observation: 1clickwebsite.ai and WPConvert.ai both bet that WordPress stays the delivery format. Lovable creates the supply of sites; WPConvert.ai monetises the step after it. That makes it a complement to the big builders, not a competitor.

## What can solo founders learn from WPConvert.ai?

- **Build on someone else's wave.** AI site builders create the demand WPConvert.ai serves.
- **Bridge two ecosystems.** Modern AI output and WordPress hosting do not talk to each other by default.
- **Offer credits beside plans.** Pay-as-you-go captures one-off agency jobs.
- **Ship a developer surface.** An API, CLI and MCP let agencies automate conversions.
