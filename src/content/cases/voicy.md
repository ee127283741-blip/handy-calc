Most software companies keep their margins private. Voicy prints a ceiling on its pricing page: the price will never be more than 20% above total costs ([founder-reported, Voicy](https://usevoicy.com)). The dictation app, founded in November 2025 per TrustMRR, still showed $12,406 in MRR and $205,049 in all-time revenue a year later ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/voicy)). A large share of that came from one-time sales.

## What does Voicy do on a desktop?

Voicy turns speech into text in any app or website on Mac and Windows, and in the browser through an extension. A user speaks, Voicy transcribes, and the text lands where the cursor is. The TrustMRR listing claims over 99% accuracy in 50 languages and typing three times faster ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/voicy)).

The site adds an offline mode on desktop for 25 languages, over 500 million words dictated and 10,000+ users ([founder-reported, Voicy](https://usevoicy.com)). Its comparison table puts built-in Apple and Windows dictation at about 90% accuracy. These are company figures, and we have not tested them.

## How does Voicy's cost-plus pricing work?

Voicy's pricing works as a promise about margin, not a market rate. The site states the rule in one line.

> "Our price will never be more than 20% above our total costs." — Voicy pricing ([founder-reported, Voicy](https://usevoicy.com))

| Plan | Price | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Free trial | 30 minutes of recording | [founder-reported, Voicy](https://usevoicy.com) |
| Pro | $8.49 a month or $82 a year | [founder-reported, Voicy](https://usevoicy.com) |
| Lifetime | $260 once | [founder-reported, Voicy](https://usevoicy.com) |
| Team | $6.79 per user a month, 3-user minimum | [founder-reported, Voicy](https://usevoicy.com) |

Students and people with disabilities get 20% off. The cost-plus rule is a marketing message as much as a policy: it tells buyers that Voicy is cheap on purpose, and it invites comparison with pricier rivals.

Voicy had $12,406 in MRR from 1,535 active subscriptions in November 2026, and $19,704 in revenue over the last 30 days ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/voicy)). All-time revenue was $205,049.

The listing names SEO and YouTube as marketing channels. Team size is not disclosed.

Voicy is a smaller, cheaper rival to Wispr Flow in desktop dictation, and larger than Talknotes was at the time of its interview. All three sell voice as a faster way to write.

Our observation: Wispr Flow is backed by large venture rounds, as our Wispr Flow case details, while Voicy is bootstrapped, per TrustMRR, and competes on price in writing. A Lifetime plan fits that strategy. Buyers who dislike subscriptions pay once, which brings cash early but caps future revenue from each of them.

## What takeaways does Voicy offer bootstrapped app makers?

- **A public margin cap is a pitch.** Voicy's 20% rule makes "cheap" credible, and the app still reached $205,049 in revenue in a year ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/voicy)).
- **Lifetime plans front-load cash.** Revenue far above MRR shows how much one-time sales can carry a young app.
- **Ride a category others educate.** Well-funded dictation rivals teach buyers the habit, and a cheaper option can catch the price-sensitive ones.
- **Cross-platform reach matters for utilities.** Mac, Windows and a browser extension cover most places people type.
