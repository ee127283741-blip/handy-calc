Car enthusiasts post their cars constantly, but few of them can hire a photographer, a location and a camera crew. GarageLab replaces that shoot with a phone upload. The app, founded in Türkiye in April 2026 by Ahmet Hakan Eroğlu, reached 106 active subscriptions and $4,188 in MRR by November 2026 ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/garagelab)). In about seven months it had collected $12,087, and it is one of the few car-focused AI apps in this library.

## What can GarageLab do with one photo of a car?

GarageLab turns one photo of a vehicle into realistic cinematic photos and videos in seconds. The listing names social posts, wallpapers, listing visuals, concept shots and fan scenes as uses, all without organising a traditional photo shoot ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/garagelab)).

> "Turn any car photo into cinematic AI visuals and videos." — GarageLab value proposition on TrustMRR ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/garagelab))

The audience is enthusiasts, social media creators, dealerships and automotive communities. Dealerships are the interesting group, because listing visuals are a business need, not a hobby.

## How does GarageLab find car enthusiasts?

GarageLab markets through Meta Ads, TikTok and Instagram ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/garagelab)). Those are visual channels, and a cinematic car video is itself the ad: the product's output is the kind of content that performs on those feeds.

The tech stack is React Native and Expo for the app, with RevenueCat, PostgreSQL and Hetzner on the back end. The app is free to download and earns through in-app purchases and subscription plans. With only 11 App Store ratings, the review count is still small compared with the subscriber base.

## What lessons does GarageLab offer AI app makers?

- **Pick a passion, not a feature.** Car enthusiasts already spend on their cars; GarageLab sells to that spending.
- **Make the output the ad.** Cinematic car clips are native content for TikTok and Instagram, the channels GarageLab uses.
- **Serve hobbyists and businesses at once.** Dealerships need listing visuals, which widens the market.
- **Mobile billing tools speed up launch.** RevenueCat-verified revenue reached $12,087 within about seven months ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/garagelab)).
