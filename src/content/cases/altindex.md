Retail investors read Reddit threads and congress trading reports by hand. AltIndex does it for them every day and boils the result down to a single number per stock. In November 2026 that product had $16,340 in monthly recurring revenue and $678,082 in all-time revenue ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/altindex-llc)), with a team of two to five people. It was also listed for sale.

## What signals does AltIndex turn into a stock score?

AltIndex collects alternative data on about 3,000 US stocks and turns it into one score from 0 to 100 per stock. The company-written TrustMRR listing names the inputs: Reddit, TikTok, Instagram, news, job postings, and insider and congress trades, with history back to 2021 ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/altindex-llc)). Users can put the score on their own portfolio and get alerts.

The homepage pitches it as an AI analyst for a portfolio and lists products such as AI stock picks, stock alerts, an MCP server for AI tools, and data for institutions. It claims 80,000+ investors ([founder-reported, AltIndex homepage](https://altindex.com/)), while the TrustMRR listing says about 71,000 users.

## How much revenue does AltIndex earn from subscriptions and its API?

AltIndex had $16,340 in MRR and 639 active subscriptions in November 2026, and it collected $12,228 in the 30 days before the snapshot ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/altindex-llc)). All-time revenue was $678,082. TrustMRR checks these figures through a Stripe API key.

Profit is described as positive in the founder message, but the amount is not disclosed.

## Why is Quincy Miller selling AltIndex?

The founder message on TrustMRR says the team is moving on to a new media venture, not that the product stalled. Quincy Miller lists what a buyer gets: 71,000 accounts, 700+ paying subscribers and a B2B data API that had just launched.

> "Our team is going all-in on a new media venture, so AltIndex deserves an owner who can give it full focus." — Quincy Miller ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/altindex-llc))

The same message credits mostly organic growth through Reddit, SEO and newsletters. The listing's marketing channels match: a blog, a newsletter, SEO, app store optimization, X, Reddit and TikTok. The app holds a 4.6 rating on the App Store from 20 ratings and 2.7 on Google Play from 12.

## How does AltIndex compare with SoldComps and Apiframe?

AltIndex is the smallest of these three Stripe-verified businesses by MRR, but it is the only one that sells the same data both to consumers in an app and to developers through an API.

| Company | What it sells | MRR (Nov 2026) | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| AltIndex | Alternative data stock scores, app and API | $16,340 | [verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/altindex-llc) |
| [SoldComps](/cases/soldcomps/) | eBay sold-listing data for resellers | $25,931 | [verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/soldcomps) |
| [Apiframe](/cases/apiframe/) | API access to AI image models | $35,032 | [verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/apiframe) |

Our observation: SoldComps and AltIndex both repackage public data that users could collect themselves but will not. Apiframe shows how far a pure API can go; AltIndex's API had only just launched, so its revenue mix is still mostly app subscriptions.

## What lessons does AltIndex offer data product builders?

- **Compress many sources into one number.** AltIndex's core product is a single 0 to 100 score built from many feeds, which is easier to sell than raw data.
- **Annual plans smooth churn but lump cash.** With 76% of subscribers on annual plans, monthly cash and MRR can diverge, as AltIndex's $12,228 against $16,340 shows.
- **One dataset, several products.** The same data feeds an app, an API and an AI assistant connector.
- **Selling is not failing.** The founder message frames the sale as a change of focus, with the business described as profitable.
