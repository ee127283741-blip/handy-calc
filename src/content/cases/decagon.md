Decagon valuation numbers move faster than its revenue disclosures. In June 2025 investors valued the AI customer support startup at $1.5B. Less than six months later, a $250M Series D priced it at $4.5B ([media-reported, TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/03/04/decagon-completes-first-tender-offer-at-4-5b-valuation/)). Over the same period Decagon gave no new revenue figure at all. The last one dates from late 2024, when it said ARR had passed eight figures. For a company valued in the billions, that is an unusually thin public record.

## What does Decagon sell, and who buys it?

Decagon sells AI agents that handle customer service conversations for large companies. Jesse Zhang is its CEO and co-founder, and TechCrunch described the company as less than three years old in March 2026 ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/03/04/decagon-completes-first-tender-offer-at-4-5b-valuation/)). It had more than 100 large customers by then, including Avis Budget Group, 1-800-Flowers, Quince, Oura Health and Away Travel ([media-reported, TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/03/04/decagon-completes-first-tender-offer-at-4-5b-valuation/)). Built In adds that it targets travel, healthcare, retail and financial services, and that Block is also a customer ([Built In](https://builtin.com/articles/decagon-raises-250m-series-d-4b-valuation-20260130)).

The size of the market is the pitch. TechCrunch cites a Gartner estimate of 17 million contact center agents worldwide. Decagon's pricing is not disclosed.

## How did the Decagon valuation triple in six months?

The jump came from one large round in a hot category. Coatue Management and Index Ventures led a $250M Series D in January 2026 at $4.5B, which Built In described as triple the valuation from under six months earlier ([media-reported, Built In](https://builtin.com/articles/decagon-raises-250m-series-d-4b-valuation-20260130)). Six weeks later Decagon ran its first tender offer at the same price, letting more than 300 employees sell part of their vested shares to investors including Coatue, Index, a16z, Definition, Forerunner and Ribbit ([media-reported, TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/03/04/decagon-completes-first-tender-offer-at-4-5b-valuation/)).

## What is known about Decagon's ARR?

Nobody outside the company knows. TechCrunch says Decagon has not disclosed revenue since late 2024, when its annual recurring revenue passed eight figures ([media-reported, TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/03/04/decagon-completes-first-tender-offer-at-4-5b-valuation/)). Eight figures means at least $10M, and could mean much more. Current ARR, monthly revenue and total revenue are all not disclosed. Several data sites publish estimates, but we found none that cites the company, so we leave them out.

In our reading, the silence is itself information. Fast-growing startups in this market, such as Sierra, usually announce round-number milestones. Decagon chose to talk about customers and investor demand instead.

## How does Decagon compare with Sierra and Chatbase?

Decagon and [Sierra](/cases/sierra/) sell the same thing to the same buyers, but only Sierra publishes revenue. Sierra announced $200M in ARR in late May 2026 ([founder-reported via TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/08/these-ai-startups-are-growing-revenue-at-faster-and-faster-rates/)) and raised at $15B that month ([media-reported, SiliconANGLE](https://siliconangle.com/2026/05/04/ai-agent-startup-sierra-valued-15b-new-950m-funding-round/)), more than three times Decagon's price. [Chatbase](/cases/chatbase/) sits at the other end of the market: a self-serve chatbot builder that reached $64,000 MRR three months after launch ([founder-reported, Indie Hackers](https://www.indiehackers.com/post/how-a-college-student-reached-64-000-mo-in-6-months-by-being-an-ai-first-mover-ba7981f6e1)). [My AskAI](/cases/my-askai/) is closer to Chatbase, a small team selling plans that plug into existing help desks.

## What lessons does Decagon's valuation hold for founders?

- **A hot category can carry a valuation.** Decagon's price tripled in six months without a new public revenue figure.
- **Tender offers reward early staff.** Letting 300+ employees sell shares at the new price helped Decagon keep its team.
- **Silence invites comparison.** Because Sierra publishes ARR and Decagon does not, Decagon is measured against a rival's numbers.
- **Named customers stand in for revenue.** Decagon pointed to brands like Avis Budget Group and Oura Health, not dollars.
