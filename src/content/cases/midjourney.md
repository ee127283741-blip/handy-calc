Midjourney is one of the best-known AI image generators. It has also never taken venture money. In August 2022, founder David Holz told The Register, "We're self-funded. We have no investors," and said the team was about 10 people ([founder-reported, The Register](https://www.theregister.com/2022/08/01/david_holz_midjourney/)). Less than three years later, Disney and Universal said in a lawsuit that Midjourney had made $300 million the previous year ([media-reported, NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/business/business-news/disney-universal-sue-ai-image-company-midjourney-unlicensed-star-wars-rcna212369)). The most-watched number about the company came from the studios suing it, not from the company.

## Who is David Holz, and why did he start Midjourney after Leap Motion?

David Holz came to Midjourney after more than a decade running a hardware company. The Register reports that he ran Leap Motion, a hand-tracking company, for twelve years, and that it had about 100 employees when he left ([The Register](https://www.theregister.com/2022/08/01/david_holz_midjourney/)). In our reading, that history explains a lot about Midjourney: a small team on purpose, no outside money, and a founder who had already run a larger company and decided against doing it again. He described the new company as a research lab first, with a business model built around one question: do people enjoy using it?

## Why has Midjourney never taken venture capital?

Holz says Midjourney never needed it, because it made money almost from the start. In the same 2022 interview he said the company was "already profitable." He described the pricing logic plainly: work out what the compute costs, then add a margin that is "hopefully enough to feed and house us" ([founder-reported, The Register](https://www.theregister.com/2022/08/01/david_holz_midjourney/)).

> "We don't have like $100 million of somebody else's money to lose." — David Holz ([founder-reported, The Register](https://www.theregister.com/2022/08/01/david_holz_midjourney/))

The stance held after the company grew. When Meta announced a deal in August 2025 to license Midjourney's image and video technology, TechCrunch reported that Holz said the company remained independent with no investors. It also noted that Midjourney is one of the few leading AI model developers that has never taken outside funding ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/08/22/meta-partners-with-midjourney-on-ai-image-and-video-models)). The terms of the Meta deal are not disclosed.

## How much money does Midjourney make each year?

The latest sourced figure is $300 million in revenue for 2024. Disney and Universal made that claim in a lawsuit filed in June 2025 ([media-reported, NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/business/business-news/disney-universal-sue-ai-image-company-midjourney-unlicensed-star-wars-rcna212369)). Earlier figures come from the press. Entrepreneur, citing The New York Times, reported $50 million in revenue in 2022 ([media-reported, Entrepreneur](https://www.entrepreneur.com/business-news/disney-universal-sue-ai-startup-midjourney-plagiarism/493100)). TechCrunch, citing The Information, wrote that by 2023 the company was reportedly on pace for $200 million ([media-reported, TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/08/22/meta-partners-with-midjourney-on-ai-image-and-video-models)).

That is our arithmetic, not a reported monthly number. Midjourney itself has not published any of these figures. Profit, total revenue and any sourced 2025 revenue are not disclosed.

## What does a Midjourney subscription cost?

Midjourney sells monthly plans. Entrepreneur reported in June 2025 that they range from $10 a month for Basic to $120 a month for Mega ([media-reported, Entrepreneur](https://www.entrepreneur.com/business-news/disney-universal-sue-ai-startup-midjourney-plagiarism/493100)). There is no ad-supported free tier in that range, so revenue comes straight from subscriptions. Holz's cost-plus framing from 2022 still describes the model well: the higher plans mostly buy more GPU time. Video joined the product in June 2025, when Midjourney released its first video model, V1 ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/08/22/meta-partners-with-midjourney-on-ai-image-and-video-models)).

## How is Midjourney different from Photo AI, HeadshotPro and Runway?

Midjourney sells a general creative tool to anyone. [Photo AI](/cases/photo-ai/) and [HeadshotPro](/cases/headshotpro/) sell one outcome: photos of the customer. That narrow promise lets very small teams compete. Pieter Levels reported Photo AI passing $100K a month in September 2024 as a solo founder ([founder-reported, Indie Hackers](https://www.indiehackers.com/post/tech/pieter-levels-just-passed-100-000-a-month-in-revenue-with-photoai-NToMGI3ZjwSBOfTywZnG)). HeadshotPro's revenue is not disclosed, but Danny Postma told Unite.AI that his team spent a full year building custom models for one job, business headshots ([founder-reported, Unite.AI](https://www.unite.ai/danny-postma-founder-of-headshotpro-interview-series/)).

All three share a habit: they keep teams small and rely on revenue instead of large funding rounds. The difference is scale. Midjourney's $300 million 2024 figure ([media-reported, NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/business/business-news/disney-universal-sue-ai-image-company-midjourney-unlicensed-star-wars-rcna212369)) comes from training its own models, which also made it the target of a studio lawsuit. Photo AI rents hosted GPU models, and HeadshotPro builds on open-source models. For a creative AI company that took a different path in video, see [Runway](/cases/runway/).

## What lessons does Midjourney hold for bootstrapped AI founders?

- **Charge for compute from the first day.** Holz priced at cost plus a margin and said Midjourney was profitable by 2022, which made investors optional.
- **A small team can carry a large product.** About 10 people ran Midjourney in 2022, with hundreds of millions in revenue reported in later years.
- **Silence has a cost.** Because Midjourney publishes no numbers, its best-known revenue figure comes from a lawsuit against it.
- **Independence still allows deals.** The Meta licensing agreement brought in a large partner without giving up equity, and Holz still says the company has no investors.
