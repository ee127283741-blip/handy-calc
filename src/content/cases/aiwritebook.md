Plenty of AI tools can produce a chapter. AIWriteBook bets that authors want the whole pipeline, from first outline to a file Amazon will accept. In November 2026 that bet showed $12,388 in monthly recurring revenue and $167,285 in all-time revenue ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/aiwritebook)), checked through RevenueCat rather than Stripe. The founder is not named on the listing.

## What does AIWriteBook do for authors?

AIWriteBook is a book creation platform. The TrustMRR listing says an author can start from scratch or import a .docx, .pdf or .epub manuscript, and the AI learns the author's style before generating chapters ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/aiwritebook)). Each book gets character development, chapter outlines and a story bible meant to keep the plot consistent.

> "Write, edit, format and distribute books in your voice." — AIWriteBook homepage ([founder-reported, AIWriteBook](https://aiwritebook.com/))

The listing also mentions an audiobook studio, a picture book maker and a KDP Chrome extension. The homepage quotes reviewers who value exporting files ready for Amazon KDP and KDP Print.

## How much does AIWriteBook make, and why is the last month lower than MRR?

AIWriteBook had $12,388 in MRR and 427 active subscriptions in November 2026, but collected only $7,140 in the 30 days before the snapshot ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/aiwritebook)). All-time revenue was $167,285.

## Where does AIWriteBook find 20,000 users?

The TrustMRR listing estimates about 20,000 users, while the homepage claims 50,000+ authors and the listing description says 15,700+ ([founder-reported, AIWriteBook homepage](https://aiwritebook.com/)). The three counts do not match, so treat any single user number with care.

Its trust signals are public review sites. The homepage shows a 4.7 rating from 657 Trustpilot reviews and badges from AppSumo and There's An AI For That. An AppSumo presence suggests some early users came through a deal marketplace. The site is offered in eight languages, from Spanish to Japanese.

## How does AIWriteBook compare with Jenni AI and Rytr?

All three sell AI writing help, but to different writers.

| Company | Writer it targets | Billing check |
|---|---|---|
| AIWriteBook | Book authors and self-publishers | RevenueCat, $12,388 MRR ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/aiwritebook)) |
| [Jenni AI](/cases/jenni-ai/) | Students and academic writers | See case |
| [Rytr](/cases/rytr/) | General copy and marketing text | See case |

Our observation: Jenni AI narrowed from marketers to academics and Rytr stayed broad. AIWriteBook narrows by output, a finished book, and wins on workflow features such as KDP formatting that a general writer does not need.

## What are the takeaways from AIWriteBook for niche AI writing tools?

- **Sell the finished artifact.** AIWriteBook promises a publishable book, not a block of text.
- **Workflow beats raw generation.** Outlines, a story bible and KDP export are what reviewers praise on the homepage.
- **Watch cash, not just MRR.** $12,388 of MRR sat beside $7,140 of 30-day revenue ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/aiwritebook)).
- **Public reviews do the selling.** 657 Trustpilot reviews give a faceless brand social proof.
