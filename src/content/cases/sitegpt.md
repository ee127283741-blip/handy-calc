## Origin of the idea
Bhanu was building a blogging product, Feather, and prototyped a "chat with this site" feature. The feature drew more interest than the main product, so it became its own business.

## How they started
He built it during March 2023 and launched on 30 March with a tweet to about 10K followers, a Show HN post that reached the front page, and a Product Hunt launch that finished #1 of the day.

## Tools & tech stack
Remix on Cloudflare Workers, Fauna as the database, OpenAI for answers and Pinecone for vector search.

## Marketing
Building in public on X, then mostly word of mouth. He later leaned on free tools and content ("engineering as marketing") for search traffic.

## Sales & pricing
Paid-only from day one with no free plan, priced high enough to cover unpredictable API costs.

## Monthly revenue
About **$15K MRR** roughly six months after launch (founder-reported, Oct 2023).

## Total revenue
Not disclosed with a dated primary source.

## Founding date & timeline
- **Mar 2023:** Launch; ~15K visitors on day one.
- **Apr 2023:** Passed $10K MRR, then lost ~$5K to churn.
- **Oct 2023:** ~$15K MRR.

## Team size
Two founders, no employees.

## Key takeaways
- Watch which feature people ask about, then spin it out.
- Launch spikes churn; plan for month two.
- Paid-only filters out tyre-kickers when API costs are real.
