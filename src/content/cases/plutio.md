Plutio sells the opposite of an AI point tool. It promises freelancers one branded app for projects, proposals, contracts, invoices and forms, so they can stop paying for five separate subscriptions. In November 2026 it showed $19,942 in MRR and $1,502,477 in all-time revenue ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/plutio-super-work-ai)). The listing now carries the name "Super Work AI", a sign of how older SaaS products are repositioning around AI.

## Why did Leo Bassam build Plutio for freelancers?

Plutio grew out of the founder's own freelance work. Leo Bassam is named as the founder on TrustMRR, which lists March 2018 as the founding date and the United Kingdom as the country. The problem statement on the listing is blunt: running a business across dozens of apps is tough and overwhelming.

> "One app to run, grow, and automate your business." — Plutio, company description ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/plutio-super-work-ai))

The target buyer is a freelancer or small business that sends proposals, signs contracts and invoices clients, and wants one branded place for all of it. The listing claims about 17,881 users.

## What did Plutio look like in its early podcast years?

The early business was small. On a December 2019 episode of the SaaS Interviews podcast, Bassam described about 600 paying customers and said most churn came from onboarding, because the product kept changing ([founder-reported, SaaS Interviews](https://www.audioscrape.com/podcast/saas-interviews-with-ceos-startups-founders/episode/1604-would-you-sell-one-time-for-life-plans-if-youre-trying-to-build-an-enduring-saas-company-96800)). He also discussed whether one-time lifetime plans fit an enduring SaaS company, the question that titled the episode.

That onboarding problem is common for all-in-one tools. The more a product covers, the longer a new user takes to set it up, and the easier it is to quit in week one.

## What does Plutio charge for its Core, Pro and Max plans?

Plutio uses three tiers that scale by clients, contributors, AI credits, workflow actions and storage ([founder-reported, Plutio pricing](https://www.plutio.com/pricing)).

| Plan | Monthly | Annual | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Core | $19 | $190 | [founder-reported, Plutio pricing](https://www.plutio.com/pricing) |
| Pro | $49 | $490 | [founder-reported, Plutio pricing](https://www.plutio.com/pricing) |
| Max | $199 | $1,990 | [founder-reported, Plutio pricing](https://www.plutio.com/pricing) |

Core allows 9 active clients and 500 AI credits. Pro opens unlimited clients and 30 contributors, and Max adds 10,000 AI credits and 2 TB of storage. Metering AI credits by plan is how Plutio passes model costs on to heavier users.

## How much MRR does Plutio make in 2026?

Plutio made $19,942 in MRR from 905 active subscriptions in November 2026, with $1,502,477 in all-time revenue ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/plutio-super-work-ai)). TrustMRR verifies these with a Stripe API key.

MRR divided by subscriptions is about $22.03 ([calc, from TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/plutio-super-work-ai)), close to the Core plan price. That suggests most paying users sit on the cheapest tier or on older, lower-priced plans. The team size is not disclosed, so revenue per employee can't be worked out.

## How does Plutio differ from Motion and Sintra?

Plutio, [Motion](/cases/motion/) and [Sintra](/cases/sintra/) all sell to people running small operations, but they automate different things. Motion started with an AI calendar and task planner, and Sintra sells AI helpers for small-business tasks. Plutio is the system of record: the client, the contract and the invoice live in it.

In our reading, that makes Plutio slower to grow but stickier. A freelancer can drop an AI helper in a minute, while moving every client record and invoice out of Plutio takes real work. The flip side is the onboarding friction Bassam described in 2019.

## What can founders learn from Plutio's long climb?

- **Consolidation is a pitch.** Plutio sells fewer subscriptions, not more features ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/plutio-super-work-ai)).
- **Onboarding decides churn for all-in-one tools.** Bassam linked most early churn to it.
- **Meter AI by credits.** Plutio scales AI credits by plan instead of giving them away.
- **Rename around what buyers search for.** The "Super Work AI" label puts an older product in a new category.
