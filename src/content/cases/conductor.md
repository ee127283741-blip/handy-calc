QuickBooks Desktop is old software, installed on office PCs, and hard for modern apps to talk to. That difficulty is the whole business of Conductor. In November 2026 the company showed $47,194 in monthly recurring revenue and $513,304 in total revenue ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/conductor)), from a product most consumers will never see.

## Why would developers pay for a QuickBooks Desktop API?

Developers pay Conductor because QuickBooks Desktop has no simple cloud API of its own. Conductor describes itself as "a hosted API and MCP server for QuickBooks Desktop and QuickBooks Enterprise", connected through the QuickBooks Web Connector, so apps can read and write QuickBooks data in real time ([founder-reported, Conductor homepage](https://conductor.is)).

> "The best QuickBooks Desktop & Enterprise API on the planet" — Conductor homepage ([founder-reported, Conductor](https://conductor.is))

The MCP server is the newer angle. The homepage says users can connect AI tools such as Claude, ChatGPT or Cursor to their QuickBooks data. Conductor also says it deletes API request logs after 30 days and keeps no copy of customer QuickBooks data.

## How much revenue does Conductor bring in?

Conductor brought in $47,194 in MRR in November 2026, $44,271 over the last 30 days and $513,304 since its July 2022 founding ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/conductor)). TrustMRR verifies these with a Stripe API key.

The two figures disagree in an instructive way. The founder statement on the listing gives about $350K ARR, growing 7% month over month, with a projection of $500K ARR by the end of 2026. Yet Stripe-verified MRR of $47,194 times 12 is about $566K ([calc, from TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/conductor)), already above that target. The founder note is probably older than the live Stripe data. Team size is not disclosed.

## How does Conductor's per-file pricing scale?

Conductor charges $49 per QuickBooks company file per month in production, with a free trial for development and tailored Enterprise pricing for many connections ([founder-reported, Conductor homepage](https://conductor.is)). Customers are software companies that connect their own users' QuickBooks files.

That model explains the average. MRR divided by 314 subscriptions is about $150.30 per subscription ([calc, from TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/conductor)), roughly three company files each. The homepage says Conductor syncs 1,800+ businesses, which fits a pattern of each paying customer bringing many end businesses.

## How does Conductor compare with Supabase and n8n?

Conductor is a single-integration developer tool, far smaller than the two infrastructure cases it sits beside. All three sell plumbing to developers, which makes the scale gap the main lesson.

Our observation: n8n connects hundreds of apps; Conductor connects one, very deeply. A narrow API can be a solid business without broad appeal because the pain is acute and few competitors want to deal with Windows-era desktop software. Conductor's figure is also the only Stripe-verified one here.

## What does Conductor teach about boring niches?

Conductor's open data gives a rare view of a developer business. TrustMRR lists 197.2K lines of code and $2.60 in revenue per line ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/conductor)), and around 1,500 B2B users. Those numbers describe a mature codebase serving a focused audience, not a quick wrapper.

Timing also changed the pitch. A QuickBooks Desktop API built in 2022 became an "MCP server" for AI tools in 2026, which gave an old integration a new buyer: anyone wiring accounting data into an AI agent.

## What lessons does Conductor offer API builders?

- **Hard integrations are moats.** Conductor's whole product is access to QuickBooks Desktop, which is difficult to reach from modern software ([founder-reported, Conductor homepage](https://conductor.is)).
- **Price on the unit that grows with the customer.** $49 per company file scales as each software customer adds end users.
- **Recheck stated numbers against live data.** The founder's ~$350K ARR note sits below the $47,194 Stripe MRR on the same page ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/conductor)).
- **Repackage for AI agents.** Adding an MCP server put an existing API in front of a new category of buyers.
