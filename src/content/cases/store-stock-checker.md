Some products take years to reach $30K a month. Store Stock Checker took about a month. TrustMRR dates it to October 2026, and by November 9 it showed $30,421 in MRR against $30,433 in all-time revenue ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/store-stock-checker)). Nearly every dollar it has ever made came in at once, from people trying to buy a new iPhone.

## What does Store Stock Checker watch for iPhone buyers?

Store Stock Checker watches Apple Store pickup stock and sends an alert the moment a chosen phone is available. Users pick an exact model, color and storage size and a set of stores, and the service notifies them when that combination appears ([founder-reported, Store Stock Checker](https://storestockchecker.com/)). Its November 2026 homepage is built around the iPhone Duo.

The homepage counts 125,180 notifications sent across 449 Apple Stores, with alerts arriving about two seconds after stock appears. TrustMRR lists a Rust backend on Cloudflare, which fits a product whose value is speed. This is not an AI product; we include it because it shows how fast a sharp, timely utility can earn next to the AI tools on this site.

## Why did Store Stock Checker earn almost all its revenue at once?

It earned almost everything at once because demand for a new phone spikes at launch. All-time revenue of $30,433 is only $12 more than MRR ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/store-stock-checker)), so most subscriptions started in the same short window. That is the shape of a product launch riding someone else's product launch.

The open question is retention. A buyer who gets their phone has little reason to keep paying, so MRR measured during a launch spike may fall once stock settles. TrustMRR cannot show that yet because the product is so new.

## How does Store Stock Checker make money from alerts?

Store Stock Checker makes money by keeping the check free and charging for the alert. The website says checking iPhone Duo stock is free and alerts start at $5 a month ([founder-reported, Store Stock Checker](https://storestockchecker.com/)). The TrustMRR profile lists three tiers: Starter at $5, Plus at $10 and Max at $20 a month ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/store-stock-checker)).

An average near $7.63 per subscription suggests a mix weighted toward Starter with some Plus and Max buyers. The site also runs a referral program, and TrustMRR lists SEO, Reddit, X, email, Product Hunt, press and referrals as channels. The founder, Bryce, runs it as a one-person bootstrapped project and has put it up for sale.

> "Far too busy to manage this SaaS." — Bryce ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/store-stock-checker))

## What are the lessons from Store Stock Checker's launch month?

- **Ride a scheduled spike.** Apple launches create predictable, intense demand, and the product earned $30,433 in weeks ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/store-stock-checker)).
- **Charge for the alert, not the look.** Free checks build trust; paid alerts carry the value.
- **Speed is the feature.** A two-second alert and a Rust backend match a race where seconds decide who gets the phone.
- **Launch-month MRR is not a steady state.** Revenue tied to one event needs a plan for the months after it.
