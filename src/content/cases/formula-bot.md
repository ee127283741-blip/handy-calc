## Origin of the idea
Bressler saw how many people struggle to write spreadsheet formulas and how well GPT-3 translated plain English into them. Turning a sentence into a working formula was a narrow, obvious job.

## How they started
A rough free MVP went up in July and was shared on Reddit's r/excel and r/InternetIsBeautiful, where it collected more than 10,000 upvotes. About a month later he added sign-up and subscriptions.

## Tools & tech stack
Built without code: Bubble for the app, OpenAI for generation, Stripe for payments, Sendinblue for email and Localize.js for translation. The first version took about a month.

## Marketing
Reddit, a TikTok video that went viral, a Product Hunt launch, and creators who featured it without being paid.

## Sales & pricing
Free tier with limited uses, then a low-priced monthly subscription. He paid several thousand dollars in API costs before any revenue came in.

## Monthly revenue
**$3.3K MRR** in November 2022 (founder-reported). Later figures exist in secondary sources but are not dated well enough to use.

## Total revenue
**$22K** between the September launch and November 2022 (founder-reported).

## Founding date & timeline
- **Jul 2022:** Free MVP on Reddit.
- **Sep 2022:** Paid launch.
- **Nov 2022:** $3.3K MRR, 1.6K paying users.
- **Later:** Renamed Formula Bot and expanded to data analysis.

## Team size
One founder, building alone with no-code tools.

## Key takeaways
- A narrow job ("write my formula") is easy to explain and share.
- No-code is enough to validate an AI product.
- Communities like Reddit can launch a product for free.
