Many churches hand out a weekly bulletin with the week's announcements. bltn moved that job online in July 2014, long before most startups on this site existed. Twelve years later the bootstrapped US company had $250,559 in all-time revenue and $3,360 in the last 30 days ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/bltn)). The founder has kept it bootstrapped the whole time.

## What does bltn publish for church teams?

bltn turns one weekly workflow into online church bulletins, print-ready bulletins, email newsletters, text messages and social posts ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/bltn)). The goal is to cut copy-and-paste publishing and keep every announcement current for church communication teams.

> "We sell subscriptions to digital bulletin service for churches. We bill monthly." — bltn company description on TrustMRR ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/bltn))

The listing adds that bltn is made by two brothers passionate about church and technology. Justin Funk is the founder named on the page.

## Why does bltn show $38 MRR but $3,360 a month?

The two figures disagree, and the page does not explain why. The company says it bills monthly, yet TrustMRR counted only 2 active subscriptions worth $38 in MRR while $3,360 came in over the last 30 days ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/bltn)). One possibility is that much of the billing does not run through Stripe subscriptions that TrustMRR recognizes, but the listing does not say.

We report both numbers as shown. For a reader, the all-time figure is the steadier signal: a quarter of a million dollars over twelve years.

## What lessons does bltn teach about boring software?

- **Weekly rituals make durable products.** Churches publish every week, and bltn has served that job since 2014.
- **One input, many outputs.** A single workflow feeds web, print, email, text and social.
- **Read the metrics carefully.** $38 MRR and $250,559 all time describe the same company.
- **Bootstrapped can mean patient.** Twelve years without funding is a strategy, not a stall.
