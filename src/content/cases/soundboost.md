Mastering is the last step before a song is released, and for most bedroom producers it is the step they understand least. SoundBoost lets them type what they want instead: "more punch, softer cymbals". That idea, first sold under the hard-to-say name Diktatorial Suite, showed $30,654 in MRR and $329,820 in all-time revenue in November 2026 ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/soundboost-ai-formerly-diktatorial)).

## How does SoundBoost let musicians master with words?

SoundBoost replaces plugin knobs with plain-language requests. The About page says users describe the sound in words, pick a reference track or switch on Voice Mode, and get a revision back in seconds from one of six AI mastering engineers with different tastes ([founder-reported, SoundBoost](https://soundboost.ai/about)). Because each new attempt is quick, trying one more idea costs almost nothing.

The company says it wrote its own mastering engine and that Engine v4 learned on audio it generates itself, never on user uploads. It also blind tested the engine with working musicians before release. These are company statements; the page does not publish the test results. Around mastering, the toolbox now includes stem splitting and vocal removal, a music video generator, a visual creator, and free tools such as a LUFS meter.

## Why did Diktatorial Suite rename itself SoundBoost?

It renamed itself because people could not say the old name. The About page says the beta launched as Diktatorial Suite in February 2024, passed 1,000 masterings a month later and finished #4 Product of the Day on Product Hunt ([founder-reported, SoundBoost](https://soundboost.ai/about)). The rename followed in 2025; the release notes date it to June 2, 2025.

> "In 2025 we renamed ourselves SoundBoost, mostly because nobody could pronounce the old name." — SoundBoost About page ([founder-reported, SoundBoost](https://soundboost.ai/about))

The legal entity is still DIKTATORIAL Inc., per the site footer. TrustMRR lists Berkan Cesur as the founder and November 2023 as the founding date, a few months before the public beta. The same release notes also disclose a June 2025 security incident in which an accidentally exposed mailing key was replaced, an unusually candid note for a small company.

## How does SoundBoost compare with Suno and Neural Frames?

SoundBoost, [Suno](/cases/suno/) and [Neural Frames](/cases/neural-frames/) all sell AI to musicians, but at different stages of making a song.

| Company | Stage of the song | Revenue figure | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| SoundBoost | Mastering, stems, music videos | $30,654 MRR (Nov 2026) | [verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/soundboost-ai-formerly-diktatorial) |
| [Suno](/cases/suno/) | Generating the song itself | ≈$25M a month, implied from $300M ARR (Mar 2026) | [founder-reported, Unite.AI](https://www.unite.ai/suno-reaches-2-million-paid-subscribers-and-300m-arr-in-two-years) |
| [Neural Frames](/cases/neural-frames/) | Music videos from audio | $17K MRR (Oct 2023) | [founder-reported, Starter Story](https://www.starterstory.com/stories/neural-frames) |

Our observation: SoundBoost works for musicians who still write their own songs, while Suno replaces the writing. That is a smaller market but a less contested one with rights holders. SoundBoost's new music video generator also moves it onto Neural Frames' turf, a sign that small music tools are bundling to raise what each subscriber gets.

## What lessons does SoundBoost hold for music-tool founders?

- **Turn expert knobs into plain words.** Prompt-based mastering opened a technical step to beginners, and SoundBoost now counts 703,336+ masterings ([founder-reported, SoundBoost](https://soundboost.ai/about)).
- **A hard name is a real cost.** The company renamed because users could not pronounce it, and said so publicly.
- **Annual plans change the cash picture.** Revenue in the last 30 days ran well above MRR, which annual billing helps explain.
- **Bundle the next job.** Stems and music videos give the same musician more reasons to stay subscribed.
