Perplexity revenue grew about tenfold in 17 months while Google was still the default way to search. In April 2024 the AI answer engine had $20M in ARR ([media-reported, TechCrunch citing Bloomberg](https://techcrunch.com/2024/04/23/perplexity-is-raising-250m-at-2-point-5-3b-valuation-ai-search-sources-say/)). By September 2025 that figure was approaching $200M, and investors valued the company at $20B ([media-reported, TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/09/10/perplexity-reportedly-raised-200m-at-20b-valuation/)). For a product that mostly sells a $20 subscription, that is a lot of subscribers. It is also a valuation far above what the revenue alone would support.

## Who started Perplexity, and what did the founders want to replace?

Four co-founders launched Perplexity in 2022 to replace the page of blue links with one direct answer. CNBC lists them as Aravind Srinivas (CEO), Denis Yarats, Johnny Ho and Andy Konwinski ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2025/06/10/perplexity-cnbc-disruptor-50.html)). The product answers a question in plain language and cites the pages it used. By the end of 2024 it was handling 20 million questions a day, by the company's own count ([founder-reported, CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2025/06/10/perplexity-cnbc-disruptor-50.html)). Two months earlier the figure had been about 15 million a day ([media-reported, TechCrunch citing WSJ](https://techcrunch.com/2024/10/20/perplexity-is-reportedly-looking-to-fundraise-at-an-8b-valuation/)).

> "Search shouldn't be about endless links and ads — it should give the user directly what they want" — Aravind Srinivas ([founder-reported, CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2025/06/10/perplexity-cnbc-disruptor-50.html))

## How much revenue does Perplexity make each year?

Perplexity's last dollar figure is ARR approaching $200M in September 2025, from a source familiar with the company ([media-reported, TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/09/10/perplexity-reportedly-raised-200m-at-20b-valuation/)). That is roughly $16.7M a month ([calc, from TechCrunch's ~$200M](https://techcrunch.com/2025/09/10/perplexity-reportedly-raised-200m-at-20b-valuation/)). That is our arithmetic, not a reported monthly number. In June 2026 Srinivas told CNBC that annualized revenue had tripled since the start of the year, but he gave no dollar amount ([founder-reported, CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/06/03/perplexity-ceo-ai-valuations-computer-agentic.html)). Total revenue and profit are not disclosed.

## Why is the Perplexity valuation so far ahead of its sales?

Investors priced Perplexity as a possible challenger to Google, not as a $200M subscription business. The valuation rose faster than the revenue. In January 2024 it raised nearly $74M at a $540M valuation, and by March 2024 it was valued at $1B ([media-reported, TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2024/04/23/perplexity-is-raising-250m-at-2-point-5-3b-valuation-ai-search-sources-say/)). In May 2025 it was finishing a $500M raise at $14B ([media-reported, CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2025/05/12/perplexity-funding-round-comet.html)). Bloomberg then reported $100M at $18B in July, and The Information reported $200M at $20B in September. PitchBook put total funding at $1.5B ([media-reported, TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/09/10/perplexity-reportedly-raised-200m-at-20b-valuation/)). On our arithmetic, $20B is about 100 times the ~$200M ARR (calc). CNBC still described $20B as the latest reported valuation in June 2026 ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/06/03/perplexity-ceo-ai-valuations-computer-agentic.html)).

## What does Perplexity charge, and who pays?

Perplexity makes money from a free-to-paid subscription ladder, business seats and an API. Pro costs $20 a month ([founder-reported, Perplexity](https://www.perplexity.ai/hub/blog/introducing-perplexity-max)). In July 2025 the company added Max for power users. The help center lists Max at $200 a month or $2,000 a year ([founder-reported, Perplexity Help Center](https://www.perplexity.ai/help-center/en/articles/11680686-perplexity-max)). For companies, Enterprise Pro cost $40 a person a month in July 2025 ([media-reported, TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/07/02/perplexity-launches-a-200-monthly-subscription-plan/)). Perplexity has not said how many people pay or how revenue splits across these lines.

## How does Perplexity compare with TypingMind, Merlin and Magai?

All four sell paid access to AI answers. Only Perplexity has raised venture money at Google-challenger prices. [TypingMind](/cases/typingmind/) is a chat interface where users bring their own API key and buy one-time licences ([media-reported, Lemon Squeezy](https://lemonsqueezy.com/case-study/typing-mind)). Tony Dinh's TypingMind passed $500K in total revenue in about a year ([founder-reported, Starter Story](https://starterstory.com/typingmind-breakdown)), with a team of four. [Magai](/cases/magai/) bundles many models into one subscription and reached nearly $100K MRR in February 2026 for a no-code founder ([founder-reported, Indie Hackers](https://www.indiehackers.com/post/tech/from-broke-to-100k-mrr-in-four-years-hS1wen0srjGNIWZxSnV9)). That is about 1/170th of Perplexity's ~$16.7M a month (calc). [Merlin](/cases/merlin/) put an assistant inside the browser and claimed 1M users within 11 months ([Newsfile press release](https://www.newsfilecorp.com/release/187348/Merlin-AI-Reaches-Milestone-Surpassing-1-Million-Users-Within-11-Months)), but its revenue is not disclosed.

In our reading, the difference is the job each product does. TypingMind, Magai and Merlin are wrappers that give people a better way to reach other companies' models. Perplexity sells a replacement for search: the answer plus citations. That pitch is what let it charge the same $20 as its peers and raise at $20B.

For AI search inside companies rather than on the public web, see [Glean](/cases/glean/).

## What lessons does Perplexity's growth hold for founders?

- **Pick a habit people already have.** Perplexity aimed at search, a daily habit, and reached 20 million questions a day by the end of 2024.
- **Add a top tier once heavy users show up.** Max at $200 a month sits on top of the $20 Pro plan, so the most active users can pay ten times more.
- **Valuation follows the story, revenue follows later.** A $20B price on about $200M ARR shows investors were betting on the category, not on current sales.
- **Most of the numbers come from leaks.** Nearly every revenue figure here comes from unnamed sources. The CEO's 2026 "tripled" claim has no dollar figure, and total revenue is not disclosed.
