Most screen time apps say no. Rewired says "earn it". The iOS app blocks distracting apps until the user beats a short arcade challenge, and it has collected $25,997 since it was founded in January 2026, with 705 active subscriptions in November 2026 ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/rewired-screen-time-blocker)). It is run by one person, and he is now selling it.

## How does the Rewired app turn screen time into a game?

Rewired adds a deliberate step before a distracting app opens. Users pick the apps to block, play a short arcade game to earn phone minutes, and track their usage. The aim is to break the reflex of opening a feed without thinking.

> "Earn screen time through short arcade challenges before accessing distracting apps." — Rewired company description ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/rewired-screen-time-blocker))

It is a native iOS app built with Swift and SwiftUI, with Supabase on the backend and Superwall handling subscriptions. It holds a 4.8 rating from 1,394 App Store ratings ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/rewired-screen-time-blocker)).

## Why did Rewired revenue fall after the summer?

The founder links the slowdown to marketing spend. In his sale note, Nick says there has been no paid ads or UGC spending since July, and that recent revenue has declined while new purchases continue ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/rewired-screen-time-blocker)).

> "Recent revenue has declined, but new purchases continue." — Nick, founder of Rewired ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/rewired-screen-time-blocker))

The listing names Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, Threads, YouTube and Meta Ads as channels. An annual-heavy app depends on a steady flow of new buyers; when ads stop, monthly cash drops fast even while subscriptions stay on the books. He is selling to focus on school and other projects.

## What lessons does Rewired teach solo app makers?

- **A clever mechanic earns ratings.** Arcade challenges helped Rewired reach 4.8 stars from 1,394 ratings.
- **Annual plans hide churn and growth alike.** 705 subscriptions produced only $503 of cash in 30 days.
- **Paid ads can be the whole engine.** Revenue declined after ad and UGC spend stopped in July.
- **Sell with the data open.** Nick offers historical revenue, marketing costs and analytics to buyers.
