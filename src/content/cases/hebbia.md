Most enterprise software founders bring a sales co-founder. George Sivulka didn't. He started Hebbia while working on a PhD in electrical engineering at Stanford, and TechCrunch noted he had no direct business experience and no business-focused co-founder ([media-reported, TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2024/07/09/ai-startup-hebbia-rased-130m-at-a-700m-valuation-on-13-million-of-profitable-revenue/)). He still ended up selling to some of the most demanding buyers in finance. Sacra estimates Hebbia made about $466K in 2022 and reached $48M ARR by August 2026 ([third-party estimate, Sacra](https://sacra.com/c/hebbia/)).

## How did a Stanford PhD student end up building Hebbia?

Hebbia started as a research side project that became a company. Sivulka founded it in 2020 while doing his Stanford PhD. He had worked at NASA as a teenager and finished a Stanford math degree in 2.5 years ([media-reported, TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2024/07/09/ai-startup-hebbia-rased-130m-at-a-700m-valuation-on-13-million-of-profitable-revenue/)). He is still the sole founder and CEO. The first product was an AI search and summarization tool. Hebbia later rebuilt itself as an "AI analyst", and that is the pivot that took off. One detail doesn't line up: Sacra lists 2021 as the founding year, while TechCrunch says 2020 ([third-party estimate, Sacra](https://sacra.com/c/hebbia/)). Sacra places the headquarters in New York.

## What does Hebbia Matrix actually do for an analyst?

Hebbia Matrix turns a stack of documents into a grid of answers. Matrix can take in many files of any length and replies in a table that looks like a spreadsheet. Sivulka's example is pulling information from SEC filings to compare one company with its competitors ([founder-reported via TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2024/07/09/ai-startup-hebbia-rased-130m-at-a-700m-valuation-on-13-million-of-profitable-revenue/)). According to Sacra, Matrix splits each question into parallel sub-tasks, routes them to suitable models and shows citations for every answer. It also connects to data sources such as PitchBook and FactSet, and Hebbia bought FlashDocs to generate slides and memos ([third-party estimate, Sacra](https://sacra.com/c/hebbia/)).

The format works because bankers and investors already think in spreadsheets. Sivulka said 30% of all asset managers used Hebbia in 2024, for due diligence, asset pricing and research ([founder-reported via TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2024/07/09/ai-startup-hebbia-rased-130m-at-a-700m-valuation-on-13-million-of-profitable-revenue/)). Sacra's more recent count is more than 40% of the largest asset managers by AUM ([third-party estimate, Sacra](https://sacra.com/c/hebbia/)).

Total lifetime revenue, current profit and team size are not disclosed.

## Why did investors value Hebbia at 54 times ARR?

Investors paid for momentum and a foothold in finance. In July 2024 Hebbia raised a $130M Series B at about $700M, led by Andreessen Horowitz, with Index Ventures, Google Ventures and Peter Thiel also investing. TechCrunch worked out that this was about 54 times ARR ([media-reported, TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2024/07/09/ai-startup-hebbia-rased-130m-at-a-700m-valuation-on-13-million-of-profitable-revenue/)). Sacra counts $161.1M raised over four rounds ([third-party estimate, Sacra](https://sacra.com/c/hebbia/)).

The price is also high per seat. Sacra estimates a Professional seat costs about $10,000 a year and a Lite seat $3,000-$3,500, and compares the pricing to a Bloomberg Terminal ([third-party estimate, Sacra](https://sacra.com/c/hebbia/)). Deals usually start with a few power users and then spread to other teams through cheaper Lite seats.

Sivulka called Q3 2026 Hebbia's biggest quarter ever. In an October 2026 LinkedIn post he said new ARR tripled quarter over quarter, daily active users grew 5x, and gross revenue retention was above 95% ([founder-reported, LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/sivulka_q3-was-the-biggest-quarter-in-hebbias-history-activity-7518699548546871296-8GAl)).

Hebbia is much smaller than its closest peers but goes deeper in one industry. TechCrunch named [Glean](/cases/glean/) and [Harvey](/cases/harvey/) as Hebbia's closest analogues and reported, citing The Information, that both were valued at slightly over 60x ARR, compared with Hebbia's 54x ([media-reported, TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2024/07/09/ai-startup-hebbia-rased-130m-at-a-700m-valuation-on-13-million-of-profitable-revenue/)). Since then the gap has widened.

Our observation: legal AI found a big, uniform buyer in law firms, and Harvey and Legora grew far faster there. Hebbia's finance buyers pay a lot per seat, but there are fewer of them, so it started a later push into law firms from a smaller base.

## What can founders learn from Hebbia's move into finance?

- **A pivot can matter more than the first idea.** Hebbia's general search and summarization tool became an "AI analyst" for finance, and that version reached $13M ARR by 2024 ([media-reported, TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2024/07/09/ai-startup-hebbia-rased-130m-at-a-700m-valuation-on-13-million-of-profitable-revenue/)).
- **Build around how the user already works.** Matrix answers in a grid because analysts already live in spreadsheets, so the output drops straight into their existing workflow.
- **High seat prices need a land-and-expand plan.** Sacra's estimate of $10,000 for a Professional seat only works because cheaper Lite seats then spread across the firm ([third-party estimate, Sacra](https://sacra.com/c/hebbia/)).
- **Watch retention and usage, not only new deals.** Sivulka chose to highlight a 5x jump in daily users and retention above 95% ([founder-reported, LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/sivulka_q3-was-the-biggest-quarter-in-hebbias-history-activity-7518699548546871296-8GAl)), which are signs the product is sticking.
- **Silence about revenue invites estimates.** Because Hebbia has not given an official number since 2024, outsiders like Sacra fill the gap with estimates such as $48M ARR ([third-party estimate, Sacra](https://sacra.com/c/hebbia/)).
