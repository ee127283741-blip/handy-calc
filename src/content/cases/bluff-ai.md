No prompt box, no editing skills, one upload. That is the whole pitch of Bluff AI, a photo app built for Gen Z that its founders say grew entirely on organic TikTok. Founded in the United States in June 2026, it had made $27,174 by November 2026 ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/bluff-ai)). For a five-month-old bootstrapped app, that is a quick start.

## What does the Bluff AI app do with a selfie?

Bluff AI turns any selfie into either a polished photo or a convincing prank using a template and a single upload. The listing frames the problem as photo editing that normally takes skill, time and paid tools, and says Bluff replaces all three ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/bluff-ai)).

> "Transform any photo with AI. Built for Gen Z, grown entirely on organic TikTok" — Bluff AI company description ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/bluff-ai))

The founders listed are Jordan and Aron Atli Gunnarsson. Team size is not disclosed.

## Why does Bluff AI charge weekly instead of monthly?

Weekly billing is common in viral consumer apps because buyers decide in the moment. Bluff AI asks $0.99 for a 3-day trial, then $6.99 a week including 450 credits, and sells extra consumable credits in the app ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/bluff-ai)). A low trial price matches an impulse download from a TikTok video.

## What are the takeaways from Bluff AI for app makers?

- **Remove the prompt.** Templates and one upload make AI usable for anyone.
- **Organic TikTok can launch an app.** The founders credit it for all their growth.
- **Price for the impulse.** A $0.99 trial lowers the barrier to the first payment.
- **Watch the decay.** Lifetime revenue far above MRR suggests early peaks fade.
