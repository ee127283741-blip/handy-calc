Faceless YouTube channels need long videos, and long videos take time. Gomotion AI sells the shortcut: a documentary of 15 minutes or more, generated in one click and "ready within the hour", per its listing. Four months after the founding date TrustMRR shows, the tool had $73,618 in MRR and $180,870 in all-time revenue ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/iker)).

## What kind of videos does Gomotion AI make?

Gomotion AI makes long documentary-style videos for YouTube automation, the practice of running channels without a person on camera. The TrustMRR listing describes "one-click documentary videos for YouTube automation using AI" and claims finished documentaries of 15+ minutes ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/iker)).

Length is the point. Most AI video tools in this category target 60-second Shorts. A long documentary is harder to script, narrate and illustrate, which is the work Gomotion AI claims to automate. We could not find a public product site linked from the listing, so details of the workflow and output quality are not disclosed beyond these claims.

## How fast did Gomotion AI reach paying users?

Gomotion AI reached paying users very fast by its founder's own account, and the Stripe data supports the scale. TrustMRR dates the company to July 2026, and the founder, listed only as Iker and based in Spain, wrote this on the listing.

> "30k/mrr - first month" — Iker ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/iker))

The same listing claims "5,500+ users in under 3 months" and says the company is bootstrapped. The first-month figure is the founder's claim, not a Stripe reading, but by November 2026 the verified MRR was more than twice that.

## How much revenue has Gomotion AI made so far?

Gomotion AI had $73,618 in MRR from 1,581 active subscriptions in November 2026, and $91,732 in revenue over the last 30 days ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/iker)).

## How does Gomotion AI compare with Faceless.video and StoryShort?

Gomotion AI is the newest of the three and already shows the most verified monthly revenue among them. All three sell faceless video creation, but the others focus on short clips while Gomotion AI focuses on long documentaries.

Our observation: the faceless video market looked crowded by 2026, yet a newcomer found room by changing the length, not the audience. Long videos also fit YouTube's main feed rather than Shorts, which is where channel owners usually earn ad revenue. Whether churn stays low once the novelty fades is the open question, and four months of data cannot answer it.

## What lessons does Gomotion AI teach about crowded niches?

- **Change one variable in a crowded niche.** Going long instead of short gave Gomotion AI $73,618 MRR within months ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/iker)).
- **Sell to people who earn from the output.** Channel operators can link the tool's cost to ad income, which makes a $27 to $107 plan easy to justify.
- **Separate claims from verified data.** The first-month 30k figure is a founder note; the November Stripe reading is the evidence.
- **Young revenue is not proven revenue.** With a July 2026 start, retention is not yet visible in the numbers.
