Speak app revenue passed $100M a year on an annualized basis by late 2025, according to Forbes ([media-reported, Forbes via Benzinga](https://www.benzinga.com/news/topics/25/11/48975908/this-1-billion-ai-startup-backed-by-openai-khosla-ventures-just-hit-100m-revenue-racing-to-beat-duolingo)). Speak started in 2016, years before ChatGPT, as an app for practicing English out loud. Its founders, Connor Zwick and Andrew Hsu, are Thiel Fellows. The bet was that people learn to speak a language by speaking it, and AI finally made a patient, always-available conversation partner cheap enough to sell by subscription.

## How did Connor Zwick and Andrew Hsu start Speak?

Connor Zwick and Andrew Hsu founded Speak in 2016 ([Forbes via Benzinga](https://www.benzinga.com/news/topics/25/11/48975908/this-1-billion-ai-startup-backed-by-openai-khosla-ventures-just-hit-100m-revenue-racing-to-beat-duolingo)). Their premise was that many adults study a language for years but rarely get to practice speaking it. Speak's lessons ask learners to talk back to an AI tutor, which listens, corrects pronunciation and grammar and keeps the conversation going. The app now teaches six languages. In 2022 the OpenAI Startup Fund led Speak's $27M Series B ([founder-reported, Speak blog](https://www.speak.com/blog/speak-announces-27m-series-b-led-by-openai-startup-fund)).

## How much revenue does the Speak app make?

The Speak app makes more than $100M a year on an annualized basis, or about $8.3M a month (calc), per Forbes ([media-reported, Forbes via Benzinga](https://www.benzinga.com/news/topics/25/11/48975908/this-1-billion-ai-startup-backed-by-openai-khosla-ventures-just-hit-100m-revenue-racing-to-beat-duolingo)). Most of it comes from consumer subscriptions; Speak added workplace plans in 2024. Forbes put downloads at about 15 million. Speak has not published its own revenue figure, plan prices or team size, so those are not disclosed here.

## Why did investors value Speak at $1B?

Investors paid for speaking practice that other language apps did not offer. In June 2024 TechCrunch reported a $20M raise that doubled Speak's valuation, with more than 10 million users ([media-reported, TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2024/06/20/language-learning-app-speak-nets-20m-doubles-valuation)). Six months later Accel led a $78M Series C at a $1B valuation, bringing total funding to $162M ([founder-reported, Speak blog](https://www.speak.com/blog/series-c)).

## How does Speak compare with other AI education apps?

Speak is the largest consumer education business in this library. [Jenni AI](/cases/jenni-ai/) sells academic writing help to students, and [Coachvox AI](/cases/coachvox-ai/) lets coaches sell AI versions of themselves; both are far smaller and sell on the web. Speak is a mobile subscription app aimed at adults who want to talk in a new language, closer in model to [Cal AI](/cases/cal-ai/), a consumer app that also grew on monthly and yearly subscriptions.

Our reading: Speak waited years for the technology to match its idea. It started in 2016, before large language models, and its revenue passed $100M only after models could hold an open conversation. Being early cost time, but it also meant Speak already had users, lessons and investors, including OpenAI's fund, when the models arrived.

## What lessons does Speak offer consumer AI founders?

- **Pick the skill AI makes newly possible.** Open-ended speaking practice was hard to automate before large language models.
- **Sell the missing practice.** Learners can find grammar drills anywhere; a partner to talk to every day is what Speak charges for.
- **Early can still win.** A 2016 start meant Speak had a product and users when AI tutors became practical.
- **Consumer subscriptions can reach nine figures.** Forbes put Speak's annualized revenue above $100M, mostly from individual learners.
