## Origin of the idea
StackBlitz had spent years building WebContainers, a way to run Node.js inside the browser. When Claude 3.5 Sonnet arrived in mid-2024, the team saw that a strong coding model plus a full dev environment in the browser could build and run whole apps from a prompt.

## How they started
They built Bolt.new quickly on top of existing tech and announced it at ViteConf in October 2024, while the core business was small.

## Tools & tech stack
WebContainers (WebAssembly-based, in-browser runtime), Claude 3.5 Sonnet for code generation, Ruby on Rails for accounts and billing, Netlify for one-click deploys and Supabase for databases.

## Marketing
A conference keynote, demos that spread on X, a Supabase tutorial video and a fast-growing user subreddit.

## Sales & pricing
Token-based monthly plans; the team added top-ups after heavy users kept running out of tokens.

## Monthly revenue
About **$333K/month**, implied from the $4M ARR reported about four weeks after launch (Nov 2024). Higher later figures come from podcast summaries.

## Total revenue
Not disclosed.

## Founding date & timeline
- **2018:** StackBlitz founded.
- **Mid-2024:** Team tests Claude 3.5 Sonnet with WebContainers.
- **Oct 2024:** Bolt.new launched at ViteConf.
- **Nov 2024:** $4M ARR.

Not disclosed with a dated primary source.

## Key takeaways
- Years of deep tech can suddenly become valuable when models catch up.
- Pivot fast when a new capability fits your stack.
- Usage-based pricing needs easy top-ups.
