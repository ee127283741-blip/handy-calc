## Origin of the idea
Park says he spent about ten years on failed ventures before this one. Jenni first targeted content agencies and SEO writers, then became a general AI writer once GPT-3 arrived.

## How they started
Usage data showed students and researchers were the most loyal users, so the team refocused on academic writing: autocomplete in an academic tone, citations and writing grounded in uploaded papers.

## Tools & tech stack
Large language models behind an editor with features built for academia: support for more than 1,700 citation styles, a document library and citations that link generated text back to source passages.

## Marketing
A viral tweet in July 2022 brought the first surge. After that, the company ran many single-purpose TikTok and Instagram accounts posting short, deadline-themed videos aimed at students.

## Sales & pricing
Freemium with a monthly subscription for unlimited use; students convert around deadlines.

## Monthly revenue
About **$833K/month**, implied from the reported $10M+ ARR (media-reported, Dec 2025).

## Total revenue
Not disclosed as a lifetime figure. Reported annual revenue was about $1.8M in 2023 and about $8M in 2024.

## Founding date & timeline
- **Jul 2022:** Viral tweet, about $10K revenue in a day.
- **2023:** ~$1.8M revenue after the pivot to academic writing.
- **2024:** ~$8M revenue.
- **2025:** $10M+ ARR reported.

## Team size
Nine people until late 2024, 23 afterwards.

## Key takeaways
- Follow the users who stay, not the ones you planned for.
- Narrow positioning (academic) beats a generic AI writer.
- A many-accounts short-video strategy can replace paid ads.
