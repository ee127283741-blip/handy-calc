The Deepgram valuation crossed $1B in a round its CEO said the company did not need. Deepgram raised a $130M Series C at a $1.3B valuation in January 2026, led by AVP ([media-reported, Reuters](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/voice-ai-startup-deepgram-raises-133159683.html)). Scott Stephenson, its co-founder and CEO, told TechCrunch the company had been cash-flow positive the year before ([founder-reported, TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/01/13/deepgram-raises-130m-at-1-3b-valuation-and-buys-a-yc-ai-startup)). Deepgram sells the speech layer many voice AI products are built on, and voice agents made that layer more valuable.

## What does Deepgram sell?

Deepgram sells APIs for speech recognition, text-to-speech and real-time voice agents. Developers send audio and get back text, or send text and get back speech, fast enough for live phone calls. Scott Stephenson co-founded the company and leads it as CEO ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/01/13/deepgram-raises-130m-at-1-3b-valuation-and-buys-a-yc-ai-startup)). In January 2025 it said 200,000 developers used its platform ([founder-reported, Deepgram release](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20250129306075/en)). Its prices, team size and founding date are not disclosed in sources we could check.

> "Any place where there's a text field or a button click, all of those products are working on adding voice," — Scott Stephenson ([founder-reported, Reuters](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/voice-ai-startup-deepgram-raises-133159683.html))

## Does Deepgram disclose its revenue?

No. Deepgram's revenue and ARR are not disclosed. What the company has said is that it was cash-flow positive the year before its Series C and raised from a position of strength rather than need ([founder-reported, TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/01/13/deepgram-raises-130m-at-1-3b-valuation-and-buys-a-yc-ai-startup)). Third-party trackers publish revenue estimates, but none cites the company, so we leave them out. Developer count is the one usage figure Deepgram has published.

## Why did Deepgram buy a restaurant voice startup?

Deepgram bought OfOne, a Y Combinator startup building AI voice ordering for restaurants, alongside its Series C ([media-reported, TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/01/13/deepgram-raises-130m-at-1-3b-valuation-and-buys-a-yc-ai-startup)). Drive-thru and phone ordering are high-volume, noisy and time-sensitive, a hard test for speech recognition. Owning an application in that market gives Deepgram a direct product and a source of difficult real-world audio, beyond selling APIs to other companies.

## How does Deepgram compare with ElevenLabs and Retell AI?

Deepgram, [ElevenLabs](/cases/elevenlabs/) and [Retell AI](/cases/retell-ai/) sit at different layers of the same voice stack. ElevenLabs is best known for lifelike synthetic voices and has much larger reported revenue. Retell AI is a platform for building phone agents and reported passing $40M ARR in January 2026 ([founder-reported, Retell AI release](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/01/29/3228780/0/en/Upgraded-Retell-AI-Voice-Platform-Enables-Corporate-Call-Centers-to-Deploy-Infinite-AI-Sales-and-Support-Agents-Across-Voice-Chat-Email-and-SMS-Company-Revenue-Now-Exceeds-40M-ARR.html)). Deepgram is the speech engine: it turns audio into text and back, which both voices and agents need.

Our reading: infrastructure companies like Deepgram benefit from every voice agent startup, whoever wins. The risk is that model labs and cloud providers bundle speech for free or cheap. Deepgram's apparent answer is to compete on hard, real-time settings and to own a few applications, such as OfOne, itself.

## What are the takeaways from Deepgram for API businesses?

- **Raise when you don't need to.** Being cash-flow positive let Deepgram set terms in its Series C.
- **Ride the wave under the wave.** Voice agents created demand for the speech APIs underneath them.
- **Own a hard use case.** Buying OfOne puts Deepgram in noisy, real-time restaurant ordering.
- **Silence on revenue limits comparisons.** Without ARR, outsiders judge Deepgram by developers and valuation.
