Online forms are one of the most crowded corners of software, with free options everywhere. Formflow still found paying customers. By November 2026 the no-code builder had collected $160,457 in all-time revenue from Stripe, with 130 active subscriptions and $5,702 in monthly recurring revenue ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/formflow)). The gap between those numbers is the most interesting part of the story.

## What kind of forms does Formflow build?

Formflow lets people build interactive, personalized forms without code. The company-written TrustMRR listing describes it simply as a no-code online form builder ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/formflow)), and tags it No-Code, SaaS, Marketing, Analytics and Productivity for a B2B audience.

> "Formflow is a no-code online form builder that allows users to create interactive, personalized forms." — Formflow listing ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/formflow))

The website is getformflow.io. Marketing teams use forms like these for lead capture and quizzes, which is why the listing pairs it with analytics.

## How does Formflow make $160K with only $5,702 MRR?

Formflow had $5,702 MRR and $3,971 in revenue over the last 30 days in November 2026, but $160,457 all time ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/formflow)). Its plans are billed yearly, so much of the cash arrives as annual payments that MRR spreads across 12 months.

| Plan (Nov 2026) | Price | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Free | $0 a month | [founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/formflow) |
| Starter | $39 a month, billed yearly | [founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/formflow) |
| Pro | $79 a month, billed yearly | [founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/formflow) |
| Scale | $159 a month, billed yearly | [founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/formflow) |

MRR divided by 130 subscriptions is about $44 each ([calc, from TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/formflow)), close to the Starter price. The all-time figure is high for a company that lists July 2025 as its founding date; the page does not explain it, and earlier history is not disclosed.

## Why did Formflow collect less than its MRR last month?

Formflow collected $3,971 in the 30 days before the snapshot, below its $5,702 MRR ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/formflow)). With yearly billing, a quiet month for renewals can produce a low 30-day total even when MRR holds. The founder, team size and marketing channels are not disclosed, so the cause cannot be pinned down from the listing.

## How does Formflow compare with Web3Forms and Simple Analytics?

Formflow, Web3Forms and Simple Analytics all sell simple web tools with free tiers to small businesses.

| Company | What it sells | MRR (Nov 2026) | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Formflow | No-code interactive forms | $5,702 | [verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/formflow) |
| [Web3Forms](/cases/web3forms/) | Contact form API | $44,178 | [verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/web3forms) |
| [Simple Analytics](/cases/simple-analytics/) | Privacy-first web analytics | $37,790 | [verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/simple-analytics) |

Our observation: Web3Forms wins with a developer-friendly API and a huge free user base. Formflow targets non-developers who want polished, personalized forms, which supports higher plan prices but faces more competition from big form brands. Simple Analytics shows how a narrow positioning can sustain a small web tool for years.

## What lessons does Formflow offer no-code SaaS builders?

- **Annual billing shapes the numbers.** Formflow's $160,457 all-time revenue dwarfs its $5,702 MRR.
- **Watch the 30-day line.** A falling 30-day total is an early sign to check renewals.
- **Free tiers need a clear upgrade.** Four plans from $0 to $159 give users a ladder to climb.
- **Crowded markets still pay.** A form builder with 130 subscriptions shows there is room for a niche angle.
