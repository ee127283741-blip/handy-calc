Motion ARR changed source in 2025. For years Motion sold an AI calendar to individuals who wanted their day planned for them. Then in May 2025 it launched AI Employees, a bundle of agents for small businesses, and CEO Harry Qi told TechCrunch the bundle passed $10M ARR with more than 10,000 business customers within months ([founder-reported, TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/09/08/y-combinator-backed-motion-raises-fresh-38m-to-build-the-microsoft-office-of-ai-agents)). Motion's pitch moved from organizing one person's time to doing a team's work.

## How did Motion start as an AI calendar?

Motion began as a calendar and task app that automatically schedules a user's to-do list around their meetings, then reshuffles it when plans change. Harry Qi, Ethan Yu, Chander Ramesh and Omid Rooholfada founded it, and it went through Y Combinator ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/09/08/y-combinator-backed-motion-raises-fresh-38m-to-build-the-microsoft-office-of-ai-agents)). The calendar won fans among busy professionals, and Motion added project management for teams. The founding date, team size and plan prices are not disclosed in sources we could check.

## What are Motion's AI Employees?

Motion's AI Employees are agents that do routine work for small and mid-sized businesses, such as sales outreach, customer support and administrative tasks, sold as one bundle next to the calendar and project tools. TechCrunch described Motion's goal as building "the Microsoft Office of AI agents" ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/09/08/y-combinator-backed-motion-raises-fresh-38m-to-build-the-microsoft-office-of-ai-agents)). The bundle launched in May 2025.

> "Motion Raises $60M at $550M Valuation to Build the Agentic Work Suite for Businesses" — Motion press release ([founder-reported, Business Wire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20250905188051/en))

## How much ARR do Motion's AI Employees generate?

Motion's AI Employees passed $10M ARR within months of their May 2025 launch, about $833K a month (calc), with more than 10,000 business customers ([founder-reported, TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/09/08/y-combinator-backed-motion-raises-fresh-38m-to-build-the-microsoft-office-of-ai-agents)). That works out to under $1,000 a year per customer on average (calc), a small-business price point. Motion has not disclosed company-wide ARR or total revenue.

## Why are there two Motion funding figures?

Reports give two amounts for the same September 2025 announcement. SiliconANGLE reported a $38M Series C led by Scale Venture Partners, bringing total funding to $75M ([media-reported, SiliconANGLE](https://siliconangle.com/2025/09/08/motion-lands-38m-funding-expand-agentic-ai-suite-smbs/)). Motion's own press release said it raised $60M at a $550M valuation ([founder-reported, Business Wire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20250905188051/en)). The $60M may include money beyond the Series C; we list both and do not combine them.

## How does Motion compare with Fyxer AI and Magai?

Motion sells a bundle of agents; most productivity cases in this library sell one job. [Fyxer AI](/cases/fyxer-ai/) does email and meeting notes and reported $30M ARR at the end of 2025 ([founder-reported, Fyxer](https://www.fyxer.com/press)). [Magai](/cases/magai/) gives individuals access to many AI models in one subscription. Motion's AI Employees aim at small businesses that want several roles covered at once, at a low price per customer.

Our reading: Motion's existing calendar users gave the agent bundle a running start, because businesses that already planned work in Motion could add agents without a new vendor. The low average price per customer means growth depends on volume, which suits self-serve sales to small companies.

## What lessons does Motion offer productivity founders?

- **Your users can carry a new product.** Motion's calendar customers were the first buyers for its agents.
- **Bundles sell to small businesses.** One subscription that covers several roles is easier to buy than five tools.
- **Low prices need volume.** Under $1,000 a year per customer works only with thousands of customers.
- **Report funding clearly.** Two different round sizes for one announcement make outsiders guess.
