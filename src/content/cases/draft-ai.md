Many creators know what they want to say and stall at the blank text box. Draft AI is built around that gap: you talk, and the app turns the recording into social posts and scripts. In November 2026 it showed $19,894 in MRR from 1,578 active subscriptions ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/draft-ai)). Unlike most cases here, the figures come through RevenueCat, the subscription layer used by mobile apps, rather than Stripe.

## What problem does the Draft AI app solve for creators?

Draft AI targets people who post on social media but don't enjoy writing. The TrustMRR listing describes a voice-to-content app for consumer social media users and tags it under content creation, mobile apps, AI, productivity and social media ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/draft-ai)).

> "Your zero-effort content buddy." — Draft AI, value proposition ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/draft-ai))

Speaking is faster than typing for most people, and a voice note keeps the speaker's own phrasing. The AI's job is to cut and shape that raw material into a post or a script.

## Who is behind Draft AI and getdraft.io?

TrustMRR lists Valery Alexeev as the founder, with a small X following, and the product site is [getdraft.io](https://getdraft.io). The site's title frames the product as a content generator for social media from "Valery's Toolkit", which hints that Draft is one app in a personal portfolio rather than a venture-backed company.

The founding date, team size and funding are not disclosed. The listing shows that revenue tracking began on December 8, 2025, so the verified history is less than a year long.

## How does Draft AI charge users?

Draft AI sells subscriptions inside a mobile app, with a free trial up front. Exact prices are not disclosed on the TrustMRR listing or the product page we checked. Because revenue runs through RevenueCat, the subscriptions are most likely bought through the app stores, though the listing does not name the stores.

## How much monthly revenue does Draft AI earn?

Draft AI earned $19,894 in MRR and $19,662 over the last 30 days in November 2026, from 1,578 active subscriptions ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/draft-ai)). All-time revenue is not disclosed on the listing.

MRR divided by subscriptions is about $12.61 ([calc, from TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/draft-ai)). That is a typical consumer price point, and it means the business needs a large base of subscribers. The 30-day figure sits very close to MRR, which suggests few one-off payments and a steady renewal base.

## How does Draft AI compare with AudioPen and Talknotes?

Voice-to-text writing is a crowded small niche. [AudioPen](/cases/audiopen/) turns rambling voice notes into clean text, and [Talknotes](/cases/talknotes/) turns recordings into notes and tasks. Draft AI narrows the output to social media posts and scripts, and it lives on mobile.

Our observation: the three products share a core model call, so they win on the output format and the channel. AudioPen and Talknotes lean toward notes and thinking, while Draft AI leans toward publishing. That narrower job gives Draft a clearer audience and a clear reason to open the app every day.

## What are the takeaways from Draft AI for app makers?

- **Pick one output.** Draft AI turns voice into posts and scripts, not general notes ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/draft-ai)).
- **Use the subscription stack mobile buyers trust.** RevenueCat-verified revenue shows the in-app route works.
- **Low prices need volume.** About $12.61 per subscription means growth depends on the number of subscribers.
- **Short histories still count.** Less than a year of tracked revenue is enough to show demand.
