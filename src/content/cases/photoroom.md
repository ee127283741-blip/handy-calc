Photoroom revenue grew from a phone app that cleans up product photos. In December 2023, the Paris startup told TechCrunch it was on track for $50M in annual recurring revenue, three years after launch, with more than 100 million app downloads ([founder-reported, TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2024/01/09/sources-photoroom-the-ai-photo-editing-app-is-raising-50m-60m-at-a-500m-600m-valuation/)). At that point it had raised about $21M in total (calc, $64M total minus the $43M round that came next). Few AI apps have reached that revenue on that little capital.

## What does Photoroom do for online sellers?

Photoroom turns a seller's phone snapshot into a clean product photo. It removes and replaces backgrounds, and it generates new product scenes with AI. CEO Matthieu Rouif and CTO Eliot Andres co-founded it in Paris and went through Y Combinator's Summer 2020 batch, its first remote batch ([media-reported, TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2024/01/09/sources-photoroom-the-ai-photo-editing-app-is-raising-50m-60m-at-a-500m-600m-valuation/)). Most use happens on phones. TechCrunch reported that nearly 90% of traffic and usage comes through the mobile app. Brands use it too. Warner Bros, Netflix and Wolt were named as customers, and a Barbie campaign built on Photoroom's API was shared over 1 million times, the company said.

## What is Photoroom's revenue and ARR?

Photoroom's last company-reported figure is the $50M ARR target from December 2023. That is roughly $4.2M a month ([calc, from TechCrunch's $50M](https://techcrunch.com/2024/01/09/sources-photoroom-the-ai-photo-editing-app-is-raising-50m-60m-at-a-500m-600m-valuation/)). In April 2025, Tech.eu reported that Photoroom hit €50M ARR in 2023 and described it as profitable ([media-reported, Tech.eu](https://tech.eu/2025/04/16/we-have-spent-millions-on-ai-training-data-says-photoroom-ceo-who-wants-to-make-the-internet-more-beautiful/)). Note the currency. TechCrunch's figure is in dollars and Tech.eu's is in euros, and neither source reconciles the two. The analyst firm Sacra estimates $94M ARR for 2024, up 89% year over year ([third-party estimate, Sacra](https://sacra.com/c/photoroom/)). Photoroom has not confirmed it. Total revenue and profit are not disclosed.

## Why does Photoroom train its own models from scratch?

Photoroom trains from scratch so it controls what goes into its models and what they can produce. Rouif told Tech.eu that the company has spent millions of euros on buying and licensing training data ([media-reported, Tech.eu](https://tech.eu/2025/04/16/we-have-spent-millions-on-ai-training-data-says-photoroom-ceo-who-wants-to-make-the-internet-more-beautiful/)).

> "As we train our models from scratch, we control the data." — Matthieu Rouif ([founder-reported, Tech.eu](https://tech.eu/2025/04/16/we-have-spent-millions-on-ai-training-data-says-photoroom-ceo-who-wants-to-make-the-internet-more-beautiful/))

He also said the models never see sex or violence because e-commerce doesn't need it. In February 2024 the company launched its own foundation model, Photoroom Instant Diffusion, alongside its $43M round ([founder-reported, TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2024/02/27/confirmed-photoroom-the-ai-image-editor-raised-43m-at-a-500m-valuation/)). The models run at scale. Photoroom said it processes some 5 billion images a year.

## How did Photoroom grow on so little funding?

Photoroom grew on a paid app that businesses use for work. Most of the funding came after the revenue was already there. TechCrunch said its focus on business users anchored growth around a "revenue-generating business model" ([media-reported, TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2024/01/09/sources-photoroom-the-ai-photo-editing-app-is-raising-50m-60m-at-a-500m-600m-valuation/)). The pricing is built for frequent use. In the UK in January 2024, the mobile Pro tier cost £3.99 a week or £69.99 a year, with separate rates for web, Shopify, larger businesses and the API. ## How does Photoroom compare with Photo AI, Virtual Staging AI and Launch Fast?

Photoroom applies the same image technology as consumer AI photo apps, but to a business task that repeats every week. [Photo AI](/cases/photo-ai/) sells portraits of the buyer, and Pieter Levels reported more than $100K a month in September 2024 as a solo founder ([founder-reported, Indie Hackers](https://www.indiehackers.com/post/tech/pieter-levels-just-passed-100-000-a-month-in-revenue-with-photoai-NToMGI3ZjwSBOfTywZnG)). Photoroom's December 2023 target was about 40 times that monthly figure (calc). The difference, in our reading, is repeat use. A seller needs new product photos for every listing. A person needs new headshots rarely.

[Virtual Staging AI](/cases/virtual-staging-ai/) is the closest match in strategy. It also trained its own model so the output would stay faithful to a real object, a room instead of a product. It reached $1M ARR in about 10 months and sold to Zillow ([media-reported, TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2024/02/23/virtual-staging-ai-helps-realtors-digitally-furnish-rooms-within-seconds/)). Photoroom stayed independent and kept raising. [Launch Fast](/cases/launch-fast/) serves the same e-commerce sellers at an earlier step, product research for Amazon, and reported $30K MRR in June 2026 ([founder-reported, Indie Hackers](https://www.indiehackers.com/post/tech/building-a-product-in-48-hours-and-hitting-30k-mrr-as-a-non-technical-founder-wWtWIH5tmwASUbxKaLT9)).

[Lumoo](/cases/lumoo/), built by fashion veterans on Lovable, sells AI product imagery and virtual try-ons to fashion brands.

## What lessons does Photoroom offer e-commerce AI founders?

- **Pick a task that repeats.** Sellers need fresh product photos for every listing, which keeps a subscription useful long after signup.
- **Mobile can be the main product.** Nearly 90% of usage came through the app, so Photoroom met sellers on the phone they already shoot with.
- **Owning the model can be a selling point.** Training from scratch on licensed data let Photoroom tell business customers exactly what its models learned from.
- **Revenue first, big round later.** Photoroom was on track for $50M ARR before the $43M round that took total funding to $64M.
- **Say which currency and which source.** Its $50M and €50M figures come from different outlets, and Sacra's $94M is an estimate; a careful reader should keep them apart.
