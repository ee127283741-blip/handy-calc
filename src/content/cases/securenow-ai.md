Two customers. That is how many active subscriptions securenow.ai had in November 2026, and between them they produced $1,613 in monthly recurring revenue ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/securenow-ai)). For a security tool sold to SaaS founders, that is an early-stage business, but the per-customer value is far higher than most small developer tools manage.

## What does securenow.ai defend SaaS apps against?

securenow.ai protects SaaS apps from abuse without slowing growth. The company-written TrustMRR listing names bots, credential attacks and scraping as threats, and lists real-time visibility, threat detection, IP intelligence, alerts and firewall controls as features ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/securenow-ai)).

> "SecureNow helps SaaS founders protect their apps from abuse without slowing down growth." — securenow.ai listing ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/securenow-ai))

TrustMRR tags it Security, AI, SaaS, Developer Tools and Analytics.

## How is securenow.ai built and who runs it?

The founder of securenow.ai is listed on TrustMRR only as houssine, with the X account codehacker and 140 followers ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/securenow-ai)). ClickHouse is a database built for fast queries over large event logs, which suits traffic analysis. No marketing channels are listed.

## What lessons does securenow.ai hold for early security startups?

- **High value per account comes first.** About $807 per subscription shows buyers will pay for protection.
- **Usage pricing matches the threat.** Apps with more traffic face more abuse and pay more.
- **Two customers is a start, not a market.** The next step is turning one-off buyers into a repeatable channel.
- **Pick infrastructure for the data.** ClickHouse fits a product that analyses traffic at scale.
