In February 2023 Chatbase had $0 in monthly recurring revenue. Three months later it had $64,000 ([founder-reported, Indie Hackers](https://www.indiehackers.com/post/how-a-college-student-reached-64-000-mo-in-6-months-by-being-an-ai-first-mover-ba7981f6e1)). The founder, Yasser Elsaid, was a college student, and the product began as a way to chat with one long PDF. What changed in between was timing: he launched right as ChatGPT hype peaked, and he has said being early to the market mattered more than anything else.

## Why did Yasser Elsaid build Chatbase?

Elsaid wanted to chat with a long PDF. Once that worked, he realised businesses wanted the same thing for their help docs and websites: a chatbot that answers from their own content. The pain on the business side is support volume: the same questions, already answered somewhere in the docs, asked again and again.

## How does Chatbase work?

A business uploads its documents or points Chatbase at its website, and Chatbase creates a chatbot that answers from that content. The bot is placed on the customer's site through an embeddable widget. Every visitor who uses the widget sees the Chatbase product in action. The tech stack is not disclosed.

## How was Chatbase built?

Elsaid started solo and shipped quickly. Shipping quickly was the priority, because the market window was open and he believed timing mattered more than anything else. Specific technical choices are not disclosed. As of May 2023 the team was the founder plus two employees ([unverified, Indie Hackers](https://www.indiehackers.com/post/how-a-college-student-reached-64-000-mo-in-6-months-by-being-an-ai-first-mover-ba7981f6e1)).

## How did Chatbase market and sell its chatbot?

Chatbase launched on X right as ChatGPT hype peaked in early 2023, with launch tweets and demos that went viral. After that came word of mouth and the embeddable widget, which advertises the product on every customer site.

Sales were monthly self-serve plans; early on most revenue came from the higher-priced business plan.

## What challenges did Chatbase face?

A first-mover advantage is temporary. Within months, many similar tools appeared, including [SiteGPT](/cases/sitegpt/). Chatbase later grew into an AI support-agent platform, moving beyond simple document chat. Later ARR figures come from podcasts without clear dates, so current ARR is not disclosed here.

## How much MRR did Chatbase make?

Chatbase reached $64,000 MRR in May 2023 ([founder-reported, Indie Hackers](https://www.indiehackers.com/post/how-a-college-student-reached-64-000-mo-in-6-months-by-being-an-ai-first-mover-ba7981f6e1)), up from $0 at launch in February 2023 ([founder-reported](https://www.indiehackers.com/post/how-a-college-student-reached-64-000-mo-in-6-months-by-being-an-ai-first-mover-ba7981f6e1)). Total revenue is not disclosed, and current ARR is not disclosed.

The timeline is three months long: $0 MRR at launch in February 2023, then $64,000 MRR in May 2023. Later, the product grew into an AI support-agent platform.

## Why did Chatbase work?

Being first in a hype window can be worth more than a moat. Elsaid launched when attention on ChatGPT was at its highest.

He solved his own problem, then asked who would pay for it. The PDF chat he built for himself turned into a business product.

And the widget turned customers into distribution. [PDF.ai](/cases/pdf-ai/) chose a different edge in the same category: a memorable domain.

For another business-software case study in the library, see [Instantly](/cases/instantly/), the bootstrapped cold-email platform.

## What can founders learn from Chatbase?

Being first in a hype window can be worth more than a moat.

Solve your own problem, then ask who would pay for it.

An embeddable widget is free distribution. If your product appears on customers' sites, make it visible.

And expect the window to close. A first-mover lead buys time to build a broader product, which is what Chatbase did by growing into an AI support-agent platform.
