## Origin of the idea
Elsaid wanted to chat with a long PDF. Once that worked, he realised businesses wanted the same thing for their help docs and websites: a chatbot that answers from their own content.

## How they started
A quick launch on X right as ChatGPT hype peaked in early 2023. He has said being early to the market mattered more than anything else.

## Tools & tech stack
OpenAI models with retrieval over the customer's documents, built on Supabase (database, auth and vector search) and a Next.js front end.

## Marketing
Launch tweets and demos that went viral, then word of mouth and an embeddable widget that advertises the product on every customer site.

## Sales & pricing
Monthly self-serve plans; early on most revenue came from the higher-priced business plan.

## Monthly revenue
**$64,000 MRR** on 13 May 2023, up from $0 on 7 February 2023 (founder-reported).

## Total revenue
Not disclosed.

## Founding date & timeline
- **7 Feb 2023:** $0 MRR at launch.
- **13 May 2023:** $64K MRR.
- **Later:** Grew into an AI support-agent platform; later ARR figures come from podcasts without clear dates.

## Team size
Not disclosed with a dated source. He started solo.

## Key takeaways
- Being first in a hype window can be worth more than a moat.
- Solve your own problem, then ask who would pay for it.
- An embeddable widget is free distribution.
