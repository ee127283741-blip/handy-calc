In February 2023 Chatbase had $0 in monthly recurring revenue. Three months later it had $64,000 ([founder-reported, Indie Hackers](https://www.indiehackers.com/post/how-a-college-student-reached-64-000-mo-in-6-months-by-being-an-ai-first-mover-ba7981f6e1)). The founder, Yasser Elsaid, was a fourth-year college student who had interned at Tesla and Meta, and the product began as a way to chat with a single PDF. He tweeted the first version to 16 followers. The tweet went viral before Chatbase even had a pricing page.

## Why did a Meta intern walk away to build Chatbase?

Elsaid moved from Egypt to Canada in 2019 for university and, like many classmates, set his sights on a big tech job. Internships at Tesla and Meta changed his mind. The perks were great, but every day felt the same, and he decided the indie hacker path was more exciting and had more upside.

In his fourth year he started playing with the OpenAI API, inspired by solo founders like Pieter Levels. He did no validation or market research, because it seemed obvious to him that "ChatGPT for your data" would become a thing. Chatbase lets a business build a custom ChatGPT interface trained on its own documents or website and place it on its site through an embeddable widget.

## How did Chatbase go from a PDF tool to chatbots?

Chatbase started as "ChatGPT for your PDFs", the simplest use case: upload a PDF, then chat with it. The MVP took about two months. Elsaid tweeted it on 2 February 2023, the tweet went viral, and he stopped doing anything university-related to focus on Chatbase full time. He failed two classes and says it was worth it. The product then widened from PDFs to any of a business's content, served through the website widget.

| Layer | What Chatbase used (2023) |
|---|---|
| Web app | React, Next.js, Supabase |
| AI | OpenAI API, LangChain, Pinecone |
| Payments | Stripe |

Elsaid said at the time that he might need to change parts of the stack, such as the vector database or hosting, to save on costs.

## How did Chatbase find its first customers?

By being first. Elsaid believes Chatbase was probably the first "ChatGPT for your data" SaaS tool, which made it easy to go viral: people could chat with their own documents at the peak of ChatGPT hype. A few of his tweets went viral, and AI influencers started posting about it.

He knew that would not last, because influencers would not keep sharing similar tools. So he added steadier channels: launching on Product Hunt, posting stories to relevant subreddits, being active on Indie Hackers and submitting Chatbase to AI directories. The widget helped as well, since it shows the product to every visitor on a customer's site. Pricing was a subscription with four plans. Most subscribers started on the lowest plan to test, then upgraded or churned, and most revenue came from the $399 monthly plan ([founder-reported, Indie Hackers](https://www.indiehackers.com/post/how-a-college-student-reached-64-000-mo-in-6-months-by-being-an-ai-first-mover-ba7981f6e1)).

## How does Chatbase compare with SiteGPT, PDF.ai and My AskAI?

All four launched in 2023 on the same idea: an AI that answers from your own content. Their edges were different. Chatbase won on timing, arriving first and going viral. [PDF.ai](/cases/pdf-ai/) entered the crowded "chat with your PDF" niche months later and won on a memorable domain. [SiteGPT](/cases/sitegpt/) sold the same website-chatbot idea as a smaller solo business.

[My AskAI](/cases/my-askai/) shows where the category went next. Instead of a widget on the website, it plugs into help desks like Intercom and Zendesk and competes on price with their built-in AI. The lesson across the four is that the first-mover window in AI is short: Chatbase's own founder expected competition within months, and the later entrants each needed a sharper angle, whether a domain, a niche or an integration.

[Coachvox AI](/cases/coachvox-ai/) uses the same "train AI on your content" idea for creators instead of support teams, cloning a coach rather than a help centre.

## What are the lessons from Chatbase?

- **Speed beats research in a brand-new market.** Elsaid skipped validation because the demand was obvious, and shipping first is what made Chatbase go viral.
- **Plan for the hype to fade.** He added Product Hunt, Reddit, Indie Hackers and AI directories as soon as influencer posts slowed.
- **Let the product advertise itself.** An embeddable widget shows Chatbase to every visitor on a customer's site.
- **Price for the real customer.** Most users tested on the cheapest plan, but most revenue came from the $399 plan.
