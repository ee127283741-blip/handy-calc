Social media schedulers are one of the most crowded categories in SaaS, so a new one needs a sharp angle. PostPlanify picked agencies that serve local businesses, and priced by workspace instead of by seat. Founded by Hasan Cagli in May 2025, it had 48 active subscriptions and $35,567 in lifetime revenue by November 2026 ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/postplanify)). TrustMRR also shows an Ahrefs domain rating of 58 for postplanify.com, a sign that its site has built real backlink strength.

## What does PostPlanify do for social media agencies?

PostPlanify is a social media management platform for planning, scheduling and managing content across platforms. The listing says it lets teams schedule posts, track analytics, manage approvals and collaborate with unlimited users across 10 platforms from one dashboard ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/postplanify)).

> "Social media management for agencies with local business clients." — PostPlanify on TrustMRR ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/postplanify))

It also includes built-in analytics and a social inbox for comments. The company says it is trusted by agencies in 30+ countries and offers real human support on every plan.

## Why does PostPlanify charge per workspace instead of per seat?

PostPlanify charges per workspace, with Growth at $31 a month, Premium at $101 and Scale at $203, and no credit card required to start ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/postplanify)).

| Plan | Price per workspace | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Growth | $31/mo | [founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/postplanify) |
| Premium | $101/mo | [founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/postplanify) |
| Scale | $203/mo | [founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/postplanify) |

For an agency, a workspace usually maps to a client, so the bill grows with the agency's client list rather than its staff. Unlimited users removes the usual objection to adding team members and clients for approvals. The stack is Next.js, Node.js, PostgreSQL, Redis and Stripe.

PostPlanify, MyFrank and Orshot are all marketing SaaS with verified revenue, and all three sell to businesses rather than individual creators.

Our observation: MyFrank and PostPlanify both target local businesses, but MyFrank sells directly to them while PostPlanify sells to the agencies that serve them. Selling through agencies means fewer, larger accounts, yet PostPlanify still trails both peers on MRR. In a crowded scheduler market, a niche angle helps but does not remove the competition.

## What takeaways does PostPlanify offer marketing SaaS founders?

- **Pick a buyer inside a crowded category.** Agencies with local clients give PostPlanify a clear target.
- **Price on the unit that grows.** Per-workspace pricing scales with an agency's client list ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/postplanify)).
- **Remove seat friction.** Unlimited users makes approvals and client collaboration easy.
- **SEO strength takes time to show in MRR.** A domain rating of 58 sits alongside 48 verified subscriptions.
