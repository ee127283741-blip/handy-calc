## Origin of the idea
After open image models such as Stable Diffusion arrived in 2022, Levels noticed that people would pay to see themselves in photos they could never take: on a beach, in a suit, in a studio. He first tested the demand with an avatar generator and then widened it into a general "AI photographer".

## How they started
He shipped a rough version fast, posted it on X to his large following, and kept changing it in public based on what users bought. Photo AI grew out of that earlier avatar product rather than starting from zero.

## Tools & tech stack
Levels is known for a deliberately plain stack: PHP, jQuery and SQLite on a single server, with image models trained and run through hosted GPU APIs. The point is speed of shipping, not architecture.

## Marketing
Almost all distribution came from building in public on X: screenshots of new features, sample photos and revenue updates. Example images on the landing page do the selling.

## Sales & pricing
Subscription plans that include a number of trained models and photo credits per month, with discounted annual plans. Because many customers pay yearly, Levels prefers to quote monthly revenue rather than MRR.

## Monthly revenue
Levels reported passing **$100K a month** in revenue in August 2024 (founder-reported). Higher figures circulate in third-party write-ups but are not used here.

## Total revenue
Not disclosed.

## Founding date & timeline
- **Late 2022:** Avatar generator experiment after Stable Diffusion's release.
- **2023:** Rebuilt and positioned as Photo AI.
- **Aug 2024:** Founder reports $100K+/month.

## Team size
One person. Levels runs the product himself and automates support and operations where possible.

## Key takeaways
- An audience lets a solo founder test ideas in days.
- Selling an outcome ("photos of you") beats selling a model.
- A boring stack is fine if it lets you ship daily.
