Pokémon cards have become an asset class for many collectors, with sealed boxes and graded cards bought as much for value as for play. PokéItem treats a collection like a stock portfolio. Launched in France in May 2026, the app had about 55,000 users and $18,798 in monthly recurring revenue by November 2026 ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/pokeitem)), and its last 30 days were its strongest yet.

## What does PokéItem track for card collectors?

PokéItem tracks the value of every card and sealed product in a Pokémon TCG or One Piece Card Game collection, in real time. Users scan cards with their phone, and the app turns the collection into a portfolio view ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/pokeitem)).

> "PokéItem turns your TCG collections into a portfolio." — PokéItem description on TrustMRR ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/pokeitem))

Including sealed items matters. Many collectors hold unopened boxes as investments, and a tool that only values single cards misses part of their holdings.

## How is PokéItem priced?

PokéItem uses a four-tier ladder: a free plan, Premium at €5.99 a month, Premium+ at €49.99 a year and a one-time Lifetime plan at €159.99 ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/pokeitem)). The average subscription is worth about $6.27 a month (calc: $18,798 / 2,999), close to the monthly price.

About 5.5% of reported users hold a paid subscription (calc: 2,999 / ~55,000). The team is 2–5 people and bootstrapped; marketing channels are not disclosed.

## What can app founders learn from PokéItem?

- **Passionate hobbies pay.** Card collectors already spend money on their hobby, so a €5.99 subscription is a small add-on.
- **Frame the product as a portfolio.** Value tracking turns a hobby app into something users check like an investment account.
- **Offer annual and lifetime tiers.** They lifted last-30-day revenue well above MRR.
- **Niche plus speed works.** Six months from launch to $89,454 in lifetime revenue with a team of 2–5.
