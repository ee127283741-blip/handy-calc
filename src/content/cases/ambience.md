Ambience Healthcare ARR grew about 60% in five months, by one analyst's estimate. Sacra puts it at $19M at the end of 2024 and about $30M in May 2025 ([third-party estimate, Sacra](https://sacra.com/c/ambience/)). Ambience sells AI that listens to doctor-patient visits, writes the clinical note and suggests the billing codes. In July 2025 it raised $243M at a $1.25B valuation ([media-reported, Becker's](https://www.beckershospitalreview.com/healthcare-information-technology/ai/ambience-healthcare-reaches-1-25b-valuation/)).

## What does Ambience Healthcare do for doctors?

Ambience Healthcare records a visit, drafts the clinical note and proposes billing codes, which health systems then review. Mike Ng and Nikhil Buduma founded it ([company release](https://natlawreview.com/press-releases/ambience-healthcare-announces-243-million-series-c-scale-its-ai-platform)). Ng told MedCity News the goal is to remove "tens of thousands of daily clicks and keystrokes" from clinicians' work, noting that most spend only about a quarter of their time with patients ([MedCity News](https://medcitynews.com/2025/07/healthcare-documentation-startup-unicorn/)). It works with health systems such as Cleveland Clinic. Prices and team size are not disclosed.

> "Documentation has long been a source of friction." — Mike Ng ([founder-reported, company release](https://natlawreview.com/press-releases/ambience-healthcare-announces-243-million-series-c-scale-its-ai-platform))

## How much ARR does Ambience Healthcare have?

Ambience Healthcare does not publish ARR. Sacra estimates about $30M in May 2025, about $2.5M a month (calc), up from $19M at the end of 2024 ([third-party estimate, Sacra](https://sacra.com/c/ambience/)). Both are analyst estimates. At the $1.25B Series C price, $30M ARR implies roughly 42 times revenue (calc). Total revenue is not disclosed.

## Why does coding matter so much to Ambience Healthcare's pitch?

Coding ties the product to hospital revenue, not only to doctor time. A note that is written faster saves minutes; a visit that is coded correctly decides how much the health system is paid. Ng told Becker's that models must understand the coding and billing rules that apply to each visit for each patient ([Becker's](https://www.beckershospitalreview.com/healthcare-information-technology/ai/ambience-healthcare-reaches-1-25b-valuation/)). Oak HC/FT and Andreessen Horowitz co-led the $243M round ([media-reported, Becker's](https://www.beckershospitalreview.com/healthcare-information-technology/ai/ambience-healthcare-reaches-1-25b-valuation/)).

## How does Ambience Healthcare compare with Abridge and Freed?

Ambience and [Abridge](/cases/abridge/) both sell to large health systems; [Freed](/cases/freed/) sells to individual clinicians. Abridge was valued at $5.3B in June 2025, four times Ambience's price a month later, and MedCity News ranked Ambience second in valuation among AI documentation startups ([MedCity News](https://medcitynews.com/2025/07/healthcare-documentation-startup-unicorn/)). Freed charges clinicians a monthly subscription and grows without hospital sales cycles. [OpenEvidence](/cases/openevidence/) reaches the same doctors with a free, ad-funded search tool.

Our reading: Ambience competes on coding accuracy because note-taking alone is becoming a commodity. Many scribes can write a decent note. Fewer can show a CFO that codes are more complete and denials less frequent, which is the argument that justifies a health-system contract.

## What are the lessons from Ambience Healthcare for health AI?

- **Tie the product to revenue.** Coding affects what hospitals are paid, which makes the purchase easier to justify.
- **Health systems buy slowly but big.** Ambience sells to large providers rather than to individual doctors.
- **Estimates are not disclosures.** Ambience's ARR comes only from Sacra, so treat it as a range, not a fact.
- **Second place can still raise.** Ambience reached $1.25B behind Abridge's higher valuation.
