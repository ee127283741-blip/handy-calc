Otter AI revenue passed $100M ARR in March 2025, and the company did it with a small team. Otter.ai said it had fewer than 200 employees at the time, which means more than $500K of revenue per person ([founder-reported, Otter.ai release](https://salestechstar.com/price-optimization-revenue-management/otter-ai-breaks-100m-arr-barrier-and-transforms-business-meetings-launching-industry-first-ai-meeting-agent-suite/)). The last funding total it disclosed was just over $63M, back in 2021 ([media-reported, VentureBeat](https://venturebeat.com/ai/otter-ai-raises-50-million-for-its-ai-transcription-service)). Otter got there before AI meeting notes were crowded, and then held on as dozens of rivals arrived.

## How did Otter.ai start as AISense?

Otter.ai began as AISense, a speech recognition company that Sam Liang and Yun Fu founded in 2016 ([VentureBeat](https://venturebeat.com/ai/otter-ai-raises-50-million-for-its-ai-transcription-service)). Liang is CEO and Fu led engineering. The product transcribes meetings live, then makes the transcript searchable and shareable.

Remote work was the turning point. VentureBeat reported in February 2021 that Otter's revenue rose 800% in 2020 ([founder-reported via VentureBeat](https://venturebeat.com/ai/otter-ai-raises-50-million-for-its-ai-transcription-service)). By then it had transcribed more than 100 million meetings, or 3 billion minutes. That month Spectrum Equity led a $50M Series B, including $10M in convertible notes, which took Otter's total funding to over $63M.

## How much ARR does Otter.ai make?

Otter.ai passed $100M in annual recurring revenue in March 2025, which is about $8.3M a month on our arithmetic (calc). The company announced the milestone alongside a set of AI meeting agents, and said it had more than 25 million users worldwide at the time ([founder-reported, Otter.ai release](https://salestechstar.com/price-optimization-revenue-management/otter-ai-breaks-100m-arr-barrier-and-transforms-business-meetings-launching-industry-first-ai-meeting-agent-suite/)). In its December 2025 year-end release, Otter repeated the $100M figure and put users above 35 million ([founder-reported, Otter.ai release](https://martechseries.com/predictive-ai/ai-platforms-machine-learning/otter-ai-caps-transformational-2025-with-100m-arr-milestone-industry-first-ai-meeting-agents-and-global-enterprise-expansion/)). It gave no newer revenue number. Total revenue and valuation are not disclosed.

## How does Otter.ai make money from a free product?

Otter.ai gives away a capable free plan and charges individuals and teams for more minutes and features. In 2021 the free plan included 600 transcription minutes a month, Otter Pro started at $8.33 a month and the business tier cost $20 a month ([media-reported, VentureBeat](https://venturebeat.com/ai/otter-ai-raises-50-million-for-its-ai-transcription-service)). By 2025 the company was pushing larger enterprise deals. Its December release claimed an average 10:1 return for enterprise customers ([Otter.ai release](https://martechseries.com/predictive-ai/ai-platforms-machine-learning/otter-ai-caps-transformational-2025-with-100m-arr-milestone-industry-first-ai-meeting-agents-and-global-enterprise-expansion/)). In our reading, that shift explains how revenue reached $100M with tens of millions of mostly free users: a small paid share, plus bigger company contracts, carries the rest.

## How does Otter.ai compare with Fireflies.ai, Bluedot and Talknotes?

Otter and [Fireflies.ai](/cases/fireflies-ai/) are the two veterans of AI meeting notes, and both grew on very little venture money. Fireflies says it has not raised new capital since 2021, has been profitable since 2023 and reached a $1B valuation through an employee tender in June 2025 ([founder-reported, Fireflies.ai release](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/fireflies-reaches-1-billion-valuation-150000434.html)). [Bluedot](/cases/bluedot/), a newer Chrome extension that records Google Meet without a bot, reported $1.5K MRR two months after launch ([founder-reported, Starter Story](https://www.starterstory.com/stories/bluedot-0891acdc-ecac-4222-89da-e5d0ac06d882)). [Talknotes](/cases/talknotes/), which turns voice notes into text, was at $4,000 MRR in April 2024 ([founder-reported, Starter Story](https://www.starterstory.com/stories/talknotes-the-ai-voice-notes-app)).

In our view, Otter's edge was timing. It was transcribing meetings years before the pandemic, so when remote work arrived in 2020 it already had the product and the brand. Newer tools compete on features, but they start from a market where Otter and Fireflies are the default names.

## What lessons does Otter.ai hold for founders?

- **Being early can matter more than raising big.** Otter built its product before remote work took off, then grew on about $63M of disclosed funding.
- **Revenue per employee is a selling point.** More than $500K per person let Otter show efficiency, not just growth.
- **A free plan builds the funnel.** Tens of millions of users give Otter a pool of people to turn into paying teams.
- **Repeating a milestone signals a pause in disclosure.** Otter's December 2025 release restated the March $100M figure rather than a new one.
