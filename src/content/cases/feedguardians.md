A brand can pay to put an ad in front of thousands of buyers, only to have the comments under it filled with scam links and competitor plugs. FeedGuardians cleans that up automatically. The Slovenian company, founded in August 2025, had $8,265 in MRR from 40 subscriptions and $76,106 in lifetime revenue by November 2026 ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/feedguardians)).

## Why do ad comments matter to FeedGuardians customers?

FeedGuardians argues that spam and scam comments quietly hurt ad performance, so it removes spam, scam and competitor comments under Instagram, Facebook and TikTok ads and answers real buyers in the brand's voice ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/feedguardians)). Its main audience is DTC brands.

> "Spam and scam comments are quietly killing ad performance." — FeedGuardians company statement ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/feedguardians))

It also covers X, BlueSky and YouTube, centralises comments and DMs in one inbox, and lists Meta and TikTok Business Partner status.

## How does FeedGuardians make $8,265 a month?

FeedGuardians makes its money from tiered subscriptions: $8,265 MRR from 40 active subscriptions in November 2026, with $3,814 collected in the last 30 days and $76,106 all time ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/feedguardians)).

## What does FeedGuardians add beyond moderation?

FeedGuardians positions itself partly as a ManyChat alternative, adding smart DM automation to comment moderation and AI replies ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/feedguardians)). That widens the product from a defensive filter to an engagement tool. Plan prices such as $83 and $833 look like annual billing expressed per month, though the listing does not say so.

FeedGuardians, [Bazzly](/cases/bazzly/) and [Backlinker AI](/cases/backlinker-ai/) all sell AI marketing tools with Stripe-verified revenue, but FeedGuardians protects paid ads rather than chasing organic reach.

## What are the key lessons from FeedGuardians?

- **Attach to an existing budget.** Brands already paying for ads will pay to protect them.
- **Platform partnerships signal safety.** Meta and TikTok Business Partner status reassures cautious buyers.
- **Tiered pricing captures bigger accounts.** Plans run from $83 to $2,499 a month.
- **Expand from defence to engagement.** DM automation turns a filter into a ManyChat alternative.
