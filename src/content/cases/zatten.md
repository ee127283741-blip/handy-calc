Most AI agencies can build a chatbot in an afternoon. The hard part is running it for dozens of clients on WhatsApp, with leads, handoffs and follow-ups in one place. Zatten sells that missing layer. The Brazilian company, founded in February 2025, had collected $54,925 in lifetime revenue by November 2026 ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/zatten)), selling to agencies rather than to the end businesses.

## What problem does Zatten solve for AI agencies?

Zatten gives AI agencies the plumbing to manage leads, automate processes and scale operations on WhatsApp without a big technical team, according to its TrustMRR listing ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/zatten)). The company calls itself the infrastructure that AI agencies use to deliver agents on WhatsApp.

> "The all-in-one Agents Automation-CRM Plataform for WHatsApp" — Zatten company description ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/zatten))

The listing also says Zatten is a Meta Business Partner, which matters for a product that lives on top of the WhatsApp Business platform.

## How much money does Zatten bring in each month?

Zatten had $2,155 in MRR from 51 active subscriptions in November 2026, with $3,768 collected in the last 30 days and $54,925 all time ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/zatten)).

Last-30-day revenue is higher than MRR, which suggests part of the income is non-recurring, such as one-off or annual payments. The average subscription is worth about $42 a month (calc: $2,155 / 51, [from TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/zatten)). Team size is not disclosed.

## What is Zatten built with?

Zatten runs on a modern, low-ops stack: Next.js on the frontend, Supabase for the backend, Vercel hosting, OpenAI models and Stripe for billing, as listed on TrustMRR ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/zatten)). That is a common recipe for a small team shipping a B2B product quickly, and it keeps infrastructure costs predictable while subscriptions are still in the dozens.

## How does Zatten stack up against converti and Deeplead?

Zatten, [converti](/cases/converti/) and [Deeplead](/cases/deeplead/) all sell revenue tools to businesses. Zatten and converti share a reseller angle, while Deeplead sells lead generation directly.

Our observation: converti lets anyone resell AI sales agents, while Zatten narrows the job to WhatsApp delivery for AI agencies. An agency customer may serve many end clients from one account, so Zatten's 51 subscriptions could understate its reach. The trade-off is dependence on a young agency channel.

## What lessons does Zatten offer B2B2B founders?

- **Sell picks and shovels to the new agencies.** AI agencies need delivery infrastructure, and Zatten targets that need directly.
- **Platform partnerships are a trust signal.** Meta Business Partner status supports a product built on WhatsApp.
- **Watch the recurring share.** Last-30-day revenue of $3,768 against $2,155 MRR shows income beyond subscriptions.
- **A free start lowers agency friction.** Agencies can test before committing client work to the platform.
