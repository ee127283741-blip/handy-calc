Dr. Gabriella Meckler kept bringing her work home. She would open her laptop after a shift to finish patient charts, sometimes on date nights, sometimes with friends still over, often past midnight. Her husband, Erez Druk, was a software engineer, and he decided those notes were the problem worth solving. Freed, the AI scribe he built with a former colleague, passed about $1.5M in annual revenue within four months of launch ([founder-reported, Freed](https://www.getfreed.ai/blog/the-freed-story)). Sacra estimates it reached $33M ARR by August 2026 ([third-party estimate, Sacra](https://sacra.com/c/freed/)).

## Why did Erez Druk start Freed?

Erez Druk started Freed because his wife's paperwork kept following her home. He had studied mathematics and computer science at the Technion in Israel and then spent years as a software engineer at Facebook ([founder-reported, Freed](https://www.getfreed.ai/blog/the-freed-story)). Freed was his second company. On the N47 podcast he said his first startup taught him to pick users before problems or markets. He called note-writing the top pain point in clinicians' lives, one that hurts their life outside work as well as the job itself ([founder-reported, N47 podcast](https://www.n47.com/insights/how-to-scale-a-mission-driven-startup)). In January 2023 he co-founded Freed with Andrey Bannikov.

## How does the Freed AI scribe work?

The Freed AI scribe listens to a patient visit and turns the conversation into a draft clinical note, which the clinician reviews instead of writing from scratch. Sacra describes the core as speech-to-text plus large language models and says it saves doctors about three hours a day of data entry into electronic health records ([third-party estimate, Sacra](https://sacra.com/research/freed-at-13m-arr/)). On the Premier plan, Freed can push the finished note into the clinician's EHR ([founder-reported, Freed](https://www.getfreed.ai/blog/the-freed-story)).

The product now goes well beyond the note. Freed says it also covers front desk reception, coding, clinical evidence and visit prep ([founder-reported, Freed](https://www.getfreed.ai/blog/the-freed-story)). The Coding Assistant suggests ICD-10 and CPT codes, and Front Desk is an AI receptionist that answers patient calls. Freed says it is HIPAA-compliant and does not store patient recordings.

## Who built Freed, and how does the team stay close to clinicians?

Freed was built by two engineers who met at Facebook. Bannikov studied computational mathematics at Moscow State University and spent nearly a decade at Facebook as a senior engineer.

> "the best engineer I've ever worked with." — Erez Druk ([founder-reported, Freed blog](https://www.getfreed.ai/blog/the-freed-story))

Neither founder is a clinician, so the team learns by watching clinicians work. Micha Breakstone writes in Inc. that Druk and members of his team shadow physicians, and that the company values speed and empathy ([media-reported, Inc.](https://www.inc.com/micha-breakstone/values-and-startups-how-speed-and-empathy-shape-the-future-of-healthcare-tech/91068582)). Breakstone says he is a close friend of Druk and an investor in Freed, so his column is an insider's view. Freed also joined the second batch of AI Grant in 2023 ([third-party estimate, Sacra](https://sacra.com/c/freed/)).

## How did Freed win customers that incumbents ignored?

Freed sells to clinicians that most health-tech vendors overlook: independent and small-to-mid-sized private practices ([founder-reported, Freed](https://www.getfreed.ai/blog/the-freed-story)). Sacra calls this a bottom-up, product-led motion aimed at practices with fewer than 10 doctors, where the person using the product also buys it. It says Freed crossed $10M ARR in under 12 months with only four salespeople. Sacra put Freed's early price at $99 a month, against $300-600 a month for Ambience, Suki and Nuance ([third-party estimate, Sacra](https://sacra.com/research/freed-at-13m-arr/)).

Today Freed lists Starter at $39 a month billed annually for up to 40 notes, Core at $79, Premier at $119 with EHR push, and custom pricing for Groups. Every plan has a 7-day free trial with no credit card ([founder-reported, Freed](https://www.getfreed.ai/blog/the-freed-story)).

## How much ARR has Freed reached since 2023?

Freed's latest figure is an outside estimate: Sacra puts it at $33M ARR in August 2026, up from $27M at the end of 2025, a year in which it says Freed grew 66% ([third-party estimate, Sacra](https://sacra.com/c/freed/)). Dividing $33M by 12 gives roughly $2.75M a month. That is our calc, not a reported monthly figure ([calc, from Sacra](https://sacra.com/c/freed/)). Total lifetime revenue, profit, margins and team size are not disclosed.

## How is Freed different from Bluedot, Talknotes and AudioPen?

All four turn speech into notes, but Freed is the only one that picked a single profession, and its numbers are in a different league. [Bluedot](/cases/bluedot/) reported $1.5K MRR two months after launch with 500 daily active users ([founder-reported, Starter Story](https://www.starterstory.com/stories/bluedot-0891acdc-ecac-4222-89da-e5d0ac06d882)). [Talknotes](/cases/talknotes/) made about $5,000 a month in April 2024 with 900+ paying customers ([founder-reported, Starter Story](https://www.starterstory.com/stories/talknotes-the-ai-voice-notes-app)). [AudioPen](/cases/audiopen/) was at between $15K and $20K a month by February 2026 ([founder-reported, Indie Hackers](https://www.indiehackers.com/post/tech/building-an-ai-tool-in-a-half-day-hackathon-and-hitting-20k-mo-BCloDTDRfjuip0pDghap)).

Our observation: the three general note-takers sell convenience to people whose notes are optional. Freed sells relief from a task clinicians cannot skip, which Druk saw at his own dinner table. That pain supports a higher price and outside funding. The three general tools stayed small and lean, while Freed raised venture money and grew far larger.

## What lessons does Freed offer founders?

- **Pick users you can reach every day.** Druk's main lesson from his first startup was to pick users carefully, "more than the problem, more than the market" ([founder-reported, N47 podcast](https://www.n47.com/insights/how-to-scale-a-mission-driven-startup)); his user was his wife.
- **Sell to the buyers incumbents skip.** Small practices where the doctor buys the software let a self-serve product grow with only four salespeople ([third-party estimate, Sacra](https://sacra.com/research/freed-at-13m-arr/)).
- **Undercut on price when the pain is mandatory.** An early $99 price against $300-600 rivals made trying Freed an easy personal decision ([third-party estimate, Sacra](https://sacra.com/research/freed-at-13m-arr/)).
- **Plan for the core feature to become free.** Sacra notes that OpenEvidence and Doximity offer free scribes and that Epic has built-in AI charting, so Freed is expanding into coding, evidence and the front desk ([third-party estimate, Sacra](https://sacra.com/c/freed/)).
- **Watch the growth rate, not just the total.** Sacra's estimates show growth slowing from 992% in the year to August 2024 to 66% in 2025. Whether Freed keeps finding product-market fit in nearby problems is, in Druk's words on N47, what will decide its future ([founder-reported, N47 podcast](https://www.n47.com/insights/how-to-scale-a-mission-driven-startup)).
