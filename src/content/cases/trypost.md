Social media schedulers usually charge per connected account, so an agency with twenty clients pays twenty times. TryPost.it charges $19 a month for unlimited accounts and team members. Paulo Castellano launched it in Brazil in April 2026, and by early November it had 144 active subscriptions and $2,616 in monthly recurring revenue ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/trypost)).

## What does TryPost.it automate for social media teams?

TryPost.it connects a user's social accounts, helps write posts with AI and schedules them across every network from one calendar ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/trypost)). It targets creators, startups and agencies that post across several networks.

> "Stop posting to five apps by hand." — TryPost.it company description ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/trypost))

The product is pitched as running social media on autopilot with AI agents. The stack is Vue.js, Tailwind CSS and Astro on the front end, Laravel with PostgreSQL, MySQL and Redis behind it, hosted on Hetzner with Cloudflare, and OpenAI for writing.

## Why does TryPost.it sell one flat $19 plan?

A single plan with unlimited accounts and seats removes the main pricing objection agencies have with per-account schedulers ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/trypost)). It also keeps the sales page simple: one price, no tier comparison. The trade-off is that heavy users pay the same as light ones, so revenue per account cannot grow with usage.

Its listed marketing channels are SEO and affiliates.

Our observation: this is a crowded category with proven winners. Post Bridge and Postiz show the demand is real; TryPost.it competes on price and unlimited seats, an angle aimed squarely at agencies. Postiz shows that a challenger can win with a sharp positioning hook, open source in its case.

## What can SaaS founders learn from TryPost.it?

- **Flat pricing is a positioning tool.** Unlimited accounts at $19 targets the buyers per-account pricing annoys most.
- **Crowded markets still have room.** 144 subscriptions in about seven months shows demand for a cheaper option.
- **Watch the data freshness.** An expired Stripe key froze the TrustMRR numbers at November 3, 2026.
- **Early ratios reveal momentum.** All-time revenue barely above MRR means most of the growth is recent ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/trypost)).
