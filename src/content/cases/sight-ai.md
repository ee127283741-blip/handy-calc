Sight AI crossed $100,000 in lifetime revenue in under 18 months, selling a product that did not exist as a category a few years earlier: tracking whether ChatGPT-style assistants mention your brand. In November 2026 it had $10,939 in monthly recurring revenue and $101,802 all time ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/sight-ai)). It combines measurement with the content needed to fix what the measurement shows.

## What does Sight AI track in AI search?

Sight AI watches how a brand shows up in AI answers, then helps produce content to improve it. The company-written listing on TrustMRR lists three jobs: track AI visibility, uncover content opportunities, and publish SEO/GEO optimized articles ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/sight-ai)). GEO, generative engine optimization, means shaping content so AI systems cite it.

> "Track your AI visibility, uncover content opportunities, and publish SEO/GEO optimized articles" — Sight AI listing ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/sight-ai))

The website is trysight.ai, and TrustMRR files it under AI, Marketing, SaaS and Content Creation.

## What takeaways does Sight AI offer AI search founders?

- **New search habits create new categories.** Sight AI reached $101,802 in lifetime revenue within about 17 months.
- **Close the loop.** Pairing tracking with content gives customers a reason to keep paying after the first report.
- **Price for businesses, not hobbyists.** Roughly $267 per subscription points to marketing teams as buyers.
- **A small audience is not a blocker.** The founder had about 2,000 X followers at this revenue level.
