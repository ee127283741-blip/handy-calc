The Cluely revenue story is a story about one number. In July 2025, co-founder and CEO Roy Lee told TechCrunch that Cluely's ARR had doubled in a single week to about $7M ([unverified, TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/07/03/cluelys-arr-doubled-in-a-week-to-7m-founder-roy-lee-says-but-rivals-are-coming)). Eight months later he posted on X that the figure was a lie. His own snapshot from June 2025 showed $2.7M in consumer ARR and $2.5M in enterprise ARR ([founder-reported, TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/03/05/cluely-ceo-roy-lee-admits-to-publicly-lying-about-revenue-numbers-last-year/)). That is real money for a company months old. It was not $7M.

## How did an interview-cheating tool become Cluely?

Cluely grew out of a suspension. Roy Lee and Neel Shanmugam were suspended from Columbia University for building Interview Coder, an "undetectable AI-powered tool" to help engineers cheat on technical interviews ([media-reported, TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/06/20/cluely-a-startup-that-helps-cheat-on-everything-raises-15m-from-a16z)). They turned the idea into a broader product with the slogan "cheat on everything." Cluely listens to an online conversation and shows real-time notes, context and suggested questions on the user's screen, where other people on the call cannot see it ([media-reported, TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/07/03/cluelys-arr-doubled-in-a-week-to-7m-founder-roy-lee-says-but-rivals-are-coming)). The company has since rebranded as an AI meeting note-taker ([media-reported, TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/03/05/cluely-ceo-roy-lee-admits-to-publicly-lying-about-revenue-numbers-last-year/)).

## How much revenue does Cluely actually make?

Cluely's best-documented figure is about $5.2M in ARR as of June 2025. That is our sum of the two numbers in Lee's own retraction post: $2.7M consumer and $2.5M enterprise ARR ([founder-reported, TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/03/05/cluely-ceo-roy-lee-admits-to-publicly-lying-about-revenue-numbers-last-year/)). Divided by 12, it is roughly $433K a month ([calc, from TechCrunch's figures](https://techcrunch.com/2026/03/05/cluely-ceo-roy-lee-admits-to-publicly-lying-about-revenue-numbers-last-year/)). The same post listed run rates of $3.8M for consumer and $2.5M for enterprise. Revenue after June 2025 and total revenue are not disclosed. Team size is not disclosed either.

## Why did Roy Lee take back Cluely's $7M ARR?

Lee took it back on March 5, 2026, calling it "the only blatantly dishonest thing i've said publicly online" and his formal retraction ([founder-reported, TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/03/05/cluely-ceo-roy-lee-admits-to-publicly-lying-about-revenue-numbers-last-year/)). He said a stranger cold-called him and he made up a number. TechCrunch disputed that account. It said Cluely's own PR representative emailed the reporter on June 27, 2025, offered Lee for an interview and confirmed he expected the call. By then Lee had already changed his view on disclosure. At TechCrunch Disrupt in October 2025 he said:

> "What I've learned is you should never share revenue numbers." — Roy Lee ([founder-reported, TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/03/05/cluely-ceo-roy-lee-admits-to-publicly-lying-about-revenue-numbers-last-year/))

The original article also contained a second figure. Lee said a public company had doubled its annual contract to $2.5M and declined to name it ([founder-reported, TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/07/03/cluelys-arr-doubled-in-a-week-to-7m-founder-roy-lee-says-but-rivals-are-coming)). That number matches the $2.5M enterprise ARR in his later snapshot. In our reading, it suggests most enterprise revenue sat with one customer at the time.

## How did Cluely raise $20.3M on controversy?

Cluely raised on attention as much as on revenue, about $20.3M across two rounds (calc). It raised a $5.3M seed round from Abstract Ventures and Susa Ventures ([media-reported, TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/03/05/cluely-ceo-roy-lee-admits-to-publicly-lying-about-revenue-numbers-last-year/)). About two months later it raised a $15M Series A led by Andreessen Horowitz, announced with a video on X. Two investors who were not in the deal told TechCrunch they believed the post-money valuation was around $120M ([media-reported, TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/06/20/cluely-a-startup-that-helps-cheat-on-everything-raises-15m-from-a16z)). The pricing still carries the origin story. In November 2026 the pricing page listed a free Starter plan, Pro at $19.99 a month and "Pro + Undetectability" at $149.99 a month ([founder-reported, Cluely pricing page](https://cluely.com/pricing)).

## How does Cluely compare with Bluedot, Talknotes and Freed?

Cluely sells the same core job as other AI note-takers, but it pitched help during the call rather than notes after it. Lee made that case himself: meeting notes are "a proven very sticky" use case, and the problem is that they are "all post-call" ([founder-reported, TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/07/03/cluelys-arr-doubled-in-a-week-to-7m-founder-roy-lee-says-but-rivals-are-coming)). [Bluedot](/cases/bluedot/) also stays out of sight, but in a different way: it records Google Meet calls without a bot joining. It reported $1.5K MRR two months after launch ([founder-reported, Starter Story](https://www.starterstory.com/stories/bluedot-0891acdc-ecac-4222-89da-e5d0ac06d882)). [Talknotes](/cases/talknotes/) turns voice memos into notes and made about $5,000 a month by April 2024 as a solo business ([founder-reported, Starter Story](https://www.starterstory.com/stories/talknotes-the-ai-voice-notes-app)). [Freed](/cases/freed/) narrowed the same idea to one profession, clinicians.

The contrast is in how each one grew. Bluedot and Talknotes grew small and reported modest numbers. Cluely chose headlines and venture money, and its most quoted number turned out to be false. Cluely also faced copycats fast. On the day of the $7M story, a startup called Pickle said it had built Glass, a free open-source app with similar real-time notes ([media-reported, TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/07/03/cluelys-arr-doubled-in-a-week-to-7m-founder-roy-lee-says-but-rivals-are-coming)).

## What can founders learn from Cluely's ARR retraction?

- **A false number outlives the news cycle.** The $7M claim was the headline of a TechCrunch story, and the correction eight months later became a second story.
- **Report what your billing system shows.** Lee's own June 2025 snapshot, about $5.2M ARR, was already a strong figure for a startup that launched in 2025.
- **Separate ARR from run rate.** Lee's post listed both, and the consumer run rate was higher than consumer ARR; mixing the two up is how numbers drift upward.
- **One big contract is not a trend.** The $2.5M enterprise figure matches a single customer's annual contract, so enterprise revenue rested on one buyer.
- **Controversy buys attention, not trust.** It helped Cluely raise $20.3M in two rounds, but the company's founder now advises against sharing revenue at all.
