Booking software is a crowded category, and Calendesk is not a viral AI launch. It is a Warsaw company that sells scheduling, payments and branded client portals to therapists and small service businesses. In November 2026 it showed $21,468 in MRR and $1,164,015 in all-time revenue ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/calendesk)). That makes it a useful case of a slow, bootstrapped SaaS in a market most founders would call saturated.

## Who does Calendesk build its booking software for?

Calendesk sells to practitioners whose income depends on appointments. TrustMRR describes the audience as therapists, psychologists and service businesses, and lists the company as bootstrapped and based in Poland ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/calendesk)). The listing is tagged across productivity, health and fitness, education and legal, which suggests the same booking core is sold to several professions rather than one narrow niche.

The founder named on the listing is Maciej Cupial. The company itself trades through a legal entity called MPR Sp. z o.o., according to its pricing page. The team size is not disclosed on either source.

## What does the Calendesk platform include beyond a calendar?

The product bundles the parts a solo practice usually stitches together. TrustMRR lists bookings, payments, client relationship tools, subscription packages, white-label branding, client portals and an API-first setup, with integrations such as Stripe, Zoom and Google Meet. That bundle matters for a therapist who wants clients to book, pay and join a video session from one branded page.

> "B2B SaaS helping therapists, psychologists, and service businesses manage bookings, payments, and client relationships." — Calendesk, company description ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/calendesk))

Calendesk also offers a 14-day free trial, and its pricing page says no credit card is needed to register. Support runs over email and live chat in English and Polish.

## How is Calendesk priced per seat and per booking?

Calendesk charges by plan tier, with limits on staff, bookings and websites. Standard covers 5 employees and 750 bookings a month, and Pro covers 15 employees and 2,500 bookings ([founder-reported, Calendesk pricing](https://www.calendesk.com/pricing/)).

| Plan | Annual billing | Monthly billing | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Standard | $79 a month | $97 a month | [founder-reported, Calendesk pricing](https://www.calendesk.com/pricing/) |
| Pro | $117 a month | $147 a month | [founder-reported, Calendesk pricing](https://www.calendesk.com/pricing/) |
| Enterprise | Individual contract | Annual only | [founder-reported, Calendesk pricing](https://www.calendesk.com/pricing/) |

Add-ons create a second revenue line. The page lists SMS packages at $9.99 a month and email and SMS top-ups priced per message ([founder-reported, Calendesk pricing](https://www.calendesk.com/pricing/)). The same page shows 58 reviews averaging 4.8 out of 5.

## How does Calendesk compare with Zeeg AI and Motion?

All three sell time management, but to different buyers. [Zeeg AI](/cases/zeeg-ai/) is a young scheduling tool aimed at general users, and [Motion](/cases/motion/) moved from an AI calendar toward AI employees for teams. Calendesk stays with appointment-based practices and adds payments and client portals on top of the calendar.

Our reading: Calendesk earns more per account than a consumer scheduler would, because a booking page sits close to a practice's income. It also has fewer accounts to lose, so each churned clinic costs more. The AI angle is lighter here than in the other two cases, and Calendesk's verified revenue comes from workflow software rather than a model.

## What lessons does Calendesk offer bootstrapped SaaS founders?

- **Sell one core to several professions.** Calendesk tags health, education and legal on one booking product ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/calendesk)).
- **Bundle payments with scheduling.** Taking money at booking makes the tool harder to replace.
- **Add usage-based extras.** SMS and email top-ups add revenue without a new plan.
- **Expect slow, steady compounding.** About six years of selling produced the $1,164,015 all-time total.
