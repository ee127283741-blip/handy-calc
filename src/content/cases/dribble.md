Most fantasy sports wait for real games. Dribble skips the real games entirely. It is a basketball league where every match is played by the computer inside NBA 2K and streamed live by a bot, while fans own and manage the players. The one-person US project, founded in December 2025, had collected $16,571 by November 2026 ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/dribble)).

## How does the Dribble simulation league run itself?

Dribble lets players create athletes, develop careers, sign contracts and compete for championships, while GMs and coaches manage teams, trades and strategy ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/dribble)). A custom automatic streaming agent runs and broadcasts the CPU-vs-CPU games with minimal human intervention.

> "Dribble is a basketball league that runs itself" — Dribble value proposition on TrustMRR ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/dribble))

A connected website, a virtual economy and an active Discord community surround the games. The listing says the system removes the usual wait for drafts and updates: players can join a game and watch it within minutes, which suits basketball fans who enjoy live simulations.

## What does it take to build Simulation Basketball solo?

Simulation Basketball is built on Django with Stripe for payments, and the listing shows 1,187 code contributions in the last year ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/dribble)). The hard part is the streaming agent: automating game setup and broadcast is what lets one person run a league that would otherwise need staff. The founder's name is not disclosed.

## What lessons does Dribble hold for community game builders?

- **Automation makes a solo league possible.** A streaming agent replaces the people who would run games.
- **Free entry, paid depth.** Revenue arrives through Stripe even though joining costs nothing.
- **Discord is the home base.** The only listed marketing channel is the community itself.
- **Odd ideas can find buyers.** A simulated league has earned five figures in under a year.
