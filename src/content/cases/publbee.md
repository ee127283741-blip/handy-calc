Self-publishing on Amazon KDP looks easy until an author has to pick a niche and keywords that actually sell. Publbee sells the data for that decision. The Austrian SaaS reached $2,950 in MRR from 86 active subscriptions by November 2026 ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/publbee)), mostly through organic reach, according to its founder message.

## What does Publbee do for Amazon KDP authors?

Publbee gives Amazon KDP authors one platform for market research, keyword optimization and AI-assisted content creation. Its listing says users can research KDP keywords, find profitable niches, inspect competitors and create covers ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/publbee)).

The pitch is completeness: a suite that covers the entire publishing process instead of a single keyword tool. The stack listed includes React, Node.js, MongoDB, OpenAI, Google Cloud, Cloudflare and Stripe.

## Why does the Publbee founder message read like a sales pitch?

The founder message on the listing is addressed to a future owner. It says paying subscribers came mostly through organic reach with near-zero ad spend, that US and UK authors barely know Publbee exists, and that fixing dunning alone would lift MRR ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/publbee)).

> "Sole founder, out of time, not out of demand." — Nezir Basar, founder of Publbee ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/publbee))

Two details on the page do not line up. The founder field names Yasemin while the message is signed Nezir Basar, and the message says "sole founder" while the team size field says 2–5 people. We report both as listed. An asking price is not disclosed in the page text we reviewed.

Our observation: GETebook.ai and AIWriteBook sell the writing, which every aspiring author needs. Publbee sells the research for authors already treating KDP as a business, a smaller but more serious group. Its entry price of $29.99 ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/publbee)) is close to GETebook.ai's $29 ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/getebook-ai)), so the gap in MRR is about audience size more than price.

## What lessons does Publbee offer niche SaaS founders?

- **Sell to people who treat it as a business.** KDP authors chasing profitable niches will pay for data that guides their choices.
- **Organic reach can carry a product.** The founder says most subscribers came with near-zero ad spend.
- **Failed payments are lost revenue.** The founder flags dunning as a fix that would lift MRR on its own.
- **Untapped markets are an opportunity and a warning.** US and UK authors barely know Publbee, which shows both room to grow and limited reach so far.
