In 2023, AI-generated QR codes that look like paintings went viral for a few weeks. Nicolas Cella turned that short moment into a lasting business. By November 2026, QR Code AI showed $14,730 in monthly recurring revenue and $334,908 in all-time revenue ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/qr-code-ai)), long after the trend itself faded.

## What does QR Code AI make for brands?

QR Code AI generates QR codes that carry a brand's look and still scan. The company-written listing pitches it this way ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/qr-code-ai)):

> "Unleash the potential of your brand with our AI-powered custom QR codes." — QR Code AI listing ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/qr-code-ai))

The buyers are businesses and marketers who print codes on packaging, menus and posters. A plain black square works, but a code that matches the design gets more attention. The listing claims about 500,000 users, far more than the paying base.

## How much revenue does QR Code AI make in 2026?

QR Code AI had $14,730 MRR and 1,535 active subscriptions in November 2026, and it collected $15,174 in the 30 days before the snapshot ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/qr-code-ai)). All-time revenue was $334,908, verified through Stripe.

## Why did QR Code AI bet on SEO alone?

Search fits the product. People look up phrases like "custom QR code" or "QR code with logo" at the moment they need one, so ranking for those terms brings buyers with intent. SEO is the only channel the listing names ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/qr-code-ai)), and the founder's X account has about 300 followers, so social reach was not the engine.

The stack reflects a data-heavy product: Nuxt, Vue.js and TypeScript on the front end, with MongoDB, Redis and ClickHouse behind it. ClickHouse is an analytics database, which suggests scan tracking is part of what customers pay for.

## How does QR Code AI compare with Web3Forms and DataFast?

QR Code AI belongs to the same family as [Web3Forms](/cases/web3forms/): a small utility that sells in volume at a low price. Both have a free tier that pulls in many users and a modest paid base.

Our observation: [DataFast](/cases/datafast/) grew on its founder's audience, while QR Code AI grew on search with almost no personal following. Both prove a narrow product can carry a small business, but the routes are opposite. A search-led product keeps working without the founder posting every day.

## What lessons does QR Code AI teach about riding a trend?

- **Trends can outlive the hype.** The AI QR art wave passed, and the product still showed $14,730 MRR in November 2026 ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/qr-code-ai)).
- **Search captures intent.** SEO is the only listed channel, and it reaches people who need a code today.
- **Free users feed a paid core.** About 500,000 claimed users sit above 1,535 subscriptions.
- **Low prices need volume.** Roughly $9.60 per subscription means the business depends on many small customers.
