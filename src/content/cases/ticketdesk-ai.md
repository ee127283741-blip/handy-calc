Six customers, $2,219 a month. Ticketdesk AI is not a breakout, and that is what makes it useful to study: it shows the economics of a small B2B AI help desk sixteen months after launch ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/ticketdesk-ai)). Founder Vikash Rathee, who started it in India in July 2025, has now put it up for sale.

## What does Ticketdesk AI automate for support teams?

Ticketdesk AI runs AI agents that answer support tickets on email and chat, learn from a company's documentation and route complex issues to the right people ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/ticketdesk-ai)). The homepage adds a help desk, AI agent inboxes, a chatbot widget, analytics, an API and an MCP server ([founder-reported, Ticketdesk AI](https://ticketdesk.ai)).

The site claims 70% of tickets resolved by AI. That is the company's own marketing figure, not an audited one. The stack combines OpenAI and Anthropic models on Cloudflare.

## How much revenue does Ticketdesk AI make per customer?

Ticketdesk AI made $2,219 MRR from six active subscriptions in November 2026, with $2,213 collected in the last 30 days and $23,244 all time ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/ticketdesk-ai)). That works out to about $370 per subscription a month ([calc, from TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/ticketdesk-ai)).

| Plan (Nov 2026) | Price | Included | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Basic | $29 a month | 1 AI agent, 5,000 AI credits | [founder-reported, Ticketdesk AI](https://ticketdesk.ai) |
| Professional | $79 a month | 2 AI agents, 15,000 AI credits | [founder-reported, Ticketdesk AI](https://ticketdesk.ai) |
| Business | $249 a month | 5 AI agents, 40,000 AI credits | [founder-reported, Ticketdesk AI](https://ticketdesk.ai) |

An average above the $249 top plan suggests extra credits or custom deals on some accounts. With only six customers, though, one account can move the average a lot. Team size is not disclosed.

## Why is Vikash Rathee selling Ticketdesk AI?

Vikash Rathee says he is selling Ticketdesk AI to focus on other projects, not because the market is weak ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/ticketdesk-ai)).

> "I'm selling Ticketdesk AI because my focus on other ventures, not because of a lack of opportunity." — Vikash Rathee, founder of Ticketdesk AI ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/ticketdesk-ai))

His pitch to buyers is a working product in a growing category. The listing names content marketing and SEO as its only channels, which means a buyer with sales or distribution could add the growth the current owner has not pursued.

## How does a six-subscription helpdesk like Ticketdesk AI stack up against Chatbase?

[Chatbase](/cases/chatbase/) and [SiteGPT](/cases/sitegpt/) sold AI chatbots trained on a company's content earlier, during the first ChatGPT wave. Ticketdesk AI arrived later and wraps the same idea in a full help desk.

| Company | Product | Revenue figure | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ticketdesk AI | AI help desk, email and chat | $2,219 MRR (Nov 2026) | [verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/ticketdesk-ai) |
| [Chatbase](/cases/chatbase/) | Custom ChatGPT chatbot | $64,000 MRR | [founder-reported, Indie Hackers](https://www.indiehackers.com/post/how-a-college-student-reached-64-000-mo-in-6-months-by-being-an-ai-first-mover-ba7981f6e1) |
| [SiteGPT](/cases/sitegpt/) | Website chatbot | ~$15K MRR | [founder-reported, Starter Story](https://www.starterstory.com/stories/sitegpt) |

Our observation: timing mattered. Chatbase and SiteGPT grew while AI support bots were new. By 2025 the category was crowded, and Ticketdesk AI's verified numbers show how much harder the same pitch became.

## What lessons does Ticketdesk AI teach late entrants?

- **Arriving late raises the bar.** Ticketdesk AI has six customers in a category where earlier chatbots grew fast ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/ticketdesk-ai)).
- **Few customers can still pay well.** About $370 a month per account shows B2B buyers will pay for support automation.
- **SEO alone is slow for B2B.** Content and SEO are the only listed channels.
- **A clean product is sellable.** Verified revenue lets a founder exit a side project instead of shutting it down.
