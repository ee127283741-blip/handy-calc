Data Bloo has been around longer than most AI startups on this site. The Greek company started in October 2021 as a maker of marketing reports and had earned $663,945 by November 2026 ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/data-bloo)). That puts it at rank 173 on TrustMRR, far above many newer AI tools with louder launches. Its current pitch adds AI on top of a business that already worked without it.

## What problem does Data Bloo solve for marketers?

Data Bloo removes the monthly chore of building marketing reports by hand. Marketing data lives in many platforms, and the company says turning it into reports takes hours each month. Data Bloo produces ready-made, Data Studio-style reports under the customer's own branding.

> "The AI reporting platform for your marketing data" — Data Bloo company description ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/data-bloo))

On top of the reports, AI explains what changed and a chat assistant answers questions. The listing claims 20+ marketing data sources and says Data Bloo is the number one contributor on the Data Studio Gallery.

## Why is Data Bloo all-time revenue so large next to its MRR?

All-time revenue is about 100 times current MRR (calc: $663,945 / $6,422, [from TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/data-bloo)). Five years of sales at the current rate would total far less, which points to higher monthly revenue in earlier years, one-off sales, or both. TrustMRR does not show the monthly history, so the exact timeline is not disclosed.

What the data does show is a durable base: 237 subscriptions still active five years after founding.

## How does Data Bloo compare with DataFast and Simple Analytics?

Data Bloo, [DataFast](/cases/datafast/) and [Simple Analytics](/cases/simple-analytics/) all sell marketing data to small teams, but Data Bloo reports on other tools' data while the other two collect their own.

Our observation: owning the tracking script makes a product harder to remove, which may explain why the two analytics tools hold higher MRR. Data Bloo depends on Google's Data Studio ecosystem, and its new Claude and ChatGPT connectors look like a hedge against that dependency.

## Which lessons from Data Bloo apply to data products?

- **Ride an ecosystem, then widen it.** Data Bloo grew inside Data Studio and now reaches Sheets, Claude and ChatGPT.
- **Old businesses can add AI.** AI explanations sit on top of reports customers already wanted.
- **Low entry price, many accounts.** Plans from $8.33 a month scale with connected accounts ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/data-bloo)).
- **Lifetime revenue tells a longer story.** $663,945 all time shows a past larger than today's MRR suggests.
