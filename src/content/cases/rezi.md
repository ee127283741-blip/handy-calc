Rezi's first product was not software. It was a Microsoft Word resume and a guide, sold for about $9.69 ([founder-reported, Starter Story](https://www.starterstory.com/stories/ai-powered-free-ats-resume-builder)). In November 2026, the company that grew out of that file showed $241,209 in MRR and $10,497,012 in all-time revenue ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/rezi)). The route between those numbers ran through South Korea, an outsourced first build and a year of ads that did not pay back.

## Why did a graduate with a 2.2 GPA start Rezi?

Rezi started because a resume outperformed the grades behind it. Jacob Jacquet, the founder of Rezi, graduated in 2015 with a 2.2 GPA and still landed interviews at Google, Dropbox and Goldman Sachs ([founder-reported, Starter Story](https://www.starterstory.com/stories/ai-powered-free-ats-resume-builder)). Jacquet credited the resume and posted it on Reddit, where the response was strong.

The first sale tested that idea cheaply, with the Word template and best-practice guide. The software took much longer. Jacquet moved to South Korea in 2016, aged 23, and taught English for a visa. From 2015 to 2018, Jacquet looked for financing and hired an agency to build the first version.

## How does the Rezi ATS resume builder work?

The Rezi ATS resume builder writes and tailors a resume, then checks it for keywords. Its pricing page lists an AI Resume Writer and Editor, keyword targeting, real-time content analysis of bullet points and a Rezi Score based on 23 metrics ([founder-reported, Rezi pricing](https://www.rezi.ai/pricing)). The interview adds the second half of the pitch: formatting that hiring systems can parse.

The AI layer arrived after the launch. Rezi went live as a B2C SaaS in September 2019, and GPT-3 entered the workflow in December 2020. That is Jacquet's own priority claim, and we have not verified that no rival got there first.

> "we were the first resume builder to incorporate GPT into the workflow in December of 2020 with GPT-3." — Jacob Jacquet ([founder-reported, Starter Story](https://www.starterstory.com/stories/ai-powered-free-ats-resume-builder))

Jacquet later questioned the build decision, calling the agency hire a possible mistake compared with hiring in-house. By August 2024, the Starter Story page header showed 15 employees, while the interview text said 11 worked from an office in Seoul. Current headcount is not disclosed.

## Which marketing channels built Rezi's user base?

Rezi's growth came from deal platforms, giveaways and search, and paid ads failed. An AppSumo deal during COVID and a StackCommerce partnership brought early users. Then Rezi gave away its most expensive subscription at no cost, which set off viral spikes. In October 2023, that produced just under 70,000 sign-ups in a single day ([founder-reported, Starter Story](https://www.starterstory.com/stories/ai-powered-free-ats-resume-builder)).

Search does the steady work. Jacquet said acquisition outside one-off events depends on ranking for relevant keywords, including "AI Resume Builder". The August 2024 interview counted 432,638 visits in the prior 30 days, up 18% month over month, and about 2,620,883 job seekers served. A company-written description on TrustMRR now claims about 1M new users a year ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/rezi)).

## What does Rezi charge, and which bets went wrong?

Rezi charges $29 a month for Pro, or $149 once for Lifetime, and it has a free tier with limits. The free plan allows one resume and three PDF downloads, and its scoring and keyword tools are limited.

| Plan | Price (Nov 2026) | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Free | $0 | [founder-reported, Rezi pricing](https://www.rezi.ai/pricing) |
| Pro | $29 a month | [founder-reported, Rezi pricing](https://www.rezi.ai/pricing) |
| Lifetime | $149 one-time | [founder-reported, Rezi pricing](https://www.rezi.ai/pricing) |
| Enterprise | $99 a month per 200 users | [founder-reported, Rezi pricing](https://www.rezi.ai/pricing) |

Two earlier decisions show what Rezi tried and changed. It removed a $3-a-month Basic plan and added an anti-churn cancellation flow, and Jacquet said customer lifetime value rose, reaching $101.90 in August 2024 ([founder-reported, Starter Story](https://www.starterstory.com/stories/ai-powered-free-ats-resume-builder)). The harder lesson was paid acquisition. Rezi spent about 15K a month for a year chasing a return above break-even, then stopped. Ad costs were zero at the time of the interview.

Funding is less clear. TrustMRR lists Rezi as "VC-backed", while Jacquet said they avoided excessive dilution and loss of control. The amount raised is not disclosed.

Timing helped twice, in our reading. The AppSumo deal landed during COVID, and GPT-3 arrived about 15 months after launch, when Rezi already had a product to put it in. Rezi also spent four years between its 2015 start and its 2019 SaaS launch, so it was not a company born from the AI wave.

Our observation: the channels split more sharply than the products. AiApply and BetterPic both reported paid ads as part of their mix, while Rezi tested ads for a year and quit. A subscription also fits differently here. Job searches end, and headshots are mostly bought once, yet Rezi's founder still reported a $101.90 lifetime value, and Rezi also sells a $149 one-time plan. Rezi's Enterprise plan, which the company says supports over 300 organizations ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/rezi)), reaches job seekers through universities and career sites, a route the other three cases don't describe in the same way.

## What lessons does the slow rise of Rezi offer founders?

- **Sell the manual version first.** A Word file and a guide tested demand years before Rezi had software ([founder-reported, Starter Story](https://www.starterstory.com/stories/ai-powered-free-ats-resume-builder)).
- **A very cheap plan can be worth removing.** Rezi removed its $3 tier, and Jacquet reported that lifetime value rose afterward.
- **Set a stop date on paid ads.** A year at about 15K a month without the target return ended with ad spend at zero, and the 54.97% margin came after.
- **Use giveaways as distribution.** Giving away the top subscription produced just under 70,000 sign-ups in one day.
- **Keep your evidence dated.** The $241,209 Stripe figure and the $215K interview figure tell a clearer story side by side than blended into one growth claim ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/rezi)).

> "Don’t obsess over money as money seems to follow ability, commitment, and passion like an unavoidable consequence." — Jacob Jacquet ([founder-reported, Starter Story](https://www.starterstory.com/stories/ai-powered-free-ats-resume-builder))
