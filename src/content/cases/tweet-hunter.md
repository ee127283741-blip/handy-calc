## Origin of the idea
Tibo was growing his own X account and kept a swipe file of tweets that performed well. Turning that library plus AI writing help into a tool for other creators was the product.

## How they started
The MVP was built in about two weeks and sold to his own followers. The same team later built Taplio, a LinkedIn version.

## Tools & tech stack
A web app with a large searchable database of high-performing tweets, GPT-3-based writing help, scheduling and a light CRM for audience engagement.

## Marketing
The founder's own growth on X was the proof and the channel, along with affiliate and creator partnerships.

## Sales & pricing
Monthly subscriptions aimed at creators, founders and agencies.

## Monthly revenue
Not disclosed as a dated monthly figure. Tweet Hunter and Taplio combined made about $1M in 2022; see metrics above.

## Total revenue
Not disclosed.

## Founding date & timeline
- **2021:** MVP built in two weeks; $150K ARR four months later.
- **2022:** Tweet Hunter and Taplio together reached about $1M annual revenue; both sold to Lempire for $2M cash plus earn-out (media-reported by Indie Hackers and the lempire blog).

## Team size
Not disclosed.

## Key takeaways
- Your own audience growth can be the product demo.
- Clone a winning product to a second platform (X → LinkedIn).
- Selling early buys certainty; the founder later said he regretted it.
