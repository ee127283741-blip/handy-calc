Five dollars a month does not sound like a business. Multiply it by thousands of creators and six years, and it does. Liinks, a link-in-bio tool run by Charlie Clark alone, showed $31,636 in MRR and $1,799,234 in all-time revenue in November 2026 ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/liinks)). It is one of the least flashy products on this site, and one of the steadiest.

## What does Liinks offer creators beyond a list of links?

Liinks gives creators one page for their content, socials and offers, with styling, analytics and automation. The homepage says it can add links from Instagram posts automatically, track how an audience clicks, and supports multi-tab pages and QR codes ([founder-reported, Liinks](https://liinks.co/)). The Pro plan adds custom domains, multiple profiles and custom social share previews.

> "Share everything you create, sell, and stand for, from one beautiful link in bio." — Liinks website ([founder-reported, Liinks](https://liinks.co/))

The site says Liinks is loved by 10,000+ creators and businesses. Its testimonials come from an animation studio, a travel blog and a real estate agent, a reminder that "creator" here includes small businesses on Instagram. Liinks is not mainly an AI product, and we include it because its pricing and solo operation are a useful benchmark for the AI tools on this site.

## How does Charlie Clark run Liinks alone?

Charlie Clark runs Liinks as a one-person, bootstrapped company, according to TrustMRR ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/liinks)). A small product surface helps: two plans, one core page builder and integrations. Clark also has 12.5k followers on X per TrustMRR, which gives the product a channel of its own.

## How much revenue does Liinks make from $5 plans?

Liinks makes about $31K a month from 5,533 active subscriptions. TrustMRR showed $31,636 MRR, $31,299 in the last 30 days and $1,799,234 all time in November 2026 ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/liinks)).

The average is low, as the pricing implies. MRR per subscription is about $5.72 ([calc, from TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/liinks)), just above the $5 Starter price, so most customers are on the cheapest plan. Last-30-day revenue is within about 1% of MRR, the mark of a stable subscription base rather than one driven by one-off sales. Over roughly six and a half years, the lifetime total averages about $23K a month ([calc, from TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/liinks)), so today's MRR is above the long-run average and growth has been gradual. Profit is not disclosed, but with one person and a simple product, costs are likely modest; that is our inference, not a reported figure.

## How does Liinks differ from Postiz and Lychee?

Liinks, [Postiz](/cases/postiz/) and [Lychee](/cases/lychee/) are small software businesses on this site with very different prices and growth paths.

| [Postiz](/cases/postiz/) | Social media scheduling from $29 a month | $373,501 MRR, Stripe only (Nov 2026) | [verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/postiz) |
| [Lychee](/cases/lychee/) | AI tool that turns data into charts | $10K+ MRR (May 2024) | [founder-reported, Starter Story](https://www.starterstory.com/stories/lychee) |

Our observation: Liinks shows the low-price, high-count end of creator software. Postiz charges several times more per account and grew much faster. Liinks needs over 5,000 subscribers to reach the same MRR band that a $199 tool such as [Bazzly](/cases/bazzly/) reaches with under 200 ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/bazzly)), but cheap plans also tend to be the last line item a small business cancels.

## What lessons does Liinks offer solo founders?

- **Cheap and steady can compound.** $5 plans added up to $1,799,234 in lifetime revenue ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/liinks)).
- **Keep the product surface small.** Two plans and one core job make a one-person company possible.
- **Let the product market itself.** Public pages that link back helped earn a domain rating of 74.
- **Watch revenue against MRR.** A 30-day total within about 1% of MRR signals a healthy, low-churn base.
