Atlametrics describes itself in four French words: "vente de formations en ligne", the sale of online courses. Behind that plain line is a finance education platform for beginners, founded in France in February 2025 by Valerio and Benjamin. By November 2026 it had $1,452 in MRR and $19,103 in all-time revenue ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/atlametrics)), from only about 200 users.

## What does Atlametrics teach beginner investors?

Atlametrics teaches people to invest, read financial markets and manage their own money. The platform combines guided interactive courses, gamified quizzes and certifications, simplified analysis tools, a personal budgeting planner and market news, according to its listing ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/atlametrics)). The founders report more than four years of finance experience.

> "An accessible platform that helps people learn to invest, understand financial markets, and manage their money through guided courses, analysis tools, a budgeting planner, and market news." — Atlametrics listing ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/atlametrics))

## Why is Atlametrics more than a course library?

Atlametrics bundles tools with lessons. The budgeting planner, analysis tools and market news give a subscriber something to open after the course is finished ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/atlametrics)). That matters for a subscription: a pure course is often bought once and completed, while a tool can be used every week.

The ratio of paying subscribers to users is also unusually high. About 24 subscriptions from about 200 users points to a small but committed audience rather than a large free funnel.

## What lessons does Atlametrics hold for education founders?

- **Pair lessons with tools.** A budgeting planner gives subscribers a reason to stay after the course.
- **Small audiences can pay well.** About 200 users support 24 subscriptions.
- **A plain pitch can work.** The company describes itself as simply selling online courses, and lets the verified numbers speak.
- **Credibility matters in finance.** The founders lead with their years of finance experience.
