AI app builders write code, but many founders get stuck one step earlier: they do not know what the screens should look like. Sleek sells only that step. Three founders launched it in March 2026, and by November 2026 it had $17,686 in monthly recurring revenue and $191,066 collected in total ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/sleek)).

## What does Sleek design from a single prompt?

Sleek designs every iOS and Android screen of an app in one consistent style from a chat description, then exports to React, HTML or Figma ([founder-reported, Sleek](https://sleek.design)). Its target users are founders, product managers, marketers and developers who need app designs but lack design experience ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/sleek)).

> "Go from idea to beautiful app designs in minutes by chatting with AI." — Sleek value proposition on TrustMRR ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/sleek))

The Pro plan is pitched as a way to "let your coding agents design", which places Sleek as a design layer that sits before tools such as Claude Code or Cursor. The listed stack includes Next.js, Supabase, Anthropic and OpenAI.

## How did Sleek make $191,066 in eight months?

Sleek made $191,066 between its March 2026 founding and November 2026, and was running at $17,686 MRR with 523 active subscriptions ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/sleek)). Revenue in the last 30 days was $16,185, slightly below MRR.

## Which channels does Sleek use to find app founders?

Sleek lists nine marketing channels: a blog, SEO, Pinterest, Reddit, X, YouTube, affiliates, Product Hunt and word of mouth ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/sleek)). Pinterest stands out. App screen mockups are visual, and Pinterest is where many people browse UI inspiration.

Pricing is built around AI credits. Starter costs $29 a month with 3,000 credits, Pro $69 a month with 20,000 credits and Team $99 per seat a month with 30,000 credits per seat ([founder-reported, Sleek](https://sleek.design)). The average subscription is worth about $33.80 a month ([calc, from TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/sleek)), so most payers appear to be on Starter.

## How is Sleek different from Lovable and Rocket.new?

Sleek designs app screens, while [Lovable](/cases/lovable/) and [Rocket.new](/cases/rocket-new/) generate working apps. Sleek is a much smaller business, but it targets a gap those builders leave open: deciding how the product should look before code is written.

Our observation: Sleek does not need to beat app builders. It works alongside them, and its export to code and Figma is what makes that position possible.

## What lessons does Sleek hold for AI design tool builders?

- **Pick the step before the crowded step.** Sleek sells design to people who will later use AI coding tools ([founder-reported, Sleek](https://sleek.design)).
- **Exports decide whether a design tool is useful.** React, HTML and Figma output let Sleek fit existing workflows.
- **Visual products suit visual channels.** Pinterest and YouTube sit alongside SEO in Sleek's channel list.
- **Watch the early peak.** $191,066 in eight months is strong, but a down trend means retention matters next.
