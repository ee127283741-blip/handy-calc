Marketing teams produce the same image a hundred times with different names, prices and photos. Orshot turns that chore into an API call. Founded in the United Kingdom in March 2025, it had already reached $12,687 in monthly recurring revenue and $80,205 in all-time revenue in November 2026 ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/orshot-com)).

## What problem does the Orshot API solve?

Orshot automates generating and delivering images, PDFs and videos from templates, according to its TrustMRR listing ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/orshot-com)). A team designs a template once, then fills it with data through the API or an automation tool. The listing names agencies, brands, startups, developers and marketing teams as the audience.

> "Orshot is an API and Automation service for Creative Marketing" — Orshot listing ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/orshot-com))

The listing tags it as marketing, content creation, productivity and no-code, which shows it sells to both developers and non-technical marketers.

## How much money does Orshot make from 222 subscriptions?

Orshot made $12,687 in MRR from 222 active subscriptions in November 2026 and collected $11,229 in the prior 30 days ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/orshot-com)). All-time revenue was $80,205. That is about $57.15 of MRR per subscription ([calc, from TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/orshot-com)), above the $39 Launch plan. Profit is not disclosed.

The social publishing add-on is notable. It moves Orshot from making assets to also posting them, which raises the value of each customer.

## Who is behind Orshot?

The TrustMRR listing names the founder as Rishi, with 7,543 followers on X, and places the company in the United Kingdom ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/orshot-com)). Team size and marketing channels are not disclosed. The listing gives about 140 users and a domain rating of 59, which suggests search and content play some part in discovery.

## How does Orshot compare with APIFrame and aiCarousels?

[APIFrame](/cases/apiframe/) sells API access to AI image models, and [aiCarousels](/cases/aicarousels/) helps creators make social carousels. Orshot sits between them: it is an API like APIFrame, but its output is branded marketing material like aiCarousels.

Our observation: template-based generation is less flashy than AI image models, but it is predictable, and marketing teams need predictable brand assets. That may be why Orshot's average revenue per subscription sits above its entry plan.

## What lessons does Orshot teach about selling APIs to marketers?

- **Sell the repetitive job.** Orshot automates producing many versions of the same asset.
- **Serve two buyers.** An API for developers plus no-code automation for marketers widens the market.
- **Add adjacent value.** A $12 per account social publishing add-on extends the workflow ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/orshot-com)).
- **A free tier feeds an expensive ladder.** Plans run from free to $349 a month, letting usage drive upgrades.
