A chart tells a trader what price did. It does not say whether central banks, positioning or earnings agree. Econic sells that missing context, produced by a team of AI agents. Founded in April 2026 in the United States, it had $68,460 in lifetime revenue and $4,784 MRR across 127 subscriptions by November 2026 ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/econic)). Much of that came during a six-month private beta.

## What does Econic tell a trader that a chart cannot?

Econic synthesises macro, positioning, sentiment and fundamentals into timely asset bias signals, and makes the reasoning auditable ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/econic)). The problem it names is time: traders spend hours reconciling fragmented news and data feeds, and often lack traceable macro context to test a chart-based thesis.

> "The second opinion that your chart cannot give you." — Econic description ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/econic))

The listing is clear that this is not regulated financial advice, and that crypto coverage is still in development.

## Which customers does Econic serve?

Econic is built for active forex, index and stock traders and investors with at least 1 to 3 years of experience who want macro and fundamental analysis next to technical charting ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/econic)). The company claims 200+ traders and investors after a private beta of six months. Instagram is the only marketing channel listed, and Alex Tomas is named as founder.

## How does Econic differ from AltIndex and Heron Copier?

All three sell software to retail traders and investors, but each answers a different question.

Our observation: Heron Copier sells execution plumbing, and AltIndex sells data signals. Econic sells interpretation, framed as a check on the trader's own view rather than a call to act, which also keeps it clear of advice claims.

## What are the takeaways from Econic for fintech builders?

- **Position AI as a second opinion.** It complements the trader's chart instead of replacing judgment.
- **Auditability builds trust.** Traceable reasoning matters more in finance than a confident signal.
- **A private beta can earn real money.** Six months of beta preceded $68,460 in revenue.
- **Narrow the audience.** Targeting traders with 1 to 3 years of experience sharpens the product.
