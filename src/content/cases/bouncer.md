A business that sells on WhatsApp can quickly end up with more chats than one phone can handle. Bouncer, a Malaysian startup founded in October 2025, sells a way to keep up. About a year in, it had 35 active subscriptions and $26,272 in lifetime revenue ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/bouncer)), and its founder, Afif Rus, has about 19.8k followers on X.

## What does Bouncer add on top of WhatsApp?

Bouncer adds three layers to WhatsApp: a unified team inbox so several staff can answer chats, a sales CRM to track leads, and AI-powered workflows to automate replies ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/bouncer)). The goal is to let one team handle thousands of conversations.

> "Automate WhatsApp conversations at scale." — Bouncer company description ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/bouncer))

TrustMRR files the product under the Sales market, which fits a tool meant to turn chat volume into tracked leads and closed deals.

## Does a founder audience help Bouncer find customers?

A large personal following is the most visible asset on Bouncer's listing. Afif Rus has about 19.8k X followers, while the bouncer.my domain had an Ahrefs Domain Rating of just 1 out of 100 ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/bouncer)). That gap suggests customers are not arriving through search yet. The founder's audience is a likely early channel, though the listing does not say where customers come from.

## What should messaging-tool founders learn from Bouncer?

- **Build where customers already talk.** Bouncer starts from WhatsApp rather than asking teams to adopt a new channel.
- **Bundle inbox, CRM and automation.** Three jobs in one tool make the subscription easier to justify.
- **A personal audience can carry early growth.** 19.8k X followers dwarf a Domain Rating of 1.
- **Small but verified beats big claims.** $26,272 in lifetime revenue is checked through Stripe.
