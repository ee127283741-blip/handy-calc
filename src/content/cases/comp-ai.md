Comp AI's TrustMRR page holds two numbers that do not seem to fit together. Monthly recurring revenue was $18,181 in November 2026, yet the same page showed $899,907 collected in the previous 30 days and $6,844,129 all time ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/comp-ai)). For a company founded in January 2025, that gap says a lot about how compliance software gets paid for: big invoices, not small monthly plans.

## What does Comp AI automate for SOC 2 and ISO 27001?

Comp AI helps companies pass security audits. Its company-written TrustMRR listing calls it a fast route to compliance with frameworks such as SOC 2 and ISO 27001 ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/comp-ai)). B2B buyers often need those reports before a larger customer will sign a contract, so the product sits close to a sales deadline.

> "The fastest way to get compliant with cyber security frameworks like SOC 2 and ISO 27001." — Comp AI listing ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/comp-ai))

TrustMRR tags it AI, SaaS, Security and Dev Tools, and the website is trycomp.ai.

## Why is Comp AI revenue so much higher than its MRR?

Comp AI had $18,181 MRR and 61 active subscriptions in November 2026, but it collected $899,907 in the last 30 days and $6,844,129 since launch ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/comp-ai)). TrustMRR checks these figures with a Stripe API key.

The page does not explain the gap. One plausible reading is that most money arrives as annual or one-off payments, which MRR does not fully capture, but the payment mix is not disclosed. Pricing and team size are not disclosed either, so the MRR figure alone understates the business.

## How is Comp AI built?

Comp AI runs on a modern TypeScript stack, according to its TrustMRR listing: Next.js, Tailwind CSS and TypeScript on the front end, with Prisma, PostgreSQL, Docker, Vercel and Stripe behind it ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/comp-ai)). Lewis Carhart, the founder of Comp AI, has about 12.8k followers on X according to the page. No marketing channels are listed, and the page shows a B2B audience of around 1,000 users.

Our observation: Simple Analytics and Web3Forms earn steady monthly subscriptions, so MRR describes them well. Comp AI is the opposite. A buyer facing an audit pays a large amount at once, which is why its 30-day revenue dwarfs MRR. Ranking compliance tools by MRR alone would miss most of their money.

## What can founders learn from Comp AI's numbers?

- **MRR is not the whole story.** Comp AI's $18,181 MRR sat beside $6,844,129 in all-time revenue.
- **Sell next to a deadline.** Audits such as SOC 2 often gate enterprise deals, which gives buyers urgency.
- **Read the 30-day number too.** On TrustMRR, last-30-day revenue can reveal annual or one-off billing.
- **Fast growth is possible in boring categories.** The company was founded in January 2025.
