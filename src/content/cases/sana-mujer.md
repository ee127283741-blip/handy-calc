Getting medical care in Spanish in the United States, without complications or high costs, is the problem Sana Mujer says it solves. The bootstrapped telehealth service, founded in November 2025, offers birth control, infection treatment and skin and hair care in Spanish, and by November 2026 it had $826 in MRR and $6,228 in lifetime revenue ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/sana-mujer)). It is an early, small business, but one aimed at a clearly defined language gap.

## What does Sana Mujer offer Spanish-speaking women?

Sana Mujer provides telehealth to Spanish-speaking women who live in the US, primarily for birth control, feminine infections and topical hair and skin treatments. The problem it names is getting medical care in Spanish without complications or high costs ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/sana-mujer)).

> "Tu profesional de salud en español que te entiende." — Sana Mujer value proposition on TrustMRR ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/sana-mujer))

The line translates as "your health professional in Spanish who understands you." The listing also notes more than 10 years in women's health. The site runs on Next.js, React and Vercel, with Stripe for payments.

## What are Sana Mujer's prices for each service?

Sana Mujer mixes monthly plans with per-visit consultations, according to its listing ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/sana-mujer)).

| Service | Price | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Birth control (anticonceptivos) | From $18 a month | [founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/sana-mujer) |
| Feminine infections | From $60 a consultation | [founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/sana-mujer) |
| Skin and hair (piel y cabello) | From $36 a month | [founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/sana-mujer) |
| Primary care (atención primaria) | From $60 a consultation | [founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/sana-mujer) |

The monthly plans feed MRR, while consultations are one-time payments. That split helps explain why recent revenue runs ahead of recurring revenue.

Sana Mujer had $826 MRR from 28 active subscriptions in November 2026. It collected $1,513 in the last 30 days and $6,228 since launch ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/sana-mujer)).

## How does Sana Mujer compare with MamaSkin and Nutree?

All three are small women's health or wellness businesses with verified revenue, but Sana Mujer is the only one that delivers clinical care.

| Company | What it sells | MRR | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sana Mujer | Spanish telehealth for women | $826 (Nov 2026) | [verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/sana-mujer) |
| [MamaSkin](/cases/mamaskin/) | Pregnancy-safe skincare checker app | $4,097 (Nov 2026) | [verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/mamaskin-pregnancy-skincare) |
| [Nutree](/cases/nutree/) | Nutrition and macro tracking app | $4,798 (Nov 2026) | [verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/nutree) |

Our observation: MamaSkin and Nutree are software, so each new user costs almost nothing to serve. Sana Mujer sells care from medical professionals, which explains its lower 50% margin but also lets it charge per consultation. Its edge is language, not technology.

## What can telehealth founders learn from Sana Mujer?

- **Language can be the whole product.** The pitch is care in Spanish that understands the patient, not a new feature.
- **Mix plans and visits.** Monthly plans build MRR while $60 consultations add one-time revenue.
- **Services carry real costs.** A 50% margin is lower than most software listings because clinicians are involved.
- **Early growth is fast but small.** 120% month-over-month growth on $826 MRR is promising, not proven.
