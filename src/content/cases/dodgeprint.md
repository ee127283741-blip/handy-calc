An online seller with shops on Etsy, Amazon, TikTok and Shopify ends up with four dashboards, four order queues and four sets of fees. DodgePrint pulls them into one. The US company, founded in July 2025, had $85,765 in all-time revenue and $7,573 in monthly recurring revenue by November 2026 ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/dodgeprint)), and its founder is now looking for someone to take it over.

## What does DodgePrint manage across Etsy, Amazon, TikTok and Shopify?

DodgePrint manages product listings, orders and analytics across Etsy, Amazon (Seller and Ads), TikTok Shop, Shopify and Magento 2. It automates order fulfillment and inventory updates, tracks financial transactions and produces reports ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/dodgeprint)).

The listed stack is heavier than most small SaaS: React on the front end, with GraphQL, PHP, Python, ClickHouse and MySQL behind it, which suits the volume of order and ad data involved. Founder names are not disclosed; the listing shows only a handle.

The case for a buyer is in the numbers: a verified $85,765 in lifetime revenue, steady 30-day collections close to MRR, and about 5,000 reported users on a four-tier plan from $0 to $149 a month ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/dodgeprint)). ## What lessons does DodgePrint hold for ecommerce SaaS?

- **Consolidation is a product.** One dashboard for four or five marketplaces is easy to explain to a busy seller.
- **Breadth raises price per account.** About $115 per subscription shows sellers pay for wider coverage.
- **Integrations are both moat and risk.** Every marketplace connection is hard to copy and can break when a platform changes.
- **Verified revenue makes a sale credible.** $85,765 checked through Stripe gives buyers a real starting point ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/dodgeprint)).
