WhisperAI revenue passed six figures a year within four months, and its founder came from real estate investing, not software. Eman Khoubian built the transcription app with Replit's AI agent and reached more than 80,000 users in over 100 languages in four months, according to Replit ([founder-reported, Replit](https://replit.com/customers/whisperai)). WhisperAI competes in a crowded market against Otter, Rev and many others. By July 2026 it said more than 330,000 professionals used it.

## How did a finance investor build WhisperAI?

Eman Khoubian, whose background is in finance and investing, including running a real estate investment firm in Los Angeles, built WhisperAI by describing it to Replit's agent ([Replit](https://replit.com/customers/whisperai)). The agent wrote the code, connected speech-to-text models and deployed the app. WhisperAI turns recordings into transcripts, summaries and notes. It is a separate product from OpenAI's open-source Whisper model, despite the similar name. Team size and founding date are not disclosed.

## What revenue has WhisperAI reported?

WhisperAI passed six figures in annual recurring revenue four months after launch, Replit's customer story says ([founder-reported, Replit](https://replit.com/customers/whisperai)). That means at least $100,000 a year, but the exact figure, monthly revenue and total revenue are not disclosed. Its July 2026 release listed unlimited transcription at $24.99 a month ([founder-reported, WhisperAI release](https://ipsnews.net/business/2026/07/21/whisperai-surpasses-330000-professionals-worldwide-and-launches-advanced-whisperai-transcription-api/)). At that price, $100,000 a year takes about 330 subscribers (calc).

## Why did WhisperAI launch an API?

WhisperAI launched an API in July 2026 so developers can use its transcription for pre-recorded audio and real-time speech-to-text, with uploads of up to 5 GB ([founder-reported, WhisperAI release](https://ipsnews.net/business/2026/07/21/whisperai-surpasses-330000-professionals-worldwide-and-launches-advanced-whisperai-transcription-api/)). Selling to developers adds a second revenue stream beside consumer subscriptions. It is an unusual step for a product built by someone who does not code: the founder now sells a developer tool.

> "WhisperAI was built for professionals who need more than a raw transcript." — Eman Khoubian ([founder-reported, WhisperAI release](https://ipsnews.net/business/2026/07/21/whisperai-surpasses-330000-professionals-worldwide-and-launches-advanced-whisperai-transcription-api/))

## How does WhisperAI compare with Otter.ai and AudioPen?

WhisperAI competes with [Otter.ai](/cases/otter-ai/), which passed $100M ARR with an engineering team built over nearly a decade ([founder-reported, Otter.ai release](https://salestechstar.com/price-optimization-revenue-management/otter-ai-breaks-100m-arr-barrier-and-transforms-business-meetings-launching-industry-first-ai-meeting-agent-suite/)), and with smaller tools like [AudioPen](/cases/audiopen/), a solo founder's app that turns voice notes into clean text. WhisperAI sits between them: far smaller than Otter, but with tens of thousands of users soon after launch. Its founder's path is the unusual part. Otter's founders are engineers; Khoubian built WhisperAI by talking to an AI agent.

Our reading: transcription has become a commodity, because strong speech models are available to anyone. That is what let a non-coder enter the market, and it is also why WhisperAI must win on packaging, pricing and features for professionals rather than on raw accuracy.

## What lessons does WhisperAI offer non-coders in crowded markets?

- **Commodity models lower the barrier.** Public speech-to-text models let a non-coder build a working transcriber.
- **Crowded markets still have room.** WhisperAI found 80,000 users despite Otter, Rev and others.
- **Package for a specific user.** WhisperAI targets professionals who need summaries and notes, not just raw text.
- **Add revenue streams as you grow.** An API for developers adds income beyond consumer subscriptions.
