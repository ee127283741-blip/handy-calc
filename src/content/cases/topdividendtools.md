Most directories struggle to earn anything. TopDividendTools, a list of tools for dividend investors, collected $10,950 in the last 30 days alone ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/topdividendtools)). It was founded in December 2025, so its $54,015 in all-time revenue arrived in under a year. The founder is listed as TopDividendETFs, an X account with 12.2k followers.

## What is TopDividendTools and who uses it?

TopDividendTools is a directory of tools and websites for dividend investing and the stock market. It helps dividend investors find tools for tracking, calculating and researching dividend investments, according to its listing ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/topdividendtools)). About 1,000 people use it.

> "The most accurate & complete directory of dividend investing tools on the internet." — TopDividendTools listing on TrustMRR ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/topdividendtools))

## Why does an X audience matter to TopDividendTools?

TopDividendTools launched with a ready audience. Its founder runs the TopDividendETFs account with 12.2k followers on X ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/topdividendtools)), a following built around the same topic. A directory needs both readers and listed tools, and an existing niche audience brings readers from day one.

Its domain rating was 22 out of 100 at the time. That is modest, but a directory gains search value as more tool pages and backlinks accumulate.

## What takeaways does TopDividendTools offer niche directory builders?

- **Bring an audience first.** A 12.2k-follower X account gave the directory instant reach.
- **Narrow beats broad.** A directory for dividend investors only is easier to own than a general finance list.
- **Most revenue may not be recurring.** About 3% of the last 30 days came from MRR.
- **Directories can move fast.** $54,015 in under a year from a December 2025 launch.
