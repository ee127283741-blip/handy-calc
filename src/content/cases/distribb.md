SEO tools usually stop at telling you what to write. Distribb sells the writing, the publishing, the backlinks and the social posts as one subscription, pitched as an AI agent that gets customers on autopilot. In November 2026 it showed $17,459 in monthly recurring revenue and $168,281 all time ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/distribb)), about 15 months after its August 2025 founding.

## What does Distribb's buy-intent SEO agent actually do?

Distribb automates the content side of search marketing. The homepage lists buyer keyword research, a content calendar, articles written and published to the customer's site, fixes for pages that slip, and a backlink exchange that promises 3 to 5 links a month ([founder-reported, Distribb homepage](https://distribb.io/)). It also answers Google reviews, posts to Google profiles and tracks when AI answers cite the customer.

> "Distribb writes and publishes articles, and builds backlinks from real businesses, so buyers find you on Google and AI search." — Distribb homepage ([founder-reported, Distribb](https://distribb.io/))

The TrustMRR listing adds a Reddit reply agent and distribution to 13 social platforms. Developers can drive it through an MCP server, a Claude connector or a ChatGPT app.

## How does Distribb price Pro against Accelerator?

The TrustMRR listing gives two plans: Pro at $97 a month and Accelerator at $495 a month, with a 7-day free trial ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/distribb)). The homepage advertised a shorter 3-day free trial in November 2026, so trial length has changed at least once.

The homepage marks videos, Instagram carousels and Quora answers as "done for you on Accelerator", which makes the top plan closer to a managed service. Target customers named on the site include agencies, local businesses, Shopify stores, clinics and SaaS companies. The listing claims 500+ businesses.

Our observation: Distribb sits between the other two. SEObot focuses on writing articles, Postiz on scheduling posts, and Distribb bundles both plus a backlink network. The bundle supports a higher average price, but it also means competing on many fronts at once.

## What should SEO tool builders learn from Distribb?

- **Bundle the outcome, not the feature.** Distribb sells "buyers find you" and does keyword, content, links and social in one plan.
- **A premium tier can be a service.** The $495 Accelerator plan adds done-for-you videos and carousels ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/distribb)).
- **Plan for AI search.** Tracking AI citations and offering an MCP server shows where the product thinks search is going.
- **Margins can stay high with automation.** The listing claims a 75% profit margin on a content-heavy product.
