The snooze button is one of the most-used buttons on a phone, and Nooze is built to take it away. The iPhone app makes you do push-ups or solve math before the alarm stops. Founded in the United States in February 2026, it had $53,100 in lifetime revenue and $4,302 in MRR by November 2026 ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/stealth-venture-46)). That is a lot of subscriptions for a simple idea in under a year.

## How does the Nooze alarm clock stop you snoozing?

Nooze is a mission alarm clock: the alarm keeps going until you finish a task. Its App Store listing names push-ups, math problems, making your bed, spoken affirmations and an object hunt as missions, with camera-powered challenges, streaks and badges ([Apple App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id6759581682)).

> "Nooze is the mission alarm clock that makes you complete a task before your alarm turns off." — Nooze App Store description ([Apple App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id6759581682))

The app is published by Nura Labs Inc. and is aimed at heavy sleepers and chronic snoozers.

## What does Nooze make from 1,639 subscriptions?

Nooze had $4,302 MRR from 1,639 active subscriptions in November 2026, with $3,779 collected in the last 30 days and $53,100 all time ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/stealth-venture-46)). Revenue is verified through Superwall, the paywall platform the app uses.

## Why does Nooze market on TikTok and the App Store?

TrustMRR lists App Store optimization and TikTok as Nooze's marketing channels ([TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/stealth-venture-46)). An alarm that forces push-ups is easy to show in a short video, and "alarm clock" is a common App Store search.

The stack fits a small team shipping fast: React Native and Expo on the frontend, Convex on the backend and Superwall for paywalls. The team is bootstrapped. The founder is listed only as Ryan.

Our observation: STOPPR and Nooze both take a familiar app category and add one forcing mechanism. Cal AI shows the ceiling when a daily habit app finds a huge audience. Nooze has many subscribers but low revenue per user, so its next step is likely pricing, not features.

## What can sleep app makers learn from Nooze?

- **Turn a pain into a mechanic.** Forcing a task before the alarm stops is the whole product.
- **Visual features sell on TikTok.** Push-up alarms are easy to film and share.
- **Many cheap subscriptions add up.** 1,639 subscriptions produced $4,302 MRR.
- **Paywall tools matter.** Superwall handles monetisation, so a small team can focus on the app.
