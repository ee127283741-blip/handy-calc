Glam Up app revenue was never published on its own, but its co-founder wrote a senior thesis about it. Nicole Cheung, a 2025 graduate of Claremont McKenna College, co-founded Glam Up, an AI beauty app, and passed one million downloads while still a senior ([media-reported, Claremont McKenna College](https://www.cmc.edu/news/nicole-cheung-25-co-founds-ai-beauty-app-achieves-milestone)). The Superwall Podcast says she built four apps before turning 25 with no engineering background, and that Glam Up and her next app, Sprout, made over $400K a month combined ([founder-reported, The Superwall Podcast](https://www.buzzsprout.com/2522779/episodes/19173502-nicole-cheung-she-makes-400k-mo-from-ai-apps-while-in-college)).

## What is the Glam Up app?

Glam Up is an AI beauty app on the App Store, listed as "Glam Up - Perfect Your Look" ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/-/id6480331890)). Users get AI feedback on their look and makeup. Cheung co-founded it while studying at Claremont McKenna, and her senior thesis described how she co-founded and scaled it ([Claremont McKenna College](https://www.cmc.edu/news/nicole-cheung-25-co-founds-ai-beauty-app-achieves-milestone)). The names of her co-founders, the team size and the prices are not disclosed in these sources.

> "From 0 to 1 Million Users in 6 Months: How I Built My Viral App" — Nicole Cheung's senior thesis title ([founder-reported via Claremont McKenna College](https://www.cmc.edu/news/nicole-cheung-25-co-founds-ai-beauty-app-achieves-milestone))

## How did Nicole Cheung build Glam Up without an engineering background?

Cheung's role was product, design and distribution rather than code. The Superwall Podcast introduces her as having built four apps before 25 with no engineering background ([The Superwall Podcast](https://www.buzzsprout.com/2522779/episodes/19173502-nicole-cheung-she-makes-400k-mo-from-ai-apps-while-in-college)). Glam Up reached a million users within six months, according to the episode. The college credits professors Darren Filson and Josh Jones with helping on her next app, Prep AI.

## How much money do Glam Up and Sprout make?

Glam Up and Sprout made over $400K a month combined, and Sprout alone reached $250K MRR eight months after launch, Cheung said ([founder-reported, The Superwall Podcast](https://www.buzzsprout.com/2522779/episodes/19173502-nicole-cheung-she-makes-400k-mo-from-ai-apps-while-in-college)). Subtracting Sprout leaves about $150K a month for Glam Up (calc). That split is ours; she did not give a Glam Up figure. Total revenue is not disclosed.

## How does Glam Up compare with Umax and Cal AI?

[Umax](/cases/umax/) and [Cal AI](/cases/cal-ai/) are the closest comparisons: consumer AI apps built by very young founders that analyse a photo of you or your food. Umax scored selfies and claimed large revenue that Fortune could not verify; Cal AI's sales were later confirmed in an acquisition press release. Glam Up sits between them: a college-verified download milestone, but revenue only from the founder.

Our reading: what Cheung repeated was the format, not the code. After Glam Up she built a second app, and her own figures imply it out-earned the first. Like the growth-marketer founder of [Plinq](/cases/plinq/), she treats distribution as the core skill.

## What can app founders learn from Glam Up?

- **Ship a visual result.** A beauty score or makeup analysis is easy to share, which helps an app spread.
- **Repeat the playbook.** Cheung's figures imply her second app, Sprout, earned more than the first.
- **Skills beyond code count.** She is presented as having no engineering background.
- **Separate verified and self-reported numbers.** The college confirms downloads; revenue comes from the founder.
