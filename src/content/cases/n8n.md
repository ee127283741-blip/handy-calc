n8n ARR passed $40M in 2025, six years after Jan Oberhauser started the Berlin company on his own. CRN reported in October 2025 that n8n had more than $40M in annual recurring revenue as of March 2025 ([media-reported, CRN](https://www.crn.com/news/ai/2025/nvidia-backed-ai-startup-n8n-raises-180m-hits-2-5b-valuation)). The product had existed since 2019 as a workflow automation tool that users could run on their own servers for free. What changed was AI: teams started using n8n to wire language models into their apps and build agents, and the paid cloud version took off.

## How did Jan Oberhauser build n8n?

Jan Oberhauser founded n8n in Berlin in 2019 and built it as a node-based editor: each step in a workflow is a box, and users connect boxes to move data between apps ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/N8n)). He released the source under a "fair-code" license, so anyone can self-host the Community edition for free while n8n sells a managed cloud and enterprise features. That gave n8n a large free user base among developers long before AI made automation a hot category. Its plan prices could not be confirmed on the official page for this update, so pricing is listed as not disclosed.

## Why did n8n's valuation jump in 2025?

AI agents turned n8n from a niche automation tool into infrastructure. Bloomberg reported in August 2025 that n8n's valuation had gone from $350M to $2.3B in four months ([media-reported, Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2025-08-07/accel-leading-round-for-ai-startup-n8n-at-2-3-billion-valuation)). In October 2025 the company closed a $180M Series C at a $2.5B valuation, led by Accel, with Nvidia's NVentures taking part, bringing total funding to about $240M ([media-reported, CRN](https://www.crn.com/news/ai/2025/nvidia-backed-ai-startup-n8n-raises-180m-hits-2-5b-valuation)).

> "We're building for a future where AI-driven automation becomes as essential as Excel once was" — Jan Oberhauser ([founder-reported, Ventureburn](https://ventureburn.com/n8n-series-c-funding/))

## How much revenue does n8n earn?

n8n had more than $40M in ARR as of March 2025, or about $3.3M a month (calc), CRN reported in October 2025 ([media-reported, CRN](https://www.crn.com/news/ai/2025/nvidia-backed-ai-startup-n8n-raises-180m-hits-2-5b-valuation)). Oberhauser told Bloomberg that revenue had risen about tenfold in the past year, but he declined to give an exact number ([founder-reported via PYMNTS](https://www.pymnts.com/news/investment-tracker/2025/ai-agent-startup-n8n-valued-at-2-5-billion-after-series-c/)). PitchBook also counted more than 230,000 active users as of March 2025. Total revenue and team size are not disclosed.

## How does n8n compare with Lovable and Replit?

n8n grew the slow way and then the fast way. [Lovable](/cases/lovable/) and [Bolt.new](/cases/bolt-new/) were AI-first products that reached big ARR within months of launch. [Replit](/cases/replit/) is the closer match: like n8n, it spent years as a developer tool with a free tier before AI agents made its revenue jump. n8n's $40M ARR is smaller than those vibe-coding numbers, but n8n sells to companies that run its workflows every day, often on their own servers.

Our reading: the self-hosted free edition is both n8n's growth engine and its ceiling. It built trust with developers and spread n8n into companies without a sales team. It also means many users never pay, which may be why n8n's ARR trails its usage. The tenfold revenue growth suggests the cloud product is converting more of those users now that AI workflows need hosted reliability.

For a no-code AI agent platform that once pitched itself as the "Zapier of AI" and reported high seven figures of revenue in July 2025, see [Lindy](/cases/lindy/) ([founder-reported, The SaaS Podcast](https://saasclub.io/podcast/lindy-flo-crivello-450/)).

## What can open-source founders learn from n8n?

- **A free self-hosted edition builds a long runway.** n8n had years of developer adoption before AI made automation urgent.
- **Ride the new use case, not a new product.** n8n did not rebuild for AI; it let users plug models into the tool they already knew.
- **Valuations move with category timing.** The same company went from $350M to $2.3B in four months when agents became the topic.
- **Fair-code needs a paid reason.** Hosting, scale and enterprise controls are what turn free users into ARR.
