AI coding tools can now build a whole website, but the result often looks generic. cuedesign sells the fix: hand-picked interactions from the best sites on the web, each with code and a carefully tuned prompt. The solo project launched in September 2026 and had $3,541 in lifetime revenue by November 2026, $2,105 of it in the last 30 days ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/cuedesign)).

## What is cuedesign's taste layer for AI?

cuedesign is a curated library of 300+ web components and interactions, each with code and an AI prompt tuned over 30 to 80 rounds, delivered into AI editors through the Cue MCP ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/cuedesign)).

> "AI can build any website now. What it can't do is make it look good." — cuedesign company description ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/cuedesign))

It targets designers, developers, frontend engineers, Framer creators and vibe coders. The stack listed is React, Supabase, Cloudflare, Vercel and Node.js.

## What can AI tool builders learn from cuedesign?

- **Sell what AI does badly.** Taste and visual polish are the gap cuedesign targets.
- **Ship into the editor.** An MCP puts the product where AI coders already work.
- **Lifetime deals front-load cash.** Founding plans brought quick revenue but little MRR.
- **Curation is real work.** Tuning each prompt over dozens of rounds is the moat.
