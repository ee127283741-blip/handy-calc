Supabase ARR grew with every app that someone else's AI built. Supabase is an open-source database and backend, and in 2025 the fastest-growing AI app builders started creating a Supabase project for each app their users generated. Sacra estimates Supabase's ARR rose from $30M at the end of 2024 to about $70M in September 2025 ([third-party estimate, Sacra](https://sacra.com/research/supabase-at-70m-arr-growing-250-yoy/)). Investors noticed: its valuation went from $2B to $5B in four months.

## What is Supabase, and who started it?

Supabase is a hosted Postgres database with the extras most apps need: user logins, file storage, real-time updates and serverless functions. It is open source and positions itself as an alternative to Google's Firebase. Paul Copplestone, the CEO, and Ant Wilson co-founded it. By October 2025 more than 4 million developers used it ([media-reported, TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/10/03/supabase-nabs-5b-valuation-four-months-after-hitting-2b/)). Its founding date, team size and current prices are not disclosed in sources we could check.

## How did vibe coding drive Supabase ARR?

Vibe coding gave Supabase a new kind of customer: apps created by AI agents. TechCrunch reported that coding platforms such as Lovable and Bolt run on Supabase ([media-reported, TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/10/03/supabase-nabs-5b-valuation-four-months-after-hitting-2b/)). When a non-developer asks one of those builders for an app with logins and saved data, the builder needs a backend, and Supabase was the one wired in. Sacra estimated about $70M ARR in September 2025 and about $170M in May 2026 ([third-party estimate, Sacra](https://sacra.com/research/supabase-170m-year-growing-221-yoy/)), or about $14.2M a month (calc). These are analyst estimates, not company figures.

## How much has Supabase raised?

Supabase raised two large rounds four months apart. A $200M Series D at a $2B valuation in June 2025 was followed by a $100M Series E at $5B in October 2025, co-led by Accel and Peak XV, bringing total funding to $500M ([media-reported, TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/10/03/supabase-nabs-5b-valuation-four-months-after-hitting-2b/)). Copplestone told Fortune that the company lets employees sell part of their shares in each round ([founder-reported, Fortune via inkl](https://www.inkl.com/news/exclusive-supabase-raises-100-million-at-5-billion-valuation-as-vibe-coding-soars)). Supabase's own revenue and total revenue are not disclosed.

> "Every single round we have allowed our employees to sell 25% of their vested stock," — Paul Copplestone ([founder-reported, Fortune via inkl](https://www.inkl.com/news/exclusive-supabase-raises-100-million-at-5-billion-valuation-as-vibe-coding-soars))

## How does Supabase compare with Lovable and Bolt.new?

Supabase sells to the companies in this library that sell to vibe coders. [Lovable](/cases/lovable/) and [Bolt.new](/cases/bolt-new/) charge users to generate apps; Supabase charges for the database and backend those apps run on. That makes Supabase a supplier whose usage rises with theirs, much as [Emergent](/cases/emergent/) earns hosting fees after an app is built. The risk is the same relationship in reverse: an app builder that runs its own backend no longer needs Supabase for new apps.

Our reading: Supabase is the clearest "picks and shovels" winner of the vibe-coding wave. It did not need to win the app-builder race, only to be the default backend for whoever did. Its open-source roots helped: developers already trusted Postgres, and AI agents could use well-documented, standard tools.

## What lessons does Supabase hold for infrastructure startups?

- **Be the default for someone else's growth.** Supabase grew as AI app builders created a backend for each new app.
- **Open source can be a distribution channel.** A free, standard Postgres stack made Supabase easy for both developers and AI agents to adopt.
- **Valuations follow category heat.** Supabase went from $2B to $5B in four months as vibe coding took off.
- **Supplier risk is real.** Builders that launch their own backends can reduce how many new apps start on Supabase.
