Drive from Vienna to Bucharest and you cross several countries that each want their own road vignette. Vignette ID sells all of them in one app. The company, founded in January 2022 and listed in Ukraine, had collected $655,729 all time by November 2026, with $27,886 in the last 30 days ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/vignette-id)). Its MRR was just $42, and that gap is the most interesting thing about it.

## How does Vignette ID simplify road tolls in Europe?

Vignette ID lets a driver buy vignettes for multiple cars and multiple countries at once, instead of visiting each national system separately ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/vignette-id)). It covers Slovenia, Austria, Switzerland, Slovakia, the Czech Republic, Hungary, Bulgaria, Romania and Moldova, plus Austrian tunnels, and adds a route planner.

> "The first multi-state road payment app in nine European countries" — Vignette ID company description on TrustMRR ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/vignette-id))

The problem is concrete: vehicles without a valid e-vignette may not use toll roads and can be fined.

## Which channels does Vignette ID use to reach drivers?

Vignette ID lists a wide channel mix on TrustMRR: Google Ads, app store optimization, SEO, Instagram, Threads, TikTok, X, YouTube, email marketing, influencers, partnerships, press and a referral program ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/vignette-id)). Search intent is strong here, because drivers look up vignettes right before a trip.

The listing also links to a public GitHub organization and documentation site, and says the company received support from the European Innovation Council in 2026. The tech stack spans Next.js, Vue.js, Swift, Supabase, PostgreSQL, Python, Stripe and PayPal on Hetzner and Vercel.

## What takeaways does Vignette ID give marketplace founders?

- **Aggregate fragmented government systems.** Nine national vignette schemes in one checkout is the product.
- **MRR can badly misread a business.** $42 MRR sits next to $655,729 in lifetime revenue.
- **Trip-driven, urgent purchases reward search.** Drivers buy right before a trip, so SEO and ads fit.
- **Small teams can run regulated flows.** A team of 2 to 5 people handles payments across nine countries.
