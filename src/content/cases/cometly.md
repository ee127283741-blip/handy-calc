Ad platforms each claim credit for the same sale, and marketers pay for the confusion. Cometly sells the referee. In November 2026 its attribution software showed $195,775 in MRR and $10,199,144 in all-time revenue ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/cometly)), from only 272 active subscriptions. That ratio says more about the business than the headline.

## What problem does Cometly solve for advertisers?

Cometly tells a company which ads actually produced revenue, across every ad platform at once. Its TrustMRR listing describes marketing attribution and analytics for SaaS companies, with AI that lets customers chat with their ads data. The pricing page frames the core plan in one sentence.

> "Full-funnel attribution for teams ready to see exactly which ads drive revenue." — Cometly pricing page ([founder-reported, Cometly pricing](https://www.cometly.com/pricing))

Both plans include unlimited connections to Meta, Google, LinkedIn, TikTok, Microsoft, Reddit and Snapchat, and the company says it connects to more than 70 integrations. The Enterprise plan pipes attribution data into a warehouse, internal systems and AI agents.

## Why doesn't Cometly publish its prices?

Cometly does not show dollar prices because its plans scale with traffic, and it prices the top tier through sales. The [pricing page](https://www.cometly.com/pricing) lists Core, billed monthly or annually with 20% off for annual, and Enterprise, quoted by the sales team. It estimates monthly pageviews from sessions, using about 1.5 pageviews per visit, which hints that volume drives the quote.

| Plan | What the page says | Price shown | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Core | Full-funnel attribution; 20% off annual | Not shown | [founder-reported, Cometly pricing](https://www.cometly.com/pricing) |
| Enterprise | Warehouse and AI agent data export | Contact sales | [founder-reported, Cometly pricing](https://www.cometly.com/pricing) |

Hidden pricing filters out small buyers. The revenue numbers below suggest that is the point.

## How does Cometly compare with Postiz and Poppy AI?

Cometly charges far more per customer than either of these marketing tools, and it earns less in total each month. All three sell to marketers, but they sit at different points: Postiz publishes the content, Poppy AI helps write it, and Cometly measures what the paid side returned.

| Company | What it sells | Monthly figure | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cometly | Ad attribution, ~$720 per subscription | $195,775 MRR (Nov 2026) | [verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/cometly) |
| [Postiz](/cases/postiz/) | Open-source social media scheduler | $373,501 MRR, Stripe only (Nov 2026) | [verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/postiz) |
| [Poppy AI](/cases/poppy-ai/) | AI workspace for writing marketing copy | About $400K a month (May 2025) | [founder-reported, Starter Story](https://www.starterstory.com/stories/poppyai-write-emails-that-sell) |

Our observation: Postiz and Poppy AI grew with many buyers paying small amounts. Cometly took the opposite route, a few hundred accounts paying hundreds of dollars each. That model needs fewer customers to work, but each lost account hurts more, and attribution only proves its worth for companies that already spend real money on ads.

## What lessons does Cometly hold for B2B founders?

- **Sell the measurement, not the media.** Cometly sits above every ad platform and earned $10,199,144 all time doing it ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/cometly)).
- **A small customer count can be enough.** 272 subscriptions carried almost $200K of MRR.
- **Hidden prices fit volume-based products.** When cost scales with traffic, a quote can beat a public price table.
- **AI can be a layer, not the product.** Chatting with ad data is a feature on top of attribution, not the reason the business exists.
