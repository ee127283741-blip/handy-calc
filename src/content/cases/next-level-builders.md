Selling to founders who want to build micro-SaaS is its own small industry. Next Level Builders, founded in Vietnam in October 2025, sells that audience playbooks, templates and a community. It had collected $17,578 by November 2026, yet its MRR was only $747 ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/next-level-builders)). Annual billing explains most of that gap.

## What do members get from Next Level Builders?

Next Level Builders offers expert-curated playbooks, proven templates and a builder community for solopreneurs and micro-SaaS founders who want to launch and scale faster ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/next-level-builders)). It reports about 500 users and serves both B2B and B2C buyers.

> "Stop wasting time figuring it out alone." — Next Level Builders listing on TrustMRR ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/next-level-builders))

The founder, listed as Duy, has a sizeable X audience according to the listing. Team size is not disclosed. The listing tags its market as community, education, SaaS, content creation and productivity, and Polar handles payments. Its domain, nextlevelbuilder.io, has an Ahrefs Domain Rating of 45, high for a company this young.

## Why is the Next Level Builders MRR so much lower than its cash?

Both plans are billed annually: Premium at $468 a year and VIP at $948 a year ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/next-level-builders)). Annual plans bring cash up front, which lifts last-30-day revenue whenever new members join, while MRR spreads each payment across twelve months. A few annual sign-ups can make a strong month without moving MRR much.

## What lessons come from Next Level Builders?

- **Founders will pay to skip the learning curve.** The whole offer is saved time and proven templates.
- **Annual billing reshapes the numbers.** Cash runs well ahead of MRR.
- **An audience is the channel.** The founder's X following is the obvious route to members.
- **Two tiers keep pricing simple.** Premium and VIP are the only paid choices.
