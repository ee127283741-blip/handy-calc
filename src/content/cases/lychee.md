The idea for Lychee came from watching someone struggle. The founder's girlfriend started a new job that suddenly involved data, opened Excel and asked for help. He had used Excel since fifth grade and worked in econometrics, but teaching it showed him how steep the learning curve really is. He built a basic upload-and-chart tool in 24 hours. Less than two months later, Lychee had over 11,000 users and more than $10K MRR ([founder-reported, Starter Story](https://www.starterstory.com/stories/lychee)).

## Who is the serial launcher behind Lychee?

The founder goes by Misterr Pink online. He has more than 15 years of startup experience, including roles in funded teams, and runs a larger energy trading project for big businesses and government alongside his solo products. In the ten months before the interview he launched five projects on Product Hunt and at least eight more elsewhere, including DiFunk, an AI design tool.

Across all those solo projects, he said, he made over $100K in under 10 months ([founder-reported, Starter Story](https://www.starterstory.com/stories/lychee)). Lychee was the standout.

> "My rule before building anything is I absolutely need to validate the idea before I even write a line of code." — Misterr Pink ([founder-reported, Starter Story](https://www.starterstory.com/stories/lychee))

## What does Lychee AI do that Excel doesn't?

Lychee strips the spreadsheet down to what an everyday employee needs: upload data, see it as a grid you can sort, and get a chart. Lychee AI adds analysis on top, for example connecting a Twitter account, pulling in your own and competitors' tweets, and finding the best times to post. The target user is anyone who is not a power user of Excel, which he sees as a large, underserved group.

The first version was ruthlessly minimal. He reused table and charting code from another project and built a React front end on Next.js, MongoDB for data and Stripe's no-code checkout. It took about 24 hours, including breaks, sleep and exercise, and cost under $400 ([founder-reported, Starter Story](https://www.starterstory.com/stories/lychee)).

## How does Lychee's launch formula work?

He follows the same steps for every product:

| Step | What he does |
|---|---|
| 1. Foundational backlinks | Registers the company on LinkedIn, Crunchbase, Google Business, Pinterest and similar sites |
| 2. AI directories | Lists the product on boards like There's An AI For That, spending $250–$350 at most |
| 3. Communities | Posts on relevant subreddits and Discord groups and tweets throughout the build |
| 4. Copy | Hired a professional copywriter to learn how to describe the product in one line |
| 5. Throwaway launch | Launches a smaller project first to warm up an email list of about 20,000 past users |
| 6. Product Hunt | Launches the real product once the audience is warm |
| Source | [founder-reported, Starter Story](https://www.starterstory.com/stories/lychee) |

Lychee earned its first dollar on 6 March 2024, before the public launch. By 24 March it had made $1,500 from 4,458 page visits and 200 registered users ([founder-reported, Starter Story](https://www.starterstory.com/stories/lychee)).

## What revenue has Lychee AI reached?

By May 2024, less than two months after its soft launch, Lychee had more than 11,000 users and over $10K MRR ([founder-reported, Starter Story](https://www.starterstory.com/stories/lychee)). He had raised the lifetime price from $29.99 to $69.99, added monthly and annual plans and put more features behind a paywall. Total revenue for Lychee alone is not disclosed.

For larger clients, he said he can spin up a dedicated database and AI setup, which he expected to lift his base MRR significantly.

## How does Lychee compare with Formula Bot and AudioPen?

[Formula Bot](/cases/formula-bot/) attacks the same pain, spreadsheets that most people find hard, but keeps users inside Excel by writing formulas for them. Lychee tries to replace the spreadsheet for people who never wanted one. Both founders came from data-heavy careers and built for the colleague who asks for help.

[AudioPen](/cases/audiopen/) shares Lychee's speed: a working product in a day or less and a launch to an existing audience. The difference is Lychee's repeatable launch system. Backlinks, directories, communities, a warm-up launch and Product Hunt turn every new product into a test, which is how one person ships a dozen products in under a year.

## Which lessons from Lychee fit serial launchers?

- **Watch someone struggle with a tool you know well.** The girlfriend's Excel problem was the whole insight.
- **Make the first version tiny.** Upload, grid, chart: 24 hours and under $400 ([founder-reported, Starter Story](https://www.starterstory.com/stories/lychee)).
- **Turn launching into a checklist.** The same steps for every product make each launch cheaper.
- **Raise prices once demand is proven.** The lifetime plan more than doubled to $69.99.
