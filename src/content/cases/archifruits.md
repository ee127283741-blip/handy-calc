Architectural visualisation used to mean hours in 3D software per image. Archifruits.ai sells the shortcut: upload a photo, pick a style, get a render. The tool was founded in November 2025, and within a year it had collected $74,450 in all-time revenue ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/archifruits-ai)). Its monthly numbers, though, show a product past its first peak.

## What does Archifruits.ai do for architects?

Archifruits.ai turns ordinary photos into polished renders. The company-written listing says users upload up to 3 images, choose a style and receive before-and-after visuals, for architects, architectural designers and visualization professionals ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/archifruits-ai)).

> "Transform interior and exterior photos into professional architectural renders with Archifruits AI." — Archifruits.ai listing ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/archifruits-ai))

The product is B2B. A designer uses it to show a client a concept quickly, before paying for a full render.

## How much revenue does Archifruits.ai make one year in?

Archifruits.ai had $4,333 MRR and 101 active subscriptions in November 2026, with $3,170 collected in the last 30 days and $74,450 all time ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/archifruits-ai)). Stripe verifies the figures.

## How did Archifruits.ai build its product so fast?

The stack is short. The listing names React Native and Next.js on the front end and Stripe on the back end ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/archifruits-ai)). React Native suggests a mobile app alongside the web tool, which suits designers taking photos on site. The image models behind the renders are not named.

Marketing channels are not listed. The founder, Fethullah Ekşi, has about 1,200 followers on X, enough for a launch audience but not a growth engine on its own.

## How does Archifruits.ai compare with Interior AI and Virtual Staging AI?

Archifruits.ai works in the same space as [Interior AI](/cases/interior-ai/) and [Virtual Staging AI](/cases/virtual-staging-ai/): image models that restyle rooms and buildings. The buyers differ. Interior AI targets homeowners and general users, and Virtual Staging AI sells to real estate agents.

Our observation: Archifruits.ai picks a narrower professional buyer, architects, which supports a higher price per subscription but a smaller market. The sharp gap between all-time revenue and current MRR also shows how fast image-AI tools can spike and then settle as rivals copy the feature.

## What takeaways does Archifruits.ai offer image-AI founders?

- **Pick a professional niche.** Selling to architects gives Archifruits.ai about $43 per subscription.
- **Launches spike, then settle.** $74,450 all time against $4,333 MRR shows a strong early peak ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/archifruits-ai)).
- **Simple input wins.** Three photos and a style choice replace hours of 3D modelling.
- **Plan for copycats.** Image models are shared tools, so differentiation must come from workflow and audience.
