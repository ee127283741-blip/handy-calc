Marea Alcalina reports $369 in monthly recurring revenue, a figure that would place it near the bottom of any MRR ranking. Yet the Mexican company collected $6,542 in the last 30 days and has taken in $383,857 since October 2020 ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/marea-alcalina)). It has been running for six years, and most of its money does not arrive as subscriptions.

## What does Marea Alcalina build for Latin American businesses?

Marea Alcalina creates online stores instantly using AI. Businesses publish a digital catalog; customers view it, choose products and place orders. The listing says it simplifies catalogs, orders, payments and inventory for businesses in Latin America ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/marea-alcalina)).

> "Create digital catalogs without complications." — Marea Alcalina value proposition on TrustMRR ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/marea-alcalina))

The site is mareaalcalina.com, with an Ahrefs Domain Rating of 17 out of 100 shown on the listing. Stripe handles payments. Pricing and team size are not disclosed.

## What lessons does Marea Alcalina offer e-commerce tool builders?

- **MRR is not the whole business.** $369 MRR next to $6,542 in 30-day revenue shows most income comes from elsewhere.
- **Regional focus can last.** A product built for Latin American businesses has been selling since 2020.
- **Simple promises sell.** The pitch is a catalog without complications, not a long feature list.
- **Disclose the mix.** Without pricing or a revenue split, buyers and readers have to guess where the money comes from.
