Jack Friks built an audience before he built Post Bridge. By November 2026 his X account had 186.9k followers, and the scheduler he launched in September 2024 showed $49,650 in monthly recurring revenue and $574,190 collected in total ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/post-bridge)). It is a tidy example of a creator building the tool creators keep asking for.

## What problem does Post Bridge solve for creators?

Post Bridge publishes one post to many social accounts at once. The homepage headline is "Post to all your social accounts from one dashboard", and it supports 10 platforms: X, Instagram, LinkedIn, Facebook, TikTok, YouTube, Bluesky, Threads, Pinterest and Google Business ([founder-reported, Post Bridge homepage](https://www.post-bridge.com)).

> "Publish everywhere in 30 seconds, not 30 minutes." — Post Bridge homepage ([founder-reported, Post Bridge](https://www.post-bridge.com))

The product goes beyond copy-paste posting. Paid plans include scheduling, carousel posts, bulk video scheduling, API access and an "MCP agent connect" feature that lets AI agents post through Post Bridge.

## Is Post Bridge priced per account or per seat?

Post Bridge prices by the number of social accounts, not by user. Marketer costs $39 a month for 15 accounts, and Operator costs $99 a month for 99 accounts plus $1 for each extra account ([founder-reported, Post Bridge homepage](https://www.post-bridge.com)). Only Operator adds team member invites.

The average subscription is worth about $30.33 a month ([calc, from TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/post-bridge)), below the $39 Marketer price. Yearly billing, which the homepage says gives two months free, and first-month discounts are the likely reasons, though the split is not disclosed. Free users get 5 posts a month.

## How did Jack Friks grow Post Bridge without a sales team?

Jack Friks grew Post Bridge mostly through his own audience. The TrustMRR listing shows 186.9k followers on his X account and a Domain Rating of 55 for the site ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/post-bridge)), and the homepage lists "founder support" as a plan feature.

That last detail matters. Selling to creators while being a visible creator yourself turns every build-in-public post into marketing, and lets customers talk to the maker directly. The tech stack is light too: Next.js on the front end, Supabase and Stripe on the back end.

## How does Post Bridge compare with Postiz and Leadmore AI?

Post Bridge and Postiz sell the same core job, cross-posting and scheduling, but Postiz is much larger by revenue. Leadmore AI adds a third angle from the marketing category: AI that creates the content rather than distributes it.

Our observation: Postiz used open source as its distribution channel, while Post Bridge used its founder's personal audience. Both are verified on Stripe, both sit in a market with large incumbents, and both show that a narrow, cheaper scheduler can still find thousands of paying users.

## What lessons does Post Bridge teach audience-led founders?

- **Build for the audience you already have.** A creator with 186.9k X followers built a tool for creators ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/post-bridge)).
- **Price on the unit customers count.** Post Bridge charges by social account, which scales with agencies without per-seat friction.
- **Keep the plan list short.** Two paid plans at $39 and $99 make the choice easy.
- **Add hooks for AI agents early.** API access and MCP agent connect put Post Bridge where automated posting is heading.
