Every developer building on large language models eventually asks the same question: which API is cheapest for this job? Price Per Token answers it with a live comparison table, and it has started charging for that data. In November 2026 it showed $2,300 in MRR and $7,400 in all-time revenue ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/price-per-token)), from just three active subscriptions.

## What data does Price Per Token track for LLM developers?

Price Per Token tracks pricing for major LLM APIs, including OpenAI, Anthropic, Google and Mistral. The company-written listing says it provides live LLM pricing and benchmarks for AI agents, for AI developers and companies using LLM APIs, with about 600 users ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/price-per-token)).

> "The best place to compare LLM APIs." — Price Per Token listing ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/price-per-token))

## How does Price Per Token make $2,300 a month from three customers?

Price Per Token had $2,300 in MRR in November 2026, the same amount it collected in the prior 30 days, from 3 active subscriptions ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/price-per-token)). All-time revenue was $7,400. TrustMRR checks these numbers through a Stripe API key.

MRR divided by three subscriptions is about $767 each ([calc, from TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/price-per-token)). That is far above a consumer price, which suggests B2B buyers paying for data access, but the plans behind it are not disclosed. The listing says only that users can get started for free.

## Why does Price Per Token's free table matter?

The free comparison table is the marketing. A Domain Rating of 54 out of 100 on the listing is high for a site founded in August 2025, and developers searching for model prices land on it ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/price-per-token)). Marketing channels and team size are not disclosed. Founder Alex Ellman shows 240 followers on X, so traffic did not come from a personal audience. The stack is Nuxt on the front end with Django, Render and Stripe behind it.

## How does Price Per Token compare with TypingMind and Simple Analytics?

All three are tools that sell to developers and technical founders, with very different shapes.

| Company | What it sells | Customer count shape |
|---|---|---|
| Price Per Token | LLM pricing data | 3 subscriptions |
| [TypingMind](/cases/typingmind/) | Chat interface for many models | Many individual buyers |
| [Simple Analytics](/cases/simple-analytics/) | Privacy-first web analytics | Over a thousand subscriptions |

Our observation: TypingMind and Simple Analytics earn from many small customers. Price Per Token earns from a few large ones, using a free public table to find them. That is closer to a data business than a SaaS tool.

## What lessons does Price Per Token hold for data sites?

- **Free data, paid access.** A public comparison table can feed a handful of high-value subscriptions.
- **Pick a fast-changing dataset.** LLM prices move often, so up-to-date data has value.
- **Three customers can be a business.** $2,300 of MRR came from 3 subscriptions.
- **Concentration cuts both ways.** Losing one customer would remove about a third of MRR.
