AbMaxx borrows the "maxxing" language of looks apps and points it at one muscle group. Founded in March 2026, it showed $21,516 in MRR, 6,001 active subscriptions and $195,994 in all-time revenue by November ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/abmaxx)). Its founder's message on the same page is unusually frank: the app works, and the founder no longer has time for it.

## How does the AbMaxx app analyze your abs?

AbMaxx uses AI to analyze a user's abs and then builds a plan tailored to their physique and goals ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/abmaxx)). The plan covers customized workouts, guidance and progress tracking, and it adapts as the user progresses.

The listing frames the problem against generic fitness apps: most give everyone the same routine, while AbMaxx says it targets weak areas and builds on strong ones. The app is built in SwiftUI with RevenueCat for subscriptions, and it showed a 4.7 App Store rating from 2,230 ratings at the time of the snapshot.

AbMaxx made $21,516 in MRR in November 2026, and $40,105 in the 30 days before the snapshot ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/abmaxx)). All-time revenue was $195,994 after about eight months.

## Why is the founder of AbMaxx stepping back?

The founder, who signs as Lino, says time is the problem, not the product. The message on TrustMRR says the app is running and ready to grow, but the founder is busy with other projects ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/abmaxx)). The page sits among startups for sale, though an asking price for AbMaxx is not disclosed.

> "We're the #1 app for abs and there's basically no competition in this space." — Lino ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/abmaxx))

That claim is the founder's own, and we haven't checked it against App Store rankings. The marketing listed is short-form social and paid: Instagram, TikTok and Meta ads. A buyer would be taking over a working ad-driven funnel, so the economics of those ads matter more than anything else, and they are not disclosed.

## What lessons does AbMaxx offer app builders?

- **Narrow beats broad.** An app about one muscle group reached $21,516 in MRR within eight months ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/abmaxx)).
- **Borrow a cultural hook.** The "maxxing" name ties AbMaxx to a trend its audience already knows.
- **Analysis before plan.** Scoring the user first makes the personalized plan feel earned.
- **Founder time is a constraint.** A working app can still go up for sale when the founder has too many projects.
