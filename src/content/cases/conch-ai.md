Conch AI revenue jumped to $500K in one month because of one video made by someone who did not build the product. Daniel Kwon's college friends had built Conch AI, a Chrome extension for students, and asked him to market it. He posted a TikTok, told his co-founder it would reach 10 million views, and it reached about 150 million. That single video drove Conch AI to $500K in a month, The Superwall Podcast reports ([founder-reported, The Superwall Podcast](https://www.buzzsprout.com/2522779/episodes/18141051-daniel-kwon-he-made-2-2m-from-building-simple-apps-here-s-how-no-code)).

## What does Conch AI do for students?

Conch AI is an AI writing and study tool for students that began as a Chrome extension. Its Product Hunt page lists features in its own words:

> "never get writer's block again, humanize AI-written text" — Conch AI's Product Hunt description ([founder-reported, Product Hunt](https://www.producthunt.com/products/conch-ai))

The same description mentions flashcards and notes from files or live recordings and chatting with files. Latka lists Tejas Sharma as CEO and describes the company as bootstrapped ([Latka](https://getlatka.com/companies/getconch.ai)). Pricing is not disclosed in the sources we used.

## How did Daniel Kwon join Conch AI without a tech background?

Two college friends asked Kwon to help market their startup around late 2022 or early 2023. In a Korean interview he said he knew nothing about tech at the time but wanted to try something new ([maily.so](https://maily.so/josh/posts/vpzl889xrk9)). A Chinese-language analysis on Woshipm describes the split: the friends built the Chrome extension and Kwon handled content promotion ([Woshipm](https://www.woshipm.com/operate/6412539.html)). His skill was making videos people watch.

## How much revenue did Conch AI make from one TikTok?

One TikTok of about 150 million views drove Conch AI to $500K in one month, according to The Superwall Podcast ([founder-reported, The Superwall Podcast](https://www.buzzsprout.com/2522779/episodes/18141051-daniel-kwon-he-made-2-2m-from-building-simple-apps-here-s-how-no-code)). Across Conch AI and two later apps, Kwon's products have generated $2.2M. Monthly recurring revenue and Conch AI's total revenue are not disclosed. The Woshipm analysis says the product was later sold, without naming a buyer or price.

## Why did a university threaten to sue Conch AI?

The Superwall Podcast says a university threatened to sue over Conch AI ([The Superwall Podcast](https://www.buzzsprout.com/2522779/episodes/18141051-daniel-kwon-he-made-2-2m-from-building-simple-apps-here-s-how-no-code)). Woshipm describes a lawyer's letter about the use of a school logo and about the tool's AI-detection bypass feature, and says the product later shifted toward broader student writing and research ([Woshipm](https://www.woshipm.com/operate/6412539.html)). Our reading: the Product Hunt line "humanize AI-written text" helps explain why schools objected.

## How does Conch AI compare with Jenni AI and Turbo AI?

[Jenni AI](/cases/jenni-ai/) and [Turbo AI](/cases/turbo-ai/) also sell AI writing and study help to students. Jenni AI narrowed to academic writing after seeing which users stayed; Turbo AI grew into a large note-taking app and says it has been profitable throughout. Conch AI's story is about distribution: one marketer's video, not product changes, produced its biggest month. [Coconote](/cases/coconote/) is another student AI app whose co-founder describes himself as not especially technical.

Our reading: in student AI, a viral video can create a huge month, but features that look like cheating invite legal and reputational risk that more careful rivals avoid.

## What can non-technical co-founders learn from Conch AI?

- **Distribution is a co-founder skill.** Kwon's video did more for revenue than any feature.
- **Expect to be wrong about virality.** He predicted 10 million views and got about 150 million.
- **Features carry risk.** An AI-detection bypass drew a university's legal threat.
- **One big month is not MRR.** Conch AI's $500K month is not a recurring figure.
