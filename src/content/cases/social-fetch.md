Scraping social media is a maintenance job that never ends: platforms change their pages, block proxies and break parsers. Social Fetch charges developers to make that someone else's problem. The UK API, founded in March 2026, collected $9,971 in the last 30 days and $25,850 since launch by November 2026 ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/social-fetch)). Its last-30-day cash was almost twice its MRR, which says a lot about how API buyers pay.

## What data does the Social Fetch API return?

Social Fetch returns profiles, posts, comments, videos, transcripts, metrics and engagement signals from TikTok, Instagram, YouTube, X, LinkedIn, Facebook, Reddit and more. The listing says one API covers 240 endpoints on 25 platforms and handles proxies and platform changes ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/social-fetch)).

> "No scraper maintenance. We handle proxies, browser changes, and platform breakages." — Social Fetch on TrustMRR ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/social-fetch))

The company also reports 99.8% uptime and an MCP server for Claude, Cursor and other AI agents. It names reputation management, brand protection, media monitoring, influencer marketing and research teams as users.

Social Fetch had $5,323 in MRR in November 2026 from 124 active subscriptions, with $9,971 collected in the last 30 days and $25,850 all time ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/social-fetch)).

## Why does Social Fetch sell credit packs as well as subscriptions?

Social Fetch sells both because API usage is lumpy. The listing offers 100 free credits, then packs of $14 for 1,000 credits, $47 for 25,000 and $379 for 230,000, alongside monthly subscriptions ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/social-fetch)).

A research team pulling data for one project wants a pack; a monitoring tool polling every day wants a plan. Marketing runs through SEO, a blog, Google Ads and YouTube Ads. One oddity: the team size field says 2 to 5 people, while the additional info says the product is built and run solo.

## How does Social Fetch compare with Image-Charts and BestTime?

Social Fetch, Image-Charts and BestTime are all data APIs sold to developers, but they are at very different stages.

Our observation: Image-Charts and BestTime sell data they generate or compute themselves. Social Fetch sells access to other platforms' data, which carries more platform risk, because any of the 25 sources can change or block it. The upside is speed: Social Fetch reached $25,850 all time within about eight months of its March 2026 founding.

## What are the lessons from Social Fetch for API founders?

- **Sell the maintenance, not the data.** The pitch is no scraper upkeep, which is the real cost developers want gone.
- **MRR can understate a usage business.** $9,971 in 30-day cash against $5,323 MRR shows how much comes from packs ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/social-fetch)).
- **Offer both packs and plans.** One-off projects and always-on monitoring buy differently.
- **Ship for AI agents early.** An MCP server puts the API inside Claude and Cursor workflows.
