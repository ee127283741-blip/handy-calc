Heavy AI users often pay for three or four chatbots at once. Mono bets they would rather pay for one. A year after its November 2025 founding, the Swiss app showed $10,855 in MRR and 393 active subscriptions ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/mono)), built by one person. It is a small, steady business in one of the most crowded categories in AI.

## What problem does Mono solve for multi-model AI users?

Mono puts leading AI models in one subscription and picks a model automatically. The company-written listing names models from OpenAI, Gemini, Claude, Perplexity and others, plus cross-model memory, research, project collaboration, custom skills, plugins and privacy features ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/mono)). The problem it names is juggling separate tools and subscriptions.

> "The world's best AI in one place" — Mono listing ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/mono))

## How much revenue does Mono make a month?

Mono had $10,855 in MRR in November 2026 and collected $10,254 in the prior 30 days ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/mono)). All-time revenue was $85,512 across 393 active subscriptions. TrustMRR checks these numbers through a Stripe API key.

MRR per subscription is about $27.62 ([calc, from TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/mono)), between the base and Pro plans. Thirty-day revenue close to MRR suggests mostly monthly billing. Profit is not disclosed, and model costs are the obvious pressure on margin for a reseller of other companies' models.

## How did Jonas Kamber build Mono as a solo founder?

The listing shows a bootstrapped company of one in Switzerland, with 921 followers on X, so Mono did not start from a big audience ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/mono)). Marketing channels are not disclosed. The stack is plain: JavaScript, CSS and HTML5 on the front end, Node.js and Stripe behind it. The audience is tagged both B2B and B2C, covering research, writing, coding, analysis and image generation.

## How does Mono compare with TypingMind and Magai?

Mono sits in the same "many models, one interface" niche as two other cases on this site.

| Company | Approach |
|---|---|
| Mono | Hosted subscription with automatic model selection |
| [TypingMind](/cases/typingmind/) | Chat front end for many models |
| [Magai](/cases/magai/) | Multi-model AI workspace subscription |

Our observation: the differentiator in this niche is no longer model access, which everyone offers, but the layer on top. Mono leans on automatic model choice and memory shared across models, which is harder for a single model vendor to copy.

## What lessons does Mono teach about aggregator AI apps?

- **Bundling is a real product.** 393 subscribers paid to avoid juggling AI subscriptions.
- **Price ladders lift the average.** MRR per subscription sits above the $20 entry plan.
- **No audience needed to start.** A founder with under 1,000 X followers reached $85,512 all time.
- **Margins are the open question.** Profit is not disclosed, and model costs sit underneath every plan.
