Pregnant women are told to avoid some skincare ingredients, but product labels do not say which bottles are safe. MamaSkin answers that one question product by product. In November 2026 the app had $4,097 in monthly recurring revenue and $33,266 in all-time revenue ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/mamaskin-pregnancy-skincare)), about a year after it was founded. It is a small app aimed at a clear moment in life.

## What does the MamaSkin app check for pregnant users?

MamaSkin rates skincare and beauty products for safety during pregnancy and breastfeeding. The company-written TrustMRR listing says it analyses real products rather than single ingredients, against a database of 85,000+ products, and gives clear risk ratings instead of generic AI advice ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/mamaskin-pregnancy-skincare)).

The homepage offers iPhone and Android apps, a web checker and an option to open it in ChatGPT. It shows an app rating of 4.5 out of 5 and user feedback from mothers in the UK ([founder-reported, MamaSkin homepage](https://mamaskin.app/)).

> "Pregnancy and breastfeeding skincare, without the guesswork." — MamaSkin listing ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/mamaskin-pregnancy-skincare))

## Why does MamaSkin give product search away for free?

The free tier covers unlimited product search, and Pro adds alerts when a saved product's formula changes ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/mamaskin-pregnancy-skincare)). That keeps the core safety answer free, which builds trust with worried parents, and charges for ongoing monitoring of products they keep using.

The stack is React Native on the front end with Supabase, RevenueCat and Vercel behind it. No marketing channels are listed, but the homepage points to press interviews and a brand that cited MamaSkin's assessment.

## What lessons does MamaSkin hold for niche consumer apps?

- **Own one anxious question.** MamaSkin answers "is this product safe while pregnant" and little else.
- **Free answers, paid monitoring.** Search is free; Pro charges for alerts on saved products.
- **Built-in churn shapes growth.** The audience changes every year, so acquisition never stops.
- **App revenue can be verified too.** RevenueCat verification gives an iOS and Android app the same proof as a Stripe-billed SaaS.
