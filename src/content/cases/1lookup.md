Most AI startups on revenue leaderboards sell something a person looks at. 1Lookup sells an answer a machine reads: is this phone number real, is this email deliverable, is this IP suspicious? In November 2026 that plain job showed $256,122 in MRR and $5,807,584 in all-time revenue ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/1lookup)), from a company TrustMRR dates to January 2022.

## What does the 1Lookup API actually check?

The 1Lookup API checks phone numbers, email addresses and IP addresses in real time, and returns the result to the software that asked. The company's own line on TrustMRR is short.

> "The #1 tool for validating phone, email and IP data in real time." — 1Lookup listing ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/1lookup))

The same listing says 76 data products sit behind one API and that each phone, email or IP check costs one credit. The "#1" claim is the company's own, and we have not seen a ranking that backs it. Typical buyers are teams that clean sign-up forms or lead lists before sales outreach, which is why we file 1Lookup under sales and lead generation.

## How is 1Lookup priced per lookup?

1Lookup charges a monthly plan that includes a fixed number of credits, and the price per lookup falls as the plan grows. Every plan starts with a 7-day free trial, and annual billing gives two months free.

| Starter | $99 | 20,000 | $0.0050 | [founder-reported, 1Lookup pricing](https://www.1lookup.io/pricing) |

Custom plans above one million credits start at $2,000 a month, according to the [pricing page](https://www.1lookup.io/pricing). Price displays on the page cards were garbled when we checked, so the table uses the figures in the trial text.

## How much revenue does 1Lookup make each month?

1Lookup had $256,122 in MRR from 721 active subscriptions in November 2026, and $484,429 in revenue over the last 30 days ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/1lookup)). TrustMRR verifies the figures with a Stripe API key.

| Figure (Nov 2026) | Value | Source |
|---|---|---|
| MRR | $256,122 | [verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/1lookup) |
| Revenue, last 30 days | $484,429 | [verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/1lookup) |

Two numbers stand out. MRR divided by subscriptions is about $355 per account, which sits between the Growth and Professional plans ([calc, from TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/1lookup)). And 30-day revenue is almost twice MRR. The page does not explain the gap; one-off credit purchases or annual payments would fit, but that is our guess, not a disclosed fact. Team size and profit are not disclosed.

## Why does 1Lookup earn so much from so few customers?

1Lookup earns a lot per customer because it sells to businesses that run checks at volume, not to individuals. 721 subscriptions is a small count next to consumer apps with thousands of subscribers, yet it supports a quarter of a million dollars of MRR. Usage pricing lets a single customer grow from $99 to $1,999 a month without a sales call, and the page lists a dedicated customer success manager only at the top tier. Robby Frank, the founder of 1Lookup per TrustMRR, has given no interview we could find, so the reasons behind these choices are not disclosed.

## How does 1Lookup compare with Instantly and Gojiberry AI?

1Lookup sits underneath outbound sales tools rather than competing with them, and its Stripe-verified figure is larger than either of these two founder-reported ones. The dates differ, so read the table as scale, not a race.

| Company | What it sells | Figure | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1Lookup | Phone, email and IP validation API | $256,122 MRR (Nov 2026) | [verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/1lookup) |
| [Instantly](/cases/instantly/) | Cold email sending at scale | ~$200K/mo (Oct 2022) | [founder-reported, Latka](https://getlatka.com/companies/instantly-ai/interviews/instantly-ai-raul-kaevand-2022) |
| [Gojiberry AI](/cases/gojiberry-ai/) | AI lead finding for B2B sales | $27K MRR (Dec 2025) | [founder-reported, Indie Hackers](https://www.indiehackers.com/post/tech/validating-via-services-before-building-a-27k-mrr-product-7FrT6FXChPOiUK4PbXFp) |

Our observation: Instantly and Gojiberry AI sell outcomes a salesperson can see, such as sent emails and found leads. 1Lookup sells hygiene that protects those outcomes, a bad number or email filtered out before anyone sends. That makes it less visible but also less tied to one channel. If cold email rules tighten, validation tends to matter more, not less.

## What lessons does 1Lookup hold for API founders?

- **Boring infrastructure can out-earn flashy apps.** A validation API showed $256,122 MRR with 721 subscriptions ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/1lookup)).
- **Credits make upgrades automatic.** Four tiers from $99 to $1,999 a month let usage, not negotiation, move a customer up.
- **Bundle many small data sources.** The listing claims 76 data products behind one API, which turns many vendors into one bill for the buyer.
- **Watch the gap between MRR and cash.** 30-day revenue near twice MRR suggests non-recurring sales matter here, so MRR alone understates the business.
