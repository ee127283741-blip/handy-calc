A beginner trader opens a gold chart and has no idea where to enter or where to put a stop. Setup Ai answers that with a full trade plan from a screenshot. The iPhone app launched in September 2026, and within about two months it had 133 active subscriptions and $3,364 in monthly recurring revenue ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/setup-ai-chart-analysis)).

## What does Setup Ai produce from a chart?

Setup Ai turns any forex, gold, crypto or stock chart into a trade plan: trend, key zones, entry, stop-loss and take-profit, with an AI explanation of the reasoning ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/setup-ai-chart-analysis)). It is built for beginner and intermediate retail traders and is available in English, Russian and Ukrainian.

> "Built for beginner and intermediate traders who want to understand the market, not copy signals." — Setup Ai company description ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/setup-ai-chart-analysis))

The app uses Expo and React Native, a Python and PostgreSQL back end, and RevenueCat for subscriptions. Nothing on the listing is a promise of trading results, and this case is not financial advice.

## Why does Setup Ai push a weekly plan?

Setup Ai charges $8.99 a week or $17.99 a month on the US App Store, and only the weekly plan has a free trial ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/setup-ai-chart-analysis)). Weekly billing earns more per month from users who stay, and the trial lowers the first step for curious beginners. The average subscription is worth about $25 a month (calc: $3,364 / 133, [from TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/setup-ai-chart-analysis)), above the monthly price, which suggests many users sit on the weekly plan.

Its listed channels are app store optimization, Threads, TikTok, Instagram and YouTube, all short-form channels where chart screenshots travel well.

Our observation: Heron Copier and AltIndex serve traders who already have a method. Setup Ai sells to beginners who do not, which is a bigger and more impulsive market, and a weekly subscription fits it. The risk is churn once beginners either learn the skill or stop trading.

## What should app founders learn from Setup Ai?

- **Start from a specific moment of confusion.** "Where do I enter?" is a question every new trader asks.
- **Weekly plans suit impulse audiences.** Revenue per subscription sits above the monthly price.
- **Localisation opens markets.** Russian and Ukrainian support reach traders that English-only apps miss.
- **Speed is visible in verified data.** $4,554 all time within about two months of launch ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/setup-ai-chart-analysis)).
