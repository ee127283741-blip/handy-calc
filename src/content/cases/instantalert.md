On Vinted, the best second-hand deals disappear within minutes of being listed. InstantAlert sells those few minutes back to buyers. A year after its November 2025 founding, the Dutch app showed $1,272 in monthly recurring revenue from 119 subscriptions ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/instantalert)), with most of its $3,958 lifetime revenue earned recently.

## What problem does InstantAlert solve for Vinted shoppers?

InstantAlert watches Vinted for you. Users set what they want, and the app sends a notification the moment a matching item appears ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/instantalert)). The listing names resellers, collectors and bargain shoppers as the audience.

> "Get instant notifications for new items listed on Vinted. Never miss a deal again!" — InstantAlert listing ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/instantalert))

For a reseller, speed is margin: the first message to a seller often gets the underpriced item.

## How much does InstantAlert earn in its first year?

InstantAlert had $1,272 MRR and 119 active subscriptions in November 2026, with $1,210 collected in the last 30 days and $3,958 all time ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/instantalert)). The figures come through a RevenueCat API key, the usual billing layer for mobile subscriptions.

## How does InstantAlert find users?

The listing names four channels: a blog, Reddit, TikTok and Instagram ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/instantalert)). Reseller communities on Reddit and TikTok are where people trade Vinted tips, so the channels match the buyers. The listing counts about 2,332 users. The founder, Lex Mulier, has about a dozen followers on X, so personal reach played no part.

The app is built with React Native, Expo, React and Next.js, with AWS, Python, Supabase and RevenueCat behind it. Python is a common choice for the polling jobs that check for new listings.

## How does InstantAlert compare with Store Stock Checker and SoldComps?

InstantAlert belongs with [Store Stock Checker](/cases/store-stock-checker/), which watches retail stock, and [SoldComps](/cases/soldcomps/), which helps resellers price items. All three sell information advantage to people buying or flipping goods.

Our observation: InstantAlert is tied to one marketplace, Vinted, which makes the pitch sharp but adds platform risk if Vinted changes how listings can be watched. Its tiered plans up to €27.99 a month ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/instantalert)) also show that serious resellers will pay more for faster or wider alerts.

## What takeaways does InstantAlert leave for builders of niche alert apps?

- **Speed is a product.** Being first to a new listing is worth a subscription to resellers.
- **One platform, one promise.** Focusing on Vinted makes the value obvious in a sentence.
- **Tier by intensity.** Plans from €4.99 to €27.99 separate casual shoppers from professional flippers.
- **Small and growing still counts.** $1,272 MRR within a year, from a founder with no audience ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/instantalert)).
