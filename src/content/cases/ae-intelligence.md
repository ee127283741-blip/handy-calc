About a million people use a tool whose whole job is to make AI writing look less like AI writing. That tool, Humanize AI Text, belongs to AE-intelligence, a holding company that Dilyar Buzan founded in the United Arab Emirates in June 2024. By November 2026 it had earned $865,558 in total ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/ae-intelligence)), which puts it among the larger AI writing businesses on this site.

## What does AE-intelligence's Humanize AI Text do?

Humanize AI Text rewrites AI-generated content so it reads like natural human writing while keeping the original meaning and tone. The listing says it is built to help content pass AI detectors and serves writers, students, marketers and businesses ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/ae-intelligence)).

> "AE-intelligence is a holding company for few business." — AE-intelligence company description ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/ae-intelligence))

The page also says the product supports multiple languages, needs no login for free use and is rated 4.3 out of 5 on Chrome.

## Why does a free unlimited plan still convert at AE-intelligence?

The free plan offers unlimited words, yet 1,123 people pay. With about a million stated users, only around 0.1% hold a subscription (calc: 1,123 / 1,000,000). A free unlimited tier acts as a search and Chrome-store magnet, and the paid tier only has to catch a sliver of heavy users. The paid plan details are not disclosed on the page.

## How does AE-intelligence differ from other AI writing tools?

AE-intelligence rewrites text that already exists, while the two comparison cases generate new text.

Our observation: a humanizer rides on top of every other AI writing tool, so its market grows as generators spread. That also ties it to the arms race with AI detectors, a dependency the generators do not share in the same way.

## What are the main lessons from AE-intelligence?

- **Build a layer on top of a trend.** Humanize AI Text grows with every AI writing tool.
- **A generous free tier drives scale.** Unlimited free words helped reach about a million users.
- **Small conversion can still pay.** 1,123 subscriptions carry $29,035 MRR.
- **Holding-company numbers blur the picture.** Per-product revenue is not disclosed.
