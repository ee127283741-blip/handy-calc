Most AI app builders end up with a drawer full of accounts: one for an image model, one for video, one for music, each with its own SDK and invoice. Apiframe sells the way out of that drawer. In November 2026 it showed $35,032 in MRR and $530,295 in all-time revenue ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/apiframe)), from a single API that fronts more than 70 generative media models.

## What problem does Apiframe solve for AI app developers?

Apiframe replaces many model providers with one integration. Its TrustMRR profile says the product removes the need to manage multiple provider accounts, SDKs and invoices ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/apiframe)). The audience it names is wide: AI content apps, ad creative tools, e-commerce visuals, short-form video tools, music apps, studios and agencies.

> "One API for AI image, video, and music generation." — Apiframe website ([founder-reported, Apiframe](https://apiframe.ai/))

The homepage claims 9,800+ developers and companies and lists models from ByteDance, MiniMax, Alibaba, OpenAI, Black Forest Labs and Suno ([founder-reported, Apiframe](https://apiframe.ai/)). TrustMRR adds that Apiframe is an official partner of BytePlus, Alibaba Cloud and MiniMax. The founder is listed only as Renaud, the company is based in Singapore, and headcount is not disclosed.

## How do Apiframe's credit plans work?

Apiframe charges for usage, not seats. Every plan includes the full catalogue, and what changes between tiers is the monthly credit allowance, concurrency and support ([founder-reported, Apiframe pricing](https://apiframe.ai/pricing)). Credits are one shared currency: each model spends them at its own rate, and credits are refunded automatically if a job fails.

| Plan | Price (Nov 2026) | Credits a month | Concurrent jobs | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Basic | $39 a month ($19 first month) | 4,000 | 5 | [founder-reported, Apiframe pricing](https://apiframe.ai/pricing) |
| Pro | $99 a month | 10,000 | 12 | [founder-reported, Apiframe pricing](https://apiframe.ai/pricing) |
| Growth | $399 a month | 40,000 | 50 | [founder-reported, Apiframe pricing](https://apiframe.ai/pricing) |
| Scale | $749 a month | 75,000 | 100 | [founder-reported, Apiframe pricing](https://apiframe.ai/pricing) |
| Enterprise | Custom | Custom | 200–500+ | [founder-reported, Apiframe pricing](https://apiframe.ai/pricing) |

Top-ups cost $0.01 a credit for paid teams ([founder-reported, Apiframe pricing](https://apiframe.ai/pricing)). A free trial gives 100 credits after a phone or $1 check, which the page says exists so free credits are not abused.

## How does Apiframe compare with Magai and Typing Mind?

All three resell access to other companies' models, but they sell to different buyers. Apiframe sells to developers through an API, while [Magai](/cases/magai/) and [Typing Mind](/cases/typingmind/) sell a chat interface to end users.

| Company | Buyer | Pricing model | Revenue figure | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Apiframe | Developers building media apps | Monthly credits from $39 | $35,032 MRR (Nov 2026) | [verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/apiframe) |
| [Magai](/cases/magai/) | People who want many chat models in one app | One subscription, tiers by usage | Nearly $100K MRR (Feb 2026) | [founder-reported, Indie Hackers](https://www.indiehackers.com/post/tech/from-broke-to-100k-mrr-in-four-years-hS1wen0srjGNIWZxSnV9) |
| [Typing Mind](/cases/typingmind/) | Users who bring their own API key | One-time licences | Not disclosed | [media-reported, Lemon Squeezy](https://lemonsqueezy.com/case-study/typing-mind) |

Our observation: the aggregator idea works at both ends of the stack, but the economics differ. Typing Mind pushes model costs onto the user's own key. Magai and Apiframe carry model costs and price by volume. Apiframe adds a twist the others do not describe: official partnerships with model makers, which the homepage uses as a selling point for new video models.

## What can founders learn from Apiframe's API business?

- **Sell the integration, not the model.** Apiframe owns no flagship model, yet one key across 70+ models earned $530,295 to date ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/apiframe)).
- **Price in one currency.** A shared credit balance lets a customer switch from an image model to a video model without a new contract.
- **Guard the free tier.** A phone or $1 check before 100 free credits is a cheap fix for abuse in a business with real compute costs.
- **Watch revenue beyond MRR.** Last-30-day revenue above MRR shows that usage on top of plans can matter as much as the subscriptions themselves.
