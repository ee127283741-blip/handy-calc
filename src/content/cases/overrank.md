Freelance writers and agencies charge by the article or by the month. overrank charges one flat monthly price and promises an article a day, written and published without a human touching it. Seven months after an April 2026 founding, that offer showed $12,703 in monthly recurring revenue and $77,532 in all-time revenue ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/overrank-seo)).

## What does overrank automate for small businesses?

overrank automates the whole content loop: keyword research, writing, images, meta tags, internal links and publishing. The homepage says it writes and publishes keyword-targeted articles to a customer's site and works with every website platform, including custom HTML ([founder-reported, overrank homepage](https://overrank.ai/)). It also advertises a free audit, a Google Maps product and an affiliate program.

The pitch is aimed at owners who know SEO works but cannot spend hours per post. The homepage says the average blog post takes more than four hours to produce.

> "Let AI write & publish SEO content for you. Every day. Forever." — overrank listing ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/overrank-seo))

overrank had $12,703 in MRR and 311 active subscriptions in November 2026. It collected $11,336 over the previous 30 days and $77,532 all time ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/overrank-seo)).

## Which channels does overrank use to sell SEO?

The listing names nine channels: content marketing, a blog, SEO, Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, YouTube, Google Ads and Meta Ads ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/overrank-seo)). That mix is unusual for an SEO tool. It pays for ads on top of ranking its own content, which suggests organic growth alone was not the plan.

The homepage claims 1,156+ verified businesses ([founder-reported, overrank homepage](https://overrank.ai/)), the same user count shown on TrustMRR. Customer quotes there compare the cost with freelance writers.

## How does overrank compare with SEObot and SEO Stack?

All three sell AI-assisted SEO content to small teams. overrank is the newest and the cheapest at entry.

| Company | Approach | MRR | Source |

Our observation: overrank is about the closest copy of the SEObot idea, a set-and-forget content engine, at a lower revenue level. The difference is distribution. overrank lists paid ads across Google and Meta, while the other two lean more on SEO and founder audiences.

## What are the takeaways from overrank's first months?

- **A flat price is easy to sell.** Average revenue per subscription sits near the $39 list price ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/overrank-seo)).
- **Late entrants can still sell.** A crowded AI SEO category did not stop a seven-month-old product from reaching 311 subscriptions.
- **Paid ads fill the gap.** An SEO product that buys traffic is admitting that rankings take time, even for itself.
- **Watch the 30-day line.** Revenue below MRR is an early signal to check churn before calling growth.
