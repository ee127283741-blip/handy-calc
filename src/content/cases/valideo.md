A maintenance technician finishes a job, then spends the evening typing up what was done. Valideo removes that evening. The French app, founded in May 2025, lets technicians send an intervention report from the field in under a minute, and by November 2026 it had $33,223 in all-time revenue and $4,514 in monthly recurring revenue ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/valideo)).

## What problem does Valideo solve for field technicians?

Valideo simplifies creating and sending intervention reports directly from the job site, which the company says eliminates the need for evening data entry ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/valideo)). It targets technicians in any sector: maintenance, energy, construction, industry, security, cleaning, control and inspection.

> "Your intervention report in less than 1min" — Valideo company description ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/valideo))

The app is built with Expo, so one codebase serves phones, and billing runs through Stripe.

## Which Valideo plan fits a solo technician or a team?

Valideo has four tiers: a free plan with 5 reports a month, Independent at €30 a month, Team at €69 and Pro at €99 ([founder-reported, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/valideo)). The free limit is low enough that anyone doing daily jobs hits it in a week, which pushes active technicians to a paid plan quickly.

## What lessons does Valideo offer B2B app builders?

- **Sell the hour you give back.** "No more evening data entry" is a concrete benefit any technician understands.
- **Keep the free tier tight.** Five reports a month lets people try it but not live on it.
- **Local markets still pay.** A French-language app reached $33,223 all time ([verified, TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/startup/valideo)).
- **Horizontal within a job type.** Targeting every sector that sends intervention reports widens the market without changing the product.
