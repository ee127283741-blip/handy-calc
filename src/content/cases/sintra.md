Sintra ARR reached about $12M roughly a year after launch, and the company started as a weekend project by people who ran marketing agencies. Sintra, based in Lithuania, sells small business owners a team of AI helpers for marketing, customer support, sales and other jobs. Tech.eu reported in June 2025 that it had over 40,000 paying customers as it raised a $17M seed round led by Earlybird ([founder-reported, Tech.eu](https://tech.eu/2025/06/10/lithuanian-ai-startup-sintra-secures-17m-seed-empowering-smbs-with-ai-helpers/)). Its pitch is an "AI business in the box" that a shop owner can set up in minutes.

## What are Sintra's AI helpers?

Sintra's AI helpers are specialised assistants, each focused on a job such as marketing, customer support or sales, that a small business owner can chat with to get work done ([Tech.eu](https://tech.eu/2025/06/10/lithuanian-ai-startup-sintra-secures-17m-seed-empowering-smbs-with-ai-helpers/)). The idea is that owners onboard a team of helpers rather than learn another software product. Pricing is not disclosed in the sources we used.

> "What small business owners need isn't just another software product – they need assistants that provide real help – and that feel natural and intuitive" — Chris Sidlauskas, Sintra CEO ([founder-reported, Tech.eu](https://tech.eu/2025/06/10/lithuanian-ai-startup-sintra-secures-17m-seed-empowering-smbs-with-ai-helpers/))

## Who started Sintra, and were they engineers?

Sintra's CEO, Chris Sidlauskas, came from marketing and e-commerce agencies, not engineering. Reports describe him and Rokas Judickas starting a marketing agency to help local small businesses as teenagers ([BeBeez](https://bebeez.eu/2025/06/10/global-impact-from-a-garage-how-lithuanian-ai-startup-raised-e14-9-million-to-help-smes-with-ai-teammates/)). The founding team is not consistent across sources: some also list Vasaris Kaveckas, described as a developer, and others name Titas Jurkonis. So Sintra is a company founded by agency operators with technical help, not a solo non-coder build.

## How did Sintra reach $12M ARR in about a year?

Sintra grew from a weekend project to about $12M in ARR in just over a year, Tech.eu reported ([founder-reported, Tech.eu](https://tech.eu/2025/06/10/lithuanian-ai-startup-sintra-secures-17m-seed-empowering-smbs-with-ai-helpers/)). The jump came after a relaunch: Sintra 2.0 launched in May 2024 and reached €877K, about $1M, in ARR 57 days later ([media-reported, BeBeez](https://bebeez.eu/2025/06/10/global-impact-from-a-garage-how-lithuanian-ai-startup-raised-e14-9-million-to-help-smes-with-ai-teammates/)). About $12M ARR is roughly $1M a month (calc). Total revenue is not disclosed.

## Why are Sintra's revenue figures reported in both dollars and euros?

The same June 2025 announcement was reported in both currencies. Tech.eu and ARR Club use about $12M ARR ([ARR Club](https://www.arr.club/sintra/sintra-arr-hit-12m-in-year-one-started-as-a-weekend-project)), while BeBeez reported €10.5M ARR and a €14.9M round ([BeBeez](https://bebeez.eu/2025/06/10/global-impact-from-a-garage-how-lithuanian-ai-startup-raised-e14-9-million-to-help-smes-with-ai-teammates/)). These look like the same numbers converted at different rates, not different growth. We use the dollar figures as reported and do not convert them.

## How does Sintra compare with Fyxer AI and Magai?

[Fyxer AI](/cases/fyxer-ai/) is the closest parallel: its founders ran an executive assistant agency, then turned that service into an AI assistant and grew ARR very fast. Sintra's founders also came from agencies serving small businesses. [Magai](/cases/magai/) bundles many AI models in one subscription for individuals, built by a non-technical founder on no-code tools. Sintra differs by packaging AI as job roles, a marketer or support agent, rather than as models or features.

Our reading: agency founders know exactly which tasks small clients pay people to do. Sintra turned those tasks into named helpers, which is easier to sell to a shop owner than "access to GPT".

## What lessons does Sintra hold for agency founders?

- **Sell roles, not models.** Sintra's helpers are named after jobs small businesses already pay for.
- **Relaunch when the first version stalls.** Sintra 2.0 reached about $1M ARR in 57 days.
- **Agency experience is customer research.** Years serving small clients shaped what Sintra built.
- **Check currencies before comparing.** $12M and €10.5M describe the same ARR.
