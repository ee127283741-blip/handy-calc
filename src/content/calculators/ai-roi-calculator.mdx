  - q: How many hours does AI save per week?
    a: It varies widely by role and task, and self-reported savings tend to be optimistic. Start with a conservative 1 hour per active user per week, then replace it with numbers you measure in a pilot, for example by timing the same task with and without the tool.
  - q: What is a loaded hourly cost?
    a: It is the full cost of an hour of an employee's time, including wages, payroll taxes, benefits and overhead, not just the hourly wage. If you only know salary, dividing annual salary by about 2,000 working hours gives the wage part.
  - q: Why are seats charged for everyone but savings only for adopters?
    a: That is the conservative case. Many teams buy seats for everyone while only some people use the tool regularly. If you only pay for active users, set adoption to 100% and the employee count to the number of active seats.

    a: Only if the time is used for other valuable work or reduces overtime or hiring. Treat the value figure as capacity freed up, not cash saved, unless you can point to a specific cost that goes down.

## Worked example: a 10-person office

Ten employees get a $25-a-month AI seat. Half of them use it regularly and each saves 1 hour a week. Their loaded cost is $40 an hour, and training and setup cost $1,000 once.

1. Active users: 10 × 50% = 5.
2. Hours saved per month: 5 × 1 hour × 52 ÷ 12 weeks = **21.7 hours**.
3. Monthly value: 21.67 × $40 = **$866.67**.
4. Tool cost: 10 × $25 = $250 a month, so net benefit = $866.67 − $250 = **$616.67 a month**.
5. First-year ROI: value $10,400 vs cost $250 × 12 + $1,000 = $4,000 → ($10,400 − $4,000) ÷ $4,000 = **160%**.
6. Payback: $1,000 ÷ $616.67 = **1.6 months**.

At 10% adoption the same setup loses money: 4.3 hours ($173.33) of value against $250 of seats.

## How the ROI calculation works

<code class="formula">hours saved per month = employees × adoption % × hours saved per week × 52 ÷ 12</code>

<code class="formula">net monthly benefit = hours saved × loaded hourly cost − employees × seat cost</code>

<code class="formula">first-year ROI % = (12 × monthly value − (12 × seat costs + setup)) ÷ (12 × seat costs + setup) × 100</code>

<code class="formula">payback months = setup cost ÷ net monthly benefit</code>

- Seats are paid for every employee; only the adopting share saves time.
- If net monthly benefit is zero or negative, the rollout never pays back.
- **Limitations:** it does not model quality gains or errors, ramp-up time, or price changes. Saved time is capacity, not guaranteed cash.
