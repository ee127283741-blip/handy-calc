## Worked example: a US cake recipe in a UK oven

A US recipe says to bake at **375 °F**, and your oven is marked in Celsius.

1. Subtract 32: 375 − 32 = 343.
2. Multiply by 5/9: 343 × 5 ÷ 9 = **190.6 °C**, so set the dial to **190 °C**.
3. Gas Mark: (375 − 250) ÷ 25 = **Gas Mark 5**.
4. For a fan oven, a common adjustment is 20 °C lower: about **170 °C**.

## How the conversion works

<code class="formula">°C = (°F − 32) × 5/9  ·  °F = °C × 9/5 + 32</code>

<code class="formula">Gas Mark n (1–10): °F = 250 + 25 × n  ·  Gas Mark ¼ = 225 °F, ½ = 250 °F</code>

- The Fahrenheit–Celsius formulas are exact, from the definitions of the two scales.
- The Gas Mark scale is a UK oven scale. Marks 1 to 10 rise in steps of 25 °F.
- **Limitations:** real ovens often run hotter or cooler than the dial. An oven thermometer is the best check.
