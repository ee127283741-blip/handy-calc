## Worked example: days until Christmas 2026

How many days are there from **Sunday, November 1, 2026** to **Friday, December 25, 2026**?

1. Days left in November after the 1st: 30 − 1 = 29.
2. Days in December up to the 25th: 25.
3. Total: 29 + 25 = **54 days**, which is 7 weeks and 5 days.
4. Weekdays: 7 full weeks contain 7 × 5 = 35 weekdays. The remaining 5 days (Sunday, December 20 to Thursday, December 24) include 4 weekdays, for **39 weekdays**.

## How the date calculation works

<code class="formula">days = end date − start date (+ 1 if both ends are counted)</code>

- Each date is converted to a count of days since a fixed reference date in the Gregorian calendar; the difference of the two counts is the answer.
- Weekdays are counted by checking each day in the range and skipping Saturdays and Sundays.
- If the end date is before the start date, the result is negative.
- **Limitations:** public holidays are not removed. In the US, see the OPM list of federal holidays; other countries publish their own lists.

## Days in each month (Gregorian calendar)

| Month | Days | Month | Days |
|---|---|---|---|
| January | 31 | July | 31 |

A common year has 365 days (52 weeks and 1 day); a leap year has 366.
