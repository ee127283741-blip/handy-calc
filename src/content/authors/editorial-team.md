The Editorial team is the group responsible for every prompt and tutorial on this site. We are not
presenting ourselves as accountants, financial advisers or employees of any AI provider, and we do
not claim credentials we do not hold.

**How we work.** Drafts of page text and code are produced with the help of AI tools (large language
models). Every published price is checked by hand against the provider's official pricing page and
stored with the date it was last verified; every formula is covered by automated tests that compare
its output with known answers.

**Corrections.** If you find an error or an outdated price, please see the Contact page. We fix
confirmed errors and update the "Last updated" date on the affected page.
