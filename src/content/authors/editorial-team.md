The Editorial team is the group responsible for every case on this site. We are not
presenting ourselves as accountants, financial advisers or employees of any AI provider, and we do
not claim credentials we do not hold.

**How we work.** Drafts of page text and code are produced with the help of AI tools (large language
models). Every published figure is checked against its cited source and
stored with the source date and a provenance label.

**Corrections.** If you find an error or an outdated figure, please see the Contact page. We fix
confirmed errors and update the "Last updated" date on the affected page.
