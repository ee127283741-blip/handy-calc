The Editorial team is the group responsible for every calculator and guide on this site. We are not
presenting ourselves as certified chefs, contractors or professional fiber artists, and we do not
claim credentials we do not hold.

**How we work.** Drafts of page text and code are produced with the help of AI tools (large language
models). Every published number, formula and reference value is then checked by hand against the
cited source (for example the King Arthur Baking ingredient weight chart, NIST unit definitions or the
Craft Yarn Council standards), and every calculator is covered by automated tests that compare its
output with known answers.

**Corrections.** If you find an error, please see the Contact page. We fix confirmed errors and update
the "Last updated" date on the affected page.
