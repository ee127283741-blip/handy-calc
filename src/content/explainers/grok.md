Grok is the AI assistant most closely tied to X, the platform where founders like [Tweet Hunter](/cases/tweet-hunter/)'s Tibo built their audiences. Its main difference from other assistants is live access to what is happening on X right now.

## What is Grok AI?

Grok is an AI assistant from xAI. xAI describes it as "AI that is useful for everyone" that lets you "chat, search, reason, and create — all in one place" ([xAI](https://x.ai/grok)). It is available on the web, on iOS and Android, on X and through Grok Bot, and developers can use it through xAI's API.

## What can Grok do?

| Feature | What xAI says it does |
|---|---|
| Search | Searches the web and X live, with citations from primary sources |
| Multi-agent mode | Several agents work in parallel; each shows its reasoning |
| Imagine | Text-to-image and text-to-video; videos up to 15 seconds at 720p, images up to 2K |
| Voice | Natural conversations with sub-second latency |
| Files and vision | Summarises documents and PDFs; analyses screenshots and photos |
| Memory and Canvas | Remembers preferences; long-form editing with suggestions |
| Source | [xAI](https://x.ai/grok), checked 2 November 2026 |

It also supports more than 30 languages, custom instructions and shareable conversations with synced history across devices.

## How much does Grok cost?

xAI says Grok is free to try on the web and in the apps, and that SuperGrok adds higher limits, priority access and multi-agent reasoning ([xAI](https://x.ai/grok), checked 2 November 2026). Grok models are also part of [Cursor](/what-is/cursor/)'s plans: Cursor's pricing page lists a shared usage pool for Cursor and Grok starting with its $8 Plus plan ([Cursor pricing](https://cursor.com/pricing)).

## How does Grok compare with ChatGPT and Perplexity?

[ChatGPT](/what-is/chatgpt/) is broader, with plugins, Codex and always-on agents. [Perplexity](/what-is/perplexity/) is a dedicated answer engine with citations on every response. Grok sits between them: a general assistant whose search is strongest on live news and conversation on X. For founders who market on X, that makes it useful for tracking trends and replies in real time.
