Microsoft Copilot brings AI to the apps most office workers already use every day. That is a big deal for small AI products: [Formula Bot](/cases/formula-bot/), for example, built a business writing Excel formulas with AI, and Microsoft now puts its own AI assistant inside Excel.

## What is Microsoft Copilot?

Microsoft describes Copilot as "an AI-powered assistant designed to help you be more productive" ([Microsoft](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-copilot/for-individuals)). Its main home is Microsoft 365, where Microsoft calls it "the AI for work" and lists it in Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Outlook, OneDrive and Teams.

## How much does Microsoft Copilot cost?

For individuals, Copilot comes with Microsoft 365 subscriptions rather than as a separate product.

| Plan (US) | Monthly | Yearly |
|---|---|---|
| Microsoft 365 Personal | $9.99 | $99.99 |
| Microsoft 365 Family | $12.99 | $129.99 |
| Microsoft 365 Premium | $19.99 | $199.99 |
| Microsoft 365 Pro | $99.99 | Monthly billing only |
| Source | [Microsoft](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-copilot/for-individuals), checked 2 November 2026 | |

Microsoft notes that AI features are available only to the subscription owner and cannot be shared, even on Family plans.

## What can you do with Copilot in Office apps?

The value is context. Because Copilot lives inside the documents, spreadsheets, emails and meetings you already have, you can ask it to work with your own files instead of copying them into a separate chat window. That is the same "put AI where people work" idea behind browser assistants like Merlin.

## How does Copilot compare with ChatGPT and Gemini?

[ChatGPT](/what-is/chatgpt/) is a standalone assistant that also offers Excel, Word, PowerPoint and Google Sheets extensions on paid plans. [Gemini](/what-is/gemini/) plays the same role inside Google's apps that Copilot plays inside Microsoft's. The companies also cooperate: TechCrunch reported that OpenAI is working with Microsoft to connect its [dots](/what-is/chatgpt-dots/) agents to Microsoft's Agent 365 security controls ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/29/openai-launches-dots-its-bubbly-agentic-avatar/)). For most people the choice follows the office suite they already use.
