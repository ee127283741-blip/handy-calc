ChatGPT made AI assistants mainstream, and it shaped many businesses in this library. [TypingMind](/cases/typingmind/) started as a better interface for ChatGPT, [Chatbase](/cases/chatbase/) began as "ChatGPT for your data", and [Magai](/cases/magai/) was born from its founder's frustrations with ChatGPT. Here is what ChatGPT is today.

## What is ChatGPT?

ChatGPT is OpenAI's AI assistant. You can chat with it, upload files, create images, talk to it by voice, run deep research, build projects and schedule tasks. It runs on OpenAI's GPT model family; the pricing page lists models including GPT-6.1 Sol, GPT-6 Astra, GPT-6 Sol and GPT-6 Luna, alongside earlier GPT-5.6 models ([OpenAI](https://chatgpt.com/pricing/), checked 2 November 2026).

It is available on the web, iOS and Android, and through desktop apps.

## What can ChatGPT do?

OpenAI's feature list has grown well beyond chat. It includes search, memory with past chats, image generation, voice with video, deep research, data analysis, study mode, a built-in browser, plugins and skills, scheduled tasks, projects and extensions for Excel, Word, PowerPoint and Google Sheets. Two features stand out for builders: [Codex](/what-is/codex/), OpenAI's coding agent, and [dots](/what-is/chatgpt-dots/), always-on agents that work toward goals in the background on the Pro plan.

## What are the ChatGPT plans?

OpenAI shows prices in local currency, and they vary by country, so this page lists what each plan includes rather than a single price.

| Plan | Who it is for (OpenAI's description) | Highlights |
|---|---|---|
| Free | Trying out ChatGPT | Unlimited text chats; limited uploads, images, voice, research, memory and Codex |
| Go | Longer conversations | More messages with tools, uploads, images and voice; may include ads |
| Plus | Advanced work and productivity | Advanced reasoning with GPT-6, projects, scheduled tasks, custom GPTs, expanded Codex |
| Pro | Research and coding | Three usage tiers, Pro reasoning with GPT-6 Astra, longer Codex sessions, a dot agent, monthly API credits |
| Business and Enterprise | Teams | Shared workspaces and admin controls |
| Source | [OpenAI pricing](https://chatgpt.com/pricing/) | Checked 2 November 2026 |

OpenAI says content may be used to train its models on these plans, with an opt-out available.

## How does ChatGPT compare with Claude and Gemini?

[Claude](/what-is/claude-ai/) and [Gemini](/what-is/gemini/) are its closest rivals, each with free tiers, paid plans, coding agents and APIs. ChatGPT's distinguishing pieces are its Go plan, its plugin catalogue and its always-on dots on Pro. Claude pairs its assistant with Claude Code and Claude in Chrome; Gemini is woven into Google's apps and storage. Products built on top of these assistants, like TypingMind and Magai, exist because many users want one interface for several of them.
