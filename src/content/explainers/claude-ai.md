Claude is the AI assistant that many of the founders in this library build with. [Bolt.new](/cases/bolt-new/) launched on a Claude model, and the non-technical founder behind [Launch Fast](/cases/launch-fast/) builds with Claude Code. Here is what Claude is, what it does and what it costs.

## What is Claude?

Claude is an AI assistant made by Anthropic. Anthropic describes it as "a helpful, intuitive, and powerful collaborator you can put to work on real tasks" ([Anthropic](https://claude.com/product/overview)). You can chat with it to write, research, analyse files and reason through problems, or let it carry out longer tasks on your behalf.

Claude is also a family of models. Anthropic lists four tiers: Mythos (which includes Fable), Opus, Sonnet and Haiku. Larger tiers are built for harder reasoning; smaller ones are faster and cheaper.

## Where can you use Claude?

Almost everywhere you work. Anthropic says you can use Claude on the web, on desktop or on your phone, and that "Claude lives inside Chrome, ready wherever you browse." Developers reach the same models through the Claude Platform API, and programmers can use [Claude Code](/what-is/claude-code/) in the terminal, editor, desktop app or browser.

| Surface | What it is for |
|---|---|
| Claude.ai (web, desktop, mobile) | Chat, files, projects and Claude Cowork tasks |
| Claude in Chrome | Browsing and acting on web pages |
| Claude Code | Coding agent for software projects |
| Claude Platform (API) | Building your own products on Claude models |

## What can Claude do?

According to Anthropic's product page, Claude can search the web for current information, connect to the apps you already use (including interactive MCP apps), remember context from past conversations, run several tasks at the same time and run work again on a schedule you choose. A permissions setting makes Claude show its plan and wait for your approval before acting, and you can build your own connectors, MCP servers and local desktop extensions ([Anthropic](https://claude.com/product/overview)).

For startups, the most common uses in our case library are coding and product writing. [Gojiberry AI](/cases/gojiberry-ai/), for example, lists Claude alongside Gemini and ChatGPT in its stack.

## How much does Claude cost?

Claude has a free plan. Pro costs $20 a month, or $17 a month billed annually; Max costs $100 a month with five times Pro's usage; and Ultra costs $200 a month with ten times Pro's weekly usage and no five-hour limit ([Anthropic pricing](https://claude.com/pricing), checked 2 November 2026). Team plans start at $25 per seat a month ($20 billed annually), and Enterprise is $20 per seat a month billed annually plus usage at API rates.

## How does Claude compare with ChatGPT and Gemini?

[ChatGPT](/what-is/chatgpt/) from OpenAI and [Gemini](/what-is/gemini/) from Google are Claude's closest rivals. All three are general AI assistants with free tiers, paid plans, coding agents and APIs. The practical differences are in each company's ecosystem: ChatGPT bundles Codex and always-on "dots" agents, Gemini plugs into Google apps and storage, and Claude pairs its assistant with Claude Code and Claude in Chrome. Many founders use more than one, choosing per task.
