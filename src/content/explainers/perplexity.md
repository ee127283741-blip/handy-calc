Perplexity changed how many people search: instead of ten blue links, you get one answer with its sources numbered underneath. For founders that matters twice, as a research tool and as a new place customers discover products. Founders in this library, like the team behind [ChatSEO](/cases/chatseo/), build tools around exactly that shift.

## What is Perplexity AI?

Perplexity is an AI-powered answer engine. According to its help centre, it "uses advanced AI to search the internet in real-time," reads your question, looks through the web and condenses what it finds into a single reply ([Perplexity](https://www.perplexity.ai/help-center/en/articles/10352895-how-does-perplexity-work)). It draws on sources such as articles, websites and journals.

## How does Perplexity cite its sources?

Citations are the core of the product. "Each answer includes numbered citations linking to the original sources," Perplexity says, so readers can check a claim or open the original page. That makes it closer to a search engine than a chat assistant: the answer is only as good as the pages it cites.

| Feature | How it works |
|---|---|
| Real-time search | Searches the web for each question |
| Citations | Numbered links to the original sources in every answer |
| Model choice | Runs on models such as GPT-5 and Claude 4.6 Sonnet; "Best" picks per question |
| Pro Search | Limited per day on Free, extended on Pro |
| Files and apps | Pro can build reports, spreadsheets, dashboards and simple web apps |
| Source | [Perplexity help centre](https://www.perplexity.ai/help-center/en/articles/10352895-how-does-perplexity-work) |

## How much does Perplexity cost?

Perplexity has a free plan with a limited number of Pro Searches per day, and a paid Perplexity Pro plan with extended Pro Search and a model selector ([Perplexity](https://www.perplexity.ai/help-center/en/articles/10352895-how-does-perplexity-work), checked 2 November 2026). Its help centre has a collection on subscription plans and billing for current prices in your region.

## How does Perplexity compare with ChatGPT and Grok?

[ChatGPT](/what-is/chatgpt/) and [Grok](/what-is/grok/) can search the web too, but they are general assistants first. Perplexity is search first: every answer is grounded in cited pages. Grok's twist is live search across X; ChatGPT's is the breadth of everything else it does.

## Why does Perplexity matter for founders?

Because AI answer engines are becoming a traffic source. When Perplexity cites your page, readers can click through. Optimising for that is often called GEO, generative engine optimisation. [Leadmore AI](/cases/leadmore-ai/)'s founder was building a GEO product for exactly this reason. The practical advice is the same as good SEO: clear, sourced, specific pages that answer a question directly.
