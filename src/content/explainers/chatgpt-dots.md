"ChatGPT dots" is one of the fastest-rising AI searches of late 2026, and it is easy to confuse with the dots in ChatGPT's interface. Dots are a product: OpenAI's always-on agents. This page explains what they are, who can use them and how they compare with other agents.

## What are ChatGPT dots?

Dots are AI agents that keep working when you are not chatting. OpenAI announced them at its DevDay event on 29 September 2026 and calls them "remarkably capable, always-on agents built to handle everything" ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/29/openai-launches-dots-its-bubbly-agentic-avatar/)). TechCrunch reported that they run on GPT-6 Astra and are not tied to a particular device or interface: you give a dot a goal, and it pursues that goal in the background with minimal oversight.

## How do dots work?

You can launch a dot from [ChatGPT](/what-is/chatgpt/) or [Codex](/what-is/codex/) and message it through Slack, Teams and other workplace tools, with text messaging described as coming soon. "Specialist dots" can be given their own identities, credentials and tools through existing systems, and OpenAI is working with Microsoft to connect them to Agent 365 security controls.

OpenAI's examples include a dot that monitors customer feedback and implements fixes, and one that reruns an analysis whenever new experimental data arrives. OpenAI's stated vision is "teams of Dots working together on your behalf."

## How do dots compare with Codex and Hermes Agent?

TechCrunch noted that much of what dots do was already possible with Codex and similar agent tools; dots package those abilities around independent, long-running work. [Hermes Agent](/what-is/hermes-agent/) from Nous Research is an open-source alternative that also runs continuously, remembers past sessions and talks to you through Slack, Telegram and other apps, but you host it yourself and choose the model. Dots trade that control for a managed service inside ChatGPT.

## Should you rely on a dot for important work?

Not without checking. Dots act with minimal oversight, which is the point, but agents still make mistakes. Start with tasks where errors are easy to spot, such as monitoring and reporting, before giving a dot credentials to change real systems.
