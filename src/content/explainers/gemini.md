Gemini is Google's answer to ChatGPT and Claude, and it is built into products billions of people already use. In this library, [Gojiberry AI](/cases/gojiberry-ai/) lists Gemini in its stack alongside Claude and ChatGPT. Here is what Gemini is and how its plans work.

## What is Google Gemini?

Gemini is two things: Google's AI assistant, and the family of AI models behind it. Google calls Gemini "your personal Google AI assistant" and describes Gemini 4 Argon as its latest frontier model, built to handle difficult tasks end to end ([Google](https://gemini.google/about/)). Other model names on Google's pages include Gemini Omni Flash, Flash and Flash-Lite, Nano Banana for images and Genie 3, a world model.

## What can Gemini do?

Google's feature list includes Gemini Live (voice conversations), image, video and music generation, Deep Research, Personal Intelligence, Canvas, Storybook, a long context window, Gems (custom assistants) and importing your memory from other assistants. Related Google products include Google Flow for video, Gemini Notebook, Google Antigravity for building software and Project Genie. You can use Gemini in its app, on the web at gemini.google.com, on desktop and directly inside Google apps through Workspace.

## What are the Google AI plans?

| Plan | What it adds (Google's description) |
|---|---|
| Free | Flash-Lite, image generation and editing, Deep Research, Gemini Live, Canvas, Gems, 15 GB storage |
| Google AI Plus | 2x free limits, Flash models, video generation, 200 Flow credits, 400 GB storage |
| Google AI Pro | 4x free limits, Gemini 4 Argon, Pro and Flash models, basic Antigravity access, 5 TB storage |
| Google AI Ultra | Highest access, up to 20x Pro limits, Project Genie, YouTube Premium, 20 TB+ storage |
| Gemini Enterprise | Business at US$21/seat/mo; Standard or Plus at US$30/seat/mo |
| Source | [Google AI plans](https://gemini.google/subscriptions/), checked 2 November 2026 |

Consumer prices are shown in local currency and vary by country; Google lists Gemini Enterprise seats in US dollars.

## How does Gemini compare with ChatGPT and Claude?

[ChatGPT](/what-is/chatgpt/) and [Claude](/what-is/claude-ai/) offer similar assistants, plans and coding tools. Gemini's advantage is distribution: it sits inside Google's apps and its plans bundle storage and other Google services, so for people who live in Gmail, Docs and Drive it can be the most convenient option. ChatGPT leads with plugins and always-on agents; Claude pairs its assistant with Claude Code and Claude in Chrome.
