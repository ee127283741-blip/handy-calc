```text
Act as an experienced estate agent copywriter. Write a listing for this property.

Facts (use only these; mark any assumption as [confirm]): {{property_facts}}.
Location: {{location}}.
Likely buyer: {{buyer_profile}}.
Compliance: {{compliance_notes}}.

Deliver:
1. Headline (max 10 words) leading with the strongest fact.
2. Main description (150–200 words): start with the feature that sets it apart, then walk through the home in the order a viewer would, then the location. Use specific facts (sizes, distances, ages of fittings) over adjectives.
3. Key features: 6–8 bullets.
4. Portal version (80 words) and a social post (40 words plus a call to book a viewing).
5. A photo shot list of 10 images in recommended order, with what each should show.

Rules: describe the property, not the people who should buy it (no "perfect for young professionals" or similar wording that could be discriminatory); no superlatives that cannot be proven; do not mention anything not in the facts.
```

## Tips

- Check measurements and travel times yourself; mistakes here can breach consumer protection rules.
- Fair housing rules differ by country; in the US, avoid wording about family status, religion, disability or nationality.
