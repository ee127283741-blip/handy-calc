```text
Cinematic concept art of {{scene}}. {{time_and_weather}}. Mood: {{mood}}.
Focal point: {{focal_point}} — the brightest and most contrasting element in the frame, placed on a third.
Depth: clear foreground (dark silhouetted rocks or plants framing the bottom corners), midground (the main structure), background (atmospheric, desaturated, fading into fog) — strong aerial perspective.
Lighting: cool ambient twilight with a warm accent light from the focal point; light shafts through fog; rim light on edges facing the sky.
Composition: wide establishing shot, low horizon, leading lines guiding the eye to the focal point; small human figure for scale.
Style: painterly digital matte painting, cinematic colour grading, high detail in the focal area and simplified shapes elsewhere.
Aspect ratio 21:9.

Negative prompt: cluttered composition, multiple focal points, flat lighting, oversaturated, text, watermark, frame, signature, low detail, distorted perspective.
```

## Tips

- Ask for 3 thumbnails in black and white first to pick the strongest value composition, then add colour.
- Do not reference living artists by name; describe the qualities you want instead.
