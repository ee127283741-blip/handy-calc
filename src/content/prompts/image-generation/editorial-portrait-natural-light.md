Editorial environmental portrait of {{person}} in {{location}}. Expression: {{mood}}.
Composition: subject on the right third, waist-up, environment visible on the left to tell her story; eye level; slight foreground element softly out of focus for depth.
Light: soft natural window light from the left as the only key, gentle falloff to shadow on the far side of the face, no flash, catchlights visible in the eyes.
Camera: 35mm lens look at f/2.8, focus on the nearest eye, natural background blur, fine film-like grain.
Skin and detail: realistic skin texture with pores and fine lines, natural imperfections, no retouching or beauty filter; hands anatomically correct.
Colour: muted, natural palette, slightly warm highlights, gentle contrast.
Style: documentary magazine portraiture, honest and unposed.
Aspect ratio 4:5.

Negative prompt: plastic skin, airbrushed, beauty filter, extra fingers, deformed hands, distorted face, cartoon, oversaturated, studio backdrop, text, watermark, harsh flash.

## Tips

- Describe people generically; never use a real, identifiable person without their consent.
- If hands look wrong, ask for a composition where hands rest naturally or are partly out of frame.
