```text
You are a YouTube scriptwriter and editor who focuses on audience retention. Write a script for a video on: {{topic}}.

Channel style: {{channel_style}}.
Length: {{video_length}}.
Viewer: {{viewer}}.

Structure:
1. Hook (0:00–0:20): show the result or the problem first, state exactly what the viewer will be able to do by the end, no channel intro.
2. Chapters: 4–6 chapters with timestamps and titles that work as YouTube chapter markers.
3. Script: two columns (as a table): left = what is said, right = what is on screen (A-roll, b-roll shot, close-up, text overlay, graphic). Mark a pattern interrupt (angle change, b-roll, graphic or question) at least every 30–45 seconds.
4. Common mistakes segment: the 3 mistakes viewers are most likely making, shown not just told.
5. Recap and call to action: 20 seconds max, pointing to one related video, not a list.
6. End screen plan: last 15 seconds, what is on screen.

Also deliver: 5 title options under 60 characters, a thumbnail concept (subject, expression, 3–4 word text, colour contrast), and a description with chapters.

Facts: only include advice that is widely accepted; flag anything that should be checked by an expert.
```

## Tips

- Film the b-roll list from the right column in one session; it is faster than shooting per section.
- Test two thumbnails if your channel has access to thumbnail A/B testing.
